Division 16 of 25 · CIR Series

Cable, Connector & Circuit Records

The 25 forms that establish complete electrical-circuit traceability for the AGL system — from cable procurement, drum and identification through route, installation, jointing, termination, connector control, testing, series-circuit topology, energization, fault history, modification, isolation, restoration and final circuit history. This is the master traceability and lifecycle-record layer for every AGL series circuit, because a single fault on one primary cable, joint, connector or transformer can affect an entire operational section. It integrates strongly with Divisions 02, 04, 06, 08, 09, 11, 12, 13 and 14.

A · Cable Master Records

5 of 5 forms
CIR-01 Cable Schedule Schedule
Purpose

Maintains the master schedule of all AGL primary and secondary cables associated with a project or installation.

Key Fields
  • Project/Airport / drawing reference / circuit reference / cable schedule revision
  • Cable ID, cable type, size, conductor material, insulation type, voltage rating
  • Manufacturer, part number, cable drum number, cable length
  • From / to / route / circuit ID / installation date
  • Installed length / remaining length / joint and termination locations
  • Status: planned, procured, delivered, installed, tested, energized, modified, abandoned
  • Prepared by / checked by / approved by
CIR-02 Cable Drum Register Register
Purpose

Provides complete traceability of cable from delivery through installation.

Key Fields
  • Drum ID, manufacturer, cable type, size, length, batch/lot number
  • Manufacturing date, supplier, certificate reference
  • Delivery date, PO number, inspection status, drum and cable-end condition
  • Test certificate reference
  • Cable used, project/circuit, start/end location, length installed, balance length
  • Final status: fully used / partially used / returned / damaged / rejected
CIR-03 Cable Identification Record Record
Purpose

Establishes unique identification of every installed cable.

Key Fields
  • Cable ID / circuit ID / cable type / size
  • From / to / route / drum ID / installation date
  • Cable marker, cable tag, phase/polarity where applicable
  • Circuit number and directional identification
  • Identification installed / drawing updated / cable correctly traced
Feeds CIR-13 Circuit Identification.
CIR-04 Cable Laying Record Record
Purpose

Documents actual installation of AGL cable.

Key Fields
  • Cable ID, drum ID, circuit, route, start point, end point, date, installation team
  • Trench/duct readiness, cable handling, minimum bending radius, pulling method
  • Cable protection, separation requirements, route condition
  • Warning/identification system
  • Planned length vs actual length, installation status, remarks
Feeds CIR-05 Cable Route Record.
CIR-05 Cable Route Record Record
Purpose

Establishes the physical route and location of each cable.

Key Fields
  • Cable ID / circuit ID / start / intermediate points / end
  • Duct/trench, crossing points, manholes/pits, base cans, joint locations
  • Drawing reference, as-built reference, GPS/coordinate information
  • Route photographs and revision
  • Route verified / requires correction
Feeds the as-built drawing and asset/cable database.

  • Continuity, insulation resistance, functional test
  • Installer / inspector / QA-QC approval

CIR-08 Cable Test Record Test Record
Purpose

Provides the master record of electrical tests performed on installed cables.

Key Fields
  • Cable ID, circuit ID, cable size, length, from/to, test date
  • Test equipment ID, calibration status, operator
  • Insulation resistance, continuity, earth/ground-related tests
  • Test voltage, test duration, measured result, acceptance criterion, pass/fail

Feeds CIR-09 Cable Insulation Record and CIR-10 Cable Continuity Record.
CIR-09 Cable Insulation Record Test Record
Purpose

Records insulation-resistance measurements for cable sections and series circuits.

Key Fields
  • Cable ID, circuit ID, test section, test voltage, test duration
  • Instrument ID, temperature/environmental conditions
  • Measured insulation resistance vs minimum acceptance requirement
  • Pass/fail and remarks
  • Trend: previous value → current value → change → trend
CIR-10 Cable Continuity Record Test Record
Purpose

Confirms electrical continuity of installed cable conductors and circuit paths.

Key Fields

  • Polarity where applicable, connection condition
  • Pass/fail

How Division 16 Connects

These 25 forms build one-to-one traceability at cable, connector and circuit level — from procurement to final decommissioning — so that virtually every AGL failure, maintenance event, commissioning activity and performance issue can be traced back to a specific circuit, cable, joint, connector, transformer or fixture location.

Frequently Asked Questions

Quick answers about the 25 Division 16 Cable, Connector & Electrical Circuit forms, how they are grouped, and how they connect to the rest of the library.

Q1What is Division 16 — Cable, Connector & Electrical Circuit Records in the AGL Engineering Forms & Templates library?

Division 16 is a set of 25 cable, connector & electrical circuit records (CIR-01 to CIR-25) in the AGL Engineering Forms & Templates library. It establishes complete electrical-circuit traceability for the AGL system, from cable procurement, drum and identification through route, installation, jointing, termination, connector control, testing, series-circuit topology, energization, faults, modification, isolation, restoration and final circuit history. The forms are grouped into 9 categories: Cable Master Records, Cable Joints & Terminations, Cable Testing, Series Circuit Records, Circuit Operation & Faults, Connector Control, Circuit Modification & Extension, Circuit Isolation & Restoration and Master Circuit History.

Q2Which forms are included in Cable Master Records, Cable Joints & Terminations and Cable Testing?

Cable Master Records (5 forms): CIR-01 Cable Schedule, CIR-02 Cable Drum Register, CIR-03 Cable Identification Record, CIR-04 Cable Laying Record, CIR-05 Cable Route Record; Cable Joints & Terminations (2 forms): CIR-06 Cable Joint Record, CIR-07 Cable Termination Record; Cable Testing (3 forms): CIR-08 Cable Test Record, CIR-09 Cable Insulation Record, CIR-10 Cable Continuity Record.

Q3Which forms are included in Series Circuit Records, Circuit Operation & Faults and Connector Control?

Series Circuit Records (3 forms): CIR-11 Series Circuit Record, CIR-12 Circuit Schedule, CIR-13 Circuit Identification; Circuit Operation & Faults (2 forms): CIR-14 Circuit Energization Record, CIR-15 Circuit Fault Record; Connector Control (5 forms): CIR-16 Connector Installation, CIR-17 Connector Torque Record, CIR-18 Connector Inspection, CIR-19 Connector Replacement, CIR-20 Connector Failure Record.

Q4Which forms are included in Circuit Modification & Extension, Circuit Isolation & Restoration and Master Circuit History?

Circuit Modification & Extension (2 forms): CIR-21 Circuit Modification Record, CIR-22 Circuit Extension Record; Circuit Isolation & Restoration (2 forms): CIR-23 Circuit Isolation Record, CIR-24 Circuit Restoration Record; Master Circuit History (1 form): CIR-25 Circuit History Register.

Q5What is the Series Circuit Record and the Circuit History Register?

The Series Circuit Record (CIR-11) establishes the complete topology of an AGL series circuit and is described on the page as one of the most important records in the division. The Circuit History Register (CIR-25) is the master historical record of every AGL series circuit.

Q6What is the difference between the Cable Schedule, Cable Drum Register, Cable Identification, Cable Laying and Cable Route records?

The Cable Schedule (CIR-01) is the master schedule of primary and secondary cables. The Cable Drum Register (CIR-02) gives traceability from delivery through installation, and the Cable Identification Record (CIR-03) establishes unique identification of each installed cable. The Cable Laying Record (CIR-04) documents actual installation, and the Cable Route Record (CIR-05) establishes each cable's physical route and location.

Q7Which forms control circuit isolation, restoration and energization?

The Circuit Isolation Record (CIR-23) establishes controlled isolation of a circuit before maintenance, modification or emergency work, and the Circuit Restoration Record (CIR-24) documents its safe return to service. The Circuit Energization Record (CIR-14) records every controlled energization after installation, maintenance, isolation or testing.

Q8Can the forms in Division 16 be downloaded and filled in?

Yes. Each form has a Download as Word button that produces an uncontrolled sample Word template with letterhead, a document-control block, ruled entry fields and a three-tier approval table, ready to print or fill on-screen. The forms can also be searched by keyword or form ID.

Q9How does Division 16 connect to the rest of the AGL documentation system?

Division 16 is the electrical backbone of the documentation system. The cable schedule and drum register feed installation and route records, which feed jointing, termination and connector control, then testing and series-circuit topology, then energization, fault management, modification and isolation records. It lets virtually every failure, maintenance event and commissioning activity be traced back to a specific circuit, cable, joint, connector, transformer or fixture location.
