LED & Light Source Comparison

LED DRIVER VS TRANSFORMER-BASED LAMP SYSTEM

Electronic Control vs Electromagnetic Transformation — an LED driver is an electronic power-conditioning device designed to operate LEDs correctly, while a transformer-based lamp system primarily uses a transformer to provide the required voltage or current to a conventional lamp. The two components perform fundamentally different jobs and should not be treated as interchangeable.

07 · Intensity Control

How Output Is Adjusted

LED Driver

An LED driver can regulate electrical conditions electronically: control signal → LED driver → LED current control → light output.

Transformer-Based Lamp

Lamp intensity may be influenced by the electrical supply provided to the lamp: supply level → transformer output → filament temperature → light output.

Thus, the intensity-control mechanisms can be quite different between the two systems.

08 · Efficiency

Where the Electrical Energy Goes

LED Driver System

Electrical input → driver losses → LED → light. The driver has electrical losses, but LEDs generally offer higher source efficiency.

Transformer + Filament Lamp

Electrical input → transformer losses → hot filament → light + heat. Filament systems generally convert a larger proportion of electrical energy into heat.

11 · Thermal Behaviour

Heat at the Source

LED

LED produces light and heat; LEDs require thermal management — a defined thermal path/heat sink — to protect the semiconductor junction.

Filament Lamp

The filament reaches very high temperature, producing both heat and light. Filament lamps operate with very high source temperatures.

13 · Common Misconceptions

Myths vs Facts

Myth: "An LED driver is just a transformer"
Fact

Not necessarily. A transformer mainly transfers electrical energy and changes voltage/current relationships through electromagnetic induction. An LED driver is designed to provide the controlled electrical conditions required by LEDs and may contain rectifiers, switching electronics, current regulators, control circuits, and protection circuits.

Myth: "An LED system does not need a transformer"
Fact

Not necessarily. In an AGL system, an LED fixture may still operate downstream of an isolation transformer, while also containing an internal driver: CCR → Isolation Transformer → LED Driver → LED.

Myth: "A transformer controls LED current"
Fact

Only in a limited sense when combined with other circuitry. A transformer by itself is generally not equivalent to a properly designed LED current-regulating driver.

15 · Engineering Verdict

LED Driver vs Transformer-Based Lamp System

Engineering Verdict

An LED driver is active electronic power control for LEDs. A transformer-based lamp system is electromagnetic power transformation followed by a filament lamp.

In AGL, the isolation transformer and LED driver should not be considered interchangeable. The transformer forms part of the power-transfer and isolation arrangement, while the driver provides the controlled electrical conditions needed by the LED source.

LED SystemSupply → driver → controlled current → LED → light.
Lamp SystemSupply → transformer → lamp → heat + light.

16 · FAQ

Frequently Asked Questions

What is the simplest way to remember the difference?

Driver controls an LED. Transformer supplies a lamp.

Does an LED fixture in an AGL circuit still need an isolation transformer?

Yes, potentially. An LED fixture may still operate downstream of an isolation transformer while also containing its own internal driver: CCR → Isolation Transformer → LED Driver → LED.

Can a transformer regulate LED current by itself?

Only in a limited sense when combined with other circuitry. A transformer alone is generally not equivalent to a properly designed LED current-regulating driver.

Why does an LED fixture have more internal electrical complexity?

Because it typically retains the isolation transformer used in the AGL series circuit and adds an LED driver stage, LED array, and thermal management on top.

Which system generally requires more frequent light-source replacement?

Transformer-based lamp systems typically require more frequent lamp replacement, whereas LED systems usually have lower routine source-replacement needs.

Where does most of the failure risk sit in each system?

In LED systems, electronic component failure in the driver/LED is more significant. In transformer-based lamp systems, failure focus is usually on the lamp/filament and the transformer.

IEC safety requirements (61347-2-13) and performance requirements (62384) for d.c./a.c.-supplied electronic controlgear (drivers) for LED modules.

Final Engineering Note

Do not treat the AGL isolation transformer and an LED fixture's internal driver as the same component in a project specification — confirm which one a given requirement or fault actually concerns.

An LED driver is not a transformer — one electronically regulates power for a semiconductor source, the other transforms supply for a conventional lamp.
