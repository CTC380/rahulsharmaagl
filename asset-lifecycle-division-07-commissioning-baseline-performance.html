Commissioning & Baseline Asset Performance

The technical gateway between installation/handover and the operational lifecycle of the AGL asset. Division 06 establishes that the equipment has been installed, identified, documented and handed over — Division 07 proves that the installed asset performs correctly, establishes its initial measurable condition, and creates the baseline against which all future inspection, maintenance, testing, reliability and condition assessment will be judged.

Initial Issue

The question this division answers — Does the installed asset perform correctly, what is its verified starting condition, and what baseline values will every future inspection, test and condition assessment be compared against?

Where Division 07 Sits in the Lifecycle

Division 07 should be directly aligned with your AGL Testing & Commissioning Manual: the Manual defines how each measurement and commissioning test is performed, while the Asset Lifecycle Guide defines how the resulting verified values become the permanent baseline of the asset and are used throughout its life.

COM-02 Electrical Baseline
Purpose

Establishes the initial measured electrical condition of the AGL system.

Depending on the Asset, Record
  • Voltage
  • Current
  • Insulation resistance
  • Earth resistance
  • Continuity
  • Circuit resistance
  • Leakage/current characteristics
  • CCR output
  • Transformer electrical characteristics
  • Protection operation
  • Control signals

COMMISSIONING CURRENTPERIODIC CURRENTTRENDABNORMAL DEVIATIONINVESTIGATION

COM-03 Photometric Baseline
Purpose

Establishes the initial optical performance of the AGL system. This should be one of the strongest links between Division 07 and your AGL Photometry Testing Manual.

Depending on the System, Establish
  • Intensity
  • Photometric distribution
  • Horizontal/vertical performance where applicable
  • Beam characteristics
  • Alignment
  • Color/chromaticity where applicable
  • Uniformity
  • Light output
  • Compliance status
Applicable Assets

Runway lights, taxiway lights, approach lights, PAPI, and other photometrically controlled AGL systems.

Record

  • Model
  • Serial number
  • Rating
  • Circuit served
  • Input voltage
  • Output current

  • Output regulation
  • Power factor, where applicable

  • Temperature/thermal observations where applicable

Lifecycle Use

COM-14 Initial Reliability Baseline
Purpose

Establishes the starting point for future reliability analysis. At commissioning, there may not yet be enough operational history to calculate meaningful long-term failure rates — so the initial reliability parameters and assumptions are recorded instead.

Record
  • Equipment type
  • Manufacturer
  • Model
  • Criticality
  • Expected service life
  • Reliability data supplied by manufacturer
  • Known failure modes
  • Warranty period
  • Spare availability
  • Redundancy
  • Single points of failure
  • Initial defects
  • Commissioning failures, if any
Later

Operational history can progressively establish failure rate, MTBF, MTTR, repeat failures and reliability trend — linking Division 07 to your later Failure Database & Reliability Engineering functions.

COM-15 Initial Condition Assessment
Purpose

Establishes the physical and technical condition immediately after commissioning.

Assess
  • Physical — damage, corrosion, contamination, mechanical condition
  • Electrical — connections, insulation, earthing, electrical performance
  • Optical — lens, light output, alignment, photometric performance
  • Civil — base can, mounting, foundation, drainage, surrounding surface
  • Documentation — asset identification, as-built, test records, manuals

COM-17 Baseline Performance Report
Purpose

Consolidates the commissioning results into a single controlled lifecycle document.

Recommended Report Structure
  • A. Asset Identification — Asset ID, equipment, manufacturer, model, serial number, location
  • B. Installation — installation date, commissioning date, project, contractor, as-built reference
  • C. Electrical Performance — electrical, CCR, transformer and cable results
  • D. Photometric Performance — fixture, PAPI and relevant photometric measurements
  • E. Functional Performance — controls, monitoring, alarms, switching, dimming
  • F. Energy Performance — initial energy measurements
  • G. Condition — initial condition rating, photographs, defects
  • H. Reliability — initial reliability information
  • I. Acceptance — acceptance status, outstanding actions
  • J. Lifecycle Baseline — final approved baseline values

Most Important Design Principle

Division 07 should remain the technical gateway between installation/handover and the operational lifecycle of the AGL asset. It does not install the equipment (Division 06) and it does not run day-to-day operations (Division 08) — it proves the installed asset performs correctly and freezes its verified starting condition as the permanent baseline.

The commissioning baseline is the birth certificate of an operational AGL asset. At commissioning, the airport should know what asset is installed, where it is installed, its initial condition, how it performs electrically, photometrically and functionally, how much energy it consumes, its initial reliability expectation, what documentation supports it, its approved baseline values, and whether it has been formally accepted.

Recommended Commissioning & Baseline Frameworks

Two structures are recommended for the underlying AGL Testing & Commissioning Manual and this Guide to share as common reference points.

Five Commissioning Levels
Level 1 — Physical VerificationIs the correct equipment installed correctly?
Level 2 — Electrical VerificationIs the electrical system safe and technically correct?
Level 3 — Functional VerificationDoes the equipment perform its intended function?
Level 4 — Performance VerificationDoes it achieve the required performance?
Level 5 — Lifecycle BaselineWhat is the verified starting condition for future asset management?
Six-Level Baseline Hierarchy
Level 1 — Asset BaselineIdentity + location + condition
Level 2 — Component BaselineFixture, transformer, cable, sign, PAPI, CCR
Level 3 — System BaselineSeries circuit, CCR, ALCMS, control
Level 4 — Performance BaselineElectrical, photometric, functional, energy
Level 5 — Operational BaselineActual operating behaviour
Level 6 — Reliability BaselineInitial reliability assumptions and future failure history

This baseline hierarchy makes the subsequent Inspection, Testing, Preventive Maintenance, Failure Analysis and Asset Condition Assessment divisions much stronger.

Frequently Asked Questions

Quick answers about Commissioning & Baseline Asset Performance in the AGL Asset Lifecycle Guide.

Q1What is Division 07 — Commissioning & Baseline Asset Performance in the AGL Asset Lifecycle Guide?

Division 07 is the technical gateway between handover and operations. It covers 19 topics on commissioning verification, electrical/photometric/functional baselines, CCR/transformer/cable/fixture/PAPI/sign/ALCMS baselines, energy and operational baselines, initial reliability, condition assessment, baseline records and asset acceptance.

Q2What is commissioning verification?

Commissioning verification confirms that the installed AGL system satisfies the approved design, specifications and commissioning requirements. It checks items such as correct equipment, location, configuration, circuit, wiring and connections.

Q3Which baselines are established at commissioning?

Division 07 covers electrical, photometric, functional, energy, operational and initial reliability baselines. It also has baselines for CCRs, transformers, cables, fixtures, PAPI, signs and ALCMS.

Q4What does the electrical baseline measure?

It establishes the initial measured electrical condition of the AGL system, including voltage, current, insulation resistance, earth resistance, continuity and circuit resistance.

Q5What is the photometric baseline?

It establishes the initial optical performance of the AGL system, covering intensity, photometric distribution and beam characteristics. It is one of the strongest links to the AGL Photometry Testing Manual.

Q6Why is the initial reliability baseline different from other baselines?

At commissioning there may not yet be enough operational history to calculate meaningful long-term failure statistics. The initial reliability baseline therefore records the starting point for future analysis, such as equipment type, manufacturer, model, criticality, expected service life and manufacturer-supplied reliability data.

Q7How are baseline results recorded?

Every baseline measurement should have a controlled test record with asset ID, equipment, test type, date, location and operating condition. The baseline performance report then consolidates the results into a single controlled lifecycle document.

Q8When should acceptance criteria be defined?

Asset acceptance criteria should be defined before commissioning begins. They cover installation, electrical, functional, photometric and control and monitoring compliance, and after successful commissioning the asset moves to a defined lifecycle status.
