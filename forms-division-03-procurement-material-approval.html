Division 03 of 25 · PROC Series

Procurement & Material Approval Forms

The 25 forms that control every material entering an AGL project — from the first requisition to the final closeout. Grouped into Material & Purchase Initiation, Technical Compliance & Evaluation, Material Delivery & Inspection, Traceability & Certification, and Spare Parts & Procurement Control, this division is the procurement-control chain of the AGL project documentation system: Material Requirement → Purchase → Technical Submittal → Compliance → Vendor Approval → PO Technical Review → Delivery → Receipt Inspection → Traceability → Certificates → Warranty → Spare Parts → Procurement Closeout.

Initial Issue

Where this fits — Division 02 decides what the AGL system needs to be; this division is where that decision turns into real, traceable materials on site. Every item procured under this division should trace back to an approved selection sheet from Division 02, and no purchase order is released until the material has technical approval — nor is procurement considered closed (PROC-25) until every certificate, warranty, and traceability record is filed.

Frequently Asked Questions

Quick answers about the 25 Division 03 Procurement & Material Approval forms, how they are grouped, and how they connect to the rest of the library.

Q1What is Division 03 — Procurement & Material Approval Forms in the AGL Engineering Forms & Templates library?

Division 03 is a set of 25 procurement & material approval forms (PROC-01 to PROC-25) in the AGL Engineering Forms & Templates library. It controls every material entering an AGL project, from the first requisition to procurement closeout, as a procurement-control chain covering purchase, technical approval, delivery, inspection, traceability, certificates, warranty and spares. The forms are grouped into 5 categories: Material & Purchase Initiation, Technical Compliance & Evaluation, Material Delivery & Inspection, Traceability & Certification and Spare Parts & Procurement Control.

Q2Which forms are included in Material & Purchase Initiation and Technical Compliance & Evaluation?

Material & Purchase Initiation (6 forms): PROC-01 Material Requisition, PROC-02 Purchase Requisition, PROC-03 Material Approval Form, PROC-04 Technical Submittal Form, PROC-05 Vendor Data Sheet, PROC-06 Manufacturer Data Sheet; Technical Compliance & Evaluation (6 forms): PROC-07 Product Compliance Sheet, PROC-08 Technical Compliance Matrix, PROC-09 Material Comparison Sheet, PROC-10 Vendor Comparison Sheet, PROC-11 Approved Vendor Register, PROC-12 Purchase Order Technical Review.

Q3Which forms are included in Material Delivery & Inspection and Traceability & Certification?

Material Delivery & Inspection (5 forms): PROC-13 Material Delivery Record, PROC-14 Material Receipt Inspection, PROC-15 Incoming Material Inspection, PROC-16 Material Rejection Report, PROC-17 Material Return Form; Traceability & Certification (4 forms): PROC-18 Material Traceability Record, PROC-19 Material Certificate Register, PROC-20 Certificate of Conformance Register, PROC-21 Warranty Document Register.

Q4Which forms are included in Spare Parts & Procurement Control?

Spare Parts & Procurement Control (4 forms): PROC-22 Spare Parts Procurement Form, PROC-23 Long Lead Item Tracker, PROC-24 Procurement Status Report, PROC-25 Procurement Closeout Checklist.

Q5What is the difference between the Product Compliance Sheet, the Technical Compliance Matrix and the comparison sheets?

The Product Compliance Sheet (PROC-07) determines, category by category, whether a proposed product complies with the project requirements. The Technical Compliance Matrix (PROC-08) goes further with a requirement-by-requirement comparison against the specification, recording deviations and reviewer status. The Material Comparison Sheet (PROC-09) compares several material options against one another, and the Vendor Comparison Sheet (PROC-10) compares competing suppliers side by side before a final vendor selection.

Q6What forms control materials when they arrive at site?

The Material Delivery Record (PROC-13) records the arrival of materials. The Material Receipt Inspection (PROC-14) checks quantity, physical condition, identification and documentation on receipt, and the Incoming Material Inspection (PROC-15) is the formal three-level quality inspection before materials enter project stock or installation. Materials that fail are documented in the Material Rejection Report (PROC-16), and the Material Return Form (PROC-17) controls their return to the supplier.

Q7How are traceability, certificates and warranties controlled in Division 03?

The Material Traceability Record (PROC-18) follows each material from procurement through to final installation. The Material Certificate Register (PROC-19), Certificate of Conformance Register (PROC-20) and Warranty Document Register (PROC-21) keep certificates and warranty information controlled and findable. The Procurement Closeout Checklist (PROC-25) confirms that every procurement record has been transferred into the project documentation system.

Q8Can the forms in Division 03 be downloaded and filled in?

Yes. Each form has a Download as Word button that produces an uncontrolled sample Word template with letterhead, a document-control block, ruled entry fields and a three-tier approval table, ready to print or fill on-screen. Checklist-format forms such as Incoming Material Inspection (PROC-15) download with a checkbox and a verified-by line for each item. The forms can also be searched by keyword or form ID.

Q9How does Division 03 connect to Division 02 and the rest of the library?

Division 02 decides what the AGL system needs to be, and Division 03 turns that decision into real, traceable materials on site. Every item procured should trace back to an approved selection sheet from Division 02, and no purchase order is released until the material has technical approval. Procurement is not considered closed until every certificate, warranty and traceability record is filed, and those records feed later commissioning, maintenance and failure analysis.
