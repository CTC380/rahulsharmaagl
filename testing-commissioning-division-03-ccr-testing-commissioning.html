Division 03 · CCR · Performance-Critical Power Control

CCR Testing & Commissioning

The Constant Current Regulator is the heart of every series AGL circuit — it does not just supply power, it maintains a precise, regulated current through the entire circuit regardless of load, temperature, or step command. Division 03 verifies that the CCR is correctly installed, electrically safe, correctly supplied, capable of holding the required output current, stable under operating conditions, responsive to intensity-step commands and load changes, capable of detecting and protecting against faults, and correctly integrated with local, remote, and communication/control functions.

Philosophy: a CCR should never be accepted solely because it produces output current. Division 03 treats the CCR as a performance-critical AGL power-control device, not a supply panel — acceptance demands correct supply, correct current, regulation, stability, step control, protection, alarms, local control, remote control, communication, and safety, all verified and documented together.

Acceptance Layers

The CCR commissioning evidence should establish five layers before the unit is released to system-level commissioning.

LayerQuestion It Answers
1. Installation IntegrityIs the CCR correctly installed?
2. Electrical IntegrityIs the CCR electrically safe and correctly connected?
3. Regulation PerformanceDoes it produce and maintain the required circuit current?
4. Control & ProtectionDoes it respond correctly to commands, faults, alarms, and emergency conditions?
5. Integration ReadinessCan it communicate correctly with the larger AGL control system?

Before Testing Begins

Two conditions should be closed out before energization and functional testing of the CCR begins.

Do Not Energize If

  • Major physical damage exists
  • Incorrect equipment has been installed
  • Input/output wiring is uncertain
  • Earth connection is incomplete
  • Control wiring is unidentified
  • Required covers or safety barriers are missing
  • Critical punch-list items remain open

Division Boundary

  • Division 03 verifies the CCR unit itself and its own communication function
  • Division 14 verifies complete ALCMS system integration
  • Division 19 verifies complete SCADA/system-level integration
  • The dedicated Earthing & Bonding division provides comprehensive earth continuity and resistance testing
  • A CCR is never accepted on output current alone — see the acceptance principle below

A

Pre-Energization & Supply

2 topics
TC-CCR-001CCR Pre-Energization Inspection
Verification Checks
  • Verify CCR identification/tag, manufacturer, model, serial number
  • Verify rated input, rated output, number of intensity steps
  • Verify circuit designation and installation location
  • Inspect enclosure, doors, locks, hinges, and covers
  • Inspect cooling/ventilation arrangements and internal components
  • Inspect terminals, wiring, busbars, contactors/switching devices
  • Inspect control components, display, indicators, and alarm devices
  • Verify correct location, secure mounting, cable entry, and cable glands
  • Verify input, output, control, and earth connections, and auxiliary supply
  • Verify clearance, access, and environmental condition
  • Confirm labels and warning signs are fitted and legible
TC-CCR-002CCR Input-Supply Verification
Verification Checks
  • Verify supply identification and rated voltage
  • Measure input voltage, phase-to-phase and phase-to-neutral voltage
  • Verify frequency and phase configuration where applicable
  • Verify supply polarity where applicable
  • Verify input cable identification and termination
  • Verify protective device rating and setting
  • Verify earthing and isolation arrangement
  • Record all applicable measured supply parameters
Acceptance / Record

Acceptance Criteria: the input supply satisfies the approved project/design requirement and the CCR manufacturer's requirement before energization proceeds.

B

Output & Regulation Performance

4 topics
TC-CCR-003CCR Output-Current Verification
Verification Checks
  • Verify output current at each applicable intensity step
  • Confirm correct circuit current for the connected AGL circuit
  • Record current indication on the CCR display where applicable
  • Measure current using an appropriate calibrated instrument
  • Confirm agreement between indicated and measured values
  • Record results against the project/manufacturer acceptance criterion for each step
Acceptance / Record

Acceptance Criteria: measured output current at every applicable step agrees with the CCR indication and satisfies the project/manufacturer criterion.

TC-CCR-004CCR Current Regulation Test
Verification Checks
  • Verify current regulation and stability of the regulated current
  • Verify correct response to each intensity command
  • Command each applicable step, allow stabilization, then measure and record current
  • Compare measured current against the acceptance criterion for that step
  • Verify repeatability across repeated commands to the same step
  • Evaluate for under-current, over-current, or unstable current
  • Evaluate for incorrect step current or a difference between indicated and measured current
Acceptance / Record

Acceptance Criteria: the CCR maintains the commanded series-circuit current within the applicable acceptance limits at every step, repeatably.

TC-CCR-005CCR Output Stability Test
Verification Checks
  • Observe output current and output voltage where required
  • Observe display indication and alarm status during steady-state operation
  • Watch for fluctuation, oscillation, or unexpected step changes
  • Watch for trip events during the observation period
  • Assess stability under normal load, different intensity steps, and representative circuit loading where specified
Acceptance / Record

Acceptance Criteria: output remains within the applicable specified operating limits without unexplained fluctuation or nuisance tripping.

TC-CCR-006CCR Intensity-Step Verification
Verification Checks
  • Verify the minimum applicable step, intermediate steps, and maximum step
  • Confirm correct sequence through all steps
  • Confirm correct output current and correct indication at each step
  • Confirm correct control response for each commanded step
  • Record commanded step, actual step, output current, indication, and result
Acceptance / Record

Acceptance Criteria: every commanded intensity level produces the correct CCR output current, indication, and control response, in the correct sequence.

C

Transition & Load Response

2 topics
TC-CCR-007CCR Step Transition Test
Verification Checks
  • Test representative transitions: low→medium, medium→high, high→medium, medium→low, minimum→maximum, maximum→minimum
  • Verify correct transition with no abnormal delay
  • Verify no unexpected trip and no unstable output during transition
  • Verify correct final current is reached and held
  • Verify correct indication and correct control response
  • Record from-step, to-step, command, response, final current, and result
Acceptance / Record

Acceptance Criteria: every tested transition settles cleanly at the correct final current, with correct indication and no abnormal delay, trip, or instability.

TC-CCR-008CCR Load-Response Test
Verification Checks
  • Verify current regulation under changing connected AGL circuit load
  • Verify output stability through the load change
  • Verify correct protection response where a protection function is expected to engage
  • Confirm no unexpected alarm and no nuisance trip occurs
  • Confirm correct recovery after the load change is complete
  • Test only under normal, reduced, increased, or representative circuit conditions permitted by the approved procedure
D

Protection, Alarms & Faults

3 topics
TC-CCR-009CCR Protection-Function Test
Verification Checks
  • Identify applicable protection functions: overcurrent, overvoltage, undervoltage, overtemperature, earth fault, output fault, input fault, internal equipment fault, circuit fault, emergency trip
  • Establish the protection condition using the approved test method
  • Verify the CCR detects the condition and takes appropriate protective action
  • Verify correct alarm/fault indication appears
  • Verify the CCR returns to a safe state
  • Verify reset/recovery operates correctly where applicable
  • Use manufacturer-approved simulation/test facilities where available
TC-CCR-010CCR Alarm Test
Verification Checks
  • Verify alarm generation for each applicable condition
  • Verify local indication, audible alarm where provided, and visual alarm
  • Verify alarm display detail and remote alarm transmission where applicable
  • Verify alarm reset operates correctly
  • Verify alarm history where applicable
  • Record alarm condition, local indication, remote indication, correct response, and reset
Acceptance / Record

Acceptance Criteria: every applicable alarm generates, indicates locally and remotely as designed, and resets correctly.

TC-CCR-011CCR Fault Indication Test
Verification Checks
  • Verify fault detection and fault indication for each applicable fault type
  • Verify fault code/message and display indication
  • Verify local alarm and remote alarm where applicable
  • Verify fault logging where provided
  • Verify fault reset and recovery
Acceptance / Record

Acceptance Criteria: the displayed fault corresponds logically with the actual test condition, and the unit resets and recovers correctly.

E

Emergency & Control

3 topics
TC-CCR-012CCR Emergency-Operation Test
Verification Checks
  • Verify emergency stop and emergency shutdown functions where provided
  • Verify emergency control and the resulting safe output condition
  • Verify local emergency indication and remote indication where applicable
  • Verify recovery/reset and restart behavior after the emergency condition clears
Acceptance / Record

Acceptance Criteria: the CCR enters the required safe condition when an emergency command is initiated, and recovers correctly.

TC-CCR-013CCR Remote-Control Test
Verification Checks
  • Verify remote ON/OFF command and intensity-step command
  • Verify status feedback and command acknowledgement
  • Verify alarm feedback, fault feedback, and communication status
  • Confirm the sequence: remote command → CCR response → status feedback → confirmation
Acceptance / Record

Acceptance Criteria: every remote command produces the correct CCR response with confirmed status feedback through the authorized remote-control interface.

TC-CCR-014CCR Local-Control Test
Verification Checks
  • Verify local ON/OFF and step selection
  • Verify control switches, push buttons, and selector switches
  • Verify local/remote selection and indications
  • Verify status feedback and emergency controls at the local interface
  • Confirm the selected operating mode produces the intended control behavior where local/remote modes exist
Acceptance / Record

Acceptance Criteria: the local control interface produces correct, unambiguous control behavior consistent with the selected local/remote mode.

F

Display, Communication & Electrical Integrity

5 topics
TC-CCR-015CCR Display Verification
Verification Checks
  • Verify input status, output current, and output voltage where displayed
  • Verify intensity step and operating mode indication
  • Verify alarm status and fault status indication
  • Verify temperature/status information where provided
  • Verify communication status and event/fault information where available
  • Compare physical condition, display indication, and actual measured condition
Acceptance / Record

Acceptance Criteria: the display accurately and consistently communicates the CCR's actual operating status.

TC-CCR-016CCR Communication Test
Verification Checks
  • Verify the communication link and CCR identification over the link
  • Verify status communication, current/intensity information
  • Verify alarm communication and fault communication
  • Verify remote command and command acknowledgement over the link
  • Verify communication-loss indication
Acceptance / Record

Acceptance Criteria: the CCR's own communication function is verified correct — full ALCMS/SCADA/PLC system integration is confirmed separately in Divisions 14 and 19.

TC-CCR-017CCR Earth Verification
Verification Checks
  • Verify the CCR earth conductor, earth terminal, and earth connection
  • Verify bonding and panel/enclosure bonding
  • Verify earth continuity and physical condition
  • Perform earth continuity and earth resistance/system verification electrical tests where specified
Acceptance / Record

Acceptance Criteria: CCR protective earthing is verified sound before and during commissioning; comprehensive earth continuity/resistance/bonding testing is completed by the dedicated Earthing & Bonding division.

TC-CCR-018CCR Insulation Test
Verification Checks
  • Verify applicable input circuit insulation
  • Verify internal electrical insulation where testable
  • Verify control-circuit insulation where applicable
  • Verify earth leakage condition and insulation resistance
  • Isolate/disconnect sensitive electronic components before testing where required
TC-CCR-019CCR Control-Wiring Test
Verification Checks
  • Verify input terminals, output terminals, and control supply
  • Verify remote-control wiring and local-control wiring
  • Verify alarm wiring, interlock wiring, and emergency wiring
  • Verify communication interface and status feedback wiring
  • Verify terminal identification and wiring continuity
  • Compare CCR wiring diagram, physical wiring, and terminal numbering
G

Acceptance & Closeout

3 topics
TC-CCR-020CCR Functional Acceptance
Verification Checks
  • Confirm completion of pre-energization inspection and input supply verification
  • Confirm completion of output current, current regulation, output stability, intensity steps, and step transitions
  • Confirm completion of load response, protection functions, alarms, and fault indications
  • Confirm completion of emergency operation, remote control, local control, and display verification
  • Confirm completion of communication, earth verification, insulation testing where applicable, and control wiring
  • Record final status: PASS, FAIL, RETEST, CONDITIONAL, or N/A
Acceptance / Record

Acceptance Criteria: correct supply + correct current + regulation + stability + step control + protection + alarm + local control + remote control + communication + safety, all verified and documented together — a CCR is never accepted on output current alone.

TC-CCR-021CCR Commissioning Checklist
Verification Checks
  • Identification: tag, manufacturer, model, serial number, rating, circuit designation
  • Physical installation: enclosure, mounting, doors/covers, internal condition, ventilation, cable entries, labels
  • Electrical: input supply, input voltage, earthing, insulation, control wiring, output circuit
  • Performance: output current, current regulation, output stability, intensity steps, step transitions, load response
  • Protection & alarms: protection functions, alarm functions, fault indication, emergency operation
  • Control & communication: local control, remote control, display, communication, status feedback
  • Documentation: test sheets, calibration records, punch-list, results evaluated, deviations, retests, commissioning report
Acceptance / Record

Acceptance Criteria: every item across all seven checklist sections is closed out or formally accepted with documented conditions before the CCR is released.

TC-CCR-022CCR Commissioning Report
Verification Checks
  • Record project information: airport, project, contract/package, CCR location, circuit designation, client, contractor, commissioning agency
  • Record CCR identification: manufacturer, model, serial number, rating, number of steps, software/firmware version where applicable
  • Record test equipment: instrument, model, serial number, calibration certificate, calibration validity
  • Compile test results for every topic in this division
  • Log deviations/punch points with severity, corrective action, retest, and status
  • Record final acceptance status: PASS, FAIL, CONDITIONAL, or RETEST REQUIRED
  • Obtain sign-off: tested by, checked by, contractor representative, client/consultant representative, date
Acceptance / Record

Acceptance Criteria: the report provides complete traceability from identification through test results to final sign-off, and closes Division 03 for release to system-level commissioning.
