CAREER & CERTIFICATION GUIDANCE IN AIRFIELD GROUND LIGHTING

Airfield Ground Lighting is a specialised field within airport electrical and airside infrastructure. It combines electrical engineering, airport operations, visual guidance, lighting technology, safety, testing, maintenance and aviation standards. This guide shows the career paths, skills, training options and preparation steps to build expertise in it.

AGL CERTIFICATIONS & TRAINING PROGRAMS

A certificate documents completion of a training programme. Practical AGL competence also comes from understanding systems and applying that knowledge in the field. There is no single certification that universally defines an AGL career; requirements depend on role, employer and project.

ICAO / Indian Aviation Academy

ICAO Training lists Aeronautical Ground Lights Serviceability Standards and Maintenance, developed by Indian Aviation Academy, New Delhi. A five-day instructor-led technical course for airport operations executives and airside maintenance engineers, with DGCA aerodrome inspectors/auditors and aerodrome planning executives as secondary groups.

How to choose AGL training

Certificate vs practical skill. A certificate supports your profile but should not replace understanding. Aim for: Knowledge + Practical Exposure + Troubleshooting + Documentation + Standards Awareness.

Interview Preparation

HOW TO PREPARE FOR AN AGL INTERVIEW

Do not prepare only by memorising definitions. Be ready to explain how a system works, identify a fault, interpret a test result or describe how you would approach an airport project.

Troubleshooting: “One runway light is not working. What will you do?”

Don’t answer “replace the light.” Show engineering thinking:

  1. Confirm the symptom
  2. Identify the circuit
  3. Check whether other fixtures are affected
  4. Check transformer and connector connections
  5. Take electrical measurements safely
  6. Trace the fault systematically
  7. Identify the failed component
  8. Rectify the cause
  9. Test the circuit again
  10. Document the result

Structure every answer

  1. Definition: what it is
  2. Function: what it does
  3. Application: where it is used
  4. Technical principle: how it works
  5. Practical example: a field case
  6. Safety / standards: where relevant

Scenario drills

  • Complete circuit unavailable: CCR → cable → connectors → transformers → fixtures
  • One fixture out: fixture, secondary connection, transformer, local wiring
  • Several fixtures out: series circuit, primary cable, connector, CCR, common issue
  • Lights work but photometry fails: alignment, condition, contamination, lamp/LED, geometry, measurement conditions, operating current
