Approach Lighting System SOPs

Controlled procedures for the planning interface, installation, structures, fixtures, cabling, transformers, connectors, inspection, testing and photometric verification of approach lighting systems — applicable to ALS, SALS, SSALR, MALSR, MALSF, ALSF-type systems, PALS and other approved approach lighting configurations.

Initial Issue

Important — The exact approach lighting configuration, length, bar geometry, fixture spacing, elevations, aiming, colors, intensity, structure dimensions, frangibility, obstruction requirements and photometric criteria shall always be taken from the approved aerodrome lighting drawings, applicable ICAO/FAA/DGCA requirements, approved obstacle/approach-lighting study, manufacturer instructions and project specification.

SOP-204 Approach Lighting Installation
Purpose

To establish a controlled procedure for installing an approach lighting system in accordance with the approved design and aerodrome requirements.

Pre-Installation Verification

Verify:

  • Approved approach lighting layout.
  • Runway centreline reference.
  • Threshold reference.
  • Approach lighting system type.
  • Fixture schedule.
  • Light-bar locations.
  • Crossbar locations.
  • Pole/structure locations.
  • Fixture elevations.
  • Cable routes.
  • Transformer locations.
  • Connector arrangement.
  • Obstacle/frangibility requirements.
  • Access and airside safety controls.
Procedure
  1. Review approved approach lighting drawings.
  2. Establish runway centreline and threshold reference.
  3. Verify all structure locations.
  4. Verify fixture positions and elevations.
  5. Inspect foundations/supports.
  6. Install poles/structures.
  7. Install fixtures.
  8. Install primary/secondary cabling as applicable.
  9. Install transformers.
  10. Install connectors.
  11. Verify fixture orientation.
  12. Verify elevation.
  13. Verify alignment.
  14. Inspect mechanical installation.
  15. Perform electrical tests.
  16. Conduct functional testing.
  17. Conduct photometric verification where required.
  18. Complete documentation.
Acceptance

The system shall match the approved:

Geometry + Elevation + Alignment + Optical Configuration + Electrical Configuration + Control Configuration.

SOP-205 Approach Light Alignment

Critical Principle — Approach lighting alignment must be assessed as a complete visual guidance pattern, not merely as individual fixture alignment.

Purpose

To ensure approach light fixtures are correctly aligned with the approved approach lighting geometry.

Reference

Alignment shall be established from the approved:

  • Runway centreline.
  • Threshold reference.
  • Approach lighting centreline.
  • Crossbar geometry.
  • Fixture aiming requirements.
Procedure
  1. Establish runway centreline reference.
  2. Establish threshold reference.
  3. Verify each approach-light station.
  4. Check lateral position.
  5. Check longitudinal position.
  6. Check fixture orientation.
  7. Check optical axis.
  8. Check adjacent fixtures.
  9. Correct deviations.
  10. Record measurements.

SOP-217 Approach Light Functional Testing

System-Level Principle — Do not accept the system solely because every fixture illuminates. Verify that the complete approach lighting pattern functions correctly.

Purpose

To verify correct operation of the approach lighting system following installation, repair or maintenance.

Pre-Test

Confirm:

  • Work area clear.
  • Personnel clear.
  • Tools removed.
  • FOD inspection complete.
  • Electrical connections complete.
  • Circuit correctly configured.
  • Control system available.
  • ATC/Operations coordination complete where required.
Procedure
  1. Obtain permission to energize.
  2. Energize the approach lighting system.
  3. Operate through applicable intensity levels.
  4. Inspect the complete lighting pattern.
  5. Verify correct ON/OFF operation, color, intensity, sequence and fixture response.
  6. Confirm no flickering, no intermittent operation and no abnormal illumination.
  7. Check control-system response where applicable.
  8. Record defects.
  9. Restore required operating configuration.
  10. Complete documentation.

Division-Wide Requirements

The master workflow, quality hold points, common failure modes and inspection checklist that apply across all 15 Division 10 procedures.

A. Master Workflow

The following sequence should be applied end-to-end for every approach lighting installation:

C. Common Failure Modes

  • Incorrect Centreline PositionCan distort the intended approach guidance pattern.
  • Incorrect Fixture ElevationCan change the geometry and visual presentation of the approach system.
  • Pole SettlementCan gradually alter fixture elevation and alignment.
  • Fixture MisalignmentA fixture can illuminate normally while directing light incorrectly.
  • Structural DamageImpact, corrosion or loose supports can compromise system alignment and safety.
  • Connector/Transformer FailureCan cause individual fixture failure or intermittent operation.
  • Optical ContaminationDirt or moisture can reduce useful optical output.
  • Incorrect Fixture ConfigurationAn incorrect optical/color configuration can result in a non-compliant system even when the fixture operates.
  • Cable DamageImproper routing or mechanical damage can cause intermittent or permanent circuit failure.
  • Photometric DegradationA visually illuminated system may still fail objective photometric requirements.

Division 10 — Approach Lighting Golden Rule

Division 10 — SOP Approval Principle

Each project should adapt these SOPs to its own approved aerodrome lighting drawings, applicable ICAO/FAA/DGCA requirements, approved obstacle/approach-lighting study, fixture and transformer manufacturer's instructions, and applicable photometric acceptance criteria.

Approved project drawings, the applicable regulatory requirements and manufacturer's instructions shall take precedence wherever they impose more restrictive requirements.

Quick Reference

A short answer on approach lighting replacement, taken from SOP-214 in Division 10. Confirm all criteria against the approved design and the current standard.

Approach lighting replacement

Approach lighting replacement follows SOP-214. Obtain work authorization and coordinate with Airport Operations/ATC as required. Identify the defective fixture, isolate the circuit, apply LOTO where applicable and verify a safe electrical condition. Inspect the surrounding structure, disconnect the connector, remove the defective fixture and inspect the mounting arrangement. Install the approved replacement, connect the secondary cable, then verify orientation, height and alignment and apply the specified torque. Finish with electrical testing, functional testing, photometric verification where required and the work-clearance process.

Frequently Asked Questions

Quick answers about Approach Lighting SOPs in the AGL SOP Library.

Q1What is Division 10 — Approach Lighting SOPs?

Division 10 is a set of 15 procedures (SOP-204 to SOP-218, Rev 0) for approach lighting systems. It covers the planning interface, installation, structures, fixtures, cabling, transformers, connectors, inspection, testing and photometric verification for ALS, SALS, SSALR, MALSR, MALSF, ALSF-type systems, PALS and other approved configurations.

Q2Where do approach lighting geometry and photometric criteria come from?

From the approved aerodrome lighting drawings, applicable ICAO, FAA and DGCA requirements, the approved obstacle/approach-lighting study, manufacturer instructions and the project specification. This includes configuration, length, bar geometry, spacing, elevations, aiming, colors, intensity, structure dimensions and frangibility.

Q3Is an approach lighting system accepted when every fixture lights up?

No. The system-level principle in SOP-217 says the complete approach lighting pattern must be verified as functioning correctly, including operation through the applicable intensity levels, not just individual fixtures illuminating.

Q4Can generic photometric limits be used?

No. Photometric acceptance values must come from the applicable standard, approved project specification or equipment/system requirement (SOP-218). Verification may cover intensity, distribution, uniformity, color, horizontal and vertical aiming, intensity steps, individual fixture output and the system-level pattern.

Q5What is the approach lighting installation workflow?

Approved design, centreline and threshold reference, structure location, foundation inspection, pole or structure installation, alignment and height check, fixture installation and alignment, cable, transformer and connector installation. Then electrical, mechanical and optical inspection, FOD/area inspection, controlled energization, functional testing, photometric verification, defect rectification, retest and final acceptance.

Q6What must be verified before installing approach light poles?

The approved pole location, type and height, foundation, structural details, frangibility requirement, wind and environmental design, cable route and access arrangements (SOP-208). After installation, verticality, mechanical security and the frangible arrangement where required are checked.

Q7How is approach light height verified?

SOP-206 uses approved elevation data and a survey reference to measure pole, fixture and crossbar elevation and compare it with the approved design. It checks consistency between stations, ground clearance, structural settlement and the fixture-to-centreline relationship.

Q8How is approach lighting replacement carried out?

Approach lighting replacement (SOP-214) covers work authorization, isolation and LOTO, structure and mounting inspection, installation of the approved replacement and secondary cable connection, then orientation, height, alignment and torque checks, electrical and functional testing, photometric verification where required, and work clearance.
