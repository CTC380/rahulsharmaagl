Digital Engineering

Division 05 establishes the digital engineering foundation of the AGL Innovation Hub. Its principal areas are digital engineering, digital twins, BIM, GIS, cloud engineering platforms, digital documentation and simulation models. The core objective is to create a continuously usable digital representation of AGL assets, systems, engineering information and lifecycle activities — from design and construction through commissioning, operation, maintenance and eventual replacement.

Most Important Design Principle

Division 05 should remain the digital backbone, not the intelligence layer. It creates the digital representation and engineering information structure — the digital twin, BIM/GIS models, circuit and equipment models, documentation and simulation — while the analytics, prediction and diagnosis sit in Division 03 — AI for AGL, and the real-time field data feeding the models comes from Division 04 — IoT & Connected AGL.

Its strongest downstream connections are to Division 12 (AGL Automation) and Division 13 (Digital AGL Control & Monitoring), which consume the digital models and workflows, to Division 14 (Advanced Photometry) and Division 15 (Intelligent Maintenance), which contribute and consume asset condition information, and to the AGL Asset Lifecycle Guide and Asset Lifecycle Master Database, which the digital asset model and digital lifecycle management topics feed directly.

This transforms the AGL documentation philosophy from "documents about assets" into "a connected digital engineering representation of the assets themselves."
