Innovation Research & Collaboration

Division 22 is the external knowledge, research-partnership, academic-collaboration and open-innovation engine of the AGL Innovation Hub. Where Division 21 converts innovations into IP, products and commercial opportunities, Division 22 builds the external ecosystem that continuously supplies new knowledge, research capability, technologies, talent, ideas and collaboration opportunities — connecting AGL engineering problems with universities, research institutions, airports, manufacturers, technology companies and startups to create validated solutions faster than any single organization could develop alone.

Most Important Design Principle

Division 22 should remain the external ecosystem layer, not the internal execution layer. It brings new knowledge, partners, technologies and research capability into the system — through universities, research institutes, airports, manufacturers, technology companies and startups — while the building, testing, benchmarking and commercialization of what that ecosystem produces sit in Divisions 18–21.

Its strongest connections are to Division 19 (PoC & Pilot Projects), through collaborative PoCs and airport pilots, and to Division 21 (IP, Patents & Commercialization), through the collaboration-to-IP pathway that runs from discovery through invention identification, patent registration, prototyping, validation and protection to commercialization.

The guiding principle is simple: "Do not innovate alone when the right external capability can accelerate the solution — but always retain strong technical governance, IP clarity, evidence-based validation and controlled knowledge transfer." This makes Division 22 the continuous research engine that keeps the entire AGL Innovation Hub supplied with new ideas, scientific knowledge, technologies, partners and commercialization opportunities.
