Division 07 · CCR · Power-Conversion & Regulation Asset

CCR Maintenance

The dedicated engineering chapter for the Constant Current Regulator — the electrical heart of the AGL series-circuit system. Where Division 06 addresses the physical light fixture, Division 07 treats the CCR specifically as a power-conversion, regulation, protection, control and monitoring asset, covering its mechanical, electrical, thermal, control and protection functions.

Core principle: a CCR is not considered healthy merely because it produces output current. Its power input, current regulation, protection, cooling, control, monitoring and communication must all remain satisfactory — verified as applicable to the maintenance performed.

Pre-Maintenance Requirements

Because the CCR is a power-conversion asset, electrical isolation and verification close out before any external or internal work begins.

Work Planning

  • Identify CCR asset, circuit and location
  • Review previous maintenance history and defects
  • Confirm manufacturer's approved documentation for equipment-specific requirements
  • Confirm task, tools, spares, PPE and test instruments

Electrical Safety

  • Apply safe isolation / LOTO requirements
  • Verify safe electrical condition before internal work
  • Identify earthing / bonding requirements
  • Confirm controlled energization plan for restoration

Operational Coordination

  • Obtain required work authorization
  • Coordinate with ATC / airport operations where applicable
  • Coordinate ALCMS / remote-control implications
  • Establish work limits and communication arrangements

Documentation

  • CCR identification, manufacturer/model, location
  • Date, maintenance type, team / reference
  • Previous condition and known findings
  • Maintenance activity and parts used

Frequently Asked Questions

Quick answers about ccr maintenance, based on the topics in this division.

8 QUESTIONS
Q1What is CCR maintenance in the AGL Maintenance Handbook?

It is the dedicated engineering chapter for the Constant Current Regulator, the electrical heart of the AGL series-circuit system. Where Division 06 addresses the physical light fixture, Division 07 treats the CCR as a power-conversion and regulation asset.

Q2What does Division 07 of the AGL Maintenance Handbook cover?

Division 07 (CCR Maintenance) contains 33 topics in five groups: Group A External Maintenance (6 topics), Group B Internal Maintenance (9 topics), Group C Electrical Maintenance (7 topics), Group D Control & Monitoring (6 topics) and Group E Performance (5 topics).

Q3Is a CCR healthy just because it produces output current?

No. Its power input, current regulation, protection, cooling, control, monitoring and communication must all remain satisfactory, verified as applicable to the maintenance performed.

Q4What electrical safety sequence applies to CCR maintenance?

CCR work sits on a mandatory sequence of safe isolation, verifying the safe condition, maintenance, testing and controlled energization. The task-level sequence is Inspect, Clean, Tighten/Repair, Test, Verify and Record.

Q5What must be completed before CCR maintenance starts?

Four requirement groups: work planning (asset, circuit, history, manufacturer's documentation, tools, spares, PPE and test instruments), electrical safety (isolation or LOTO, verifying a safe condition, earthing and bonding, and a controlled energization plan), operational coordination (work authorization, ATC or airport operations, ALCMS and remote-control implications), and documentation.

Q6How should a CCR fault be diagnosed?

The fault-diagnosis logic prevents the common mistake of replacing CCR components when the real problem may be in the input supply, control system, communication system or field circuit. It checks the incoming supply, status and alarms, protection, cooling, internal condition, output current, control command, communication and the AGL field circuit before identifying the root cause.

Q7How does the CCR decision matrix work?

It maps findings to condition and typical action, for example a dirty filter is Deteriorating and is cleaned or replaced, unstable output is High concern and investigated immediately, and a protection malfunction is Critical and corrected before normal operation. These are maintenance decision categories, not universal regulatory acceptance thresholds, so always evaluate against the applicable CCR specification and approved operating limits.

Q8When is CCR maintenance complete?

Only when the physical condition, cooling system, electrical condition, protection, control, monitoring and output performance have been verified as applicable to the work done. Cleaning alone, fan replacement alone or output current alone does not equal CCR maintenance completion.
