Division 13 · PAP · Optical / Angular Performance

PAPI / APAPI Maintenance

The dedicated maintenance division for Precision Approach Path Indicator and Abbreviated PAPI systems, with particular emphasis on preserving optical accuracy, mechanical stability, alignment, and operational performance. Covers routine maintenance and full restoration of optical and mechanical performance.

Why PAPI is different: a PAPI unit can remain electrically operational while its optical or angular performance has deteriorated. This division treats alignment, elevation, beam characteristics and optical condition as critical maintenance parameters — electrical operation ≠ complete PAPI health.

PAPI/APAPI Maintenance Philosophy

Every PAPI/APAPI maintenance task in this division follows the same underlying sequence, and is judged against four independent conditions — a unit that illuminates normally but is misaligned is still defective.

Are the optical components clean, undamaged, and transmitting correctly?

Angular Condition

Is the unit correctly aligned and providing the intended beam/elevation characteristics?

Safety & Maintenance Control

Before and after any PAPI/APAPI maintenance activity, these requirement groups must be closed out.

Before Maintenance

  • Identify the PAPI/APAPI asset and confirm location
  • Confirm applicable circuit
  • Coordinate airside access/work requirements
  • Establish the required electrical safe condition where applicable
  • Protect the optical components from contamination
  • Ensure maintenance activity does not disturb the alignment reference unnecessarily

After Maintenance

  • Restore the unit correctly
  • Complete required testing
  • Perform alignment/performance verification where applicable
  • Complete post-maintenance inspection
  • Record all changes

Frequently Asked Questions

Quick answers about papi / apapi maintenance, based on the topics in this division.

8 QUESTIONS
Q1What is PAPI / APAPI maintenance in the AGL Maintenance Handbook?

It is the dedicated division for Precision Approach Path Indicator and Abbreviated PAPI systems, with particular emphasis on preserving optical accuracy, mechanical stability, alignment and operational performance.

Q2What does Division 13 of the AGL Maintenance Handbook cover?

Division 13 (PAPI / APAPI Maintenance) contains 39 topics in three groups: Group A Preventive Maintenance (20 topics), Group B Corrective Maintenance (12 topics) and Group C Performance Maintenance (7 topics).

Q3Why is PAPI maintenance different from ordinary fixture maintenance?

A PAPI unit can remain electrically operational while its optical or angular performance has deteriorated. The division treats alignment, elevation, beam characteristics and optical condition as critical maintenance parameters, so a unit that illuminates normally but is misaligned is still defective.

Q4What four conditions are assessed independently?

Physical condition (housing, mounting, foundation, hardware), electrical condition, optical condition (clean, undamaged optical components transmitting correctly) and angular condition (correct alignment and the intended beam and elevation characteristics).

Q5What is the PAPI/APAPI maintenance sequence?

Eight steps: Inspect, Clean, Check Mechanical Condition, Verify Alignment, Verify Optical Performance, Correct, Test and Record.

Q6What safety and control steps apply before and after PAPI work?

Before maintenance: identify the unit and its location, confirm the circuit, coordinate airside access, establish the electrical safe condition where applicable, protect the optical components from contamination, and avoid disturbing the alignment reference unnecessarily. After maintenance: restore the unit correctly, complete the required testing, verify alignment and performance where applicable, complete a post-maintenance inspection and record all changes.

Q7How should an abnormal PAPI indication be troubleshot?

Confirm the electrical supply, then check the unit condition, optical contamination, lamp or LED, power supply, connector and cable, mechanical condition, alignment, elevation and beam performance. Correct the defect, repeat the applicable verification, complete post-maintenance inspection, return to service and record.

Q8When is PAPI maintenance complete?

Not when the light turns on. It is complete only once mechanical condition, electrical condition, optical condition, alignment, elevation, beam or indication performance, functional verification and documentation have each been satisfactorily addressed.
