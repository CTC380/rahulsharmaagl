Common Questions

Frequently Asked Questions

Q1What types of partnership does Precision AGL Solutions offer?

Four types: Project & Engineering Partners, Product & Channel Partners, Testing & Technical Service Partners, and Knowledge & Training Partners.

Q2Who can become a partner?

Airport contractors, AGL specialists, consultants and engineering firms, electrical contractors, system integrators, product manufacturers, distributors and representatives, testing and inspection organizations, airport O&M service providers, and training and technical organizations.

Q3What do Project & Engineering partnerships cover?

Collaboration on airport AGL projects that need technical coordination, engineering support, documentation, testing, inspection, commissioning or project execution, across runway and taxiway lighting, approach lighting, PAPI, CCR and series-circuit systems and AGL cabling.

Q4What do Testing & Technical Service partnerships cover?

Specialized technical activities such as AGL photometry, field inspection, system verification, electrical testing, fault diagnosis, installation support, commissioning assistance and technical reporting.

Q5What qualities does Precision AGL look for in a partner?

Technical capability, market understanding, professional reliability, a long-term collaboration mindset, and respect for shared standards including technical requirements, documentation, testing, safety and professional engineering practice.

Q6How do I submit a partnership enquiry?

Complete the partnership enquiry form with your name, company, job title, business activity, email, mobile number, location and partnership interest. The team will review it and contact you. Submitting opens your email client with the enquiry pre-filled.
