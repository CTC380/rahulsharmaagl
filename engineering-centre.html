Quick answers

Frequently Asked Questions

Q1What is the AGL Engineering Centre?

The AGL Engineering Centre is a collection of 69 in-depth engineering guides on Airfield Ground Lighting, covering the physics of the series circuit, lighting-system design, power and control equipment, installation, testing, NOTAM reporting and the ICAO, FAA and EASA standards behind it all.

Q2What disciplines does the Engineering Centre cover?

Fifteen, from Foundations, Standards and Compliance and Lighting Systems through power, control, installation, testing, PAPI, photometry and maintenance, to Field Operations, Safety and Response (5 guides), Reference, Diagrams and Documentation (5), and Apron, LED and Careers (4).

Q3Where should I start in the Engineering Centre?

The Foundations discipline is labelled Start here. It contains All About Airfield Ground Lighting (the complete guide), The Physics of AGL, and AGL Design and Electrical Engineering.

Q4Which guides cover ICAO, FAA and EASA standards?

The Standards and Compliance discipline has three guides: ICAO Annex 14 AGL Requirements, FAA AC 150/5340 Series Explained, and EASA CS-ADR-DSN and AGL Compliance.

Q5Which guides cover airfield lighting systems?

The Lighting Systems discipline covers runway lighting, approach lighting, taxiway lighting, the PAPI engineering guide, apron lighting and stand guidance systems, and obstruction lighting and marking.

Q6Which guides cover runway safety and surface guidance?

The Surface Guidance and Runway Safety discipline covers runway guard lights and stop bars, runway incursion prevention and SMGCS, airfield signage and taxiway guidance signs, and airfield markings and signage integration.

Q7Which guides cover power, control and protection equipment?

The Power, Control and Protection discipline covers the constant current regulator (CCR), isolating transformers, cables and connector engineering, ALCMS, emergency backup power, and surge protection and lightning mitigation.

Q8Which guides cover installation, testing and operations?

The Installation, Testing and Operations discipline covers AGL installation and commissioning, testing and inspection, photometry, and NOTAMs (Notice to Airmen).

Q9Does the Engineering Centre cover LED, portable and solar lighting?

Yes. The Fixture and Power Technologies discipline has guides on LED airfield ground lighting, portable airfield lighting (PAGL) and solar airfield lighting (SAGL).

Q10How do I find a specific guide on the Engineering Centre page?

Use the search box to filter guides by keyword, for example CCR, PAPI or photometry, or use the discipline links to jump straight to a group of guides.

Q11How does the Engineering Centre relate to the Knowledge Hub?

The Engineering Centre is the set of 69 in-depth engineering guides, while the AGL Knowledge Hub is the broader platform organised around lifecycle divisions. The Engineering Centre page links back to the Knowledge Hub.

Q12Who can help apply this engineering to an airfield?

The page states that Precision AGL Solutions supports design, photometry, testing and compliance, and provides a Get in touch link for enquiries.
