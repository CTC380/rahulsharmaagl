01 · Overview

What Is an AGL Contractor in India?

An AGL contractor in India is a specialist contractor responsible for executing Airfield Ground Lighting (AGL) work at airports and aerodromes — a discipline significantly more specialized than conventional electrical contracting.

AGL contracting combines airport operations, electrical systems, optical performance, pavement interfaces, visual guidance, control systems and aviation requirements. The Indian market shows it can cover the complete lifecycle: in a recent AAI expression of interest, agencies were invited for the design, manufacture, supply, installation, testing, commissioning and maintenance of AGL equipment for CAT-II/III(B) systems at various AAI airports under the Make in India policy.[1]

AGL Contractor vs Consultant, Electrical Contractor, Supplier and Turnkey Provider

Vs electrical contractor

An electrical contractor may handle cable installation, panels, power supply and terminations. AGL contracting also requires knowledge of runway and taxiway lighting, optical alignment, PAPI, photometry, series-circuit transformers, CCRs, airside safety and aerodrome visual-aid requirements. General electrical experience does not automatically equal AGL experience.

Vs lighting supplier

A supplier provides equipment; a contractor provides site execution (supply → install → test → commission). A supplier may be responsible only for supply. Define these responsibilities clearly.

Vs turnkey AGL company

A turnkey provider can handle design, supply, installation, testing, commissioning and maintenance, which simplifies coordination. The airport should still verify that the provider genuinely holds each claimed capability.

05 · Runway, Taxiway & Approach

Airfield Ground Lighting Installation: Runway, Taxiway and Approach Contractor

Installation covers setting out, foundations, light bases, cable and transformer installation, connector assembly, fixture mounting, alignment, electrical testing and photometry.

Runway lighting

Edge, threshold, end, centreline, touchdown zone and approach lights. Work must be coordinated with airport operations because it may need runway closures, work windows or controlled airside access.

Taxiway lighting

Edge and centreline lights, stop bars, runway guard lights and illuminated signs — coordinated with pavement, drainage, markings, signs, aircraft movement, surface movement systems and control systems. In complex networks, installation errors can cause operational problems even when individual fixtures work.

Approach lighting

Needs accurate setting out and optical alignment: centreline fixtures, crossbars, elevated structures, inset lights, sequenced lighting, PAPI and associated electrical equipment. Work from approved coordinates and elevations — improvised field positioning can create optical and compliance problems.

07 · Electrical

AGL Electrical Works: CCR, Transformers, Cables, Connectors and Bases

Series-circuit airport lighting is not the same as conventional building electrical distribution. The basic chain is below.

Power supply
CCR
Primary series cable
Isolation transformer
Secondary connector
AGL fixture

CCR installation

Scope: installation, incoming power, output connections, control wiring, remote control, monitoring, alarms, configuration, commissioning. Testing covers input supply, output current, current steps, controls, alarms, monitoring and protective functions. FAA projects have dedicated CCR and regulator-monitor specifications; for Indian projects take the requirement from the applicable project and authority documents.

Isolation transformers

For runway, taxiway, approach and centreline lights, PAPI and signs: identification, housing/base placement, primary and secondary connection, cable routing, connector installation, inspection. The installed unit must match the approved equipment schedule and circuit design.

Cable installation

Primary series cable, secondary cable (transformer to fixture), protection (ducts, conduits), terminations (CCR, transformers, connectors, junction boxes), and testing (continuity, insulation resistance, circuit checks). Poor installation can cause problems years after completion.

Connector installation

Critical electrical interfaces. Check correct connector, cable preparation, secure assembly, insulation, sealing, mechanical condition and strain. Defects cause open circuits, intermittent operation, increased contact resistance, local heating and failure.

Light bases & housings

Light bases (shallow/deep), transformer housings, junction boxes and mounting assemblies. Key requirements: location, elevation, alignment, drainage, cable routing, concrete installation and fixture interface.

Wider electrical scope

CCR feeders, series circuits, terminations, control wiring, monitoring, power distribution, grounding and secondary power interfaces. Electrical testing should be completed before final commissioning.

08 · Installation Types

In-Pavement, Elevated and Airport Sign Installation

In-pavement AGL

Needs close coordination with the civil / pavement contractor: excavation or core drilling, base installation, transformer housing, cable connection, fixture mounting, elevation adjustment, sealing and drainage. After pavement works, verify fixture elevation, alignment, mechanical seating, optical condition, electrical connection and photometry where required.

Elevated AGL

Attention to mounting, elevation, alignment, frangibility, cable routing and mechanical stability. Install the complete support assembly as approved — improvised rigid supports or non-approved modifications can affect the safety characteristics of the installation.

Airport signs

Taxiway, runway designation, location, direction, mandatory and distance signs: foundation → sign installation → power → control → monitoring → testing. Verify both the physical legend and its electrical/operational function.

Photometry contractor

Photometry is increasingly important in Indian airport projects. A specialist can provide runway, taxiway, approach-lighting and PAPI photometry, intensity measurement, colour/chromaticity, beam coverage, alignment, data processing, reporting and retesting. The workflow is field measurement → data evaluation → deficiency identification → corrective recommendation → retest → final report, with results traceable to the exact fixture location and operating condition.

AAI records show dedicated photometric contracting: a 2025 Patna Airport photometry work order was awarded to M/s A K Consulting, and a 2026 AAI tender at Ranchi called for in-field AGL photometric testing using vehicle-mounted and handheld devices as part of compliance with DGCA observations.[2] Photometric capability is a useful differentiator when selecting a contractor.

12 · CAT-II / III & Low Visibility

AGL Contractor for CAT-II / CAT-III and Low-Visibility Operations

Several systems must operate together, so the contractor may have to coordinate approach lighting, runway edge, centreline and touchdown zone lighting, taxiway centreline, stop bars, PAPI, secondary power, ALCMS/ILCMS, CCRs, monitoring and maintenance.

AAI's 2026 EOI refers specifically to CAT-II/III(B) systems and requires relevant design, manufacturing, supply, installation, testing, commissioning and maintenance experience.[1]

A contractor at an airport with low-visibility procedures must consider operational lighting selection, serviceability, standby power, switching, monitoring, fault response and maintenance windows. Current Indian AIP material for Kolkata requires appropriate selection and close monitoring of CAT II/III AGL facilities and specifies enhanced inspection during Low Visibility Procedures.[4] Low-visibility work requires operational awareness as well as technical installation capability.

LED conversion

Requires system-level engineering. Verify fixture, CCR, transformer and control compatibility, monitoring, intensity, colour and photometric output. A lower-power LED fitting should not simply be connected to an existing circuit without confirming electrical compatibility.

Live airport operations

Plan for work during approved closure windows, temporary isolation, phased circuit shutdown, ATC coordination, FOD inspections, rapid restoration, contingency arrangements and protection of active AGL circuits. An airport cannot be treated like an ordinary construction site; prepare a method statement showing how the existing operation is protected.

Safety

Procedures should cover airside access, ATC coordination, runway/taxiway closures, work permits, electrical isolation, high-voltage safety, vehicle operations, FOD prevention, temporary works, emergency response, communication, night work and weather restrictions.

Civil, electrical and optical works

Civil: foundations, bases, housings, trenches, ducts, drainage, pavement interfaces — coordinate tolerances with final optical alignment. Electrical: feeders, series circuits, terminations, grounding, testing before commissioning. Optical: fixture alignment, PAPI aiming, photometry — the contractor must understand not only how to connect the light but where the light needs to go.

Maintenance handover

Before completion, transfer knowledge to the airport maintenance team (system operation, CCR, transformers, connectors, fixtures, PAPI, ALCMS, troubleshooting, testing, preventive maintenance) and provide maintenance manuals, spare-parts list, recommended maintenance tasks, equipment settings, test baselines and warranty information.

Warranty support

A contract should define warranty period, covered equipment, response time, replacement procedure, labour, transport, spare parts and emergency support. For critical airport systems, warranty should cover both equipment defects and the practical process of returning the system to service.

20 · FAQ

Frequently Asked Questions

What is an AGL contractor?
An AGL contractor is a specialist contractor responsible for executing airport Airfield Ground Lighting work, including installation, testing, commissioning and, depending on the contract, supply, design and maintenance.
What does an AGL contractor do in India?
An AGL contractor can install runway and taxiway lights, approach lighting, PAPI, CCRs, transformers, cables, connectors, signs and ALCMS/ILCMS and perform testing, photometry, commissioning and maintenance.
What is an SITC AGL contractor?
An SITC contractor provides Supply, Installation, Testing and Commissioning of AGL equipment and systems.
Can an AGL contractor also provide design?
Yes. Some contractors have in-house engineering teams and provide design as part of a turnkey scope. The contract should clearly define design responsibility.
Can an AGL contractor install PAPI?
Yes. A specialist PAPI contractor can handle foundation, unit positioning, alignment, aiming, electrical connection, functional testing and photometry.
Can an AGL contractor perform runway photometry?
Yes. Some contractors provide field photometry directly, while others engage specialist photometric organizations. AAI procurement records show dedicated field photometry contracts at Indian airports.
What is the difference between an AGL contractor and an airport lighting consultant?
The consultant provides specialist engineering and advisory services, while the contractor normally performs physical project execution. Some organizations provide both.
Can a normal electrical contractor install airport lighting?
Some electrical work can be performed by electrical contractors, but specialist AGL installation requires knowledge of airport visual aids, series circuits, CCRs, transformers, optical alignment, PAPI, photometry and airside procedures.
What qualifications should an AGL contractor have?
The exact qualification depends on the project. Relevant airport experience, technical staff, equipment, testing capability, financial capacity, safety systems and compliance with the tender's qualification criteria are important.
What should an AGL contractor provide after installation?
The handover should normally include test reports, photometry, commissioning records, as-built drawings, equipment documentation, calibration certificates, O&M manuals, spares and training records as required by the contract.
What is CAT-II/III AGL contracting?
It refers to AGL work associated with airport systems supporting Category II/III or comparable low-visibility operations. Such projects can require enhanced runway, taxiway, approach, control, monitoring and secondary-power systems.
Why is photometry important for an AGL contractor?
Photometry verifies that installed lighting produces the intended optical performance. It can identify low intensity, alignment problems, colour issues and optical degradation.
What is the role of an AGL contractor during commissioning?
The contractor completes installation, prepares the equipment for testing, supports electrical and functional testing, rectifies deficiencies, supports photometry and submits final commissioning documentation.
What is an AGL maintenance contractor?
An AGL maintenance contractor provides preventive and corrective maintenance for airport lighting systems after commissioning.
Can an AGL contractor support existing airport lighting?
Yes. Specialist contractors can undertake rehabilitation, troubleshooting, fixture replacement, cable work, CCR upgrades, PAPI upgrades, photometry and maintenance.

Keyword Reference

AGL Contracting Services in India

Airport lighting contractor India

An airport lighting contractor in India installs, tests and commissions airfield lighting to the approved design and the applicable standards. Work on a live airfield also needs permits, safe working methods and coordination with airport operations.

Airfield lighting installation company

An airfield lighting installation company supplies skilled teams for cable laying, bases, fixtures, transformers, CCRs and signs. Good installation practice, supervision and records matter as much as the equipment itself.

Airfield lighting turnkey project

In an airfield lighting turnkey project one contractor handles design, supply, installation, testing and commissioning, and hands over a working system with documentation. The scope, interfaces with civil works and acceptance criteria should be defined in the contract.

Airfield lighting EPC contractor

An airfield lighting EPC contractor takes responsibility for engineering, procurement and construction under one contract. It suits larger projects, where design, supply and site work need to be managed together.

Airport lighting retrofit contractor

An airport lighting retrofit contractor upgrades an existing system, for example to LED or a new control system, while the airport stays in operation. The existing circuits, CCRs and transformers must be checked for compatibility before changes are made.
