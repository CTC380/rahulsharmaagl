Training Effectiveness Check

Training shall not be considered successful merely because attendance was recorded. Evaluate:

  • Stage 1 — Attendance — Did the person attend?
  • Stage 2 — Knowledge — Did the person understand the subject?
  • Stage 3 — Practical Skill — Can the person perform the task?
  • Stage 4 — Field Performance — Can the person perform correctly under real working conditions?
  • Stage 5 — Outcome — Did training reduce errors, repeat failures, unsafe practices, incorrect testing, documentation errors and equipment damage?

Quick Reference

A short answer on AGL maintenance training, taken from SOP-467 in Division 22.

AGL maintenance training

AGL maintenance training (SOP-467) develops standardized maintenance competency. The modules cover runway and taxiway fixtures, approach lights, PAPI, signs, transformers, connectors, cables, CCRs, earthing, ALCMS, testing and documentation. The training philosophy puts four things first: correct diagnosis, correct repair, correct testing and correct documentation.

Frequently Asked Questions

Quick answers about Training, Competency & Knowledge Management SOPs in the AGL SOP Library.

Q1What is Division 22 — Training, Competency & Knowledge Management SOPs?

Division 22 is a set of 20 procedures (SOP-461 to SOP-480, Rev 0) for AGL personnel qualification, induction, technical and practical competency training, refresher training, competency assessment and certification, training-record management, lessons-learned documentation and technical knowledge transfer.

Q2What is the Golden Rule of AGL training?

Training creates knowledge, practical assessment demonstrates skill, competency authorization permits independent work, and lessons learned ensure that individual experience becomes organizational knowledge.

Q3How is AGL personnel competency assessed?

SOP-461 uses knowledge tests, practical demonstration, field observation and interview across areas such as airside safety, electrical safety, LOTO, CCR operation, transformers, cables, connectors, lighting systems, testing, photometry, emergency response and documentation. Competency is rated from Level 1 (awareness) through Level 5 (trainer or technical mentor).

Q4What is the competency development flow?

Induction, basic AGL training, safety training, task-specific training, supervised practical work, theory and practical assessment, a competency decision, authorized tasks, periodic performance monitoring, refresher or recertification, advanced competency and, finally, trainer or technical mentor.

Q5What does LOTO training include?

Energy identification, isolation, lock application, tagging, verification, proving dead, stored energy, series-circuit hazards such as open-circuit and induced voltage, electric shock rescue and first-aid awareness, temporary earthing where required, release of isolation and re-energization (SOP-465). Personnel must demonstrate the LOTO process under controlled supervision in a practical assessment.

Q6How is practical skill assessed?

SOP-475 evaluators assess preparation, PPE, work authorization, LOTO, tool selection, technical procedure, workmanship, testing, fault diagnosis, FOD control, documentation and emergency awareness, rating from 0 (not demonstrated) to 5 (can train others).

Q7How is field experience turned into organizational knowledge?

SOP-479 records lessons from failures, incidents, emergencies, repeated defects, commissioning problems, installation errors, maintenance findings, photometric failures, connector failures, cable faults and lightning events. Each lesson records what happened, technical cause, detection, corrective and preventive action, the lesson, and which SOP, design or training document should be updated.

Q8What does AGL maintenance training cover?

AGL maintenance training (SOP-467) builds standardized maintenance competency across runway and taxiway fixtures, approach lights, PAPI, signs, transformers, connectors, cables, CCRs, earthing, ALCMS, testing and documentation, with emphasis on correct diagnosis, repair, testing and documentation.
