Digital AGL Control & Monitoring

The digital supervisory-control, monitoring and operational-visibility layer of the AGL Innovation Hub. Division 13 transforms conventional AGL control and monitoring into a connected, real-time, intelligent and traceable digital environment — in which AGL equipment, circuits and control systems can be monitored, diagnosed and managed from authorized operational interfaces. It sits between Smart AGL / IoT / Automation and the higher-level AI, asset-intelligence and future airport control architecture.

Initial Issue

The question this division answers — How do we transform conventional AGL control and monitoring into a connected, real-time, intelligent and traceable digital environment in which AGL equipment, circuits and control systems can be monitored, diagnosed and managed from authorized operational interfaces?

Division 13's Role in the Innovation Hub

Divisions 02–04 make the AGL intelligent, analytical and connected. Division 12 automates the resulting processes. Division 13 brings all of it together into one digital operational control-and-monitoring environment through which equipment, circuits, alarms, data and engineering decisions are managed — feeding upward into asset intelligence and future airport control architecture.

DIG-01 Smart ALCMS
Purpose

Develop the next generation of Airfield Lighting Control and Monitoring Systems (ALCMS) with enhanced visibility, diagnostics, automation and data integration.

Potential Capabilities
  • Centralized AGL control & real-time equipment status
  • CCR, circuit and fixture-status monitoring
  • Alarm management, fault visualization
  • Historical data, trend analysis, remote diagnostics
  • Maintenance information, asset integration
  • Digital dashboards, event logging, system-health monitoring

DIG-02 Digital CCR Control
Purpose

Create enhanced digital monitoring and control capabilities for Constant Current Regulators.

Potential Parameters
  • CCR status, output current, intensity step
  • Alarm status, trip status, operating condition, temperature
  • Historical performance, runtime, maintenance status

DIG-03 Remote AGL Control
Purpose

Enable authorized personnel to monitor and control AGL functions from approved remote interfaces.

Potential Functions
  • Lighting ON/OFF, intensity selection, circuit status
  • Alarm acknowledgement, equipment status, system diagnostics
  • Operational monitoring, event review
