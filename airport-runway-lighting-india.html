02 · Definition

What Is Runway Lighting?

Runway lighting is the system of visual aids installed to define and identify the runway and to provide pilots with appropriate visual guidance during aircraft operations. Depending on the runway and its operational requirements, the lighting system may include:

Runway edge lightsThreshold lightsRunway end lightsRunway centreline lightsTouchdown zone lightsApproach lightingRunway-related visual guidanceAssociated control & monitoring systems
03 · Main Runway Lighting Systems

The Six Core Runway Lighting Applications

Each application serves a distinct visual-guidance purpose within the overall runway environment.

Runway Edge Lighting

Defines the lateral limits of the runway — identifying the edges, maintaining lateral orientation and supporting operations in reduced ambient lighting, using elevated or inset fixtures.

Threshold Lighting

Identifies the beginning of the portion of the runway available for landing — the visual transition from approach environment to runway threshold to runway surface.

Runway End Lighting

Identifies the end of the runway and provides an important visual indication during runway operations as part of the overall lighting arrangement.

Runway Centreline Lighting

Provides longitudinal visual guidance along the centreline, associated with runways requiring enhanced visual guidance and precision operations.

Touchdown Zone Lighting

Provides visual guidance in the touchdown area via multiple inset fixtures installed in defined patterns, for runway configurations requiring additional visual references.

Approach Lighting

Extends visual guidance beyond the runway environment into the approach area, supporting the transition from approach to threshold to runway.

Runway Lighting Requirements India

Runway lighting requirements in India depend on the runway, its operational category, the approach lighting type and the airport's approved configuration. They are typically drawn from ICAO requirements as implemented through DGCA, AAI specifications, airport-specific requirements and the tender's technical documents, with FAA specifications where the project references them.

FAQs

Frequently Asked Questions

What runway lighting applications are used at Indian airports?
Runway edge, threshold, runway end and centreline lighting, touchdown zone lighting, approach lighting and related visual guidance systems, depending on the airport configuration.
What is the typical AGL circuit for runway lighting?
A constant current regulator feeds a primary cable through primary connectors to an isolating transformer, then a secondary connector to the light fixture.
Do Indian airports use CAT II/III runway lighting configurations?
Yes. Indian AIP publications show active use of different runway and approach lighting configurations, including CAT I and CAT II/III applications at Indian aerodromes.
What is the difference between elevated and inset runway lights?
Elevated fixtures are mounted above the pavement surface and are evaluated for mounting, frangibility and clearance. Inset fixtures are installed within the pavement, typically at the centreline or touchdown zone, and are evaluated for pavement installation, mechanical loading and water ingress protection.
Why did a runway light fail even though the fixture looks fine?
A runway light failure does not necessarily mean the fixture itself is defective. The fault can exist anywhere in the series-circuit chain, from the secondary connector through the isolating transformer, primary connector, primary cable and series circuit back to the CCR, so systematic testing is used rather than assuming the fixture is the cause.
Can an existing runway fixture simply be swapped for an LED fixture?
Not without a system-level check. An LED retrofit should be evaluated for CCR compatibility, series-circuit characteristics, transformer compatibility, cable condition, connector arrangement, control and monitoring, and photometric performance, since a simple fixture swap without evaluating the rest of the circuit can create compatibility issues.
