AI for AGL

Division 03 is the core intelligence division of the AGL Innovation Hub. Its purpose is to investigate how Artificial Intelligence can be applied to AGL inspection, maintenance, diagnostics, engineering, testing, photometry, failure analysis, asset management and operational decision-making. The uploaded framework specifically identifies AI-based predictive maintenance, AGL failure prediction, AI fault diagnosis, image-based inspection, automated compliance checking and AI-assisted engineering/design as major areas. The overall objective: convert AGL data into engineering intelligence, predictions, diagnostics and actionable recommendations.

AI for AGL — Master Architecture

Division 03 is recommended to be structured around five layers, with human technical validation as the final and non-negotiable layer for safety-critical AGL applications.

Layer 1 — AGL Data: Asset + Failure + Maintenance + Inspection + Testing + Photometry + IoT
Layer 2 — AI Processing: Machine Learning + Computer Vision + Pattern Recognition + Predictive Analytics
Layer 3 — Engineering Intelligence: Prediction + Diagnosis + Classification + Anomaly Detection + Risk Assessment
Layer 4 — Decision Support: Recommendation + Prioritization + Maintenance Planning + Engineering Assistance
Layer 5 — Human Technical Validation: Engineer Review → Technical Decision → Approved Action

Division 03 — Ultimate Vision

The long-term objective is an AI-enabled AGL Engineering Intelligence Platform: AGL field data (sensors, fixtures, CCRs, transformers, cables, signs, PAPI, photometry) feeds into asset, inspection, test, failure, maintenance and photometry data, which AI detects, classifies, diagnoses, predicts, analyzes and turns into recommendations. That engineering intelligence — asset health, failure risk, root cause, maintenance priority, engineering recommendation — goes to the engineer to review, verify, decide and approve, before any action (inspect, test, repair, replace, monitor, optimize) is taken.

That makes Division 03 — AI for AGL the intelligence engine connecting the AGL Failure Database, Smart AGL, IoT, Digital Engineering, Photometry, Predictive Maintenance and Asset Lifecycle systems — while keeping final safety-critical engineering decisions under qualified human technical control.
