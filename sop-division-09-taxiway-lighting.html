Taxiway Lighting SOPs

Controlled procedures for taxiway edge and centreline lighting, curved and rapid-exit/high-speed taxiways, junction lighting, lead-on/lead-off systems, stop bars, runway guard lights, and the replacement, cleaning, optical, electrical, mechanical and operational verification of taxiway AGL fixtures and circuits.

Initial Issue

Technical Control — Exact taxiway lighting type, spacing, colors, locations, aiming, circuit configuration, stop-bar logic, interlocking, intensity settings and operational requirements shall be taken from the approved aerodrome lighting drawings, applicable ICAO/FAA/DGCA requirements, ALCMS/SMGCS philosophy where applicable, manufacturer's instructions and project specification.

SOP-185 Taxiway Edge Light Installation
Purpose

To ensure taxiway edge lights are installed at approved locations and provide continuous visual guidance along the taxiway.

Pre-Installation Checks

Verify:

  • Approved taxiway lighting layout.
  • Fixture type.
  • Fixture location.
  • Taxiway designation.
  • Circuit identification.
  • Base/can condition.
  • Transformer.
  • Secondary cable.
  • Fixture height.
  • Color configuration.
  • Frangibility requirements.
Procedure
  1. Identify the approved fixture location.
  2. Verify base/can condition.
  3. Inspect fixture.
  4. Verify transformer and secondary connection.
  5. Install mounting/coupling arrangement.
  6. Install fixture.
  7. Verify fixture height.
  8. Align fixture with taxiway geometry.
  9. Connect secondary cable.
  10. Apply specified torque.
  11. Inspect optical surface.
  12. Complete operational verification.
Acceptance

The fixture shall be:

  • Correctly positioned.
  • Correctly oriented.
  • Correctly elevated.
  • Mechanically secure.
  • Properly connected.
  • Free from optical contamination.
  • Operational.

SOP-203 Taxiway Lighting Operational Verification
Purpose

To verify correct operation of the taxiway lighting system following installation, maintenance or repair.

Pre-Test Checks

Confirm:

  • Work permit/authorization complete.
  • ATC/Operations coordination complete where required.
  • Work area clear.
  • Personnel clear.
  • Tools removed.
  • FOD inspection completed.
  • Circuit correctly restored.
  • Stop-bar/guard-light interfaces considered.
  • Control system available.
Procedure
  1. Obtain permission to energize/test.
  2. Energize the required taxiway circuit.
  3. Select required intensity/operational setting.
  4. Observe the complete lighting section.
  5. Verify correct ON/OFF condition, color, intensity and optical direction.
  6. Verify no flickering or intermittent operation.
  7. Verify correct sequence where applicable.
  8. Test stop bars where applicable.
  9. Test runway guard lights where applicable.
  10. Verify lead-on/lead-off operation where applicable.
  11. Verify control/ALCMS response where applicable.
  12. Record defects.
  13. Return system to required operational configuration.
  14. Complete documentation.

Master workflow, special interface checks, hold points, common failure modes and the master checklist that apply across all 19 Division 09 procedures.

A. Taxiway Lighting Master Workflow

The recommended workflow is:

APPROVED DESIGN TAXIWAY/INTERSECTION SURVEY FIXTURE LOCATION VERIFICATION BASE/CAN INSPECTION TRANSFORMER & SECONDARY CABLE VERIFICATION FIXTURE INSTALLATION HEIGHT VERIFICATION ALIGNMENT VERIFICATION CONNECTOR CONNECTION TORQUE VERIFICATION OPTICAL INSPECTION ELECTRICAL INSPECTION MECHANICAL INSPECTION FOD INSPECTION CONTROLLED ENERGIZATION OPERATIONAL VERIFICATION CONTROL/INTERLOCK VERIFICATION PHOTOMETRIC VERIFICATION WHERE REQUIRED FINAL RELEASE & DOCUMENTATION

C. Taxiway Lighting Quality Hold Points

  • HP-01 Layout — Verify fixture positions against approved drawings.
  • HP-02 Base/Can — Inspect base/can before fixture installation.
  • HP-03 Fixture — Verify correct fixture and optical configuration.
  • HP-04 Alignment — Verify fixture alignment with taxiway geometry.
  • HP-05 Connection — Verify transformer, secondary cable and connector.
  • HP-06 Stop Bar — Verify stop-bar location, alignment and control logic.
  • HP-07 Guard Light — Verify runway guard light location and operational behavior.
  • HP-08 Optical Inspection — Verify optical surfaces and color.
  • HP-09 FOD — Complete FOD inspection before operational testing.
  • HP-10 Operational Test — Verify complete taxiway lighting operation.
  • HP-11 Control System — Verify applicable ALCMS/interlocking/control functionality.
  • HP-12 Final Release — Release only after all applicable defects and interface issues are closed.

D. Common Failure Modes

  • Misaligned edge light — inconsistent visual guidance along the taxiway.
  • Incorrect centreline position — compromises intended centreline guidance.
  • Junction ambiguity — incorrect arrangement makes route identification difficult.
  • Curve misalignment — fixtures work electrically but curve guidance reads inconsistently.
  • Stop-bar control failure — physically functioning bar, but incorrect control/interlock.
  • Runway guard light failure — reduces conspicuity of a runway-entry point.
  • Optical contamination — dirty lenses/prisms reduce useful light output.
  • Connector failure — moisture, corrosion or poor mating causes intermittent fixtures.
  • Incorrect fixture type — wrong color/optical configuration despite illumination.
  • Control-system mismatch — operates locally but fails to respond to ALCMS/control philosophy.

Division 09 — Golden Rule

"Taxiway lighting must be treated as a complete guidance and control system — not as a collection of individual lamps. Location, spacing, alignment, color, optical performance, electrical integrity, stop-bar/guard-light operation and control-system response must work together to provide unambiguous aircraft guidance."

Division 09 — SOP Approval Principle

Each airport should adapt these SOPs to its own approved aerodrome lighting drawings, applicable ICAO/FAA/DGCA requirements, ALCMS/SMGCS philosophy, manufacturer's instructions and project specification.

Approved drawings and the applicable control/interlocking philosophy shall take precedence wherever they impose more restrictive or more specific requirements than these SOPs.

Quick Reference

Short answers on the taxiway lighting checklist and taxiway light replacement, taken from Division 09. Confirm all criteria against the approved layout and the current standard.

Taxiway lighting checklist

The taxiway lighting checklist in this division is the Master Taxiway Lighting Checklist. It runs from layout and identification (approved layout, designation, fixture type and location, circuit), through installation (base/can, transformer, secondary cable, connector, height, alignment, torque), to condition (optical surface, prism or lens, colour, electrical and mechanical condition, frangible arrangement). It then covers the special interfaces: junction, curve, rapid-exit, lead-on, lead-off, stop-bar and runway guard lights, and ALCMS interface where applicable. It closes with FOD inspection, operational test, photometric verification where required, defect closure, documentation and system release.

Taxiway light replacement

Taxiway light replacement follows SOP-198. Obtain work authorization and coordinate with Airport Operations/ATC as required. Identify the defective fixture, isolate the circuit, apply LOTO where applicable and verify a safe condition. Then remove the connector and the defective fixture, inspect the base/can and transformer, and install the approved replacement. Connect the secondary lead, verify alignment, apply the specified torque and inspect the optical surfaces. Finish with operational verification and the work-clearance procedure.

Frequently Asked Questions

Quick answers about Taxiway Lighting SOPs in the AGL SOP Library.

Q1What is Division 09 — Taxiway Lighting SOPs?

Division 09 is a set of 19 procedures (SOP-185 to SOP-203, Rev 0) for taxiway edge and centreline lighting, curved and rapid-exit or high-speed taxiways, junction lighting, lead-on and lead-off systems, stop bars and runway guard lights. It also covers replacement, cleaning, and optical, electrical, mechanical and operational verification.

Q2Where do taxiway lighting type, spacing and stop-bar logic come from?

From the approved aerodrome lighting drawings, applicable ICAO, FAA and DGCA requirements, ALCMS/SMGCS philosophy where applicable, manufacturer's instructions and the project specification. This covers type, spacing, colors, locations, aiming, circuit configuration, stop-bar logic, interlocking and intensity settings.

Q3How is a stop bar verified?

Stop-bar operation is verified as part of the complete control and interlocking system, not only as individual lamp operation (SOP-195). Checks include correct location, spacing and alignment, correct red indication where applicable, correct control response and interlock, and correct restoration after test.

Q4What must be checked for runway guard lights?

Runway guard lights are evaluated together with the associated runway-entry warning arrangement (SOP-197). The procedure verifies approved location, fixture type, mounting, orientation, elevation, electrical connection, control interface and operational performance.

Q5What special attention do rapid exit taxiways need?

The transition between runway lighting and rapid-exit taxiway guidance needs special attention (SOP-191). Verify the centreline and edge-light arrangement, fixture sequence, optical orientation, inset fixtures, connections and control response.

Q6What are the interface checks for taxiway junctions?

Correct guidance path, no visual ambiguity, correct centreline and edge lighting, a correct stop-bar arrangement and correct control logic.

Q7What is the taxiway lighting installation workflow?

Approved design, taxiway or intersection survey, fixture location and base/can verification, transformer and secondary cable verification, installation, then height, alignment, connector and torque verification. Then optical, electrical and mechanical inspection, FOD inspection, controlled energization, operational and control/interlock verification, photometric verification where required, and final release with documentation.

Q8What is checked in taxiway operational verification?

Work permit and ATC/Operations coordination are complete where required, the area, personnel and tools are clear, FOD inspection is done and stop-bar and guard-light interfaces are considered. After permission to energize, the team observes the complete lighting section for correct ON/OFF condition, color, intensity and optical direction (SOP-203).

Q9What is on the taxiway lighting checklist?

The Master Taxiway Lighting Checklist covers layout and identification, installation (base/can, transformer, cable, connector, height, alignment, torque), optical, electrical and mechanical condition, the special interfaces (junction, curve, rapid-exit, lead-on, lead-off, stop-bar, runway guard lights, ALCMS), FOD inspection, operational test, photometric verification where required, documentation and system release.

Q10How is taxiway light replacement done?

Taxiway light replacement (SOP-198) starts with work authorization, ATC coordination as required, circuit isolation, LOTO where applicable and a safe-condition check. The defective fixture is removed, the base/can and transformer are inspected, the approved replacement is installed and connected, then alignment, torque and optical surfaces are verified, followed by operational verification and work clearance.
