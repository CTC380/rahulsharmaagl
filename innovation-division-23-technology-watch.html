Technology Watch & Future Trends

Division 23 is the continuous technology-intelligence, horizon-scanning and future-readiness division of the AGL Innovation Hub. While Division 22 builds relationships with universities, research institutes, airports, manufacturers and startups, Division 23 continuously watches what is happening globally, identifies emerging technologies, and determines which developments could affect AGL engineering, airport operations, products and future investment. It operates as an AGL Technology Intelligence & Early-Warning System — identifying emerging technologies early, assessing their maturity, and converting relevant trends into technology opportunities, research topics, PoCs and future roadmaps.

TWF-13 Technology Disruption
Purpose

A dedicated early-warning function. Technology disruption occurs when an emerging technology significantly changes the economics, engineering or operating model of an existing technology.

Examples of Questions
  • Could AI replace parts of manual inspection?
  • Could autonomous vehicles change airfield inspection?
  • Could smart fixtures change conventional maintenance?
  • Could digital twins change engineering documentation?
  • Could new communications replace traditional monitoring architectures?
  • Could new materials significantly extend component life?
Objective

The objective is not to predict every disruption — it is to identify credible disruption scenarios early enough to respond.

Most Important Design Principle

Division 23 should remain the forward-looking intelligence layer of the innovation programme. It watches, assesses and forecasts — it does not itself execute research, build prototypes, run PoCs, or commercialize technology. Those functions belong to Divisions 18–22, which Division 23 feeds continuously.

Its flagship output — the Annual AGL Technology Outlook — should answer what changed, what emerged, matured, became commercially relevant or declined, what could disrupt AGL, what should be investigated, prototyped or monitored, and what should be retired, alongside recommended roadmap changes across 1, 3, 5 and 10+ year horizons.

Core philosophy: Watch → Understand → Assess → Prioritize → Research → Innovate → Prototype → Pilot → Benchmark → Protect → Commercialize → Adopt → Watch Again.

This makes Division 23 — Technology Watch & Future Trends the forward-looking intelligence layer of the entire AGL Innovation Framework, ensuring the programme does not only solve today's AGL problems, but continuously identifies the technologies that could define the next generation of Airfield Ground Lighting and future airport infrastructure.
