03 · CCR

Constant Current Regulators

The CCR is one of the principal electrical components of an AGL system, providing controlled series-circuit current to operate connected lighting equipment.

Controlled series-circuit currentIntensity stepsCircuit controlProtectionMonitoringFault indicationControl-system interface

The CCR must be selected according to circuit configuration, connected load, required current, intensity levels, control/monitoring requirements and the applicable project specification.

05 · Primary Circuit

Primary Cables & Primary Connectors

Primary Cables

Form the AGL series circuit connecting the CCR with the field components.

  • Cable type, voltage & current
  • Circuit length & insulation
  • Installation method & environment
  • Jointing, termination & testing

Primary Connectors

Connect the series-circuit cable to equipment such as isolating transformers.

  • Cable compatibility & electrical rating
  • Correct connector type
  • Environmental protection
  • Inspection & replacement compatibility

Cable condition is important during maintenance and LED retrofit projects; incorrect or damaged connections can affect a single fixture or the wider circuit.

06 · Secondary Connection

Secondary Connectors & L-823 Plugs / Receptacles

Secondary Connectors

Connect the transformer secondary side to the lighting fixture — particularly important when replacing fixtures during rehabilitation or LED retrofit.

  • Fixture type & cable
  • Electrical rating & configuration
  • Mechanical compatibility
  • Existing system arrangement

L-823 Plugs & Receptacles

Specialised airport series-circuit connection systems where applicable.

  • Primary & secondary connections
  • Transformer & fixture interfaces
  • Maintenance replacement
  • Existing-system rehabilitation
07 · Runway Lighting

Runway Lighting Components

The fixture type depends on the intended application and approved runway configuration, and can involve both elevated and inset fixtures.

Edge Lights

Visual definition of the lateral runway boundaries — fixture type, mounting, position, orientation and photometric performance.

Threshold Lights

Identify the runway threshold — colour requirement, optical performance and electrical connection.

Runway End Lights

Identify the end of the runway — position, orientation, transformer and testing.

Centreline Lights

Longitudinal guidance for enhanced-guidance runway configurations, requiring careful positioning and alignment.

Touchdown Zone Lights

Typically inset fixtures, transformers, secondary connectors, primary cable/connectors and CCR — per the runway design.

08 · Taxiway Lighting

Taxiway Lighting Components

Taxiway lighting supports aircraft movement through the airside taxiway network. The exact combination depends on the airport layout and operational requirements.

Taxiway Edge Lights

Define the lateral boundaries of the taxiway — position, orientation, mounting, transformer and photometric performance.

Taxiway Centreline Lights

Longitudinal guidance through designated taxi routes — installation quality and alignment are important for the intended guidance pattern.

FAQs

Frequently Asked Questions

What electrical components make up an AGL system?
Constant Current Regulators, isolating transformers, primary cables, primary connectors, secondary connectors, and L-823 plugs and receptacles.
What lighting components are part of the ecosystem?
Runway lights, taxiway lights, approach lights, threshold lights, runway end lights, centreline lights and touchdown zone lights.
What maintenance components support ongoing operation?
GLF spares, cable joint kits, replacement connectors, and lamps and LED components.
Why can't a similar-looking connector or fixture always be used as a replacement?
Two components can look similar but have different electrical, mechanical or optical characteristics. Replacement should be confirmed against the existing cable, connector type, transformer, fixture and circuit before procurement.
What should be checked before introducing LED components into an existing AGL system?
The LED component should be evaluated together with the existing CCR, series circuit, transformer, cable, connector, control/monitoring system and photometric performance — not assessed as a standalone product.
