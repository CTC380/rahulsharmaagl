Electrical Testing & Commissioning Comparison

CCR TESTING VS SERIES CIRCUIT TESTING

Two commissioning tests that are often lumped together but check completely different halves of an AGL system — CCR testing verifies that the Constant Current Regulator is producing and controlling the required current, while series circuit testing verifies that the complete field circuit connected to it is electrically healthy. One is about the power source; the other is about the path and equipment that source feeds.

13 · Engineering Verdict

CCR Testing vs Series Circuit Testing

Engineering Verdict

CCR testing verifies the performance, regulation, protection, control, and monitoring of the Constant Current Regulator.

Series circuit testing verifies the electrical integrity and performance of the complete AGL series circuit connected to the CCR. Both are required for commissioning: the CCR test confirms the power source is doing its job, and the series circuit test confirms the field it feeds — cables, connectors, isolation transformers, and fixtures — is sound.

CCR TestIs the power source producing and controlling the required constant current?
Series CircuitIs the complete field circuit electrically healthy and operating correctly?

Practical AGL sequence: CCR test → "Power source OK?" → Series circuit test → "Field circuit OK?" → Functional test → "Complete AGL system OK?"

14 · FAQ

Frequently Asked Questions

What is the fundamental difference between CCR testing and series circuit testing?

CCR testing verifies that the Constant Current Regulator itself is producing and controlling the required current correctly. Series circuit testing verifies that the complete field circuit connected to the CCR — cables, connectors, isolation transformers, and fixtures — is electrically healthy.

Does a CCR passing its test mean the AGL circuit is healthy?

No. The CCR can pass its own test while the field circuit still has an insulation fault, an open circuit, a damaged connector, or a failed isolation transformer. Both tests need to be performed independently.

Does a healthy series circuit mean the CCR is working correctly?

No. The field circuit can have good continuity, insulation, and connections while the CCR itself has a regulation, protection, or output-current problem. A healthy circuit does not prove the CCR is functioning correctly.

What instruments are typically used for each test?

CCR testing typically uses a multimeter, current meter, and the control/monitoring interface. Series circuit testing typically uses an insulation resistance tester, resistance/impedance test equipment, and circuit monitoring equipment.

Where do continuity and insulation resistance testing fit in?

Both are part of series circuit testing, not CCR testing. Continuity verifies the intended conductive path is complete, while insulation resistance verifies the circuit insulation prevents excessive leakage.

What's the correct troubleshooting order when runway lights aren't working correctly?

Check the CCR output first. If the CCR output is correct, move to the series circuit to check whether it's healthy. If the circuit is healthy too, check the fixtures and transformers. This separates a power-source problem from a field-circuit problem.

Is a functional test of the complete system still needed after individual testing?

Yes. A system-level functional test verifies the interaction between the CCR and the field circuit together, and for acceptance/commissioning it's often as important as the individual equipment tests.

15 · Standards & Reference Basis

Governing Documents

For an actual project design, always verify the current edition of the applicable documents. Actual current values, tolerances, and test criteria must come from the CCR manufacturer, approved design, and project specification.

ICAO Annex 14 / FAA Advisory Circulars

Governing requirements for AGL series-circuit design, CCR performance, and lighting system operation.

IS 12291 & CCR Manufacturer Documentation

Indian standard (Constant Current Regulators for Airport Lighting Systems) and manufacturer-specific test procedures for CCR output current, current steps, and protection functions.

DGCA / AAI Requirements

For Indian airport projects, also verify applicable DGCA requirements, AAI requirements, airport-specific technical specifications, project drawings and contract specifications.

Final Engineering Note

Do not select acceptance values from a generic web diagram. Final test criteria should always be developed from the current governing standard, approved design, and manufacturer-approved installation arrangement.

CCR testing and series circuit testing are not two versions of the same test — one confirms the source of controlled current, the other confirms the path and equipment receiving it.
