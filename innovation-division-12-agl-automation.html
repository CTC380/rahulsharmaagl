AGL Automation

Division 12 is the automation execution layer of the AGL Innovation Hub. Where Division 09 focuses on engineering innovation, Division 12 focuses on turning repetitive AGL inspection, testing, commissioning, reporting and maintenance activities into controlled digital and automated workflows. The objective: reduce repetitive manual work, improve consistency and traceability, accelerate fault identification and create closed-loop AGL maintenance and engineering workflows — while retaining appropriate human verification and safety controls.

AGL Automation — Master Architecture

Division 12 is recommended to be structured around seven levels, running from raw data collection through to human review, controlled action and feedback into the failure and engineering-intelligence database.

Level 1 — Data Collection: Sensors + Test Equipment + Inspection Systems + Photometry + IoT + Technician Inputs
Level 2 — Automated Processing: Data Capture + Calculation + Image Processing + Trend Analysis + Fault Detection
Level 3 — Automated Engineering: Testing + Compliance + Engineering Calculations + Condition Assessment
Level 4 — Automated Workflow: Alerts + Work Orders + Asset Updates + Reports
Level 5 — Human Review: Engineer + Supervisor + Authorized Technician
Level 6 — Controlled Action: Maintenance + Repair + Replacement + Commissioning + Operational Decision
Level 7 — Feedback: New Data → Asset History + Failure Database + Engineering Intelligence

Automation Levels

Not every process needs full autonomy. Five levels are recommended, applied separately to inspection, testing, commissioning and maintenance.

Level 0 — Manual: Technician performs the complete process
Level 1 — Digital Recording: Technician performs activity; data is digitally recorded
Level 2 — Assisted Automation: System performs calculations, comparisons and reporting
Level 3 — Conditional Automation: System automatically detects conditions and initiates predefined workflows
Level 4 — Highly Automated: System performs inspection/testing/workflow activities with limited human intervention
Level 5 — Autonomous: System independently performs the complete process within an approved operational envelope, with appropriate human oversight and safeguards
