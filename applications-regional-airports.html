AIRFIELD GROUND LIGHTING FOR REGIONAL AIRPORTS

Regional airports provide important aviation connectivity and require practical, reliable airfield visual guidance infrastructure — ranging from essential runway lighting to complete runway, taxiway and approach lighting systems.

Depending on the airport configuration and project scope, AGL requirements may include new installation, airport expansion, rehabilitation, component replacement, retrofit, testing, commissioning and maintenance. Our application-focused approach considers the complete system — lighting fixtures, series circuits, electrical infrastructure, control systems and long-term maintainability.

01 · Applications

AGL Applications for Regional Airports

The actual lighting configuration depends on the runway characteristics, operational requirements and approved project design.

Runway Applications

Depending on the airport configuration, runway AGL applications support take-off, landing and runway operations.

  • Runway edge lighting
  • Threshold lighting
  • Runway end lighting
  • Runway centreline lighting
  • Approach lighting
  • Other applicable visual guidance systems

Taxiway Applications

Supports aircraft movement between runways and other operational areas, considered together with the airport layout and electrical system.

  • Taxiway edge lighting
  • Taxiway centreline lighting
  • Holding-position visual guidance
  • Runway guard lighting
  • Associated airfield signs
  • Other applicable taxiway guidance systems

Visual Approach Guidance

Forms an important part of the overall airport visual guidance arrangement, depending on the approved airport configuration.

  • PAPI
  • APAPI where applicable
  • Approach lighting
  • Runway alignment guidance
  • Other visual approach guidance systems

Taxiway lighting should be considered together with the airport layout, electrical system and operational requirements — and the appropriate visual approach guidance system always depends on the runway, approach requirements and approved project configuration.

08 · FAQs

Frequently Asked Questions

What AGL equipment is commonly required at a regional airport?
The requirement depends on the airport layout and operational requirements and may include runway, taxiway, approach, visual guidance and airfield sign systems together with the associated electrical infrastructure.
Can regional airport AGL systems be upgraded in phases?
Yes. Existing infrastructure can be evaluated and upgraded as part of phased airport development, rehabilitation or component-replacement projects.
Can existing AGL infrastructure be reused during an upgrade?
Existing infrastructure may be retained where technically suitable. Compatibility and condition should be evaluated before integrating new equipment.
Can LED AGL be used at regional airports?
LED technology can be considered for suitable new-installation and retrofit applications, subject to the project requirements and compatibility with the AGL electrical system.
What electrical components are part of a regional airport AGL system?
Depending on the installation, the system can include CCRs, series-circuit cables, connectors, isolating transformers, secondary connections, lighting fixtures, signs and control equipment.
Is complete replacement necessary when upgrading a regional airport AGL system?
Not necessarily. Depending on the condition of the existing system, projects may involve selected component replacement, rehabilitation, retrofit or phased upgrades.
