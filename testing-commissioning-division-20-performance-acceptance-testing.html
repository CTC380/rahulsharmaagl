Division 20 · PAT · Final Performance Gate

Performance Acceptance Testing

Division 20 verifies that the complete installed AGL system achieves the required operational, electrical, photometric, control, reliability and availability performance under actual site conditions and in accordance with the approved project specifications and acceptance criteria. It is the performance gate that sits after commissioning, functional testing, photometry and system integration — it does not repeat those procedures, it takes their evidence and renders the final engineering judgement.

Philosophy: the fundamental question Division 20 answers is — does the completed AGL system not only operate, but actually deliver the required performance consistently and reliably? Every applicable parameter is carried through the same chain: Specified Performance → Measured Performance → Acceptance Decision.

Where Division 20 Sits

Division 20 takes the evidence generated by Divisions 02–19, adds the required performance measurements, and makes the final engineering judgement — it should not simply repeat previous test procedures.

DivisionMain Purpose
16 — Photometric TestingMeasure the optical characteristics of the installed lighting
17 — FATVerify equipment before site installation/dispatch
18 — SATVerify the installed system meets site acceptance requirements
19 — System Integration TestingVerify that different systems communicate and work together correctly
20 — Performance Acceptance TestingVerify that the completed system actually achieves the required overall performance

Before Testing Begins

Two conditions should be established before performance acceptance testing of any AGL system begins.

Acceptance Criteria Are Project-Specific

  • Required performance values come from the contract specification, approved technical specification and approved drawings — never an assumed universal figure
  • Availability requirements must come from the approved project requirement/specification where one is defined
  • Photometric acceptance criteria are the applicable criteria referenced by the project, not a generic benchmark
  • CCR, circuit and electrical performance values are taken from the CCR specification and applicable project documentation

Division Boundary

  • Division 16 establishes the photometric testing methodology and measurement results; Division 20 evaluates whether those results demonstrate acceptable system performance
  • Divisions 02–19 generate the underlying commissioning, functional, photometric and integration evidence that Division 20 builds on
  • Division 20 is treated as the performance gate after commissioning, functional testing, photometry and system integration
  • No performance area is accepted until measured evidence is compared against the applicable specified requirement

A

Overall System Performance

1 topic
TC-PAT-001Overall System Performance Verification
Verification Checks
  • Verify overall system operation and operational continuity
  • Verify lighting availability and system stability
  • Verify circuit performance and equipment response
  • Verify CCR performance and control-system response
  • Verify ALCMS/monitoring performance
  • Verify photometric performance and emergency operation
  • Compile the master performance record across all applicable parameters
Evaluate
  • Specified Performance → Measured Performance → Acceptance Decision
  • Record parameter, required performance, measured performance, deviation, and result
Acceptance / Record

Acceptance Criteria: the complete AGL system, evaluated as an integrated operational system, achieves the required performance across every applicable parameter with no critical deficiency.

B

Lighting Subsystem Performance

5 topics
TC-PAT-002Runway Lighting Performance
Verification Checks
  • Verify runway edge, threshold, runway end, centreline and touchdown-zone lighting, and runway guard lights where applicable
  • Verify correct operation, required intensity, and intensity-step operation
  • Verify circuit loading and circuit stability
  • Verify switching response and control response
  • Verify photometric performance, light availability, and fault response
Acceptance / Record

Acceptance Criteria: the installed runway lighting system's measured performance matches the runway design requirement across every applicable subsystem.

TC-PAT-003Taxiway Lighting Performance
Verification Checks
  • Verify taxiway edge lighting and centreline lighting
  • Verify clearance bars, exit lighting, and rapid-exit taxiway lighting where applicable
  • Verify switching, intensity control, and circuit performance
  • Verify control response and photometric performance
Acceptance / Record

Acceptance Criteria: operational availability, lighting response, uniformity where applicable, and correct circuit and control operation are confirmed across the installed taxiway lighting system.

TC-PAT-004Approach-Lighting Performance
Verification Checks
  • Verify light operation, light-bar operation, and crossbar operation
  • Verify alignment, intensity, and colour
  • Verify circuit performance, control, and photometric performance
  • Verify system availability
Hold Point

Hold Point: the assessment considers the complete installed approach-light system, not only individual fixtures.

TC-PAT-005PAPI Performance
Verification Checks
  • Verify unit operation and correct unit sequence
  • Verify alignment, elevation setting, and beam performance
  • Verify colour indication and intensity
  • Verify photometric performance and electrical performance
  • Verify control, monitoring, and system availability
Acceptance / Record

Acceptance Criteria: the required PAPI/APAPI performance matches the measured site performance across alignment, beam, colour, and availability.

TC-PAT-006Sign Performance
Verification Checks
  • Verify correct message, illumination, brightness, and colour
  • Verify visibility and uniformity where applicable
  • Verify electrical performance and switching
  • Verify control, monitoring, and fault indication
Acceptance / Record

Acceptance Criteria: the sign remains operational, the correct message is displayed, illumination meets the specified requirement, and control/monitoring operate correctly.

C

Electrical & Control Performance

4 topics
TC-PAT-007CCR Performance
Verification Checks
  • Verify input supply, output current, and current regulation
  • Verify output stability, intensity steps, and step transition
  • Verify load response, protection, and alarm response
  • Verify control response, local/remote operation, and communication
  • Verify fault response
  • Record output current, regulation, stability, load response, and step response against required values
Hold Point

Hold Point: the exact CCR performance acceptance values must be taken from the CCR specification and applicable project documentation, not assumed.

TC-PAT-008Circuit Performance
Verification Checks
  • Verify circuit continuity and insulation condition
  • Verify circuit resistance, operating current, and circuit loading
  • Verify transformer performance and connector condition
  • Verify fixture operation, circuit stability, and fault response
Evaluate
  • CCR → Primary Circuit → Transformer → Secondary → Fixture
Acceptance / Record

Acceptance Criteria: the entire circuit performs as an integrated electrical path, end to end, under the applicable operating condition.

TC-PAT-009Control-System Performance
Verification Checks
  • Verify command response, switching response, and intensity commands
  • Verify local control and remote control
  • Verify ALCMS response, HMI response, PLC response, and SCADA response where applicable
  • Verify status feedback, alarm response, event logging, and interlocks
Performance Chain
  • Operator Command → Control System → Field Equipment → Feedback
Acceptance / Record

Acceptance Criteria: the complete command-to-feedback chain performs correctly and within the required response time for every applicable control path.

TC-PAT-011Electrical Performance
Verification Checks
  • Verify supply voltage, operating current, circuit current, and circuit loading
  • Verify insulation performance, continuity, earth resistance, and earth continuity
  • Verify transformer performance, CCR output, auxiliary supply, and control supply
  • Evaluate stability, correct operating range, and abnormal conditions
  • Evaluate protection operation, electrical losses where applicable, and system response under expected operating conditions
  • Record system, parameter, required, measured, and status
Acceptance / Record

Acceptance Criteria: every applicable electrical parameter operates within its correct range and responds correctly under expected and abnormal operating conditions.

D

Photometric Performance

1 topic
TC-PAT-010Photometric Performance
Verification Checks
  • Verify applicable systems: runway, taxiway, approach, PAPI/APAPI, and airfield signs
  • Evaluate light intensity, beam alignment, and colour
  • Evaluate uniformity, distribution, and aiming
  • Evaluate visibility and applicable intensity settings
  • Compare measured photometry against applicable acceptance criteria
Note

Note: Division 16 establishes the photometric testing methodology and measurement results; Division 20 evaluates whether those results demonstrate acceptable system performance — photometric performance is treated as a performance acceptance parameter, not merely a measurement exercise.

E

Availability & Emergency Performance

2 topics
TC-PAT-012System Availability Verification
Verification Checks
  • Verify equipment availability, circuit availability, and lighting availability
  • Verify CCR availability, control-system availability, and monitoring availability
  • Verify communication availability and backup/standby availability where provided
  • Consider equipment unavailable, circuit unavailable, and communication failure
  • Consider control-system failure, corrective maintenance condition, and standby-system operation
  • Record total installed, available, unavailable, availability, requirement, and result per system
Hold Point

Hold Point: the acceptance criterion for availability must come from the approved project requirement/specification where one is defined.

TC-PAT-013Emergency-Operation Performance
Verification Checks
  • Verify emergency command and emergency lighting response
  • Verify CCR response and control-system response
  • Verify ALCMS indication and alarm response
  • Verify standby supply where applicable
  • Verify recovery and return to normal operation
Test Sequence
  • Emergency Condition → Emergency Control → AGL System Response → Field Lighting Response → Monitoring/Alarm → Recovery
Acceptance / Record

Acceptance Criteria: the AGL system correctly performs the complete emergency sequence and recovers to normal operation as specified.

F

Compliance & Closeout

2 topics
TC-PAT-014Performance Against Project Specification
Verification Checks
  • Compare every applicable performance parameter against the contract specification
  • Compare against the approved technical specification and approved drawings
  • Compare against approved equipment requirements and approved testing procedure
  • Compare against applicable project acceptance criteria
  • Complete the performance compliance matrix across all 12–14 performance areas: requirement, specification, measured result, compliance, remarks
  • Assign the final acceptance category: PASS, PASS WITH MINOR OBSERVATIONS, CONDITIONAL ACCEPTANCE, RETEST REQUIRED, or FAIL
Hold Point

Hold Point: the final acceptance decision is based on objective measured evidence — this is the final technical comparison before certification.

TC-PAT-015Performance Acceptance Report
Verification Checks
  • Record cover page: airport, project, AGL system, PAT reference, test date, contractor, consultant, client
  • Record purpose/scope, reference documents, and test equipment with calibration status
  • Compile performance test results, photometric performance, and electrical performance
  • Compile CCR performance, control-system performance, and availability assessment
  • Compile emergency-operation performance and the specification compliance matrix
  • Document punch list/NCR items and corrective action and retesting closure
  • State the final performance assessment and obtain approval sign-off: contractor, testing engineer, consultant, client/authorized representative
Acceptance / Record

Acceptance Criteria: a single consolidated performance record with a formal certification statement is produced — this is the principal technical evidence supporting final AGL system performance acceptance.
