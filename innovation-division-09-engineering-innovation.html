ENG-14 Engineering Optimization
Purpose

Identify the best engineering solution among multiple technically viable alternatives.

Optimization Variables
  • Reliability
  • Energy
  • Lifecycle cost
  • Maintenance
  • Installation
  • Equipment quantity
  • Cable requirements
  • Operational performance
  • Environmental impact
  • Future expandability
Optimization Concept

Instead of: "Find any compliant design" — move toward: "Find the best technically compliant lifecycle solution."

Example

Design A — lower initial cost but higher maintenance. Design B — higher initial cost but longer service life and lower lifecycle cost. An optimization model should be able to compare the two systematically.

Most Important Design Principle

Division 09 converts all upstream inputs — smart lighting, AI, IoT, digital engineering, robotics, sustainability and product data — into advanced engineering methods, automated calculations and decision support. It sits as the natural engineering intelligence layer between the technology divisions and actual AGL design decisions.

Automation across circuit design, cable sizing, voltage-drop, transformer and CCR selection should improve speed, consistency and traceability, but engineering tools must never become uncontrolled black boxes — each must expose its input, calculation method, assumptions, result and verification, and each output remains subject to qualified engineering review and technical accountability.

In the overall Innovation Hub, Division 08 creates better AGL products, Division 09 creates better ways to engineer them, Division 04 supplies connected data, Division 03 provides intelligence, and Division 05 provides the digital engineering environment. This gives the framework a clear progression from product innovation → engineering innovation → intelligent engineering decision-making.
