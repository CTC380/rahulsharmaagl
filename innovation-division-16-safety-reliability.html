Division 16 of 25 · SR Series

Advanced Safety & Reliability Innovation

Division 16 is the safety, resilience and reliability engineering division of the AGL Innovation Hub. It moves AGL from conventional compliance and reactive maintenance toward systems designed to prevent failures, tolerate failures, detect degradation early, maintain essential functionality and recover rapidly from abnormal conditions. Its core philosophy is a single closed loop: Prevent → Detect → Tolerate → Protect → Recover → Learn.

SR-02 Reliability-by-Design
Purpose

Design AGL systems to achieve required reliability before installation, rather than attempting to improve reliability only after failures occur.

Areas

  • Reliability targets
  • Component reliability

Key Concept

Instead of asking:

How do we repair this system when it fails?

design should ask:

How can the probability and consequence of failure be reduced from the beginning?

Safety & Reliability Maturity Model

A recommended way to develop this capability progressively, level by level.

LEVEL 1

Reactive Safety

Safety action occurs after failures/incidents.

LEVEL 2

Preventive Safety

Known hazards are addressed through planned controls.

LEVEL 3

Safety-by-Design

Safety is integrated during engineering design.

LEVEL 4

Condition Monitoring

Critical assets are continuously or periodically monitored.

LEVEL 5

Predictive Reliability

Failure and degradation are predicted.

LEVEL 6

Resilient AGL

Systems are designed to tolerate defined failures and recover effectively.

LEVEL 7

Intelligent Safety

AI, analytics, monitoring and reliability models provide continuous safety-performance intelligence.

Most Important Design Principle

Division 16 should become the safety, resilience and reliability engineering division of the AGL Innovation framework — moving AGL from conventional compliance and maintenance toward systems designed to prevent failures, tolerate failures, detect degradation early, maintain essential functionality and recover rapidly from abnormal conditions.

Its core philosophy is a single closed loop: Prevent → Detect → Tolerate → Protect → Recover → Learn. It connects strongly with Division 03 (AI for AGL), Division 09 (Engineering Innovation), Division 13 (Digital AGL Control & Monitoring) and Division 15 (Intelligent Maintenance & Predictive AGL), and with the Failure Records & Reliability framework.

This makes Division 16 the natural safety-and-reliability backbone connecting the innovation programme with the existing failure database, asset lifecycle management, predictive maintenance, digital ALCMS and engineering decision-support framework — transforming AGL from "documents about compliance" into "systems engineered for safety, reliability and resilience."
