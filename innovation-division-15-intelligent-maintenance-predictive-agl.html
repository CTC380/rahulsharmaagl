Intelligent Maintenance & Predictive AGL

Division 15 is the intelligence layer of the AGL maintenance system. It uses the automation, digital monitoring and advanced photometric intelligence established in Divisions 12–14 to answer the question that matters most: what is likely to fail, when, how serious is the risk, and what maintenance action should be taken before the failure affects AGL availability or operational performance. The overall progression modelled by this division is Preventive Maintenance → Condition-Based Maintenance → Predictive Maintenance → Prescriptive Maintenance → Intelligent AGL Maintenance.

Concept

Expected Service Life is not necessarily equal to Actual Remaining Useful Life. An asset with a nominal 15-year service life could require replacement earlier or remain serviceable longer depending on actual condition and operating environment.

PDM-05 Condition-Based Maintenance

PDM-14 Failure-Risk Prediction
Purpose

Move beyond predicting failure alone and determine the risk associated with failure. A useful conceptual model is:

Failure Risk = Probability × Consequence
Probability Factors
  • Asset condition
  • Degradation
  • Age
  • Failure history
  • Test results
  • Operating conditions
Consequence Factors
  • Runway criticality
  • Taxiway criticality
  • Redundancy
  • Operational impact
  • Safety significance
  • Availability impact
Result

An older asset does not automatically become the highest maintenance priority. A relatively new asset with rapidly deteriorating condition and high operational criticality may have greater immediate risk.

Predictive Maintenance Maturity Model

Division 15 positions AGL maintenance along a six-level maturity progression.

LevelStageDescription
1PreventiveMaintenance based mainly on time or usage.
2Condition-BasedMaintenance based on measured asset condition.
3PredictiveData is used to predict degradation or failure.
4Risk-BasedFailure probability and operational consequence are combined.
5PrescriptiveSystem recommends the most appropriate maintenance action.
6Intelligent MaintenanceAI, automation, asset intelligence and engineering judgment operate as one integrated maintenance system.
