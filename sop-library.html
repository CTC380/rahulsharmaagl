Frequently Asked Questions

Quick answers about the AGL SOP Library and how its 22 divisions are organized.

Q1What is the AGL SOP Library?

The AGL SOP Library by Precision AGL Academy is a collection of 480 Standard Operating Procedures for airfield ground lighting, organized into 22 divisions. It covers testing, maintenance and field operations, drawn from real airport project experience. All 22 divisions are published (Rev 0, initial issue).

Q2How is the library organized?

By division, each with its own SOP number range. Divisions 01–02 cover airside safety and electrical safety/LOTO; 03–07 cover planning, civil works, cabling and isolation transformers; 08–12 cover runway, taxiway and approach lighting, PAPI/VASI and signage; and 13–15 cover CCR and series circuits, ALCMS and earthing. Divisions 16–19 cover inspection, testing and photometry, maintenance and commissioning, and 20–22 cover emergency response, documentation and training.

Q3Which division covers electrical isolation and LOTO?

Division 02 — Electrical Safety, LOTO & Isolation (SOP-26 → SOP-50, 25 procedures). Airside work permits and runway or taxiway proximity rules are in Division 01 — Airside Safety & Work Permit (SOP-01 → SOP-25, 25 procedures).

Q4Where can I find procedures for runway, taxiway and approach lighting, PAPI and signs?

Runway Lighting is Division 08, Taxiway Lighting Division 09, Approach Lighting Division 10, PAPI / VASI Division 11 and Airfield Signage Division 12. Inspection, testing and maintenance of these systems are covered in Divisions 16, 17 and 18.

Q5Which divisions cover CCRs, ALCMS and earthing?

CCR & Series Circuit is Division 13 (25 procedures), ALCMS / Control & Monitoring is Division 14 (20 procedures) and Earthing, Bonding & Lightning Protection is Division 15 (20 procedures).

Q6Where are commissioning, emergency response and record-keeping procedures?

Commissioning & Acceptance is Division 19, Emergency Response is Division 20 and Documentation, Records & Reporting is Division 21. Training, Competency & Knowledge Management is Division 22.

Q7Do these SOPs replace an airport's own procedures?

No. The SOPs are a technical framework. Each division states that approved project drawings, manufacturer's instructions, applicable standards and the airport's own procedures, permit-to-work system, ATC instructions and local regulations take precedence, and each airport should adapt them to its own manuals and requirements.
