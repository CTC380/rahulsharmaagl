03 · Supplier Categories

Who Makes Up the Indian AGL Supply Chain

Unlike conventional outdoor lighting, AGL equipment must operate as part of an integrated airfield lighting system: CCR → Primary Cable → Primary Connector → Isolating Transformer → Secondary Connector → Light Fixture. Equipment selection should consider the individual product and its compatibility with the complete system.

07 · Lamps, LED & Signage

08 · Cable Accessories & GLF Spares

Keeping the Series Circuit Reliable

Cable Accessories

Cable joint kits, termination accessories, primary/secondary connectors and cable protection — particularly important because the AGL series circuit depends on reliable electrical and environmental connections.

GLF Spares

Lamps, connectors, transformer and fixture components, LED modules, drivers, optical components, hardware, seals and gaskets. The actual spare list should be developed from the installed equipment and airport maintenance requirements.

Airport Lighting Power Supply

Airport lighting power supply on an airfield is delivered through the series circuit: CCR, primary cable, primary connector, isolating transformer, secondary connector and light fixture. The CCR, transformers, cables and connectors must therefore be selected as one system, with the series-circuit rating, connector arrangement and control & monitoring interface verified before any item is replaced or added. For the wider electrical architecture, see AGL Electrical Architecture & Redundancy.

Manufacturers

Airfield Lighting Manufacturers & Airport Lighting Manufacturers in India

Airfield Lighting Manufacturers

Airfield lighting manufacturers design and build fixtures, regulators, transformers and cables to published standards. Ask each for type-test reports and approvals accepted by your aerodrome operator, not just a catalogue.

Airport Lighting Manufacturers

Airport lighting manufacturers differ in range: some cover the full series circuit and control, others only fixtures or cable. Check which products they make themselves and which they resell.

Airfield Lighting Manufacturer

An airfield lighting manufacturer should be able to show factory quality control, certified test results and spare parts support for the full life of the installation.

Airfield Ground Lighting Manufacturer

An airfield ground lighting manufacturer is judged on compliance with the specified standard, such as ICAO Annex 14 photometry or FAA fixture specifications, and on how long it supports each product.

Airport Lighting Manufacturer

When choosing an airport lighting manufacturer, compare certified performance, delivery time, warranty terms and after-sales support in India, along with price.

Runway Edge Lights Manufacturer

A runway edge lights manufacturer should provide photometric test reports and certification to the specified standard, along with maintenance and spare lamp or LED module information.

Constant Current Regulator Manufacturer

A constant current regulator manufacturer should be able to show compliance with the relevant standard, such as IEC 61822 or FAA CCR requirements, plus service support and spare parts.

Airfield Lighting Isolation Transformer Manufacturer

An airfield lighting isolation transformer manufacturer should show type-test results for insulation, open-circuit behaviour and temperature rise, and give clear selection guidance.

Airfield Lighting Cable Manufacturer

An airfield lighting cable manufacturer should provide material and test data, such as insulation resistance and voltage withstand results, and traceability for each batch.

Airfield Lighting Control System Manufacturer

An airfield lighting control system manufacturer should show standards compliance, redundancy options, supported CCR interfaces and a clear plan for software support.

ALCMS Manufacturer

An ALCMS manufacturer should be able to show how its control and monitoring software and hardware interface with the CCRs in use, what redundancy and alarm functions are provided, and how updates, spares and training are supported over the life of the system. See the ALCMS page for how the system works.

Suppliers & Distributors

AGL Equipment Suppliers, Distributors & Vendor List in India

AGL Equipment Suppliers

AGL equipment suppliers include manufacturers' own sales channels, authorised distributors and traders. Confirm authorisation in writing, since it affects warranty and genuine spares.

AGL Equipment Supplier India

An AGL equipment supplier in India should be clear on import or local manufacture, lead time, customs and delivery, and who handles warranty claims and site support.

AGL Equipment Distributor

An AGL equipment distributor holds stock and resells products from one or more manufacturers. Check that it is authorised for the brand and that spares and documentation are available.

Airfield Lighting Supplier

An airfield lighting supplier should supply to a clear technical specification and provide datasheets, certificates and installation guidance, so the material submitted matches what is delivered.

Airport Lighting Supplier

Airport lighting supplier selection should look at technical competence and track record on comparable airfields, not only the quoted price.

Airfield Lighting System Supplier India

An airfield lighting system supplier in India can provide the full series circuit, from regulators and cable to fixtures and control, or only part of it. Confirm that the parts are compatible as a system.

Airport Lighting Products India

Airport lighting products available in India include CCRs, isolating transformers, L-823 connectors, cables, light fixtures, lamps and signs. List each item against the BOQ and its specification.

Airfield Lighting Vendor List

An airfield lighting vendor list is a project's pre-approved set of manufacturers and suppliers. Build it from technical approval and past performance, and review it for each tender.

Best Airfield Lighting System

The best airfield lighting system is the one that meets the runway category and standards, is compatible as a whole, and can be maintained locally. It is a technical decision, not a brand ranking.

Components

Runway, Taxiway, Approach, CCR, Transformer, Cable & Connector Suppliers

Approach Lighting Supplier

An approach lighting supplier must provide frangible structures and fixtures that meet the specified approach system, along with mounting hardware, cables and installation details.

Runway Lighting Supplier

A runway lighting supplier should provide edge, threshold, end and centreline fixtures in the correct colours and beam patterns, with matching bases, transformers and connectors.

Taxiway Lighting Supplier

A taxiway lighting supplier should provide edge and centreline fixtures with the right colours, and, where needed, stop bars and guidance signs designed to work with the circuit.

Runway Edge Lights Supplier

A runway edge lights supplier should confirm the fixture type (elevated or inset), colour, intensity class and frangibility, and supply matching transformers and connectors.

Constant Current Regulator Supplier

A constant current regulator supplier should confirm output rating, number of brightness steps, standard compliance and control interface, and match the CCR size to the circuit load.

Airfield Lighting Transformer Supplier

An airfield lighting transformer supplier should provide series isolating transformers matched to lamp wattage and current, with correct insulation rating and connectors.

Airfield Lighting Isolation Transformer Supplier

An airfield lighting isolation transformer supplier should state the rating, lead type and compliance with standards such as IEC 61823 or the FAA specification, so it suits the fixtures and series circuit.

AGL Cable Supplier

An AGL cable supplier should provide single-core series cable with the specified voltage rating, insulation and sheath, supplied on drums with test certificates.

Airfield Lighting Cable Supplier

An airfield lighting cable supplier should offer the cable type, rating and certification the project specifies, plus joint kits and accessories that suit it.

L-823 Connector Supplier

An L-823 connector supplier should supply plugs and receptacles that match the cable and transformer lead, with approvals and moulding or assembly instructions, since poor connectors are a common fault source.

Airfield Lighting Control System Supplier

An airfield lighting control system supplier should provide the ALCMS hardware, software and interfaces, with FAT and SAT support. See the ALCMS page for how the system works.

ALCMS Supplier

An ALCMS supplier should provide a design suited to the airport's size and procedures, with documentation, training and long-term support for software and hardware.

Spares & Retrofit

AGL Spare Parts & Airfield Lighting Retrofit Products

AGL Spare Parts Supplier

An AGL spare parts supplier should be able to deliver lamps, lenses, gaskets, connectors and transformers quickly, so repairs are not delayed and the airfield stays within serviceability limits.

Airfield Lighting Spare Parts India

Airfield lighting spare parts in India should be stocked or available at short notice. Ask about lead time, minimum order, obsolescence policy and genuine-part guarantees.

Airfield Lighting Retrofit Products

Airfield lighting retrofit products, such as LED conversion kits or replacement fixtures, must be compatible with the existing series circuit, transformers and control, so check load, current and photometry first.

20 · Important

No Single Supplier Category Fits Every Project

There is no single supplier category that is automatically suitable for every airport project. The right supplier depends on project scope, equipment requirement, airport specification, operational category, existing AGL system, applicable standards, required testing, delivery requirements, installation scope and maintenance requirements.

A supplier should therefore be evaluated against the actual project requirement, not only against product availability or price. A practical starting point is: BOQ + Technical Specification + Existing System Details + Required Equipment — from which the required product, rating, standard, compatibility, testing and documentation can be established.

Product capability, complete technical documentation, demonstrable compliance, available type/routine/acceptance test documents, compatibility with the existing AGL system, project experience, spare availability, submittal and commissioning support, delivery within schedule, and after-sales technical support.
What is the difference between a supplier, a manufacturer and a contractor?
A manufacturer focuses on product engineering, manufacturing and testing; a supplier focuses on procurement, availability and technical submittals; a contractor executes site installation, testing and commissioning; a system integrator coordinates multiple equipment and systems. A single organisation may perform more than one of these roles.
What should be checked before replacing an AGL component?
The existing equipment's electrical characteristics, mechanical compatibility, connector, transformer, CCR, control system and applicable specification should all be verified before installing a replacement, to reduce the risk of a technically incompatible fit.
