Electrical Testing

The core electrical-testing division of the AGL Testing & Commissioning Manual. Division 02 verifies the electrical integrity, continuity, insulation, resistance, polarity, voltage, current, power, circuit connections, transformer circuits, connectors, control circuits, and auxiliary supplies of the installed AGL system — before any functional or system-level commissioning begins.

Sequence: Prepare → Isolate → Verify Safe → Test → Record → Evaluate → Accept/Reject → Report. Test methods, test voltages, permissible limits, discharge requirements, and acceptance values are always taken from the applicable project specification, approved test procedure, equipment manufacturer requirements, and applicable standards — this division defines the testing scope, not the numerical acceptance limits.

Master Electrical Testing Sequence

Division 02 is worked as a staged sequence rather than a single pass — each group below builds on the one before it, and the division closes only once evaluation and acceptance are complete.

GroupWhat It Confirms
A — Insulation & ContinuityCable and equipment insulation is intact, and the intended electrical path exists without an unintended open circuit
B — EarthingThe installed earthing system, earth electrodes, and protective bonding paths meet resistance and continuity requirements
C — Polarity & ResistanceConnections are correctly oriented, and circuit/cable resistance is consistent with design expectations
D — Electrical MeasurementsRequired supply voltage, current, power, and phase configuration are present and correct at specified test points
E — Cable & Termination TestingInstalled cables, joints, and terminations are electrically and mechanically sound
F — Circuit & Equipment VerificationPrimary and secondary circuits, transformers, and connectors are electrically verified before energization
G — Control & AuxiliaryControl circuits and auxiliary supplies are electrically sound ahead of functional testing
H — Evaluation, Acceptance & ReportingEvery result is evaluated against an approved criterion, formally accepted or rejected, and traceable to a test report

Before Testing Begins

Two requirement groups should be closed out before the Division 02 team starts working through the topic list.

Reference Package

  • Project specification, approved test procedure, and applicable standards
  • Test voltages, permissible limits, and discharge requirements for each test type
  • Circuit, cable, transformer, and connector schedules
  • Equipment manufacturer requirements for winding resistance, ratio, and rated values
  • Division 01 pre-commissioning verification record and energization authorization

Safety & Instrument Readiness

  • Confirm the circuit under test is identified and correctly isolated
  • Confirm absence of voltage before connecting any test instrument
  • Disconnect sensitive electronic equipment where the test procedure requires it
  • Confirm the correct instrument, test range, and valid calibration status
  • Confirm discharge procedure is understood and applied after every insulation test

The 23 Topics

Grouped by test focus, in the order the division is normally worked — click any topic to expand its verification checks and acceptance criteria.

A

Insulation & Continuity

2 topics
TC-ELE-001Insulation Resistance Testing
Verification Checks
  • Identify the circuit under test and confirm isolation
  • Confirm absence of voltage before connecting test leads
  • Disconnect sensitive electronic equipment if required
  • Select the appropriate insulation tester and test range
  • Apply the specified test voltage to primary, secondary, control, and auxiliary circuits as applicable
  • Record measured insulation resistance, instrument ID, and calibration status
  • Discharge the circuit after testing before restoring it
  • Confirm no unintended leakage path to earth exists
Acceptance / Record

Acceptance Criteria: measured insulation resistance meets the value specified in the project specification/approved test procedure, with the circuit safely discharged and restored only after confirming safe conditions.

TC-ELE-002Continuity Testing
Verification Checks
  • Verify cable conductor continuity and circuit continuity
  • Verify protective conductor continuity where applicable
  • Verify secondary lead, control wiring, connector, and termination continuity
  • Check for open circuits and broken conductors
  • Check for incorrect terminations and loose connections
  • Check for incorrect cable identification and poor connector contact
Acceptance / Record

Acceptance Criteria: result is recorded as continuity satisfactory for every test point; any "not satisfactory" result is investigated and corrected before proceeding.

B

Earthing

2 topics
TC-ELE-003Earth Resistance Testing
Verification Checks
  • Verify earth electrode/pit and earth conductor identification
  • Verify the earth connection and earthing system configuration
  • Confirm test point identification and test method used
  • Record instrument ID, test conditions, and measured resistance
  • Compare measured resistance against the acceptance criterion
Acceptance / Record

Acceptance Criteria: measured earth resistance meets the project acceptance criterion. Earth resistance and earth continuity are also revisited under the dedicated Earthing & Bonding Testing division.

TC-ELE-004Earth Continuity Testing
Verification Checks
  • Verify protective earth and bonding paths at equipment, transformer, panel, and CCR
  • Verify fixture, sign, and structural bonding where applicable
  • Confirm correct conductor and secure connection at each bonding point
  • Confirm continuity with no broken bonding conductor or loose connection
  • Confirm correct identification of every bonding point tested
Acceptance / Record

Acceptance Criteria: protective earth and bonding paths are confirmed electrically continuous at every typical point, with no broken or loose bonding conductor outstanding.

C

Polarity & Resistance

3 topics
TC-ELE-005Polarity Verification
Verification Checks
  • Confirm correct conductor identification and termination
  • Confirm correct polarity and connector arrangement
  • Confirm correct secondary connection
  • Confirm correct control-circuit polarity where applicable
  • Confirm correct auxiliary supply polarity where applicable
TC-ELE-006Circuit Resistance Measurement
Verification Checks
  • Measure the resistance of the complete electrical circuit or specified section
  • Compare against cable length, cable characteristics, and number of connected components
  • Compare against transformer arrangement, fixture load, and design expectations
  • Investigate unusually high resistance for poor termination, damaged conductor, or connector problems
  • Investigate unusually low resistance for incorrect configuration, short circuit, or unexpected parallel path
TC-ELE-007Cable Resistance Measurement
Verification Checks
  • Verify conductor resistance and circuit resistance
  • Verify resistance consistency and cable identification
  • Confirm test-point identification
  • Compare measured resistance against calculated/design resistance and manufacturer data
  • Investigate any significant deviation before acceptance
Acceptance / Record

Acceptance Criteria: measured cable resistance is consistent with calculated/design resistance and manufacturer data, with any significant deviation investigated and closed out.

D

Electrical Measurements

4 topics
TC-ELE-008Voltage Measurement
Verification Checks
  • Measure incoming supply, CCR input, and CCR output where applicable
  • Measure panel supply, auxiliary supply, and control supply
  • Measure transformer secondary voltage and equipment supply where applicable
  • Record measurement location, nominal voltage, measured voltage, and operating condition
  • Record circuit/intensity setting where applicable
  • Confirm measurement is performed using suitable instruments and safe working procedures
Acceptance / Record

Acceptance Criteria: measured voltage at every specified test point is within the nominal/design range, recorded with instrument ID and operating condition.

TC-ELE-009Current Measurement
Verification Checks
  • Measure CCR output and AGL series circuit current
  • Measure auxiliary supply, equipment load, and control supply current where appropriate
  • Compare measured current against expected current
  • Evaluate current stability and variation under different operating conditions
  • Investigate any abnormal current reading
Acceptance / Record

Acceptance Criteria: measured current is consistent with expected current and stable across operating conditions. Detailed CCR output-current and regulation testing is addressed subsequently under CCR Testing & Commissioning.

TC-ELE-010Power Measurement
Verification Checks
  • Measure voltage, current, and power at CCR input and CCR auxiliary supply
  • Measure power at control panels and other specified loads
  • Measure power factor and frequency where applicable
  • Evaluate measured power against applicable design/equipment requirements
Acceptance / Record

Acceptance Criteria: measured power, and power factor/frequency where applicable, is consistent with the applicable design and equipment requirements.

TC-ELE-011Phase Verification Where Applicable
Verification Checks
  • Verify phase sequence where required and phase identification
  • Verify correct phase connection at the equipment
  • Measure phase-to-phase voltage and phase-to-neutral voltage where applicable
  • Record supply identification, phase sequence, measurement, and instrument used
  • Apply this topic only where the equipment/system configuration requires phase verification
Acceptance / Record

Acceptance Criteria: phase sequence, identification, and connection are confirmed correct at every applicable AC supply point.

E

Cable & Termination Testing

3 topics
TC-ELE-012Cable Insulation Testing
Verification Checks
  • Confirm cable identification and test boundaries for primary, secondary, control, and auxiliary cables
  • Confirm test voltage and test duration where specified
  • Record measured insulation resistance for each cable/test boundary
  • Discharge the cable after test
  • Protect or disconnect sensitive electronic equipment where required by the approved test procedure
Acceptance / Record

Acceptance Criteria: measured insulation resistance for each installed AGL cable meets the applicable acceptance value, with discharge completed after every test.

TC-ELE-013Cable Joint Testing
Verification Checks
  • Confirm joint identification and location, and cable identification at the joint
  • Verify joint construction, continuity, and insulation
  • Verify termination, mechanical condition, and sealing of the joint
  • Record test results against a unique identification/location reference for every joint
Acceptance / Record

Acceptance Criteria: every cable joint present is uniquely identified, tests satisfactory for continuity and insulation, and is mechanically sound and sealed.

TC-ELE-014Cable Termination Testing
Verification Checks
  • Confirm correct cable, correct terminal, and correct conductor at every termination
  • Verify continuity, insulation, and connection integrity
  • Verify polarity where applicable and termination identification
  • Inspect general termination condition
F

Circuit & Equipment Verification

4 topics
TC-ELE-015Secondary Circuit Verification
Verification Checks
  • Verify continuity of isolation-transformer secondary, secondary cable, and fixture secondary lead
  • Verify insulation and polarity where applicable
  • Verify correct connections, connector continuity, and correct fixture connection
  • Confirm no open circuit and no unintended short circuit
Acceptance / Record

Acceptance Criteria: every secondary-side component — transformer secondary, secondary cable, connector, and fixture — is confirmed continuous, correctly connected, and free of open or short circuits.

TC-ELE-016Primary Circuit Verification
Verification Checks
  • Verify correct circuit, cable continuity, and insulation resistance
  • Verify circuit resistance, cable identification, and cable route
  • Verify terminations, joints, connector connections, and isolation transformers
  • Verify circuit configuration against design
  • Investigate open circuit, short circuit, high resistance, low insulation resistance, or incorrect connection
TC-ELE-017Transformer Electrical Testing
Verification Checks
  • Confirm transformer identification and rating
  • Verify primary and secondary winding continuity and insulation resistance
  • Verify winding resistance and voltage ratio where applicable
  • Verify primary connection, secondary connection, and connector arrangement
Acceptance / Record

Acceptance Criteria: the electrical test requirement for each isolation transformer is satisfied here; the dedicated Division 04 — Isolation Transformer Testing & Commissioning provides the full transformer testing and commissioning procedure.

TC-ELE-018Connector Continuity Verification
Verification Checks
  • Confirm connector identification and cable lead
  • Verify connector contact, mating, and locking
  • Verify continuity and overall electrical integrity
  • Investigate open contact, poor contact, incorrect mating, or damaged connector
Acceptance / Record

Acceptance Criteria: every connector in the circuit is correctly identified, mated, locked, and continuous; connector testing is later expanded to identification, continuity, contact, mating, locking, sealing, cable-lead, and electrical verification.

G

Control & Auxiliary

2 topics
TC-ELE-019Control-Circuit Testing
Verification Checks
  • Verify control-cable continuity and terminal connections
  • Verify control voltage and polarity where applicable
  • Verify input circuits, output circuits, relay circuits, and interlock circuits
  • Verify alarm circuits, indication circuits, CCR control wiring, and ALCMS control wiring where applicable
Acceptance / Record

Acceptance Criteria: control-circuit electrical integrity is confirmed here; whether the control system actually performs the required operational sequence is verified later under the ALCMS and System Integration divisions.

TC-ELE-020Auxiliary-Supply Testing
Verification Checks
  • Verify control supply, panel supply, and ALCMS supply
  • Verify communication equipment supply and lighting/control auxiliaries
  • Verify voltage, polarity where applicable, continuity, and protection
  • Verify earthing, identification, and stability where applicable
Acceptance / Record

Acceptance Criteria: every auxiliary and project-specific supply feeding control and supporting equipment is confirmed correct for voltage, continuity, protection, and earthing.

H

Evaluation, Acceptance & Reporting

3 topics
TC-ELE-021Electrical Test-Result Evaluation
Verification Checks
  • Compare every measured value against its applicable acceptance criterion
  • Carry the comparison through to a pass/fail outcome and technical review
  • Review for abnormal values, trends, outliers, and inconsistent readings
  • Review for instrument issues, test-method errors, and incorrect test configuration
  • Review environmental effects where relevant and repeated-test consistency
  • Apply corrective action where required before re-evaluation
TC-ELE-022Electrical Acceptance Assessment
Verification Checks
  • Confirm required tests are completed using correct test methods and instruments
  • Confirm calibration is valid and results are within approved limits
  • Confirm test records are complete and abnormal results have been investigated
  • Confirm failed tests have been corrected and repeated where required
  • Confirm relevant documentation is complete before formal acceptance
TC-ELE-023Electrical Test Report Preparation
Verification Checks
  • Compile project information — airport, project, contract/package, location, client, contractor, testing agency
  • Compile equipment/circuit information — circuit number, equipment ID, cable ID, transformer ID, fixture ID, panel/CCR ID
  • Compile test information — test name, procedure reference, date, location, personnel
  • Compile instrument information — manufacturer, model, serial number, calibration certificate and validity
  • Record test results — test point, parameter, condition, measured value, acceptance criterion, result
  • Record deviations — failure, abnormal reading, observation, corrective action, retest result
  • Obtain acceptance sign-off — tested by, checked by, witnessed by, approved by
Acceptance / Record

Acceptance Criteria: every electrical test is traceable to a formal test record following the recommended report structure, complete with acceptance sign-off before the division closes.

Division 02 — Acceptance Gate

Division 02 sits between installation readiness and the specialized commissioning divisions — no result proceeds forward without evaluation, and no division closes without acceptance.

1
Division 01 Complete

Installation readiness verified and energization authorized.

2
Electrical Integrity Verified

Division 02 confirms insulation, continuity, resistance, polarity, voltage, current, power, and circuit connections.

3
CCR Testing & Commissioning

Division 03 builds on verified electrical integrity for CCR-specific testing.

4
Isolation Transformer Testing

Division 04 provides the dedicated transformer testing and commissioning procedure.

5
Component/System Commissioning

Division 05 onward proceeds into fixture, runway, taxiway, control, and photometric commissioning.

6
Evaluation Before Acceptance

Every result passes through the evaluation sequence before any PASS is recorded.

7
Corrective Action

Failed or abnormal results are corrected and retested before proceeding.

8
Formal Sign-Off

The Master Electrical Test Report closes the division and hands the system forward.
