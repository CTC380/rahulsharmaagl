Division 10's Role in the Innovation Hub

Division 08 develops and evaluates future AGL products. Division 09 improves how AGL is designed, calculated, commissioned and engineered. Division 10 does neither directly — it continuously scans the technological horizon and feeds validated, readiness-assessed possibilities into every other division, functioning as the Innovation Hub's forward-looking technology radar rather than a product or engineering-method division in its own right.

EMT-16 Technology Readiness Assessment
Purpose

Determine how close an emerging technology is to practical AGL deployment. This topic becomes the governance mechanism for the entire division — the mechanism that stops Division 10 from being simply a list of futuristic technologies.

Technology Readiness Level (TRL) Scale

Emerging Technology Pilot Framework
  • Phase 1 — Identify: What technology is available?
  • Phase 2 — Define Problem: What AGL problem could it solve?
  • Phase 3 — Assess: What is its technical and operational suitability?
  • Phase 4 — Prototype: Can a small-scale demonstration be created?
  • Phase 5 — Pilot: Can it operate in a representative environment?
  • Phase 6 — Measure: Did it produce measurable benefits?
  • Phase 7 — Review: What are the risks and limitations?
  • Phase 8 — Decision: Adopt / Continue Pilot / Monitor / Reject.

Division 10 — Integration With the Innovation Hub

Division 10 acts as the forward-looking technology radar for the entire Innovation Hub, feeding validated possibilities into nine other divisions rather than developing products or engineering methods itself.

Division 01 — Innovation Strategy & Roadmap: determines which emerging technologies deserve strategic attention
Division 02 — Smart Airfield Lighting: receives emerging technologies for intelligent lighting
Division 03 — AI for AGL: evaluates future AI capabilities
Division 04 — IoT & Connected AGL: evaluates future sensors, communications and edge technologies
Division 05 — Digital Engineering: evaluates AR/VR/MR, digital twins and advanced computing
Division 06 — Robotics & Autonomous Inspection: evaluates advanced robotics and autonomous systems
Division 07 — Sustainable AGL: evaluates emerging energy and sustainable technologies
Division 08 — AGL Product Innovation: converts mature technologies into future products
Division 09 — Engineering Innovation: integrates mature technologies into engineering methodologies and tools

Ultimate Vision for Division 10

The long-term objective is for Division 10 to answer five strategic questions continuously:

What technology is emerging? — Technology Radar
What AGL problem could it solve? — Problem Mapping
Is it mature enough? — Technology Readiness
What happens if we adopt it? — Risk + Benefit + Lifecycle Assessment
When should we adopt it? — Innovation Roadmap

Division 10 therefore becomes the future-looking intelligence system of the AGL Innovation Hub: WATCH → ASSESS → EXPERIMENT → VALIDATE → INTEGRATE → ADOPT.

This matters because the framework deliberately groups AR, VR, MR, edge computing, advanced communications, quantum technologies and other emerging technologies together. The division should therefore function not simply as a list of futuristic technologies, but as a controlled technology-radar and readiness-assessment programme that determines which technologies should actually progress into AGL applications — and which should remain on watch.
