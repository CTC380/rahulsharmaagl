Spare Parts & Inventory Forms

The 25 forms that control the complete AGL spare-parts cycle — Spare Identification → Stock Registration → Min/Max/Reorder Control → Requisition → Issue → Consumption → Return → Reconciliation → Critical-Spare Management → Lifecycle → Obsolescence. Grouped into Stock Registration & Control, Transaction Control, Critical & Special Inventory, Traceability, Equipment-Specific Registers, Audit & Reconciliation, and Lifecycle, this division is the spare-parts reliability and availability layer that connects tightly with Division 08 — Maintenance and Division 11 — Asset Management, since spare demand should come from actual maintenance requirements, asset criticality, failure history and lifecycle planning.

A · Spare Parts Registration & Stock Control

5 of 25 forms
SP-01 Spare Parts Register Register
Purpose

To maintain the master register of all AGL spare parts maintained in stores, covering identification, stock, procurement and status of every part.

Key Fields
  • Spare Part ID, description, category, equipment type
  • Manufacturer, manufacturer part no., model, specification
  • Applicable asset / applicable system
  • Store location, bin location, unit
  • Current, minimum, maximum, reorder, safety, reserved and available quantity
  • Supplier, purchase order, lead time, unit cost, currency, warranty
  • Status: active, critical, slow moving, obsolete, under warranty, discontinued
Feeds every stock-control, transaction-control and equipment-specific register in this division.
SP-02 Spare Parts Stock Sheet Tracker
Purpose

To provide the current stock position of individual spare parts through a running opening, receipt, issue, return, adjustment and closing balance.

Key Fields
  • Spare ID, description, date, store, bin
  • Opening / receipt / issue / return / adjustment / closing quantity
  • Batch/lot, serial number where applicable
  • Physical quantity, system quantity, difference
  • Storekeeper, verified by, date
SP-03 Minimum Stock Level Reference Sheet
Purpose

To establish the minimum quantity of a spare that should normally be maintained, below which inventory requires management attention.

Key Fields
  • Spare Part ID, description, equipment
  • Average consumption, lead time, criticality
  • Minimum stock, safety stock, basis of calculation
  • Considers historical consumption and failure frequency
  • Considers procurement lead time and asset criticality
  • Considers operational consequence and supplier availability
  • Approved by
SP-04 Maximum Stock Level Reference Sheet
Purpose

To establish the maximum practical inventory quantity for a spare, preventing overstocking, capital blockage, expiry, obsolescence and unnecessary duplication.

Key Fields
  • Spare Part ID, description
  • Minimum level, maximum level, consumption rate
  • Lead time, storage limitation, shelf life, cost
  • Approved quantity
  • Control objective: prevent overstocking, capital blockage, expiry, obsolescence, duplication
SP-05 Reorder Level Reference Sheet
Purpose

To define the stock level at which a replenishment order should be initiated: Reorder Level = Expected Consumption During Lead Time + Safety Stock, with the exact methodology standardized by the inventory-management procedure.

Key Fields
  • Spare ID, average consumption, lead time, safety stock
  • Reorder level, supplier, procurement lead time
  • Current stock, reorder required
  • Status: above reorder level, at reorder level, below reorder level, emergency procurement required
Together with SP-03 and SP-04, the reorder trigger starts SP-06 Spare Parts Requisition.

B · Spare Parts Transaction Control

4 of 25 forms
SP-06 Spare Parts Requisition Requisition
Purpose

To request spare parts from the store for maintenance or other authorized work.

Key Fields
  • Requisition No., date, Work Order No., Asset ID
  • Equipment, location, maintenance type, requesting person
  • Spare ID, description, part no., required qty, purpose
  • Approval: Technician, Supervisor, Maintenance Engineer, authorized approver
  • Store action: issued, partially issued, not available, alternative proposed
SP-07 Spare Parts Issue Record Record
Purpose

To record actual issue of spare parts from stores against an approved requisition.

Key Fields
  • Issue No., date, Spare ID, description, part number
  • Quantity, unit, work order, Asset ID, location
  • Issued to, storekeeper
  • Verification: quantity issued, condition, batch/serial number
  • Recipient signature, stock balance
SP-08 Spare Parts Return Record Return Form
Purpose

To record unused, excess or removed parts returned to stores, including failed components returned for investigation.

Key Fields
  • Return No., work order, Asset ID, Spare ID, quantity
  • Return type: unused spare, excess material, removed serviceable component, incorrectly issued part, failed component
  • Reason for return, inspection result
  • Condition: new, serviceable, repairable, failed, scrap, quarantine
SP-09 Spare Parts Consumption Tracker
Purpose

To track actual spare-part consumption against maintenance activities and analyze consumption trends across equipment, location and time.

Key Fields
  • Date, WO, asset, Spare ID, description, qty, unit cost, total cost
  • Analysis by equipment, location, month, failure, maintenance type
  • Outputs: high-consumption parts, recurring component failures
  • Outputs: abnormal consumption, future procurement requirement
Feeds SP-12 Slow-Moving Spare Register, SP-13 Obsolete Spare Register, and recalculation of SP-03/04/05.

C · Critical & Special Inventory

5 of 25 forms
SP-10 Critical Spare Register Register
Purpose

To identify spare parts whose absence could significantly affect AGL operational availability or safety.

Key Fields
  • Spare ID, description, equipment, asset criticality
  • Failure consequence, current stock, minimum stock, lead time
  • Supplier, criticality class, emergency availability
  • Criticality factors: safety impact, operational impact, failure frequency, availability of alternatives
  • Status: adequately stocked, below minimum, emergency procurement, supplier risk
SP-11 Emergency Spare Register Register
Purpose

To identify parts that must be immediately available for emergency AGL restoration.

Key Fields
  • Spare ID, equipment, description, required qty, available qty
  • Storage location, emergency use, responsible person
  • Examples: critical fixture components, CCR components, isolation transformers
  • Examples: connectors, cable joint components, PAPI, sign, control/ALCMS components
  • Control: emergency stock checked, seal/security checked, expiry checked, availability verified
SP-12 Slow-Moving Spare Register Register
Purpose

To identify spare parts with very low consumption before they become a stranded, obsolete cost.

Key Fields
  • Spare ID, description, stock quantity
  • Last issue date, last receipt date, consumption rate
  • Stock age, value, reason for slow movement
  • Management action: retain, reduce stock, transfer, return to supplier, reuse, dispose
SP-13 Obsolete Spare Register Register
Purpose

To control spare parts that are no longer technically or commercially suitable for use.

Key Fields
  • Spare ID, description, applicable equipment, quantity, value
  • Obsolescence date, reason, replacement part, disposal decision
  • Reasons: equipment discontinued, manufacturer discontinued, technology replaced
  • Reasons: no supplier, no compatible equipment, shelf-life expired, specification changed
  • Action: consume, substitute, return, dispose, retain for legacy equipment
SP-14 Spare Parts Warranty Record
Purpose

To maintain warranty information for spare parts and replacement components, and track claims against manufacturer or supplier.

Key Fields
  • Spare ID, part number, manufacturer, supplier
  • Purchase date, warranty start, warranty expiry, warranty terms, applicable equipment
  • Claim: failure date, claim number, claim submitted
  • Claim: supplier response, replacement, repair, closure

D · Spare Traceability

SP-15 Spare Parts Traceability Traceability Record
Purpose

To establish complete traceability from procurement through storage, issue, installation and final use — particularly important for critical AGL components.

Key Fields
  • Spare ID, part number, serial number, batch/lot
  • Supplier, PO, receipt, store location
  • Issue, work order, asset, installation date
  • Traceability chain: Purchase Order → Delivery → Batch/Lot/Serial No. → Store Receipt
  • Chain continues: Storage Location → Issue Record → Work Order → Asset ID → Installation → Maintenance History

F · Inventory Audit & Reconciliation

2 of 25 forms
SP-23 Inventory Audit Checklist
Purpose

To verify that physical inventory matches inventory records and that storage controls are effective.

Key Fields
  • Audit areas: stock quantity, part identification, bin location, condition
  • Audit areas: shelf life, traceability, stock labeling, security, storage environment
  • Audit areas: critical spares, obsolete spares
  • Audit result: compliant, minor discrepancy, major discrepancy, corrective action required
  • Findings: physical shortage, excess stock, wrong location, unidentified/damaged/expired/obsolete item
SP-24 Stock Reconciliation Record
Purpose

To reconcile physical stock against ERP/store records and close out discrepancies.

Key Fields
  • Spare ID, system qty, physical qty, difference, reason, adjustment, approved by
  • Possible causes: unrecorded issue, unrecorded receipt, return not recorded
  • Possible causes: counting error, damage, scrap, data-entry error, wrong item identification
  • Closure: investigation completed, adjustment authorized, inventory updated, records corrected
Findings from SP-23 Inventory Audit are closed out through this reconciliation record.

SP-25 Spare Parts Lifecycle Record Record
Purpose

To track the complete lifecycle of a spare part from procurement through final consumption, replacement or disposal.

Key Fields

Closes the loop back into SP-01 Spare Parts Register and Division 11 Asset Lifecycle records.

Frequently Asked Questions

Quick answers about the 25 Division 12 Spare Parts & Inventory forms, how they are grouped, and how they connect to the rest of the library.

Q1What is Division 12 — Spare Parts & Inventory Forms in the AGL Engineering Forms & Templates library?

Division 12 is a set of 25 spare parts & inventory forms (SP-01 to SP-25) in the AGL Engineering Forms & Templates library. It controls the complete AGL spare-parts cycle, from spare identification and stock registration through requisition, issue, consumption, return, reconciliation, critical-spare management, lifecycle and obsolescence. The forms are grouped into 7 categories: Spare Parts Registration & Stock Control, Spare Parts Transaction Control, Critical & Special Inventory, Spare Traceability, Equipment-Specific Spare Registers, Inventory Audit & Reconciliation and Spare Parts Lifecycle.

Q2Which forms are included in Spare Parts Registration & Stock Control and Spare Parts Transaction Control?

Spare Parts Registration & Stock Control (5 forms): SP-01 Spare Parts Register, SP-02 Spare Parts Stock Sheet, SP-03 Minimum Stock Level, SP-04 Maximum Stock Level, SP-05 Reorder Level; Spare Parts Transaction Control (4 forms): SP-06 Spare Parts Requisition, SP-07 Spare Parts Issue Record, SP-08 Spare Parts Return Record, SP-09 Spare Parts Consumption.

Q3Which forms are included in Critical & Special Inventory, Spare Traceability, Equipment-Specific Spare Registers, Inventory Audit & Reconciliation and Spare Parts Lifecycle?

Critical & Special Inventory (5 forms): SP-10 Critical Spare Register, SP-11 Emergency Spare Register, SP-12 Slow-Moving Spare Register, SP-13 Obsolete Spare Register, SP-14 Spare Parts Warranty; Spare Traceability (1 form): SP-15 Spare Parts Traceability; Equipment-Specific Spare Registers (7 forms): SP-16 Fixture Spare Register, SP-17 Transformer Spare Register, SP-18 CCR Spare Register, SP-19 PAPI Spare Register, SP-20 Sign Spare Register, SP-21 Connector Spare Register, SP-22 Cable Spare Register; Inventory Audit & Reconciliation (2 forms): SP-23 Inventory Audit, SP-24 Stock Reconciliation; Spare Parts Lifecycle (1 form): SP-25 Spare Parts Lifecycle Record.

Q4What is the difference between the Minimum Stock Level, Maximum Stock Level and Reorder Level sheets?

The Minimum Stock Level (SP-03) sets the quantity that should normally be maintained, below which inventory needs management attention. The Maximum Stock Level (SP-04) sets the maximum practical quantity, preventing overstocking, capital blockage, expiry and obsolescence. The Reorder Level (SP-05) defines the stock level at which a replenishment order should be initiated.

Q5What is the difference between the Critical, Emergency, Slow-Moving and Obsolete spare registers?

The Critical Spare Register (SP-10) identifies spares whose absence could significantly affect AGL availability or safety. The Emergency Spare Register (SP-11) identifies parts that must be immediately available for emergency restoration. The Slow-Moving Spare Register (SP-12) identifies very low-consumption parts before they become a stranded cost, and the Obsolete Spare Register (SP-13) controls parts that are no longer technically or commercially suitable for use.

Q6Which forms control issuing spares and checking stock?

The Spare Parts Requisition (SP-06) requests parts from the store, the Spare Parts Issue Record (SP-07) records the actual issue, and the Spare Parts Return Record (SP-08) records returns, including failed components returned for investigation. The Spare Parts Consumption tracker (SP-09) tracks usage. The Inventory Audit (SP-23) and Stock Reconciliation (SP-24) verify physical stock against the records and close out discrepancies.

Q7Can the forms in Division 12 be downloaded and filled in?

Yes. Each form has a Download as Word button that produces an uncontrolled sample Word template with letterhead, a document-control block, ruled entry fields and a three-tier approval table, ready to print or fill on-screen. Checklist-format forms such as Inventory Audit (SP-23) download with a checkbox and a verified-by line for each item. The forms can also be searched by keyword or form ID.

Q8How does Division 12 connect to maintenance, asset and procurement records?

The maintenance division generates the demand and the asset-management division supplies the criticality and lifecycle context, and this division turns that demand into stocked, traceable, available spares. Every issue record should trace back to a work order and asset, and every critical spare should trace forward into min/max/reorder planning and procurement.
