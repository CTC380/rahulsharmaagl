01 · Our Approach

AGL-Focused Technical Approach

Airfield Ground Lighting is a specialised airport engineering discipline combining lighting, electrical systems, airport infrastructure, visual guidance, installation, testing, photometry, maintenance and compliance. A requirement that looks like a simple lighting-component purchase can involve several connected technical decisions — Precision AGL Solutions is positioned around this specialised AGL environment rather than general-purpose electrical or outdoor lighting.

What makes Precision AGL Solutions different for India?

How is the India AGL content structured?
As a connected structure across Products, Applications, India-specific pages, Standards, Services and Education, rather than isolated pages.
Can Precision AGL Solutions help with LED retrofit compatibility?
Yes. Before replacing an existing fixture, the approach evaluates CCR compatibility, series-circuit characteristics, transformer compatibility, cable condition, connector arrangement, control/monitoring and photometric performance.
