Sustainable AGL & Green Airports

Division 07 establishes the sustainability and environmental-performance layer of the AGL Innovation Hub. The framework specifically identifies energy-efficient lighting, renewable-energy integration, carbon reduction, sustainable materials, circular-economy practices and green procurement as its principal areas. The core objective: develop AGL systems that deliver the required operational and engineering performance while reducing energy consumption, environmental impact, material waste and lifecycle carbon.

The question this division answers — How can AGL systems achieve required operational and engineering performance while reducing energy consumption, carbon footprint, material waste and lifecycle environmental impact, without treating sustainability as separate from safety-critical technical performance?

Sustainable AGL — Master Architecture

Division 07 is recommended to be structured around six layers, running from immediate energy performance through to full lifecycle management.

Level 1 — Energy: Energy-Efficient Lighting + Low-Power Equipment + Energy Monitoring + Energy Optimization
Level 2 — Renewable Energy: Solar + Renewable Integration + Energy Storage
Level 3 — Materials: Sustainable Materials + Recyclable Components + Durable Components
Level 4 — Circular Economy: Repair + Reuse + Refurbishment + Recovery + Recycling
Level 5 — Procurement: Green Procurement + Lifecycle Cost + Lifecycle Carbon
Level 6 — Lifecycle: Design → Procurement → Operation → Maintenance → Renewal → End of Life

Sustainable AGL Performance Framework

Every sustainability innovation should ideally be evaluated using five dimensions. The fifth is essential: sustainability should not be evaluated independently of the required technical and operational performance of AGL.

Division 07 — Ultimate Vision

The long-term objective is a Green AGL Lifecycle: sustainable design leads to energy-efficient equipment, which is paired with renewable-energy opportunities, smart energy monitoring and AI-based optimization. Together these deliver longer asset life, sustainable maintenance and a repair/refurbish/reuse approach, feeding into material recovery, recycling and lower lifecycle carbon — arriving at green and high-performance AGL.
