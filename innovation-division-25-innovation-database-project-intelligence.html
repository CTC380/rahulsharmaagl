A. Standard Innovation Project Record

17 of 17 fields scoped · REC-01 → 17
REC-01Project ID
Purpose

Every innovation project receives a unique, permanent Innovation Project ID that is never reused.

Structure
INNInitiation YearCategory CodeSequential Number
Example
INN-2026-AI-001
REC-02Innovation Title
Purpose

A short, technically meaningful title that immediately communicates the engineering purpose of the project.

Example

AI-Based Automated Runway Fixture Defect Detection — not a vague title such as "AI Project."

REC-03Problem Statement
Purpose

Describes the engineering problem being solved.

Should Answer
  • What is wrong?
  • Where does it occur?
  • How frequently?
  • What is the operational consequence?
  • What is the current limitation?
  • Why does the problem require innovation?
REC-04Existing Solution
Purpose

Documents the current method before innovation, establishing the baseline against which innovation performance is later measured.

Includes
  • Existing technology
  • Existing process
  • Existing equipment
  • Current performance
  • Current limitations
  • Current cost
  • Current maintenance requirement
REC-05Proposed Innovation
Purpose

Describes the proposed technology or methodology.

Includes
  • Technology concept
  • Architecture
  • Major components
  • Operating principle
  • Intended improvement
  • Integration requirements
REC-06Expected Benefits
Purpose

Benefits should be measurable wherever possible, across five categories.

Categories
  • Technical — better performance, improved accuracy, improved reliability
  • Operational — reduced inspection time, faster fault identification, improved availability
  • Financial — reduced maintenance, energy and lifecycle cost
  • Safety — reduced personnel exposure, faster critical-defect identification
  • Sustainability — lower energy and material consumption, longer asset life
REC-07Technical Feasibility
Assess
  • Technology availability
  • Engineering feasibility
  • Integration feasibility
  • Environmental suitability
  • Testing feasibility
  • Manufacturing feasibility
  • Maintenance feasibility
  • Safety feasibility
Rating

Low / Medium / High, or a numerical score.

REC-08Implementation Roadmap
Purpose

Documents the complete development path from problem to deployment.

Path
ProblemConceptFeasibilityResearchPrototypeLab TestingPoCPilotTech. EvaluationProduct DevelopmentCommercializationDeployment
REC-09Risks & Limitations
Purpose

Every innovation record explicitly documents its limitations across eleven risk categories.

Categories

Technical · Safety · Operational · Financial · Reliability · Environmental · Cybersecurity · Supply-chain · Regulatory · User-acceptance · Commercial.

Also Records

Known limitations · Unknowns · Dependencies · Assumptions.

REC-10Pilot Validation Plan
Purpose

Defines how the innovation will be proven in the field, linking directly to Division 19 — Proof-of-Concept & Pilot Projects.

Includes
  • Pilot objective
  • Pilot site
  • Equipment
  • Baseline condition
  • Test methodology
  • Duration
  • Test parameters
  • Acceptance criteria
  • Safety requirements
  • Data collection
  • Performance evaluation
  • Final decision
REC-11Estimated Cost
Records

Research · Engineering · Prototype · Testing · Pilot · Installation · Software · Hardware · Training · Maintenance cost — distinguishing CAPEX and OPEX where possible.

REC-12Success Metrics
Purpose

One of the most important fields — every innovation needs measurable success criteria.

Examples

Reliability — MTBF improvement · Maintenance — maintenance-hours reduction · Inspection — detection accuracy · Photometry — measurement accuracy · Energy — kWh reduction · Cost — lifecycle-cost reduction · Safety — personnel-exposure reduction.

REC-13Related Standards
Records

Applicable standards — ICAO, FAA, DGCA, IEC, IS and other technical references — with standard, clause/section, applicability and compliance status.

REC-14Related Products
Links To

Runway fixture · Taxiway fixture · CCR · Isolation transformer · Connector · Cable · PAPI · Sign · ALCMS · Photometry equipment.

REC-15Related Topics
Purpose

Connects the project to the wider Innovation Framework and enables topic-based intelligence searches.

Example — AI

Predictive Maintenance · Computer Vision · Smart AGL · Automated Inspection.

REC-16References
Records

Research papers · Technical reports · Manufacturer documentation · Test reports · Standards · Patents · Airport reports · Engineering studies · Internal technical documents.

Innovation Database Relationships

The real power of Division 25 comes from the relationships between records, not the records in isolation. A single Innovation Project connects outward to a Failure Record, an Asset Record and a Product Record, and forward through Research, Prototype and PoC into a Pilot, a Technology Evaluation, and finally a Patent, a Product or a Commercial outcome — all feeding back into Lessons Learned and Future Innovation.

STAGE 1
Origin Links
Failure Record · Asset Record · Product Record
STAGE 2
Innovation Project
The master record — problem, solution, feasibility, roadmap, risk
STAGE 3
Development
Research → Prototype → PoC → Pilot
STAGE 4
Technology Evaluation
TRL, validated performance, ROI, safety/operational/sustainability/reliability impact
STAGE 5
Outcome Links
Patent · Product · Commercial launch
STAGE 6
Lessons Learned → Future Innovation
Closes the loop back into Divisions 01–17

Ultimate Vision for Division 25

Division 25 should not be treated as an Excel register alone. The long-term objective is to grow it into an AGL Innovation Intelligence Platform:

Problem → Failure → Asset
Technology → Innovation Project → Research
Prototype → PoC → Pilot → Testing
TRL → Benchmark → Patent

Every other division builds innovation. Division 25 remembers, connects and governs it.

That single source of truth is what lets the framework answer, at any time: what are we innovating, why, what technology is involved, how mature is it, where is it being tested, what did it achieve, what did it cost, what risks exist, who is collaborating, is there patent potential, which asset/product/failure does it relate to, what did we learn — and should we continue, stop, adopt, scale or commercialize it.
