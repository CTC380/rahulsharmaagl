Technology Evaluation & Benchmarking

Division 20 is the technology intelligence, comparative evaluation and technology-selection division of the AGL Innovation Framework. Division 19 asks whether an innovation works in a real environment; Division 20 asks how it compares with available alternatives, existing AGL technology and global best practice — and which technology should be selected. It establishes a structured, evidence-based technology comparison system covering products, manufacturers, performance, reliability, cost, energy, maintainability, lifecycle value and technology maturity.

Where Division 20 Sits in the Innovation Hub

Division 20 is the technology intelligence and decision-support bridge between the Innovation Strategy, the Prototype Laboratory, the 100+ PoC programme, the AGL Product Encyclopedia and eventual technology deployment. It does not build or trial technology itself — it evaluates, compares, benchmarks and scores what Divisions 18 and 19 have already built and tested, and feeds the resulting decision back into the pipeline.

Most Important Design Principle

Division 20 should establish an evidence-based AGL technology intelligence system so that future technology decisions are not based only on supplier presentations, product brochures, individual experience, or purchase price.

Instead, decisions should be based on:

Technical Performance + Safety + Reliability + Energy + Maintainability + Lifecycle Cost + Technology Maturity + Field Evidence + Scalability

The ultimate output is "Right Technology — Right Application — Right Maturity — Right Time." This makes Division 20 the technology intelligence and decision-support bridge between the Innovation Strategy, Prototype Laboratory, 100+ PoC programme, AGL Product Encyclopedia and eventual technology deployment.
