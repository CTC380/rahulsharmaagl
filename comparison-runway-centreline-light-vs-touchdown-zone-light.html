Runway Lighting Systems Comparison

RUNWAY CENTRELINE LIGHT VS TOUCHDOWN ZONE LIGHT

Two Precision-Approach Runway Lighting Systems, Two Different Visual Jobs — centreline lights provide continuous longitudinal guidance down the length of the runway, while touchdown zone lights enhance visual identification of the landing area itself. Both are critical for low-visibility precision approach and landing operations, but they answer different questions for the pilot at different moments of the approach.

02 · Layout

Runway Centreline Lights: Physical Layout

Runway Centreline Lights are installed along the longitudinal centreline of the runway, forming a single continuous line down its length.

Runway Centreline Lights (gold) run down the longitudinal centreline for the length of the runway.

They answer: "Where is the centre of the runway?"

03 · Layout

Touchdown Zone Lights: Physical Layout

Touchdown Zone Lights are installed within the touchdown zone, arranged as pairs of transverse light barrettes placed symmetrically on either side of the centreline.

Touchdown Zone Lights (cyan) form paired transverse barrettes on both sides of the centreline, confined to the touchdown area.

They answer: "Where is the preferred touchdown area?"

04 · Position on Runway

How the Two Systems Sit Together

The touchdown zone lights occupy only the initial landing area near the threshold, while the centreline lights continue longitudinally along the full runway.

TDZ lights (cyan) are confined near the threshold; centreline lights (gold) run the length of the runway.

05 · Functional Difference

What Question Does Each System Answer?

Centreline Light

Where is the centre of the runway?

Touchdown Zone Light

Where is the preferred touchdown area?

The centreline sequence is alignment → landing → rollout → take-off guidance. The touchdown zone sequence is identify → touchdown area → land aircraft → maintain visual reference.

06 · Pilot's Visual Reference

What the Pilot Actually Sees on Approach

In sequence during a landing: threshold, then the touchdown zone barrettes, then the continuing centreline.

Touchdown Zone Lights

Help the pilot recognize and confirm the touchdown area as the runway comes into view during the final stage of approach.

Centreline Lights

Help the pilot maintain directional alignment through touchdown and continuing into the landing rollout.

Together, they improve the visual information available to the pilot during landing and rollout, which becomes especially important as visibility decreases.

07 · Light Arrangement

Longitudinal Line vs Transverse Barrettes

Centreline Light

A single continuous longitudinal sequence of in-pavement fixtures running down the runway centre.

Touchdown Zone Light

Multiple transverse barrettes, arranged in symmetrical pairs, decreasing in number progressively along the touchdown zone.

08 · Colour Coding

Colour Behaviour of Each System

Touchdown zone lights typically provide a straightforward white indication throughout the touchdown zone.

Centreline light colour can vary according to the aircraft's position along the runway and the applicable lighting standard — conceptually progressing from white, to alternating white/red, to solid red toward the runway end.

Therefore: TDZ lights primarily identify the touchdown area, while centreline lights can also provide positional information along the rest of the runway. Exact colour sequencing and spacing should always be checked against the applicable ICAO Annex 14 or national standard.

09 · Coverage

Extent of Each Lighting System

Centreline Lighting

Extends longitudinally, typically along much or all of the runway length, depending on runway category and applicable requirements.

Touchdown Zone Lighting

Limited to the touchdown zone near the threshold — it does not extend along the remainder of the runway.

10 · Mounting

Fixture Type and Installation

Both systems are normally inset and flush with the pavement, since aircraft operate directly over the centreline and through the touchdown zone.

11 · Low-Visibility Operations

Role in Reduced-Visibility Landings

Both systems are important for operations in reduced visibility, and both are typically associated with lower-minima precision approach runways.

Centreline Lights

Provide continuous directional reference through touchdown and the full landing rollout.

TDZ Lights

Provide enhanced visual structure in the critical landing area at the point the pilot needs it most.

Together, they improve the visual information available to the pilot during the most demanding phase of a low-visibility landing.

12 · Relationship with Other Runway Lights

Where Each System Fits

Put simply: edge lights = where the runway sides are, centreline lights = where the runway centre is, and TDZ lights = where the touchdown area is.

14 · Common Misconceptions

Myths vs Facts

Myth: Centreline lights and TDZ lights do the same job
Fact

They serve different visual functions. Centreline lights provide longitudinal alignment along the runway; TDZ lights identify and enhance the touchdown area itself. Neither substitutes for the other.

Myth: TDZ lights run the full length of the runway
Fact

TDZ lights are confined to the touchdown zone near the threshold. Centreline lights are the system that continues down the runway's length.

Myth: Every runway has touchdown zone lighting
Fact

TDZ lighting requirements depend on the runway category and applicable approach minima — it is not universal in the way basic runway edge lighting is.

Myth: TDZ lights change colour like centreline lights near the runway end
Fact

TDZ lights typically maintain a consistent white indication throughout their coverage area, whereas centreline lights can transition toward red approaching the runway end, depending on the applicable standard.

15 · Engineering Verdict

Runway Centreline Light vs Touchdown Zone Light

Engineering Verdict

Runway Centreline Lights and Touchdown Zone Lights are complementary, not interchangeable. Centreline lights answer where the aircraft should stay aligned along the runway's length; TDZ lights answer where the preferred touchdown area is at the point of landing.

Together, both systems form part of the core visual guidance package for precision approach runways, particularly critical when visibility is reduced.

Centreline LightDefines longitudinal alignment for landing, rollout and take-off; continues along much of the runway length.
TDZ LightEnhances visual identification of the touchdown area; confined to the initial landing zone, typically white throughout.

Centreline = Alignment. TDZ = Touchdown. The exact configuration, colour sequence and applicability for a specific project should always be checked against ICAO Annex 14 or the applicable national standard.

Quick Answer

Runway Touchdown Zone Light Color

The runway touchdown zone light color is white. Touchdown zone lights are laid out in pairs of white bars on each side of the centreline in the touchdown zone. Runway centreline lights are also white along most of the runway, but they change colour near the runway end to warn the pilot of the remaining distance. Confirm the exact pattern against the applicable standard.

16 · FAQ

Frequently Asked Questions

What is the main difference between runway centreline lights and touchdown zone lights?

Centreline lights provide longitudinal guidance along the centre of the runway, while touchdown zone lights provide additional visual guidance specifically in the touchdown area of a precision approach runway.

Are touchdown zone lights installed along the whole runway?

No. TDZ lights are limited to the touchdown zone near the threshold. Centreline lights are the system that continues along much or all of the runway length.

What colour are touchdown zone lights?

Touchdown zone lights are typically white throughout their coverage area.

Do centreline lights change colour along the runway?

In some lighting systems, yes — the colour can change according to the aircraft's position, conceptually progressing from white toward red near the runway end, depending on the applicable standard.

How are touchdown zone lights arranged physically?

They are arranged as pairs of transverse light barrettes placed symmetrically on either side of the runway centreline within the touchdown zone.

Which system matters more in low visibility?

Both are considered very important in low visibility. Centreline lights provide continuous directional reference, while TDZ lights provide enhanced visual structure at the critical moment of touchdown.
