Stop Bar & Runway Guard Light Testing

Verify that Stop Bar and Runway Guard Light (RGL) systems are correctly installed, aligned, electrically healthy, operationally functional, correctly controlled and capable of providing the required visual warning/stop indication before acceptance. This division follows Division 10 — Airfield Sign Testing & Commissioning.

Philosophy: the division is intentionally split into two systems because their commissioning objectives are different. Stop Bar is steady/controlled lighting associated with runway-entry protection and stop-bar control. Runway Guard Light (RGL) is flashing warning lighting intended to enhance conspicuity of a runway holding position. Recommended sequence: Installation → Alignment → Electrical → Circuit → Illumination/Flashing → Intensity → Switching → Control → Alarm → Acceptance.

Before Testing Begins

Two conditions should be established before Division 11 testing of either system begins.

Numerical Limits Are Not Universal

  • Intensity acceptance criteria come from the approved design/specification and applicable aerodrome requirements
  • Insulation, continuity and current acceptance values are project- and equipment-specific — never apply an arbitrary universal figure
  • Electrical testing should be completed before operational energization

Division Boundary

  • Division 11 follows Division 10 — Airfield Sign Testing & Commissioning
  • Stop Bar and RGL are two distinct commissioning packages within one division — do not use the same functional philosophy for both
  • Stop-bar acceptance requires complete illumination and switching/control response; RGL acceptance requires verification of the flashing function, not merely illumination of the lamp/LED

PART A — STOP BAR
A

Stop-Bar Installation & Alignment

2 topics
TC-SBR-001Stop-Bar Installation Verification
Verification Checks
  • Verify correct stop-bar location, number of fixtures, fixture type and spacing/configuration
  • Verify correct mounting arrangement, orientation and circuit
  • Verify correct cable arrangement, connector arrangement and relationship with runway holding position
  • Inspect for missing, incorrect or damaged fixtures, incorrect location, spacing, mounting or circuit connection
  • Cross-check: approved drawing → stop-bar layout → fixture schedule → physical installation
Acceptance / Record

Acceptance Criteria: the complete stop-bar installation corresponds with the approved design.

TC-SBR-002Stop-Bar Alignment
Verification Checks
  • Verify fixture alignment, stop-bar axis and relationship to taxiway centreline
  • Verify relationship to runway holding position, fixture orientation and uniform installation geometry
  • Check for misaligned, rotated or mechanically moved fixtures, uneven installation and incorrect orientation
  • Record fixture/location, required alignment, actual condition, acceptance basis and result
Hold Point

Hold Point: alignment is verified against the approved drawing before electrical testing proceeds.

B

Stop-Bar Electrical & Circuit

2 topics
TC-SBR-003Stop-Bar Electrical Testing
Verification Checks
  • Verify circuit identification, primary circuit, isolation transformers where applicable and secondary connection
  • Verify continuity, insulation where applicable, earthing/bonding where applicable
  • Verify connector condition and cable termination
  • Tests may include continuity, insulation resistance, earth continuity, voltage, current and circuit resistance
Hold Point

Hold Point: electrical testing should be completed before operational energization.

TC-SBR-004Stop-Bar Circuit Testing
Verification Checks
  • Verify the complete circuit: CCR → primary cable → isolation transformer → secondary lead → connector → stop-bar fixture
  • Check circuit continuity, cable integrity, transformer connection and connector continuity
  • Check fixture connection, circuit loading, operating current and circuit stability
  • Evaluate abnormal conditions: open circuit, short/abnormal circuit condition, incorrect loading, high/low current, intermittent operation, connector fault, transformer fault
Hold Point

Hold Point: any abnormal circuit condition is investigated and resolved before illumination testing.

C

Stop-Bar Operation & Acceptance

5 topics
TC-SBR-005Stop-Bar Illumination Testing
Verification Checks
  • Verify all required fixtures illuminate with no dark or intermittent fixture
  • Verify correct colour, uniform appearance, stable illumination and correct response to switching commands
  • Test sequence: energize → stabilize → inspect complete stop bar → switch → recheck → record
Acceptance / Record

Acceptance Criteria: do not accept the stop bar solely because individual fixtures illuminate — verify the complete stop-bar array.

TC-SBR-006Stop-Bar Intensity Verification
Verification Checks
  • Verify correct intensity, intensity consistency and intensity setting
  • Verify CCR response, fixture response and brightness uniformity
  • Where multiple intensity settings apply, record each applicable operating level: step, commanded setting, measured/observed, acceptance criterion, result
Hold Point

Hold Point: numerical intensity acceptance criteria come from the approved design/specification and applicable aerodrome requirements.

TC-SBR-007Stop-Bar Switching Test
Verification Checks
  • Verify ON command, OFF command and correct switching response
  • Verify no delayed or abnormal operation, no unintended operation, and correct status indication where provided
  • Test sequence: command → control system → CCR/circuit → stop bar → visual confirmation
  • Where applicable, test both local switching and remote switching
Acceptance / Record

Acceptance Criteria: the stop bar switches correctly and predictably across every applicable command path.

TC-SBR-008Stop-Bar Control Verification
Verification Checks
  • Verify local control, remote control, ON/OFF command and intensity command where applicable
  • Verify status feedback, control mode, command acknowledgement and interlocks/associated control logic where applicable
  • Integrated test: control command → controller/ALCMS → CCR → stop-bar circuit → stop-bar fixtures → status feedback
Acceptance / Record

Acceptance Criteria: the command actually produces the intended stop-bar state, and the control system correctly reflects that state.

TC-SBR-009Stop-Bar Acceptance Test
Verification Checks
  • Confirm installation: location, fixture configuration, alignment and mounting verified
  • Confirm electrical: circuit identification, continuity, insulation where applicable, cable, transformer where applicable, connector and circuit current verified
  • Confirm optical: complete illumination, colour, intensity and uniformity verified
  • Confirm control: ON/OFF, local control where applicable, remote control where applicable, status feedback and interlock/control logic where applicable verified
  • Confirm punch-list reviewed, corrective actions completed, retesting completed and test records completed
Acceptance / Record

Acceptance Criteria: installation + alignment + electrical integrity + correct illumination + correct intensity + correct switching + correct control = ACCEPTED STOP BAR.

RGL Installation, Alignment & Electrical

3 topics
TC-RGL-010RGL Installation Verification
Verification Checks
  • Verify correct RGL type, location, number of fixtures and mounting
  • Verify correct orientation, relationship to runway holding position, circuit and cable arrangement
  • Verify correct support structure
  • Check for incorrect location, orientation, damaged fixture, loose mounting, incorrect circuit or incomplete installation
  • Cross-check: approved layout → RGL schedule → physical installation
Acceptance / Record

Acceptance Criteria: the RGL installation matches the approved layout and schedule.

TC-RGL-011RGL Alignment Verification
Verification Checks
  • Verify fixture orientation, horizontal alignment and vertical alignment where applicable
  • Verify direction of visibility, relationship to approaching traffic and alignment with approved design
  • Check for incorrect aiming, tilted or rotated fixture, loose mounting and obstructed view
  • Record RGL ID, required alignment, actual alignment, acceptance basis and result
Hold Point

Hold Point: alignment is verified against the approved design before electrical testing proceeds.

TC-RGL-012RGL Electrical Testing
Verification Checks
  • Verify circuit identification, supply, cable and transformer where applicable
  • Verify connector, fixture, continuity, insulation where applicable and earthing/bonding where applicable
  • Verify operating current
  • Tests may include continuity, insulation resistance, earth continuity, voltage, current and circuit resistance
Hold Point

Hold Point: electrical condition is confirmed healthy before the flashing-operation test.

E

RGL Functional, Control & Acceptance

5 topics
TC-RGL-013RGL Flashing-Operation Test
Verification Checks
  • Verify flashing operation, correct sequence and correct synchronization where applicable
  • Verify correct flash pattern, stable flashing, no missed flashes and no abnormal flickering
  • Verify correct operation of all RGL units
  • Test: command/power ON → observe flashing → verify sequence → verify synchronization → record
  • Check for one fixture not flashing, incorrect sequence, unequal timing, intermittent or unstable flashing, driver/controller fault
Acceptance / Record

Acceptance Criteria: RGL acceptance requires verification of the flashing function, not merely illumination of the lamp/LED.

TC-RGL-014RGL Intensity Verification
Verification Checks
  • Verify correct intensity, intensity consistency and correct operating setting
  • Verify fixture output and brightness balance
  • Where multiple operating conditions apply, test each and record RGL ID, operating condition, required/expected, measured/observed and result
Acceptance / Record

Acceptance Criteria: intensity is consistent and correct across every applicable operating condition.

TC-RGL-015RGL Control Verification
Verification Checks
  • Verify ON/OFF, local control where applicable, remote control where applicable and automatic operation where applicable
  • Verify control-system status, command response and interlocks where applicable
  • Integrated sequence: control command → controller → RGL circuit → flashing fixtures → status/feedback
Acceptance / Record

Acceptance Criteria: the RGL responds correctly to every applicable control command.

TC-RGL-016RGL Alarm Verification
Verification Checks
  • Verify where provided: RGL failure alarm, lamp/LED failure indication and controller fault
  • Verify circuit fault, power failure, communication failure, flashing failure and remote alarm/status
  • Test sequence: create approved test/fault condition → RGL detects condition → alarm generated → local/remote indication → fault cleared → system recovery
  • Record alarm, test condition, local indication, remote indication, recovery and result
Acceptance / Record

Acceptance Criteria: every applicable alarm/fault function is correctly detected, indicated and recoverable.

TC-RGL-017RGL Acceptance Test
Verification Checks
  • Confirm installation: correct location, fixture type, mounting, orientation and alignment verified
  • Confirm electrical: circuit identified, continuity, insulation where applicable, cable, connector, transformer where applicable and current verified
  • Confirm functional: flashing operation, flash sequence, synchronization where applicable, intensity and stable operation verified
  • Confirm control: local control where applicable, remote control where applicable, automatic operation where applicable and status feedback verified
  • Confirm alarm: fault alarm, failure indication and recovery verified
  • Confirm punch-list reviewed, corrective actions completed, retest completed and documentation completed
Acceptance / Record

Acceptance Criteria: installation + alignment + electrical integrity + correct flashing + correct intensity + correct control + correct alarm = ACCEPTED RGL.

Critical Commissioning Principle

The two systems should not be commissioned using exactly the same functional philosophy — each answers a different critical question.

Stop Bar

"Does the stop bar correctly illuminate, switch and respond to the control system?"

Correct Installation + Correct Alignment + Electrical Integrity + Correct Illumination + Correct Intensity + Correct Switching + Correct Control = ACCEPTED STOP BAR

Runway Guard Light

"Does the RGL provide the required flashing warning function and respond correctly to its control and fault-monitoring system?"

Correct Installation + Correct Alignment + Electrical Integrity + Correct Flashing + Correct Intensity + Correct Control + Correct Alarm = ACCEPTED RGL

Runway Lighting Test Quick Reference

Stop bars and runway guard lights protect the runway entry, so runway-level checks apply to them. The main runway edge, centreline and threshold commissioning is in Division 06 and photometry is in Division 16. Confirm all criteria against the approved specification and the current standard.

Runway lighting commissioning checklist

A runway lighting commissioning checklist for stop bar and RGL circuits covers: installation, location and alignment; circuit, cable and transformer identification; electrical and circuit tests; illumination of every fixture; intensity setting; switching and control, local, remote and automatic where provided; flashing operation for RGL; alarms where provided; then the acceptance test and the master status record.

Runway lighting commissioning test

The runway lighting commissioning test for these systems proves the whole chain from CCR to fixture. For a stop bar, it proves correct steady illumination and controlled switching. For RGL, it proves correct flashing operation and synchronization. Results are recorded as PASS or another documented status before acceptance is signed.

Runway light photometric testing

Runway light photometric testing measures light output against the approved photometric specification. It is a separate activity from the functional checks in this division and is covered in Division 16. Include stop bar and RGL fixtures in photometric testing only where the contract or specification requires it.

Runway lighting rain testing

Runway lighting rain testing checks how fixtures, connectors and seals behave in wet conditions. Typical checks are that there is no water ingress at the fixture, lens or connector, that insulation and continuity remain acceptable after exposure to moisture, and that the lights still illuminate and switch normally. The method and acceptance limits come from the project specification, not from this page.
