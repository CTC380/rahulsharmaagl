Airfield Lighting Fixture Testing

Every runway, taxiway, approach, and PAPI/apron fixture reaches the field after Division 04 has released the isolation transformer feeding it — but the transformer being healthy says nothing about the fixture bolted on top of it. Division 05 verifies that each Airfield Lighting Fixture (ALF) is correctly installed, identified, rated, mechanically secure, correctly positioned and oriented, optically intact, electrically connected, illuminated and operational before it is accepted into the AGL system.

Philosophy: a fixture is evaluated as a complete chain — correct fixture → correct rating → correct location → correct height → correct mounting → correct alignment → correct orientation → healthy optical components → correct secondary connection → healthy connector → electrical integrity verified → correct illumination → correct switching → correct intensity → accepted fixture. A fixture is not accepted merely because it turns ON — fixture-specific photometric values, tolerances, mounting heights, alignment tolerances and electrical acceptance limits are always taken from the approved drawings, project specification, manufacturer's documentation and applicable standard, never assumed from this topic list.

The airfield lighting fixture is evaluated as a complete chain — every link has to hold before the fixture is accepted, distinguishing physical installation acceptance, electrical acceptance, optical/visual acceptance, and functional acceptance.

Before Testing Begins

Two conditions should be established before any airfield lighting fixture is worked through this division.

Numerical Limits Are Not Universal

  • Mounting heights, alignment tolerances, and intensity values are taken from the approved drawings and project specification, not assumed from this topic list
  • Electrical acceptance limits and insulation test voltages come from the fixture manufacturer's documentation and applicable standard
  • Do not apply inappropriate insulation-test voltages to LED drivers or other electronic modules — follow the manufacturer's approved test method
  • A fixture with an incorrect rating is not accepted merely because it illuminates

Division Boundary

  • Division 04 verifies and releases the isolation transformer feeding each fixture before Division 05 begins
  • Connector identification, continuity, contact, mating, locking, sealing, and cable-lead verification also sit within the broader Cable & Connector Testing scope
  • CCR output-current verification (electrical supply) is distinct from fixture intensity verification (light-output behaviour) — both should be correlated during commissioning
  • Fixture control response confirmed by ALCMS is subsequently verified again as part of the wider system-control testing

A

Identification & Rating

3 topics
TC-ALF-001Fixture Installation Verification
Verification Checks
  • Verify fixture location, type, and orientation
  • Verify mounting arrangement, base/can arrangement, and mounting hardware
  • Verify cable entry, secondary connection, and connector arrangement
  • Verify clearance from surrounding objects and installation height where applicable
  • Cross-check: approved drawing → fixture schedule → physical fixture → installation location
  • Check for incorrect fixture type, incorrect location, loose mounting, incorrect orientation, damaged mounting hardware, cable strain, or improper connector installation
Hold Point

Hold Point: any mismatch against the approved drawing or fixture schedule is resolved before proceeding.

TC-ALF-002Fixture Identification Verification
Verification Checks
  • Record fixture ID, type, location, and circuit number
  • Record associated transformer ID and associated connector ID where applicable
  • Record manufacturer, model, and serial number where available
  • Verify physical fixture identification corresponds with approved drawings, asset register, fixture schedule, circuit schedule, and as-built documentation
Acceptance / Record

Acceptance Criteria: every fixture is uniquely and traceably identified across every reference document.

TC-ALF-003Fixture Rating Verification
Verification Checks
  • Verify fixture type, rated current, and rated voltage where applicable
  • Verify power rating and intensity class/rating where applicable
  • Verify environmental rating, LED module specification, and optical configuration where applicable
  • Cross-check: approved specification ↔ datasheet ↔ nameplate/marking ↔ installed fixture
Hold Point

Hold Point: a fixture with an incorrect rating is not accepted merely because it illuminates.

B

Positional & Mechanical Verification

4 topics
TC-ALF-004Fixture Mounting Verification
Verification Checks
  • Inspect mounting bolts, nuts, washers, base, mounting plate, supports, and brackets
  • Verify fixing arrangement and mechanical stability
  • Confirm no loose fixture, excessive movement, damaged mounting hardware, or unstable mounting
  • Verify correct mounting configuration
  • Record applicable torque verification where torque requirements are specified
Acceptance / Record

Acceptance Criteria: the fixture is mechanically secure with no loose, damaged, or unstable mounting hardware.

TC-ALF-005Fixture Height Verification
Verification Checks
  • Verify fixture elevation, mounting height, and base elevation
  • Verify finished-surface relationship and vertical positioning
  • Record fixture ID, required height, measured height, tolerance, and result
  • Investigate excessively high or low fixtures, uneven installation, settlement, or incorrect base installation
Hold Point

Hold Point: the applicable project drawing/specification defines the acceptance tolerance — do not assume a universal figure.

TC-ALF-006Fixture Alignment Verification
Verification Checks
  • Verify horizontal alignment and direction of light against the alignment reference
  • Verify fixture axis relative to runway/taxiway centreline or applicable reference
Hold Point

Hold Point: incorrect alignment can result in an apparently healthy fixture producing incorrect visual guidance — treat this as a safety-relevant discrepancy.

TC-ALF-007Fixture Orientation Verification
Verification Checks
  • Verify fixture orientation and light-emission direction
  • Verify bidirectional or unidirectional orientation where applicable
  • Verify left/right directional configuration and approach/runway/taxiway directional requirements where applicable
  • Cross-check: fixture optical orientation ↔ approved lighting layout
Acceptance / Record

Acceptance Criteria: the fixture's optical orientation matches the approved lighting layout exactly.

C

Optical Component Inspection

4 topics
TC-ALF-008Lens Inspection
Verification Checks
  • Check for cracks, scratches, contamination, discoloration, and clouding
  • Check for moisture, foreign material, damage, and incorrect lens
  • Check for loose lens and improper sealing
Acceptance / Record

Acceptance Criteria: the lens is clean and suitable for continued service.

TC-ALF-009Prism Inspection
Verification Checks
  • Inspect prism condition for cracks, damage, contamination, discoloration, and moisture where the fixture incorporates optical prisms
  • Verify correct positioning, secure installation, and freedom from optical obstruction
Acceptance / Record

Acceptance Criteria: the prism is physically and optically suitable for the intended fixture function.

TC-ALF-010Lamp Verification
Verification Checks
  • Verify correct lamp type, rating, and installation for fixtures using lamps
  • Verify lamp identification where applicable, condition, correct seating, and correct connection
  • Check for broken filament where applicable, blackening, damage, incorrect lamp, loose lamp, or poor contact
Hold Point

Hold Point: for LED fixtures, proceed to LED module verification instead of lamp verification.

TC-ALF-011LED Module Verification
Verification Checks
  • Verify correct LED module, module identification, rating, installation, and connection
  • Verify physical condition, driver/electronic module, and heat-management arrangement where applicable
  • Inspect for damaged LEDs, dark LED segments, and abnormal illumination
  • Inspect for discoloration, moisture, loose components, and damaged wiring
Acceptance / Record

Acceptance Criteria: the LED module, driver, and heat-management arrangement are correctly installed, physically sound, and free of dark or abnormal segments.

D

Electrical Connection & Integrity

4 topics
TC-ALF-012Secondary Lead Verification
Verification Checks
  • Verify the correct lead, correct length/configuration where applicable, and insulation condition
  • Verify connector termination, cable identification, mechanical protection, strain relief, and routing
  • Confirm no exposed conductor and no physical damage
  • Verify continuity, insulation, and correct connection where applicable
Acceptance / Record

Acceptance Criteria: the secondary lead connecting the isolation transformer/connector to the fixture is mechanically sound and electrically verified.

TC-ALF-013Connector Verification
Verification Checks
  • Inspect correct connector, connector identification, contact condition, mating, locking, and sealing
  • Inspect cable connection, moisture ingress, and physical damage
  • Verify correct connector-to-transformer and connector-to-fixture connection
  • Verify electrical continuity, secure mating, and correct locking
Acceptance / Record

Acceptance Criteria: connector identification, continuity, contact, mating, locking, sealing, and cable-lead verification are all confirmed — this also aligns with the broader connector-testing activities in the wider framework.

TC-ALF-014Fixture Insulation Verification
Verification Checks
  • Verify fixture wiring insulation and secondary lead insulation where the fixture design and approved test procedure permit such testing
  • Verify applicable internal insulation and insulation to earth where applicable
  • Record fixture ID, test voltage, test method, measured value, acceptance criterion, instrument ID, and result
Hold Point

Hold Point: do not apply inappropriate insulation-test voltages to LED drivers, electronic modules, or other sensitive components — follow the manufacturer's approved test method.

TC-ALF-015Fixture Continuity Verification
Verification Checks
  • Verify continuity through secondary lead, connector, fixture input, internal circuit where testable, and return path where applicable
  • Check for open circuit, loose connection, poor contact, or broken lead
  • Check for incorrect connector or incorrect termination
Acceptance / Record

Acceptance Criteria: continuity is confirmed through the complete fixture circuit with no open circuits or poor contacts.

E

Functional Verification

4 topics
TC-ALF-016Fixture Illumination Verification
Verification Checks
  • Verify fixture illuminates with correct optical output, correct colour where applicable, and correct direction
  • Confirm no abnormal flickering, no intermittent operation, and no dark sections in LED assemblies
  • Confirm no abnormal brightness; evaluate under the applicable operational intensity setting and environmental conditions where appropriate
  • Record fixture ID, operating step, illumination status, visual observation, and result
Acceptance / Record

Acceptance Criteria: the fixture produces the required, stable illumination with no abnormal or intermittent behaviour.

TC-ALF-017Fixture Operational Test
Verification Checks
  • Verify ON operation, OFF operation, and stable operation
  • Verify correct response to circuit energization and to intensity changes
  • Confirm no intermittent operation and no abnormal noise or heating where relevant
  • Test sequence: energize → observe → operate → change condition → observe → de-energize
Acceptance / Record

Acceptance Criteria: the fixture operates correctly as part of its intended AGL circuit through the full test sequence.

TC-ALF-018Fixture Switching Test
Verification Checks
  • Verify ON command, OFF command, and switching response
  • Verify correct circuit response with no delayed or unintended operation
  • Verify correct operation at applicable intensity steps
  • Where controlled by CCR, verify: CCR command → series circuit response → fixture response
  • Where controlled by ALCMS, note the fixture response is subsequently confirmed as part of the wider system-control testing
Acceptance / Record

Acceptance Criteria: the fixture responds correctly and without delay to every applicable switching command.

TC-ALF-019Fixture Intensity Verification
Verification Checks
  • Verify minimum intensity, intermediate intensity levels, and maximum intensity
  • Verify correct step response and correct optical behaviour
  • Confirm no abnormal flickering and no unexpected intensity changes
Hold Point

Hold Point: CCR output-current verification confirms electrical current supplied to the circuit — fixture intensity verification confirms resulting light-output behaviour. The two are distinct and should be correlated during commissioning.

F

Acceptance

1 topic
TC-ALF-020Fixture Acceptance Test
Verification Checks
  • Confirm installation: correct fixture, location, identification, and rating; mounting secure; height, alignment, and orientation verified
  • Confirm optical: lens and prism inspected where applicable, lamp/LED module verified, no visible optical damage, correct optical configuration
  • Confirm electrical: secondary lead and connector verified, insulation verified where applicable, continuity verified, correct electrical connection
  • Confirm functional: illumination verified, operational test completed, switching test completed, intensity verified, no abnormal operation
  • Confirm documentation: test results recorded, deviations recorded, corrective actions completed, retests completed where required, fixture accepted
  • Record final status: PASS, FAIL, RETEST, CONDITIONAL, or N/A
Acceptance / Record

Acceptance Criteria: no airfield lighting fixture is released as commissioned until its installation, identification, rating, mounting, height, alignment, orientation, optical condition, electrical connections, continuity/insulation where applicable, illumination, switching, and intensity performance have been verified and documented against the applicable approved requirements — this closes Division 05 and releases the fixture for circuit/system commissioning.

Division 05 runs as a single continuous sequence per fixture, from transformer acceptance through to release for circuit/system commissioning. The source framework treats fixture testing as a distinct activity before the later dedicated runway-lighting, taxiway-lighting, PAPI, approach-lighting, and photometric testing divisions.

Quick Reference

Two short answers that place Division 05 inside the wider commissioning programme. Confirm all acceptance criteria against the approved specification and the current standard.

Airport lighting halogen fixture: what to verify

An airport lighting halogen fixture uses a halogen lamp as its light source, where an LED fixture uses an LED module. The Division 05 sequence is the same, but the lamp stage matters more. Verify the correct lamp type and rating against the datasheet and approved drawing (see Lamp Verification), then check lamp seating and lamp identification. Inspect the lens and prism for cracks, moisture and contamination, because these change the optical output. Then verify the secondary lead and connector, insulation and continuity, controlled energization, illumination and each intensity step. A wrong lamp rating can show up as incorrect light output or shortened lamp life, so a lamp that lights is not proof of acceptance.

Airport lighting commissioning test: where fixture testing fits

An airport lighting commissioning test is run in stages. Division 05 is the fixture stage: each fixture is proven installed, aligned, electrically sound and correctly illuminated before its circuit is commissioned. The system-level tests that follow are the runway, taxiway and approach divisions, then functional, site-acceptance and performance testing. A fixture released here is ready for circuit and system commissioning, not yet accepted as part of a working system.
