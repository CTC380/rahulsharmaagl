The basics

What Is Airfield Ground Lighting Training?

Specialised education on airport lighting and its electrical infrastructure: functions, engineering principles, installation, testing and maintenance. Whether you call it an airfield lighting course, an airport lighting course or an airfield ground lighting course, the programme may include lessons, drawings, calculations, case studies, assignments, simulated faults and equipment demonstrations.

6 learning areas
01
Systems

Airfield lighting systems

Runway edge, centreline, threshold and end lights; taxiway lighting; approach and touchdown zone lighting; runway guard lights, stop bars, PAPI and signs.

02
Electrical

Electrical infrastructure

CCR operation, constant-current series circuits, isolating transformers, cables, connectors, insulation testing, earthing and counterpoise, surge and lightning protection, and electrical safety.

03
Design

Engineering design

Layouts, circuit arrangements, cable selection, voltage-drop calculations, equipment schedules, specifications and quantity estimation.

04
Photometry

Photometry and testing

Luminous intensity, beam alignment, chromaticity, measurement principles, test equipment, calibration considerations and reporting.

05
Maintenance

Maintenance and fault diagnosis

Systematic inspection, defect identification, circuit fault investigation, alarm interpretation and maintenance records.

06
Compliance

Standards and documentation

ICAO provisions, FAA advisory circulars, DGCA requirements and airport specifications, with compliance matrices, test reports and handover files.

Learning format

Online vs Practical and Classroom Training

The right option depends on your objectives and the hands-on experience you need.

3 formats
Online

AGL Online Course & Airfield Lighting Course Online India

Best for theory and principles: system fundamentals, components, ICAO/FAA references, design calculations, photometry methodology, maintenance theory and BOQ documentation. Flexible for working professionals.

Practical

Practical AGL training

Component identification, CCR and transformer demonstrations, cable and connector familiarisation, test instruments, simulated faults and photometric equipment. Work on energised or operational equipment must follow authorised procedures and safety controls. Series circuits carry dangerous current and voltage: never open, disconnect or touch a live circuit; isolate, lock out, earth and test before work.

Classroom & on-site

Classroom and on-site training

Useful for organisations training engineering, maintenance or project teams. Evaluate trainer, syllabus, facilities, location, duration, practical activities and assessment.

Before enrolling, confirm whether practical equipment access, classroom sessions or site visits are included in the quoted fee. An online course should not automatically be assumed to include hands-on training.

Control & monitoring

ALCMS Training and Advanced AGL Engineering

An ALCMS-focused course explains how the control system interfaces with field equipment and CCRs, displays status, presents alarms and supports authorised operator actions.

Advanced topics
  • ALCMS architecture and system components
  • CCR control and monitoring interfaces
  • Alarm interpretation and event logs
  • Circuit status indications; interlocks
  • SCADA fundamentals, where applicable
  • Communication and monitoring troubleshooting
  • Fault and corrective-action documentation

Other advanced subjects: circuit engineering, equipment selection, electrical calculations, photometric assessment, compliance and commissioning documentation.

Knowledge is not authorisation. Learners should distinguish between understanding control-system principles and being authorised to operate or modify an actual airport installation. Operational access and permissions depend on the airport and applicable procedures.

Questions

Frequently Asked Questions About AGL Training

18 answers
Q1Can I learn Airfield Ground Lighting online?
Yes. Fundamentals, electrical principles, components, design concepts, standards and troubleshooting methods can be studied online. Hands-on competency with actual equipment may require additional supervised practical training.
Q2Is AGL training suitable for beginners?
Introductory courses can help students, fresh graduates and electrical professionals understand airport lighting. Check the prerequisites of advanced programmes before enrolling.
Q3Is AGL training available in India?
Precision AGL Academy lists online AGL programmes and a separate Hands-On & On-Site category. Confirm delivery locations, schedules and availability with the Academy before making travel arrangements.
Q4What are the fees for AGL training in India?
Published prices range from ₹399 for Micro Courses to ₹29,999 for the Master Diploma, with separate pricing for specialist and Hands-On & On-Site programmes. Check the current course page for fees and inclusions.
Q5Does an AGL course provide certification?
The Academy lists programme-specific Academy-issued certificates. Assessment and completion requirements depend on the course, and they should not be confused with government or international regulatory accreditation.
Q6Are ICAO and FAA standards covered?
ICAO and FAA technical references are among the listed learning topics; coverage varies by course. Always verify the latest official documents and airport requirements before using standards on a project.
Q7Does online training include practical equipment?
Not automatically. Ask the Academy whether demonstrations, laboratory exercises, site visits or equipment-based assessments are included.
Q8How long does an AGL training course take?
From short courses to the six-month Master Diploma. The Advanced AGL Design & Compliance Program is three months; the Photometry Specialist and Maintenance Engineer programmes are two months each.
Q9Is previous airport experience required?
Not for introductory training. Advanced design, testing and maintenance programmes are easier to follow with a grounding in electrical engineering.
Q10Will AGL training guarantee a job?
No. Employment depends on qualifications, practical competence, experience, employer requirements and available opportunities.
Q11Can I study AGL photometry separately?
Yes. The AGL Photometry Specialist Program covers photometric principles, beam alignment, goniometer concepts, testing procedures and report preparation.
Q12How can I enrol in an AGL course?
Review the syllabus and fee on the Academy page, then contact the Academy to confirm availability, access arrangements, assessment and certification details.
Q13What does an airfield lighting engineering course cover?
An airfield lighting engineering course such as the Master Diploma covers aviation fundamentals, lighting systems, CCRs and series circuits, isolating transformers and cables, design calculations, photometry, ALCMS, maintenance, standards and project documentation.
Q14Is there an airfield lighting certification course in India?
Precision AGL Academy offers programmes that end in an Academy-issued certificate. This is not government, ICAO or FAA accreditation. Check the assessment and completion requirements of the course you choose.
Q15What are the AGL certification requirements?
They depend on the programme and on your employer. Confirm the completion criteria, whether a practical assessment or final exam applies, and what the airport requires before you work on operational equipment. A course certificate alone does not give regulatory authorisation.
Q16Is there an AGL training manual?
Learning materials are part of the programmes, and the fee checklist above lists syllabus and learning materials. If you need an AGL training manual in a particular format, such as printed or PDF, confirm it with the Academy before enrolling. Free reference material is in the AGL study notes.
Q17Is Precision AGL Academy an airfield lighting training institute?
Precision AGL Academy is an online academy focused on airfield ground lighting, founded by Rahul Sharma. When comparing any airfield lighting training institute, check the trainer, syllabus, facilities, practical activities, assessment and certificate.
Q18What does an airfield ground lighting course or airport lighting course teach?
It teaches how runway, taxiway and approach lighting, CCRs, isolating transformers, cables and control systems work together, and how to design, test, maintain and document them. Depth depends on the programme you choose.
