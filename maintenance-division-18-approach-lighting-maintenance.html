Approach Lighting Maintenance

Dedicated maintenance procedures for the complete approach lighting system — fixtures, light bars, poles, crossbars, structural members, foundations, frangible components, electrical connections, and associated isolation transformers. The objective is to preserve the geometry and continuity of the whole system, not just the operation of individual fixtures.

Core principle: Physical Integrity + Frangibility + Electrical Integrity + Geometric Accuracy + Optical Performance = Acceptable Approach Lighting Condition. A fixture can be electrically functional and still require maintenance because of incorrect alignment, incorrect elevation, structural movement, corrosion, vegetation obstruction, or a damaged frangible component.

Safety before work: isolate and lock out the affected series circuit at the CCR before touching fixtures, connectors or isolation transformers — never open a live series circuit or disconnect a primary connector on an energised circuit. Obtain airside / ATC work authorisation, and apply working-at-height controls for poles and structures.

Frequently Asked Questions

Quick answers about approach lighting maintenance, based on the topics in this division.

8 QUESTIONS
Q1What is approach lighting maintenance in the AGL Maintenance Handbook?

It provides dedicated procedures for the complete approach lighting system: fixtures, light bars, poles, crossbars, structural members, foundations, frangible components and electrical connections.

Q2What does Division 18 of the AGL Maintenance Handbook cover?

Division 18 (Approach Lighting Maintenance) contains 36 topics in three groups: Group A Preventive Maintenance (18 topics), Group B Corrective Maintenance (13 topics) and Group C Special Maintenance (5 topics).

Q3What makes an approach lighting condition acceptable?

Five integrity factors together: physical integrity, frangibility, electrical integrity, geometric accuracy and optical performance. A fixture can be electrically functional and still not be acceptable.

Q4What safety rule applies before work on approach lighting?

Isolate and lock out the affected series circuit at the CCR before touching fixtures, connectors or isolation transformers, and never open a live series circuit.

Q5What is the approach lighting maintenance sequence?

Ten steps: Inspect, Clean, Check Structure, Check Electrical System, Check Alignment, Check Elevation, Verify Visibility, Verify Illumination, Restore and Record.

Q6How should a defective approach light be troubleshot?

Start with a visual fixture inspection, then check the lamp or LED, optical component, secondary lead, connector, isolation transformer and circuit or cable. Correct the defect, test, check alignment and elevation, verify illumination and record.

Q7What does the maintenance decision matrix recommend?

A dirty fixture is cleaned, a failed lamp or LED is replaced, a misaligned fixture is realigned, incorrect elevation is corrected, loose mounting is tightened to the specified torque, a damaged frangible component is replaced, vegetation is cleared, vehicle impact is isolated, inspected and repaired, and construction displacement means restoring the geometry.

Q8What verification is required before a task is closed?

Five categories: mechanical (mounting, pole, crossbar, structure, foundation, frangible components), electrical (cable, connector, transformer, continuity and applicable insulation testing), optical (orientation, alignment, elevation, illumination), environmental (water ingress, corrosion, vegetation clearance) and final (system operation, visibility and the maintenance record).
