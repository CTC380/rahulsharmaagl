Four flagship paths, four finished pieces of work

Each program is built around the same real-world workflow used on live airport projects, and ends with a deliverable and an examination.

Master Diploma in Airfield Ground Lighting Engineering

Precision AGL Academy Diploma Certificate, Master AGL Engineer track

  • 6 months
  • 250+ hours
  • 20 modules

You finish with

  • A capstone project: a complete AGL engineering package with layouts, circuits, calculations, BOQ, compliance matrix, and a commissioning and maintenance plan
  • 10 assignments reviewed by the instructor
  • A final examination, theory and practical: circuit interpretation, design calculation, standards interpretation and fault diagnosis

You learn to

  • Read runway, taxiway, approach and PAPI layouts
  • Size CCRs, transformers and cables, and calculate voltage drop
  • Apply ICAO Annex 14, FAA AC 150/5340 and Indian standards clauses
  • Prepare a BOQ, tender documents and a compliance matrix
  • Install, test and commission an AGL system
  • Diagnose open-circuit, short-circuit and ground faults

Photometry Basics

Compliance Matrix Preparation

Frequently Asked Questions

Q1What will I finish with after a Precision AGL Academy program?

Something you built, not only hours watched. Each flagship program is organized around work products from the real airport workflow, such as a design package, a BOQ and compliance matrix, a photometry report or a fault-diagnosis plan.

Q2What are the flagship programs and how long are they?

Master Diploma in Airfield Ground Lighting Engineering (6 months, 250+ hours, 20 modules); Advanced AGL Design & Compliance (3 months, 120 hours, 8 modules); AGL Photometry Specialist (2 months, 80 hours, 8 modules); and Airport Lighting Maintenance Engineer (2 months, 60 hours, 8 modules).

Q3What does the Master Diploma capstone involve?

A complete AGL engineering package with layouts, circuits, calculations, BOQ, compliance matrix and a commissioning and maintenance plan, plus 10 instructor-reviewed assignments and a final theory and practical examination.

Q4What are micro courses?

Short courses of 2 to 5 hours, each covering a single AGL topic with a named practical outcome. Examples are CCR Fundamentals, Isolating Transformers, Photometry Basics, Fault Finding Workshop, Compliance Matrix Preparation and AGL Report Writing.

Q5What hands-on programs are available?

Live Airport Photometry Workshop (3 days), Airport Site Visit Program (2 days), Airport Design Bootcamp (5 days) and Corporate AGL Certification Program (1 week).

Q6Do I need prior airport experience?

No. No prior airport background is assumed. Electrical engineers, fresh graduates and career-switchers can build airport-specific knowledge from the ground up.

Q7Are the certificates accredited?

No. They are Precision AGL Academy-issued certificates of completion specific to each program, not government, university or ICAO/FAA accreditations.

Q8Are there learner stories yet?

The page says real learner stories will be published as they become available and that it does not publish placeholder reviews. Learners who have finished a program can share what they built and whether their name may be published.

Q9How does the Academy treat ICAO and FAA standards?

They are covered for educational reference. The Academy advises verifying project work against the latest officially published ICAO, FAA and national regulatory documents.
