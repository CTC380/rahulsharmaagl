Refund Policy

When and how a refund may be available for Precision AGL Academy programs.

Last updated: September 2026

This Refund Policy forms part of our Terms & Conditions (see Section 8). It sets out the refund windows Precision AGL Academy applies across its program categories.

1. Overview

We want participants to enroll with confidence. This policy explains when a refund may be available for Precision AGL Academy programs.

2. Weekend / Micro Courses

Given their short duration and immediate content access, Micro Courses are generally non-refundable once access has been granted. If you experience a technical issue preventing access, contact us within 48 hours of purchase for resolution.

3. Professional & Specialist Programs

A refund request made before accessing more than 10% of the course content, and within 7 days of enrollment, may be considered on a case-by-case basis, minus any payment processing fees.

4. Master Diploma

Given the scope and instructor time committed to the Master Diploma, refund requests are considered only within 7 days of enrollment and before beginning Module 3. After this point, the Academy is generally unable to offer a refund, as instructor capacity has already been allocated.

5. Workshops & On-Site Programs

Hands-On & On-Site workshops involve venue, travel and logistics commitments made in advance. Cancellations made more than 14 days before the scheduled start date may be eligible for a partial refund; later cancellations are generally non-refundable, though the Academy may offer to transfer your seat to a future batch where possible.

6. Corporate Training

Refund and cancellation terms for Corporate AGL Certification Programs are agreed individually as part of the corporate training agreement.

7. How to Request a Refund

Send your enrollment details and reason for the request via the contact page. Approved refunds are processed to the original payment method within 7–14 business days.

8. Non-Refundable Circumstances

  • Change of mind after substantial course content has been accessed
  • Failure to complete the program within the access window through no fault of the Academy
  • Violation of the Academy's Terms & Conditions
