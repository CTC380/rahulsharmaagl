Frequently Asked Questions

Q1How can I contact Precision AGL Solutions?

By email at precisionaglsolutions@gmail.com, by phone at +91 98214 96412, on WhatsApp, on LinkedIn (Precision AGL Solutions) or with the enquiry form on the page. The website is rahulsharmaagl.in.

Q2Who can send an enquiry?

Airports, consultants, contractors, engineers, technicians, manufacturers, service providers, students and aviation professionals.

Q3What can I enquire about?

AGL consultancy, photometric testing, inspection and maintenance, testing and commissioning, AGL systems and engineering, airport project support, training and Academy questions, business partnerships and knowledge or technical questions. The enquiry form also has an Other option.

Q4What information should I include for a project enquiry?

Useful information includes a BOQ or item list, technical specification, GA or layout drawing, existing system details, photometry requirement, testing requirement, airport or project location, and required quantity or scope.

Q5Can I attach files to the enquiry form?

Not directly. Email links can't carry files, so after you submit, your email app opens with the enquiry filled in and you attach the files there.

Q6How do I ask about the Academy or knowledge resources?

Use the same enquiry channel for questions about knowledge resources, technical articles, the Academy, the Component Library, the Standards Companion or other educational resources.

Q7How do I enquire about a business partnership?

Choose Become a Business Partner. Tell the team about your organization, technical capabilities, market or region, products or services, and the partnership you are exploring.
