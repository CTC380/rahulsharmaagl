Product Overview

Airfield Location Sign

What It Is

Location signs identify the taxiway, runway or other movement-area location where the sign is installed, giving pilots and vehicle operators positional information about their current location.

Precision AGL Solutions supplies location signs for taxiways, runway exits, intersections, apron movement areas and other locations needing clear positional identification.

Typical Legends

  • Taxiway designations
  • Runway designations where applicable
  • Intersection or location identifiers
  • Other approved movement-area location references

Supplied as internally illuminated (LED or other approved source) or retroreflective (non-illuminated, where the standard permits); elevated or ground-mounted; single- or double-face as required.

Location Sign Colour

Yellow on Black

ICAO-Based Aerodromes

Location signs have a yellow inscription on a black background, clearly distinct from mandatory instruction signs (white inscription on red). A stand-alone location sign normally carries a yellow border. Sign-face construction, border and optical characteristics follow the applicable standard.

Avoiding Confusion

Position and configure the sign so it does not conflict with:

  • Mandatory instruction, direction and information signs
  • Runway and taxiway markings
ICAO & FAA Applications

Standard-Specific Approach

ICAO

Location signs identify the taxiway or movement-area location of the aircraft and form part of the aerodrome visual guidance system.

  • Legend and sign location
  • Dimensions and character size
  • Colour, illumination, mounting

Establish all of these from ICAO requirements and the approved aerodrome signage plan.

FAA (L-858)

Location signs are part of the FAA runway and taxiway sign family, distinguished from mandatory instruction, direction and information signs. The current L-858 specification establishes:

  • Sign type, size and style
  • Legend and character dimensions
  • Construction, illumination, electrical configuration
  • Mounting and environmental performance
Legend & Character Height

Taken From the Approved Signage Plan

Legend Configuration

  • Taxiway letter or combination
  • Location identifier
  • Runway designation where specifically required
  • Other standardized location information

Check designation, character order, spacing, face orientation and viewing direction. Review custom legends before manufacturing: an incorrect face is an operationally misleading visual aid.

Character Height & Dimensions

Character height comes from the applicable standard, not a universal value. Factors:

  • Sign location, taxiway geometry
  • Cockpit viewing position and distance
  • Operating conditions, sign type

Overall dimensions follow character height, character count, legend arrangement, borders, illumination and mounting. FAA L-858 gives standardized configurations; ICAO projects use the approved sign schedule.

Illumination & LED

Visual Performance, Not Just Light Output

Illumination Options

  • Internal LED illumination
  • Other approved internal light sources
  • Retroreflective face (non-illuminated, where permitted)
  • Series-circuit powered illumination
  • Direct electrical supply
  • Project-specific LED driver systems

Under ICAO Annex 14 a sign must be illuminated when used in runway visual range conditions below 800 m, at night with instrument runways, and at night with non-instrument runways of code number 3 or 4. In other cases a retroreflective sign may be acceptable. Confirm the requirement for your aerodrome with the airport authority.

LED Location Sign

Benefits: long service life, less lamp replacement, efficient and stable illumination, reduced maintenance, compatibility with modern airport lighting.

LED signs must still satisfy requirements for colour, luminance, uniformity, electrical compatibility, environmental performance and optical construction.

Electrical Configuration

Series Circuit, Direct Supply or LED Driver

Series Circuit

Integrated into a constant-current airfield lighting circuit via an isolating transformer. Signs can also run from a parallel constant-voltage circuit where the airport design uses one.

Direct Supply

Dedicated electrical source where permitted by the project.

LED Driver

An LED sign may incorporate an appropriate driver or power-conversion system.

CCR Compatibility Considerations

  • Circuit current and isolation transformer rating
  • Sign load and number of signs
  • Circuit configuration
  • Brightness control and monitoring (sign output should follow the airfield brightness steps)

Confirm Before Procurement

The sign should match the complete electrical system; confirm the required configuration before ordering.

Mounting & Frangibility

Support Arrangements

Typical Components

  • Frangible support posts
  • Mounting brackets, base plates, anchor hardware
  • Sign mounting channels
  • Frangible couplings
  • Cable-entry provisions

Signs on the movement area must be frangible and mounted low enough to clear propellers and engine pods. The support must also give structural stability.

Frangibility

Signs sit close to aircraft operating surfaces, so supports must be frangible:

  • Frangible posts and breakaway couplings
  • Lightweight support assemblies
  • Controlled failure interfaces

Evaluate sign and support as a system. Do not substitute a generic breakaway component without confirming compatibility with the approved design.

Environmental Performance

Outdoor Airfield Exposure

  • Rain and humidity
  • Dust and sand
  • UV radiation
  • Temperature variation
  • Wind
  • Cleaning chemicals
  • De-icing chemicals where applicable
  • Coastal environments

Construction should provide corrosion resistance, weather protection, UV resistance, electrical sealing, drainage, mechanical durability and surface protection.

Installation Considerations

Location, Orientation and Viewing

Sign Location

Place at the approved location with adequate visibility, avoiding conflicts with:

  • Aircraft and vehicles
  • Other signs and lighting
  • Pavement markings, navigation aids

Sign Orientation

Face the intended viewing direction. Where aircraft approach from multiple directions, the approved design may need multiple faces or separate assemblies.

Viewing Conditions

  • Daylight and night
  • Low ambient illumination
  • Poor visibility
  • Rain or contamination

Minimize unnecessary visual obstruction.

Coordination With Taxiway Markings

Reinforce, Don't Conflict

Coordinate With

  • Taxiway centreline and edge markings
  • Runway-holding and intermediate holding positions
  • Direction and mandatory instruction signs
  • Taxiway lighting

Principle

The sign should reinforce the approved movement-area layout rather than introduce a conflicting designation.

Safety

Electrical Safety — Isolation and Lock-Out

WARNING — never open a live series circuit. An open series circuit driven by a constant-current regulator can produce dangerously high voltage. Opening, cutting or disconnecting any part of an energised circuit can cause serious injury or death and damage equipment.

  • De-energise the circuit at the regulator and apply the site lock-out / tag-out (LOTO) procedure before any work
  • Where the circuit must be isolated or shorted, use the series circuit cut-out switch or approved isolation device — not an improvised bypass
  • Verify the circuit is de-energised and the output is isolated before touching conductors, connectors, splices or transformers
  • Work only under the airport's permit-to-work system, using qualified personnel and the manufacturer's instructions
  • Follow the applicable national electrical safety regulations and site procedures

FAQ

Airfield Location Sign — Frequently Asked Questions

What is an airfield location sign?

A location sign identifies the taxiway, runway or other movement-area location where the sign is installed, giving pilots and vehicle operators positional information about their current location. Typical legends are taxiway designations, runway designations where applicable, and intersection or location identifiers.

What colour is a location sign?

Location signs have a yellow inscription on a black background, clearly distinct from mandatory instruction signs, which use a white inscription on red. A stand-alone location sign normally carries a yellow border, and sign-face construction, border and optical characteristics follow the applicable standard.

How does a location sign differ from a direction sign or a mandatory instruction sign?

A location sign identifies the current position, a direction sign provides route information, and a mandatory instruction sign communicates a restriction such as a runway holding position or NO ENTRY. A location sign should not be configured as a directional sign merely because both may contain taxiway designations, and this distinction matters when preparing the airport signage schedule.

What is an FAA L-858L sign?

Location signs are part of the FAA runway and taxiway sign family, and the type for location signs is L-858L, with size, style and class set under AC 150/5345-44L. Sign layout and system standards are in AC 150/5340-18H. For ICAO projects, legend, dimensions, colour, illumination and mounting come from Annex 14 and the approved aerodrome signage plan.

Must a location sign be illuminated?

Under ICAO Annex 14 a sign must be illuminated when used in runway visual range conditions below 800 m, at night with instrument runways, and at night with non-instrument runways of code number 3 or 4. In other cases a retroreflective sign may be acceptable, so confirm the requirement for your aerodrome with the airport authority.

How are the legend and character height decided?

The legend comes from the approved airport signage plan, and character height comes from the applicable standard rather than a universal value. Check designation, character order, spacing, face orientation and viewing direction, and review custom legends before manufacturing, because an incorrect face is an operationally misleading visual aid.

How is a location sign powered?

It can be integrated into a constant-current airfield lighting circuit through an isolating transformer, run from a parallel constant-voltage circuit where the airport design uses one, supplied directly from a dedicated source where permitted, or fitted with an LED driver. Check the circuit current, isolation transformer rating, sign load and number of signs, and note that sign output should follow the airfield brightness steps.

How is a location sign mounted and installed?

Signs on the movement area must be frangible and mounted low enough to clear propellers and engine pods, using frangible posts, breakaway couplings and controlled failure interfaces. Do not substitute a generic breakaway component without confirming compatibility with the approved design. Install at the approved location, facing the intended viewing direction, and where aircraft approach from several directions the design may need multiple faces or separate assemblies.

What are the common problems with location signs?

An incorrect taxiway designation can come from a wrong legend, an outdated signage plan, a manufacturing error or taxiway renaming, so verify against the latest approved signage schedule before installation. A sign that is difficult to read at night may have failed LED modules, a dirty face or a supply problem, and one that is not visible from the intended taxiway may have incorrect orientation, an obstruction or the wrong position. Physical damage from vehicle impact, aircraft contact or corrosion needs inspection of the complete sign and support.

What should be provided for a location sign quotation?

Provide the airport and project, the governing standard, the sign type, the exact legend, the character height, the quantity, whether it is illuminated or retroreflective, the LED requirement, the electrical supply and series-circuit requirements, the mounting and frangible requirements, the installation location, environmental requirements and the documentation needed. An approved airport signage schedule or sign-face drawing is particularly useful for custom configurations.

Is it safe to work on an illuminated sign on a live series circuit?

No. An open series circuit driven by a constant-current regulator can produce dangerously high voltage, and opening, cutting or disconnecting any part of an energised circuit can cause serious injury or death. De-energise the circuit at the regulator, apply the site lock-out / tag-out procedure, use the series circuit cut-out switch or an approved isolation device and work under the permit-to-work system. See the electrical safety warning on this page.

Engineering Note

Exact dimensions, luminance, electrical characteristics and mounting details are standard- and project-specific: confirm the current standard, approved sign schedule and manufacturer datasheet before procurement or installation.
