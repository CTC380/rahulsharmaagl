Future Airport Concept Maturity Model

To make the division actionable, every future concept is classified against an eight-stage maturity scale, from conceptual vision through to operational deployment.

Stage 1 — Vision: Conceptual future possibility
Stage 2 — Technology Mapping: Required technologies identified
Stage 3 — Architecture: Future system architecture defined
Stage 4 — Feasibility: Technical and operational feasibility assessed
Stage 5 — Prototype: Small-scale demonstration developed
Stage 6 — Pilot: Airport-representative pilot performed
Stage 7 — Integrated Demonstration: Multiple technologies operate together
Stage 8 — Operational Deployment: Technology becomes part of airport infrastructure

Most Important Design Principle

Division 11 should remain the highest-level future-concept division of the Innovation Hub. It looks at the airport as one integrated future system rather than at individual technologies, products or engineering methods — those live in Divisions 02–10.

The logic across the hub becomes: 01 — Strategy asks where innovation should go; 02 — Smart AGL asks how AGL becomes intelligent; 03 — AI asks how AGL becomes predictive; 04 — IoT asks how AGL becomes connected; 05 — Digital Engineering asks how AGL becomes digital; 06 — Robotics asks how AGL becomes automated; 07 — Sustainability asks how AGL becomes greener; 08 — Product Innovation asks what future products are needed; 09 — Engineering Innovation asks how to engineer better; 10 — Emerging Technologies asks what technologies are coming next; and 11 — Future Airport Concepts asks how all of these technologies transform the airport.
