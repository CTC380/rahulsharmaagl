01 · Overview

Airport Lighting Companies in India

Airport lighting is a specialized engineering field covering Airfield Ground Lighting (AGL), runway visual aids, approach lighting, PAPI, taxiway guidance, airfield signs, constant current regulators, isolating transformers, cables, connectors, control systems, photometry, testing, commissioning and maintenance.

The term "airport lighting companies in India" covers several different types of organization rather than one category of supplier. The right supplier for a consignment of transformers is not necessarily the right organization to execute a complete runway AGL installation, and a photometry specialist is not necessarily a turnkey AGL contractor.

The Indian market includes companies publicly describing capabilities across AGL manufacturing, installation, photometric testing, maintenance and project execution. The Ministry of Civil Aviation has also stated that AGL manufacturing is undertaken by various private-sector entities and that airport operators procure systems according to operational requirements and DGCA safety and regulatory requirements.[1]

02 · Company Types

Types of Airport Lighting Companies in India

The most important distinction is the scope of responsibility.

1. AGL manufacturers

Manufacture airport lighting equipment or AGL electrical components — runway and taxiway lights, PAPI, approach lighting, isolating transformers, connectors, cables, signs, CCRs and control equipment. Evaluated mainly on product design, production, quality control, testing, documentation and technical support.

2. Suppliers

Supply or distribute equipment made by themselves or others (manufacturer → supplier → EPC contractor / airport). Strengths: procurement, inventory, logistics, commercial support. Establish whether the supplier is the original manufacturer, an authorized representative, an authorized distributor, an integrator or a general trading company.

3. Installation contractors

Field implementation: survey → civil work → cable installation → transformer installation → light installation → termination → aiming → testing → commissioning. May execute runway, taxiway, approach, PAPI, signs, CCR installation, electrical systems, ALCMS integration and maintenance. Indian companies publicly advertising this include CTC Airfield (installation, transformer and connector manufacturing, signage installation, project management, training and photometric testing).[2]

4. SITC contractors

Supply, Installation, Testing and Commissioning — equipment procurement and delivery, physical and electrical installation, electrical / functional / optical tests, and integrated verification and handover. Often the appropriate model for new runway, taxiway or airport development projects.

5. Photometry companies

Measure the optical performance of installed lighting: runway, taxiway, approach-light and PAPI photometry, intensity, colour, chromaticity, coverage, configuration and aiming. AAI procurement documents show the practical role of specialist AGL photometric services, including field photometric testing and ground-lighting maintenance.[3] AKC Aerotech publicly describes airport ground-lighting and photometric testing services across Indian airports.[4]

6. Maintenance companies

Annual maintenance contracts and operational support: routine inspections, runway/taxiway and PAPI light maintenance, CCR maintenance, transformer inspection, connector replacement, cable fault investigation, photometric testing, emergency breakdown response and spares management. AAI tender listings include annual operation and maintenance of ground-lighting facilities with supply of GLF spares.[3]

7. AGL consultants

Design, engineering and technical decisions rather than physical installation: master planning, runway and taxiway lighting design, PAPI siting, photometric and electrical design, CCR sizing, cable design, specifications, BOQ, tender documentation, technical evaluation, site inspection and commissioning supervision. Consultant and contractor can be separate organizations, which is useful for independent technical review.

05 · By System

Runway, Taxiway, PAPI, CCR, Cable, Sign and Control Companies

Runway lighting companies

Supply or install edge, threshold, end, centreline, touchdown zone and approach lighting. A competent company understands the relationship between the light, AGL series circuit, CCR, transformer, cable, installation geometry and photometric performance.

Taxiway lighting companies

Blue edge lights, centreline lights, stop bars, runway guard lights, holding-position aids and guidance signs. For complex projects, understand interactions with markings, signs, ALCMS, runway incursion prevention, low-visibility operations and electrical circuits.

PAPI companies

PAPI is a precision visual approach guidance system. A capable company addresses unit supply, site survey, positioning, foundation, spacing, elevation, horizontal alignment, aiming, electrical integration, photometry, commissioning and maintenance. Do not judge PAPI on the price of four units alone — performance depends heavily on installation geometry and aiming.

CCR companies

The AGL series circuit depends on controlled current. Companies should understand series-circuit design, brightness stages, regulator ratings, control interfaces, isolation, monitoring, fault conditions, power-quality considerations where applicable and commissioning. A CCR supplier should not be selected independently of the overall AGL circuit design.

Cables & connectors

High-voltage primary cable, secondary cable, primary and secondary connector kits, transformer leads and junction arrangements. Poor or incorrectly installed connectors cause intermittent failure, insulation deterioration, moisture ingress, transformer problems and circuit faults — evaluate these suppliers as part of the whole reliability chain.

Airport signs

Runway, taxiway, holding-position, direction, location and mandatory instruction signs, coordinated with the AGL control system and operational requirements. CTC Airfield, for example, describes AGL signage installation in its portfolio.[2]

Control & monitoring

ALCMS / ILCMS-related companies may provide AGL and CCR control, status and fault monitoring, circuit identification, remote switching, brightness control, event logging and integration with airport systems — chosen to suit the airport's architecture and required automation.

06 · Testing, Commissioning & Maintenance

Testing, Commissioning and Maintenance Companies

Testing should be considered separately from supply, and commissioning verifies the complete installed system before handover.

Electrical tests

  • Insulation resistance and continuity
  • Cable and transformer testing
  • Earth / grounding verification
  • CCR performance and circuit measurements

Functional tests

  • ON/OFF operation
  • Brightness stages
  • Control operation
  • Monitoring and alarms

Photometric tests

  • Intensity, colour, chromaticity
  • Coverage and configuration
  • Aiming

AAI procurement specifications show field photometric testing as a dedicated service category, including vehicle-mounted and hand-held equipment in some work packages.[3]

A contractor that only installs equipment, without strong commissioning capability, may not be suitable for a complete SITC project.

Maintenance

Maintenance covers runway, taxiway, approach, PAPI, signs, CCR, transformers, cables, connectors and the control system. A current AAI tender for Jabalpur Airport covers annual operation and maintenance of ground-lighting facilities and supply of GLF spares for 2026–28.[3] Trained technicians, airside access capability, test instruments, emergency response, spare parts, documentation and troubleshooting expertise can be as important as the original equipment supply.

07 · Project Types

New Airports, Existing Airports, Rehabilitation and Photometry

New airports

Greenfield projects may require detailed AGL engineering, equipment supply, runway, taxiway and approach lighting, PAPI, signs, CCR, transformers, cables, control systems, installation, testing, photometry, commissioning, training, documentation and maintenance. A turnkey or SITC-oriented company may suit better than a component-only supplier.

Existing airports

Work around live runway operations, existing circuits, old and new equipment, limited closure windows, legacy cables, existing CCRs, operational safety procedures and temporary lighting. Airport experience and airside execution methodology matter greatly.

Rehabilitation & LED conversion

Conventional-to-LED conversion, runway and taxiway fitting replacement, CCR replacement, control upgrades, PAPI upgrades, cable rehabilitation, connector replacement, approach-light modification and photometric baseline establishment. The contractor should manage both the existing system and the new equipment.

Photometry from the start

Build photometry into major projects: design → installation → electrical testing → optical testing → photometry → correction → retest. Photometric data identifies incorrect aiming, low-intensity fittings, wrong colour, configuration problems, deteriorated optics and installation inconsistencies — especially important for PAPI, approach lights and precision runway lighting.

09 · Standards & Local Capability

Standards, Compliance and Why Local Indian Capability Matters

Airport lighting is safety-critical infrastructure, so procurement should be tied to the governing technical requirements. ICAO Annex 14 Volume I is a core international reference for aerodrome visual aids, and ICAO's Aerodrome Design Manual Part 4 addresses visual aids in more engineering detail. The ICAO Store lists Annex 14 Volume I as the 9th Edition (July 2022), with Amendment 18 dated 4 August 2025 and later 2026 corrigenda.[9]

For Indian projects, the applicable DGCA requirements, AAI specifications, approved aerodrome procedures and project-specific technical documents also need to be considered. A strong company works from the actual project specification rather than simply stating that a product is "ICAO compliant."

11 · FAQ

Frequently Asked Questions

Which are the best airport lighting companies in India?
There is no reliable single "best company" ranking covering manufacturers, contractors, consultants and photometry providers. The appropriate company depends on whether the requirement is equipment supply, AGL installation, SITC, photometry, PAPI, maintenance or consultancy.
How many airport lighting companies are there in India?
There is no single authoritative government-maintained list of all airport lighting companies. The Ministry of Civil Aviation has stated that AGL manufacturing is undertaken by various private-sector entities and that it does not maintain overall manufacturing-capacity information.
What is the difference between an airport lighting manufacturer and an AGL contractor?
A manufacturer primarily produces equipment, while an AGL contractor installs, tests and commissions airport lighting systems. Some companies perform both roles.
Can one company handle complete airport lighting work?
Yes. Some companies offer integrated design, supply, installation, commissioning and maintenance services, while others specialize in particular components or services.
Do airport lighting companies provide PAPI?
Some AGL companies provide PAPI supply, installation, aiming, photometry and maintenance, while others may specialize in only one part of the PAPI lifecycle.
Do airport lighting companies provide photometry?
Some do. AAI procurement documents show that in-field AGL photometry is a distinct technical service, and specialist companies in India publicly advertise photometric testing.
What should I check before selecting an AGL contractor?
Check technical capability, relevant airport experience, testing and commissioning capability, photometry, PAPI expertise, manpower, airside safety procedures, documentation, spares and post-installation maintenance support.
Are airport lighting companies in India manufacturers or importers?
Both models exist. Some companies manufacture AGL equipment in India, while others supply or integrate equipment from domestic or international manufacturers. The Ministry of Civil Aviation confirms the presence of multiple private-sector AGL manufacturing entities.
