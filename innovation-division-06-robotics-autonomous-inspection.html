Robotics & Autonomous Inspection

The robotics, autonomous inspection and automated field-data collection layer of the AGL Innovation Hub. This division covers automated airfield inspection, drones, robotic AGL inspection, robotic maintenance, autonomous measurement vehicles, automated photometry and testing, robotic fixture and sign inspection, autonomous surveying, automated defect detection, emergency inspection and future autonomous AGL maintenance — using robotics, autonomous vehicles, drones and automated measurement systems to make AGL inspection faster, safer, more repeatable, data-driven and less dependent on manual field inspection.

Most Important Design Principle

Division 06 is much more than a "drone inspection" division. It is the Innovation Hub's physical inspection and measurement automation layer, connecting the real airfield to IoT → AI → Digital Twin → Asset Management → Predictive Maintenance.

Drone and robotic visual inspection should complement, rather than automatically replace, required ground inspections and technical measurements. Robotic maintenance should focus initially on low-risk, repetitive and clearly defined tasks, with progressively higher validation and human supervision required for more complex intervention.

Fully autonomous maintenance of safety-critical AGL equipment remains a future research and validation objective, not an assumed near-term deployment. Automated Photometry is the division's flagship application given the specialist focus on AGL photometry, connecting Division 06 robotics with Division 14 advanced photometry and Division 03 AI.

Division 06 physically collects the data that Divisions 03, 04, 05, 14 and 15 turn into digital intelligence, compliance assessment and validated engineering decisions.
