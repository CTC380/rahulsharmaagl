FAQ

Frequently Asked Questions

Is the course suitable for beginners?

Yes. Micro Courses and the early modules of every flagship program start from airport and AGL fundamentals, so no prior background is assumed.

Do I need airport experience?

No. Programs are structured so electrical engineers, fresh graduates and career-switchers can build airport-specific knowledge from the ground up.

Are the courses online or classroom-based?

Micro, Professional and Specialist/Diploma programs are delivered online with instructor-led guidance. Hands-On & On-Site programs are in-person, on-site workshops.

How are assignments submitted?

Assignments and the capstone project are submitted digitally for instructor review as part of the Master Diploma and specialist programs.

Is there a final examination?

Yes, for the Master Diploma and each flagship specialist program. Micro Courses use short practical assessments instead of formal exams.

What certificate will I receive?

A Precision AGL Academy certificate of completion specific to the program you finish. These are Academy-issued certificates — not government, university or ICAO/FAA accreditations.

How long will I have access to the course?

Course access is provided for 12 months from the date of enrollment, unless a different duration is stated for a specific program at the time of enrollment.

Are ICAO and FAA standards covered?

Yes, for educational reference. Always verify project work against the latest officially published ICAO, FAA and national regulatory documents — see the Standards Disclaimer below.

Are practical sessions included?

Yes. Most programs include practical exercises, calculation assignments and case studies; Hands-On & On-Site programs add live field work.

Can companies enroll multiple employees?

Yes — see the Corporate AGL Certification Program, or contact the Academy to arrange group enrollment on any other program.

Is GST included in the displayed price?

Displayed fees exclude GST and other applicable taxes unless the program page states otherwise (see Terms & Conditions, Section 3). The Academy team will confirm the final amount at enrollment.

Do you provide placement assistance?

The Academy focuses on building job-relevant technical skills. It does not guarantee placement, employment or promotion; contact the team to ask about any current career guidance.

Standards Disclaimer: Course content is provided for educational and professional development purposes. Applicable ICAO, FAA, national regulatory requirements, airport specifications, and project-specific requirements should always be verified against the latest officially published documents.
