Isolation Transformer Testing & Commissioning

Every AGL fixture on a series circuit is isolated from the CCR by its own isolation transformer — hundreds of them per circuit, most buried, submerged, or embedded in-pavement. Division 04 verifies that each transformer is correctly identified, correctly rated, physically sound, properly installed, correctly connected on both primary and secondary sides, electrically healthy, free from unacceptable water ingress or mechanical defects, and capable of delivering the required secondary output under the applicable load condition.

Philosophy: a transformer is evaluated as a complete chain — correct transformer → correct rating → correct installation → healthy winding → healthy insulation → correct primary connection → correct secondary connection → correct connector → correct cable lead → correct load → correct output → accepted transformer. Division 02 covers the general electrical testing framework; Division 04 is the dedicated transformer-specific expansion of that scope.

Before Testing Begins

Two conditions should be established before electrical testing of any isolation transformer begins.

Numerical Limits Are Not Universal

  • Test voltages for insulation resistance testing come from the approved manufacturer/project procedure
  • Insulation resistance acceptance limits are transformer- and design-specific — never apply an arbitrary universal figure
  • Winding resistance and voltage-ratio tolerances depend on transformer design and test conditions
  • Output and loading acceptance values are taken from the applicable transformer/project documentation

Division Boundary

  • Division 02 performs the general electrical testing framework across the AGL system
  • Division 04 provides the dedicated, transformer-specific verification and commissioning scope
  • Connector identification, continuity, contact, mating, locking, and sealing also sit within the broader Cable & Connector Testing division
  • No transformer is released for AGL circuit commissioning until every applicable item in this division is closed

The 21 Topics

A

Identification & Inspection

3 topics
TC-ITX-001Transformer Visual Inspection
Verification Checks
  • Verify transformer identification and overall physical condition
  • Inspect housing condition, bushings/terminals where applicable, cable leads, and connector assemblies
  • Inspect mounting arrangement, sealing, cable entry, and protective components
  • Inspect earthing/bonding arrangement
  • Check for signs of corrosion, impact damage, overheating, water ingress, contamination, or mechanical stress
  • Check specifically for cracks, broken housing, loose parts, damaged leads/connectors, missing seals, or missing identification
  • Record result as Acceptable / Not Acceptable
Hold Point

Hold Point: any major physical defect is corrected before proceeding to energized testing.

TC-ITX-002Transformer Rating Verification
Verification Checks
  • Verify transformer type, rated input, rated output, and rated current
  • Verify rated frequency, power rating, and insulation/environmental rating where applicable
  • Verify manufacturer and model against the approved design
  • Cross-check: approved design → BOQ/specification → transformer datasheet → nameplate → installed unit
Hold Point

Hold Point: any mismatch is treated as a technical discrepancy and resolved before acceptance.

TC-ITX-003Nameplate Verification
Verification Checks
  • Record manufacturer, model, serial number, and rating from the physical nameplate
  • Record primary rating, secondary rating, and frequency
  • Record insulation information and manufacturing information where provided
  • Verify the physical nameplate matches the approved equipment schedule, project documentation, equipment datasheet, and asset register
Acceptance / Record

Acceptance Criteria: nameplate data is fully legible and consistent across every reference document.

B

Winding & Electrical Integrity

6 topics
TC-ITX-004Primary Winding Verification
Verification Checks
  • Verify primary terminals and primary identification
  • Verify primary cable connection and winding continuity
  • Verify insulation condition and correct connection
  • Confirm no open circuit and no unintended short circuit
  • Record transformer ID, primary terminal identification, test method, measurement, acceptance criterion, and result
Acceptance / Record

Acceptance Criteria: the primary winding is continuous, correctly connected, and free of open- or short-circuit condition.

TC-ITX-005Secondary Winding Verification
Verification Checks
  • Verify secondary terminals, secondary identification, and secondary cable/lead
  • Verify continuity and insulation where applicable
  • Verify connector arrangement and correct connection
  • Confirm no open circuit and no unintended short circuit
  • Cross-check: transformer secondary → secondary lead → connector → fixture circuit
Acceptance / Record

Acceptance Criteria: the secondary winding is continuous and traceable through the lead and connector to the correct fixture circuit.

TC-ITX-006Insulation Resistance Test
Verification Checks
  • Verify primary insulation and secondary insulation
  • Verify primary-to-earth and secondary-to-earth insulation where applicable
  • Verify primary-to-secondary insulation where applicable
  • Identify and isolate the transformer, and confirm absence of voltage before testing
  • Disconnect equipment where required, and select an approved insulation tester
  • Apply the specified test voltage and record insulation resistance
  • Compare against the approved acceptance criterion, then discharge and restore connections
Hold Point

Hold Point: test voltage and acceptance limits must come from the approved transformer/manufacturer procedure, not an arbitrary universal figure.

TC-ITX-007Continuity Test
Verification Checks
  • Verify primary winding continuity and secondary winding continuity
  • Verify primary connection and secondary connection
  • Verify cable leads where included in the test boundary
  • Investigate any open winding, loose terminal, poor connector, damaged cable lead, or unexpected resistance
Acceptance / Record

Acceptance Criteria: continuity is confirmed through every winding and connection within the test boundary, with no unexplained resistance.

TC-ITX-008Winding Resistance Verification Where Applicable
Verification Checks
  • Record transformer ID, winding tested, and test temperature/conditions where required
  • Measure resistance using the specified instrument
  • Record measured resistance against the applicable manufacturer/project acceptance criterion
  • Compare the measured value and record the result
Hold Point

Hold Point: do not create an arbitrary universal resistance limit — the acceptable value depends on transformer design and test conditions.

TC-ITX-009Voltage-Ratio Verification Where Applicable
Verification Checks
  • Verify primary voltage and secondary voltage
  • Calculate voltage ratio and compare against the applicable tolerance
  • Verify correct winding configuration
  • Record transformer ID, primary voltage, secondary voltage, ratio, required ratio (manufacturer/project), and result
Hold Point

Hold Point: this test is performed only using an approved method appropriate to the specific transformer design.

C

Connector & Cable Lead

2 topics
TC-ITX-010Connector Verification
Verification Checks
  • Inspect correct connector type, identification, and correct transformer connection
  • Inspect proper mating, locking, and sealing
  • Inspect correct cable lead, physical condition, and absence of contamination or moisture
  • Verify continuity, contact integrity, and correct connection electrically where applicable
Acceptance / Record

Acceptance Criteria: connectors mate, lock, and seal correctly with confirmed electrical continuity and contact integrity — consistent with the broader Cable & Connector Testing division.

TC-ITX-011Cable-Lead Verification
Verification Checks
  • Verify cable-lead identification, correct cable type, and correct cable size where applicable
  • Verify correct termination, correct connector, and correct polarity where applicable
  • Verify mechanical condition and insulation condition, with no exposed conductor
  • Verify proper routing and proper strain relief
  • Confirm traceability: transformer → cable lead → connector → fixture
Acceptance / Record

Acceptance Criteria: every cable lead is correctly identified, terminated, and mechanically sound along its full traceable path.

D

Housing & Mechanical Condition

3 topics
TC-ITX-012Housing Inspection
Verification Checks
  • Verify housing integrity — cracks, damage, deformation, and corrosion
  • Verify seals, covers, cable-entry points, and connector-entry points
  • Verify mounting points and protective coating
  • Verify drainage arrangements where applicable
Acceptance / Record

Acceptance Criteria: the housing provides the intended physical and environmental protection.

TC-ITX-013Water-Ingress Inspection
Verification Checks
  • Inspect for water inside housing, moisture, and condensation
  • Inspect for corrosion caused by moisture and damaged seals
  • Inspect for improper cable entry, open glands, and damaged connector seals
  • Inspect for standing water and evidence of previous water ingress
  • Where found: record the condition, determine the extent, inspect affected components, follow the manufacturer-approved corrective procedure, perform required electrical retesting, and record the corrective action
Hold Point

Hold Point: do not simply dry and accept a transformer found with water ingress — the full corrective and retest sequence must be completed and recorded.

TC-ITX-014Mechanical Condition Verification
Verification Checks
  • Check mounting, supports, bolts, nuts, and brackets
  • Check housing, cable entries, connector support, and earthing connection
  • Check mechanical stability
  • Confirm no loose mounting, cracked housing, excessive vibration risk, mechanical strain on cables, unsupported cable leads, or damaged connectors
Acceptance / Record

Acceptance Criteria: the transformer and its cable leads are mechanically stable with no strain, looseness, or unsupported components.

E

Installation & Connections

3 topics
TC-ITX-015Transformer Installation Verification
Verification Checks
  • Verify correct location, orientation where applicable, and correct mounting
  • Verify correct depth/position where applicable and cable route
  • Verify primary connection, secondary connection, and connector arrangement
  • Verify drainage/environmental arrangement, identification, and earthing/bonding
  • Verify accessibility where required
  • Cross-check: physical transformer location ↔ approved drawing ↔ transformer schedule ↔ asset identification
Acceptance / Record

Acceptance Criteria: the installed transformer matches the approved design, drawing, and schedule in every respect.

TC-ITX-016Primary Circuit Connection Verification
Verification Checks
  • Verify correct primary cable and correct transformer
  • Verify correct connector/termination and correct terminal
  • Verify cable identification, continuity, and insulation
  • Verify mechanical connection and circuit identification
  • Trace: CCR → primary series circuit → transformer primary
Hold Point

Hold Point: any uncertainty in circuit identity is resolved before energization.

TC-ITX-017Secondary Circuit Connection Verification
Verification Checks
  • Verify correct transformer and correct secondary cable/lead
  • Verify correct connector, correct fixture, and correct terminal
  • Verify continuity, insulation where applicable, and mechanical security
  • Verify identification
  • Trace: transformer secondary → secondary lead → connector → fixture
Acceptance / Record

Acceptance Criteria: the secondary side is fully traceable and correctly connected from transformer to the intended fixture.

F

Loading & Output

2 topics
TC-ITX-018Transformer Loading Verification
Verification Checks
  • Verify connected load and fixture/equipment rating against transformer rating
  • Verify number of connected loads where applicable
  • Verify operating condition and loading condition
  • Confirm the transformer is not subjected to an operating condition outside its approved rating
  • Record transformer ID, connected load, operating condition, measured parameters, applicable rating, and result
Acceptance / Record

Acceptance Criteria: the connected load is within the transformer's approved rating under the tested operating condition.

TC-ITX-019Transformer Output Verification
Verification Checks
  • Verify secondary voltage where applicable and output condition
  • Verify load response and correct secondary connection
  • Verify correct connected equipment response
  • Confirm output corresponds to the transformer specification and the applicable circuit operating condition
Hold Point

Hold Point: the exact output acceptance value must be taken from the applicable transformer/project documentation, not assumed.

G

Acceptance & Closeout

2 topics
TC-ITX-020Transformer Acceptance Test
Verification Checks
  • Confirm visual inspection, rating, and nameplate are verified
  • Confirm primary and secondary winding verified, insulation resistance and continuity completed
  • Confirm winding resistance and voltage ratio completed where applicable
  • Confirm connector, cable lead, housing, water-ingress, and mechanical condition are satisfactory
  • Confirm installation, primary connection, and secondary connection are verified
  • Confirm loading and output are verified
  • Confirm all applicable results are accepted, punch points closed or formally dispositioned, and test records completed
  • Record final status: PASS, FAIL, RETEST, CONDITIONAL, or N/A
Acceptance / Record

Acceptance Criteria: the complete chain — identity, rating, physical condition, winding integrity, insulation, connections, mechanical installation, applicable loading and output — is verified and documented before release.

TC-ITX-021Transformer Test Report
Verification Checks
  • Record project information: airport, project, contract/package, location, client, contractor, testing organization
  • Record transformer identification: ID, manufacturer, model, serial number, rating, primary/secondary rating, installation location, associated fixture and circuit ID
  • Record test equipment: instrument, manufacturer, model, serial number, calibration certificate, calibration validity
  • Compile inspection results: visual condition, housing, water ingress, mechanical condition, installation, connector, cable lead
  • Compile electrical results: primary/secondary winding, insulation resistance, continuity, winding resistance and voltage ratio where applicable, primary/secondary connection, output, loading
  • Log deviations/corrective actions with location, corrective action, retest, and status
  • Record final acceptance status and obtain sign-off: tested by, checked by, contractor representative, client/consultant representative, date
Acceptance / Record

Acceptance Criteria: each transformer has an individual, traceable test report — this closes Division 04 for that unit and, once all units are closed, releases the circuit for AGL fixture/circuit commissioning.
