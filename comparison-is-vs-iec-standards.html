Standards & Electrical Systems Comparison

IS VS IEC

Indian Standard vs International Electrotechnical Commission Standard — IS standards are issued by the Bureau of Indian Standards (BIS) for use within India, while IEC standards are developed internationally to harmonise electrical and electronic technical requirements. An IS standard may be identical to, based on, or adapted from an IEC standard, but the applicable edition and any national modifications must always be verified before assuming equivalence.

02 · Definitions

What Is an IS & IEC Standard?

IS

IS stands for Indian Standard, developed or adopted by the Bureau of Indian Standards. IS standards can be developed specifically for India, or they can be adoptions and modifications of international standards, including IEC standards. IS and IEC numbers should never be assumed to be interchangeable merely because their subjects are similar.

Typical relationship

  • IEC standard as international baseline
  • Indian adoption / modification by BIS
  • Resulting IS standard
  • Applied to the Indian market

IEC

IEC develops international standards for electrical, electronic and related technologies. For AGL specifically, IEC 61823 (series transformers for aerodrome lighting constant current circuits) is a leading example directly concerned with electrical installations for lighting and beaconing of aerodromes.

Typical structure

  • Design requirements
  • Construction
  • Performance
  • Testing & safety
03 · Key Difference

National Implementation vs International Baseline

The easiest way to remember the difference between IS and IEC:

IS

Indian national implementation

IEC

International technical baseline

Engineering point: Not every IS standard is derived from IEC, and not every IEC standard has an equivalent IS standard. Always establish the actual relationship rather than assuming one.

04 · AGL Example

AGL Isolation Transformer — Which Standard Applies?

For an AGL isolation transformer, the applicable international standard may be IEC 61823. If an Indian project specification calls for IEC 61823, the manufacturer should demonstrate compliance with that exact IEC standard and edition. If the project instead specifies an applicable IS standard, the manufacturer must demonstrate compliance with that IS requirement instead.

Do not substitute one for the other without an approved technical basis — the project specification determines which standard is contractually applicable.

05 · IS Adoption of IEC

When the Relationship Is Close

Sometimes an Indian Standard is a close adoption of an IEC standard by BIS. Even so, there can be:

  • National deviations
  • Additional Indian requirements
  • Different referenced standards
  • Different editions
  • Different test requirements
  • Indian environmental/application conditions

Always check the foreword, scope and referenced standards of the IS document before assuming equivalence with an IEC standard.

06 · Electrical Testing

Testing Considerations

Both IS and IEC standards can specify similar categories of test:

  • Insulation resistance
  • Dielectric withstand
  • Temperature rise
  • Leakage
  • Short-circuit performance
  • Mechanical tests
  • Environmental tests
  • Functional tests

Test voltage, duration, acceptance criteria and test method may differ. "Tested according to IEC" is not sufficient if the contract requires a particular IS standard.

07 · Cable Example

Applying the Difference to Cables

For an AGL cable, both IS and IEC cable standards may address conductor, insulation, sheath, voltage rating, temperature, mechanical properties and electrical tests. An IEC cable standard may specify the technical characteristics, while an IS cable standard may specify equivalent or modified requirements for the Indian market. For airport-specific cables, also check whether a project, airport, FAA or AGL-specific requirement applies on top of IS or IEC.

09 · Common Misconception

IS Standard Does Not Automatically Mean BIS Certification

Whether a product requires mandatory BIS certification depends on the applicable Indian regulatory/QCO framework and product category — an IS technical standard and a BIS conformity/certification requirement are related but not automatically the same thing.

Myth: An IS standard always requires mandatory BIS certification
Fact

Mandatory BIS certification depends on the applicable regulatory/QCO framework and product category, not on the existence of an IS standard alone.

Myth: IS and IEC are interchangeable whenever the subject matches
Fact

IS and IEC numbers should never be assumed interchangeable merely because their subjects are similar — the edition, scope and national deviations must be checked.

Myth: An IEC test report always satisfies an IS project requirement
Fact

If a project specifically requires an applicable IS standard, an IEC test report alone may not be sufficient unless the client/consultant accepts the substitution.

10 · Compliance Workflow

For an Indian AGL Project

A practical compliance structure runs from Indian regulatory requirements through DGCA and the airport/project specification, which then branches into applicable IS and IEC standards, feeding into manufacturer test reports and finally FAT/SAT.

Workflow: Indian regulatory requirements → DGCA → Airport/project spec → applicable IS and/or IEC standards → manufacturer test reports → FAT/SAT.

11 · Substitution Rules

Can IS Replace IEC — or IEC Replace IS?

Can IS replace IEC?

Sometimes — but only when permitted. An IS standard identical to or adopted from the applicable IEC standard may provide technical equivalence. But if the contract specifically states an IEC number, you should not simply substitute "equivalent IS standard" unless the client/consultant accepts it.

Can IEC replace IS?

Same principle in reverse. If an Indian project specifically requires the applicable IS standard, an IEC test report alone may not be sufficient. Establish whether the IS is an adoption of the IEC standard, whether editions correspond, whether Indian deviations exist, whether the project permits IEC in lieu of IS, and whether BIS certification is mandatory.

12 · Most Important Technical Point

Never Compare Only the Standard Number

Compare standard, edition, scope, normative references, test methods, acceptance criteria and national deviations. An IEC standard and an IS standard on the same technical subject may look identical at a glance but can contain different requirements once the details are checked.

14 · Engineering Verdict

Which Standard Applies Where?

Engineering Verdict

IS standards are Indian national implementations issued by BIS, while IEC standards are the international technical baseline developed by the IEC. An IS standard may be identical to, based on, or adapted from an IEC standard, but this must never be assumed without checking.

The final compliance basis should always be established from the exact editions, scopes and national deviations referenced in the project specification — not from a generic comparison alone.

15 · FAQ

Frequently Asked Questions

Is an IS standard the same as the equivalent IEC standard?

Not necessarily. An IS standard may be identical to, based on, or adapted from an IEC standard, but the applicable edition and any national modifications must always be checked.

Does an IS standard always require BIS certification?

No. Mandatory BIS certification depends on the applicable Indian regulatory/QCO framework and product category, not on the existence of an IS standard alone.

Can I submit an IEC test report if the project asks for an IS standard?

Only if the IS is confirmed as an adoption of that IEC standard, with matching editions and no relevant Indian deviations, and the client/consultant accepts the substitution.

What is the key IEC standard for AGL series transformers?

IEC 61823 — Electrical installations for lighting and beaconing of aerodromes — Series transformers for constant current circuits.

Who issues IS standards and who issues IEC standards?

IS standards are issued by the Bureau of Indian Standards (BIS). IEC standards are developed by the International Electrotechnical Commission.

What should I check before treating an IS and IEC standard as equivalent?

The edition, scope, normative references, test methods, acceptance criteria and any national deviations stated in the IS document's foreword.
