Prototype Development & Engineering Laboratory

Division 18 is the experimental development, validation and prototype-to-product engine of the AGL Innovation Hub. It takes the materials, technologies and engineering concepts released by the earlier divisions and moves them through a controlled pathway — Idea → Concept → Prototype → Laboratory Validation → Field Prototype → Pilot → Product → Deployment — so that every innovative AGL concept is technically demonstrated, tested, documented, improved and progressively matured before it is considered for operational adoption.

Most Important Design Principle

Division 18 should remain the physical and experimental engine of the AGL Innovation Hub, not the strategy or intelligence layer. It does not decide what to innovate — that comes from Division 01 (Innovation Strategy) and Division 08 (AGL Product Innovation) — and it does not define the materials or manufacturing technologies — that comes from Division 17 (New Materials & Manufacturing). Instead, Division 18 takes those inputs and proves, through a controlled and traceable pathway, whether the innovation can actually become a usable, deployable AGL product.

Its strongest connections are to Division 14 (Advanced Photometry), which anchors the optical-testing bridge, and to Division 16 (Safety & Reliability), which anchors the environmental, electrical, mechanical and reliability testing framework. The Prototype Failure Database also connects directly to the hub's wider Failure Records, Evidence & Intelligence framework and the AGL Failure Database.

Failure of a prototype is not failure of innovation — every unsuccessful test becomes engineering knowledge that feeds the next prototype iteration, closing the loop from idea to validated, deployable AGL technology.
