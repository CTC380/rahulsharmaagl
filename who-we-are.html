Our Purpose

Supporting Safer, More Reliable Airfield Lighting

Airfield Ground Lighting is a critical part of airport visual guidance and aerodrome operations. Every element — runway and taxiway lights, PAPI systems, circuits, isolating transformers, CCRs, control systems and visual aids — must work together as an integrated system. Precision AGL Solutions exists to bring greater technical clarity and practical engineering support to this field, helping aviation teams:

Understand AGL systems clearly
Design them correctly
Test their performance systematically
Maintain them effectively
Document them properly
Improve them continuously
What We Do

From AGL Design to Field Performance

Our Technical Foundation

AGL Is More Than Lighting

Our technical focus extends across the complete AGL system rather than individual fixtures alone. This systems-level approach helps connect engineering, installation, testing, operation and maintenance instead of treating each component in isolation.

Runway Lighting

Edge, centreline, touchdown zone and associated runway visual aids.

Taxiway Lighting

Centreline, edge, stop bar, runway guard and related taxiway applications.

Approach Lighting

Approach systems, threshold and runway-end visual aids and associated infrastructure.

PAPI Systems

Precision Approach Path Indicator systems, alignment, calibration and photometric verification.

AGL Electrical Infrastructure

Constant Current Regulators, series circuits, isolating transformers, primary/secondary cabling and associated connectors.

Control & Monitoring

ALCMS, CCR monitoring, PLC/SCADA interfaces and operational control concepts.

Airport Visual Aids

Airfield guidance signs, mandatory signs, information signs and related visual guidance systems.

Systems-Level Approach

Connecting engineering, installation, testing, operation and maintenance as one integrated discipline.
