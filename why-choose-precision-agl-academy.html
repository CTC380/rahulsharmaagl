WHY CHOOSE PRECISION AGL ACADEMY FOR YOUR AGL CAREER?

Airfield Ground Lighting (AGL) is a specialised airport engineering field spanning electrical systems, lighting technology, photometry, installation, testing, commissioning, maintenance and regulatory requirements. Learning individual components is not enough. You need to understand how the complete system works.

10 · Systems Thinking

Learning Beyond Individual Components

Knowing what a component is does not automatically mean understanding AGL. Knowing the definition of an isolating transformer is only the beginning.

Precision AGL Academy connects component knowledge with system knowledge.

An AGL professional also understands
  • Why it is required and where it is installed
  • How it connects to the series circuit
  • How its rating is selected
  • How primary and secondary sides work
  • How it is tested
  • What failures can occur, and how a fault affects the circuit
  • How the fault can be identified

14 · FAQ

Frequently Asked Questions

Q1What is Airfield Ground Lighting training?
Airfield Ground Lighting training is specialised learning focused on airport lighting systems, including runway and taxiway lighting, approach lighting, PAPI, signs, CCRs, series circuits, isolating transformers, testing, photometry, commissioning and maintenance.
Q2Who can learn Airfield Ground Lighting?
Students, engineers, technicians, contractors, consultants, airport professionals and existing AGL professionals can develop their knowledge through structured AGL training.
Q3What does AGL training cover?
AGL training can cover airport visual aids, runway and taxiway lighting, electrical systems, CCRs, series circuits, transformers, cables, connectors, photometry, testing, commissioning, troubleshooting and applicable technical requirements.
Q4Why is AGL photometry important?
Photometry helps verify the optical performance of airport lighting, including applicable intensity, colour, alignment, coverage and uniformity requirements.
Q5Is AGL a specialised field?
Yes. AGL combines airport operations, electrical engineering, lighting technology, optics, photometry, installation, testing, maintenance and regulatory requirements.
Q6Can AGL training help with airport projects?
Practical AGL training can help professionals understand the technical activities involved in airport lighting projects, including installation, testing, commissioning, troubleshooting, documentation and compliance.
Q7What makes Precision AGL Academy different?
Precision AGL Academy is focused specifically on Airfield Ground Lighting and connects theoretical concepts with practical systems, testing, photometry, troubleshooting, commissioning and project applications.
