Division 02 of 25 · INS-RWY Series

Runway Lighting Inspection

A standardized methodology for assessing the operational, photometric, electrical, mechanical, geometric, and physical condition of runway lighting and associated components — the largest division in the handbook, reflecting runway lighting's status as the most critical AGL asset group. Covers edge, threshold/end, centreline, touchdown-zone, and guard lighting, plus fixture mechanics, optics, LED/lamp condition, cable/connector interfaces, cleanliness, geometry, photometric condition, and defect recording.

Initial Issue

Important control — A visual runway lighting inspection must not be treated as a substitute for formal photometric testing, electrical testing, insulation-resistance testing, circuit testing, CCR testing, or scheduled preventive maintenance. Where an inspection identifies a condition that cannot be confirmed visually, the condition should be escalated for the appropriate technical test or engineering assessment.

Frequently Asked Questions

Quick answers about runway lighting inspection in the AGL Inspection Handbook.

Q1What is Division 02 — Runway Lighting Inspection in the AGL Inspection Handbook?

Division 02 is the handbook's largest division, with 76 published topics in 14 sections (A–N). It gives a standardized methodology for assessing the operational, photometric, electrical, mechanical, geometric and physical condition of runway lighting and associated components, reflecting runway lighting's status as the most critical AGL asset group.

Q2Which runway lighting types and areas does Division 02 cover?

It covers runway edge, threshold and runway end, centreline, touchdown zone and guard lighting, plus general system inspection, fixture mechanical condition, optical and lens condition, LED/lamp condition, cable, connector and electrical interface, cleanliness and environmental condition, geometry and installation, photometric condition, and defect classification and recording.

Q3What parameters is each runway fixture assessed against?

Every applicable fixture is assessed against a common checklist that includes operational status, colour, illumination, alignment, orientation, location, spacing, elevation, mounting, frangible coupling, lens/prism, LED/lamp, connector, secondary lead, base can, cable entry, water ingress, cleanliness, mechanical condition and photometric condition. Each parameter has defined status values, such as Correct / Abnormal or Normal / Reduced / Abnormal.

Q4What is the standard runway lighting inspection workflow?

The sequence is review layout, identify fixture, inspect physical condition, verify operation, check geometry, check optical condition, check electrical condition and assess photometric condition. It then continues through classify, record, escalate, correct, verify and close.

Q5How are runway lighting defects classified?

Defect status is recorded using the four-level scheme Normal, Defective, Unserviceable and Immediate Action Required, so every observation ends with a clear classification before it is recorded and escalated.

Q6Does Division 02 include photometric condition?

Yes. Section M is dedicated to photometric condition, and the parameter checklist includes a photometric condition status of Acceptable or Deficient, alongside illumination and colour checks.

Q7Can a visual runway lighting inspection replace formal testing?

No. A visual runway lighting inspection must not be treated as a substitute for formal photometric testing, electrical testing, insulation-resistance testing, circuit testing, CCR testing or scheduled preventive maintenance. Where a condition cannot be confirmed visually, it should be escalated for the appropriate technical test or engineering assessment.

Q8Do airport procedures take precedence over this handbook?

Yes. This handbook is an uncontrolled educational reference, not an approved procedure. The airport's approved inspection schedule, SMS procedures, ATC instructions, NOTAM procedures and local aerodrome regulations always take precedence, and the current revision of any official document should be verified before use.

Q9Can I download a printable checklist for each inspection topic?

Yes. Each inspection topic has a Download as Word option that produces an uncontrolled Word checklist template (description, a tick-box inspection table and an inspector sign-off block) for personal reference and internal training, to be reviewed and adopted under the user’s own document control.
