Division 13 · EBT · Protective Earth & Equipotential Bonding

Earthing & Bonding Testing

Division 13 verifies that the AGL earthing and bonding system is correctly installed, electrically continuous, mechanically secure and capable of providing the intended fault-current, equipment-protection and surge/lightning-protection paths before system acceptance. It establishes a complete verification chain from the earth electrode/earth pit through the earthing conductors, equipment bonds and protective systems — covering the CCR, panels, transformers, signs and AGL fixtures.

Philosophy: a good earth-pit resistance value does not by itself prove that every AGL component is correctly earthed and bonded, and an equipment earth-continuity result does not by itself demonstrate that the earth electrode/system meets its required performance. The complete chain — earth electrode → earth conductor → earth bar/system → equipment earth → bonding → earth continuity → earth resistance → lightning protection → surge protection → test-result evaluation → acceptance — has to hold together. Division 13 is the dedicated protective-earth and equipotential-bonding verification division, while individual equipment electrical tests remain in their respective commissioning divisions.

Chain of Acceptance

The earthing and bonding system is evaluated as a complete chain — every link has to hold before the system is accepted.

LinkWhat It Confirms
Earth ElectrodeThe physical earth pit and electrode are correctly constructed, connected and in sound condition
Earth ConductorThe conductor from electrode to earth bar is correctly sized, routed, identified and terminated
Earth Bar/SystemThe earth bar/terminal correctly collects and distributes the earthing system to connected equipment
Equipment EarthCCR, panels, transformers, signs and fixtures are each correctly connected to the earthing system
BondingMetallic components that must remain at the same potential are correctly bonded together
Earth ContinuityA continuous, low-resistance earth path exists from equipment to the earth bar, with no open circuit
Earth ResistanceThe earth pit/electrode/system resistance meets the approved project requirement
Lightning ProtectionAir terminals, down conductors and earth connections function as an integrated protective system
Surge ProtectionSPDs are correctly installed with a low-impedance earth/bonding path for the intended surge current
Test-Result EvaluationEvery result is technically reviewed against approved design, standards and manufacturer requirements
AcceptanceThe complete earthing and bonding system is formally accepted and documented

Before Testing Begins

Two conditions should be established before earthing and bonding verification of any AGL component begins.

Acceptance Criteria Are Not Universal

  • Earth continuity and earth resistance acceptance criteria come from the applicable project specification, design and equipment requirements — never an assumed universal value
  • Earth-resistance results can be influenced by parallel earth paths, soil conditions, electrode arrangement and test configuration
  • The test report should record the test method and test conditions, not just the resistance value
  • Before testing: confirm the approved test method, identify parallel earth paths, determine whether disconnection is required, and ensure safe isolation

Division Boundary

  • Division 13 is the dedicated protective-earth and equipotential-bonding verification division for the AGL system
  • Individual equipment electrical tests remain in their respective commissioning divisions — CCR in Division 03, isolation transformers in Division 04, general electrical testing in Division 02
  • CCR, panel, transformer, sign and fixture earthing should be verified both physically and electrically
  • Lightning protection is assessed as an integrated system, not merely as an individual earth connection

A

Earthing System Verification

4 topics
TC-EBT-001Earth-Pit Verification
Verification Checks
  • Verify earth-pit identification, location and accessibility
  • Verify earth-pit construction, electrode connection and conductor connection
  • Verify inspection chamber/cover and mechanical condition
  • Verify protection against physical damage and drainage/condition where applicable
  • Inspect for broken cover, damaged chamber, corrosion, loose connections and exposed conductor
  • Inspect for water accumulation where undesirable, unauthorized modification and poor accessibility
  • Cross-check: approved earthing layout → earth-pit schedule → physical earth pit
Acceptance / Record

Acceptance Criteria: each earth pit is correctly identified, accessible, physically sound and matches the approved earthing layout and schedule.

TC-EBT-002Earth-Electrode Verification
Verification Checks
  • Verify electrode type, size and material
  • Verify installation arrangement and depth/installation configuration where specified
  • Verify connection to earth conductor and mechanical integrity
  • Verify corrosion condition
  • Check for wrong electrode, damaged electrode, corroded connection, inadequate connection or incorrect installation
  • Cross-check: approved earthing design → earth-electrode specification → installed electrode
Hold Point

Hold Point: any mismatch against the approved earthing design or electrode specification is resolved before acceptance.

TC-EBT-003Earth-Conductor Verification
Verification Checks
  • Verify conductor type, size, material and route
  • Verify identification, termination and mechanical protection
  • Verify connection to electrode and connection to equipment
  • Trace: earth electrode → earth conductor → earth bar/terminal → equipment
  • Check for broken conductor, loose connection, corrosion and incorrect conductor size
  • Check for poor termination, mechanical damage and unidentified conductor
Acceptance / Record

Acceptance Criteria: the earth conductor is correctly sized, routed, identified and soundly terminated at both the electrode and the equipment end.

TC-EBT-004Equipment-Earth Verification
Verification Checks
  • Verify earth terminal, earth conductor and connection integrity for each item of equipment
  • Verify mechanical tightness, continuity and identification
  • Applicable equipment includes CCR, panels, transformers, PAPI equipment, airfield signs, AGL fixtures where applicable, control equipment, auxiliary equipment and metallic enclosures
  • Check: equipment enclosure → earth conductor → earth system
Acceptance / Record

Acceptance Criteria: every applicable item of AGL equipment is correctly and securely connected to the earthing system, with continuity confirmed.

B

Bonding Verification

4 topics
TC-EBT-005Bonding Verification
Verification Checks
  • Verify bonding conductor, bonding connection and bonding points
  • Verify mechanical integrity, corrosion condition and electrical continuity
  • Check for missing bond, loose bond, broken bonding conductor and corroded bonding point
  • Check for painted/non-conductive connection surfaces where inappropriate
Acceptance / Record

Acceptance Criteria: bonding establishes electrical continuity between conductive parts that need to remain at substantially the same electrical potential.

TC-EBT-006Fixture Bonding Verification
Verification Checks
  • Verify fixture earth/bonding connection and base/can bonding where applicable
  • Verify metallic mounting arrangement, bonding conductor and connection point
  • Verify continuity for applicable AGL fixtures and associated metallic structures
  • Inspect for missing bond, loose bond, corrosion, broken conductor and poor contact
Acceptance / Record

Acceptance Criteria: each applicable fixture and its associated metallic structure is soundly bonded with continuity confirmed and logged by fixture ID.

TC-EBT-007Transformer Bonding Verification
Verification Checks
  • Verify transformer housing earth/bond connection and associated metallic enclosure
  • Verify cable-entry arrangement, bonding conductor and connection integrity
  • Check for missing bond, loose connection, corrosion, damaged conductor and poor contact
  • Verify continuity: transformer housing → bonding conductor → earthing system
Acceptance / Record

Acceptance Criteria: applicable isolation transformers and their associated metallic components are soundly bonded to the earthing system.

TC-EBT-008Sign Bonding Verification
Verification Checks
  • Verify sign enclosure, sign support and metallic structure bonding where applicable
  • Verify bonding conductor, earth connection and continuity
  • Check for missing bond, broken conductor, corrosion, loose connection and poor bonding contact
  • Trace: sign structure → bonding connection → earth system
Acceptance / Record

Acceptance Criteria: airfield signs and their associated metallic structures are soundly bonded to the earth system where applicable.

C

CCR & Panel Earth

2 topics
TC-EBT-009CCR Earth Verification
Verification Checks
  • Verify CCR earth terminal, CCR enclosure and earth conductor
  • Verify earth bar, connection to site earthing system and bonding
  • Verify continuity
  • Inspect for loose terminal, corrosion, damaged conductor, missing earth connection and improper termination
Hold Point

Hold Point: CCR earthing should be verified both physically and electrically before release.

TC-EBT-010Panel Earth Verification
Verification Checks
  • Verify panel enclosure, earth bar and protective earth conductor
  • Verify door bonding where required and metallic gland plate bonding where applicable
  • Verify internal bonding, earth termination and continuity
  • Check door bond present where required, earth bar securely connected, all required equipment earths terminated, and no loose or unidentified earth conductor
Acceptance / Record

Acceptance Criteria: AGL control, distribution and auxiliary panels are correctly and completely earthed, including door and gland-plate bonding where required.

D

Earthing & Bonding Testing

3 topics
TC-EBT-011Earth Continuity Test
Verification Checks
  • Test points may include equipment enclosure → earth bar, panel → earth bar, CCR → earth system, transformer → earth system, sign → earth system and fixture → earth/bonding system where applicable
  • Verify a continuous earth path, low-resistance connection, no open circuit and correct bonding path
  • Record equipment, test point, reference point, measured value, acceptance criterion and result
Hold Point

Hold Point: the acceptance criterion should come from the applicable project specification, design and equipment requirements — not from an assumed universal value.

TC-EBT-012Earth Resistance Test
Verification Checks
  • Measure earth-pit resistance, earth-electrode resistance and earthing-system resistance where applicable using the approved test method
  • Verify test conditions, test instrument and instrument calibration status
  • Before testing: confirm approved test method, identify parallel earth paths, determine whether disconnection is required, ensure safe isolation, and identify nearby metallic/earth connections that may affect the measurement
  • Record earth pit/system, test method, measured resistance, acceptance criterion and result
Hold Point

Hold Point: results can be influenced by parallel paths, soil conditions, electrode arrangement and test configuration — record the test method and conditions, not just the resistance value.

TC-EBT-013Bonding Continuity Test
Verification Checks
  • Verify fixture, transformer, sign, panel, CCR, structural, door and metallic enclosure bonding
  • Test principle: metallic component A → bonding connection → metallic component B
  • Check for open bond, high resistance, loose connection, corrosion and incorrect bonding point
  • Record bond ID, equipment A, equipment B, measured continuity/resistance, criterion and result
Acceptance / Record

Acceptance Criteria: every applicable bonding connection demonstrates continuity within the approved criterion, with no open or high-resistance bond.

E

Lightning & Surge Protection

2 topics
TC-EBT-014Lightning-Protection Verification
Verification Checks
  • Verify air terminals, down conductors and earth connections where applicable
  • Verify bonding, lightning-protection conductors, equipment protection and structural bonding
  • Verify connection to the earthing system
  • Inspect for broken conductor, loose connection, corrosion, incorrect routing, missing bond and damaged components
  • Where required, test the relevant lightning-protection conductor/bonding paths
Acceptance / Record

Acceptance Criteria: lightning protection is assessed as an integrated system, not merely as an individual earth connection.

TC-EBT-015Surge-Protection Verification
Verification Checks
  • Verify SPD type, location and correct connection
  • Verify line/earth connection as applicable, neutral/earth arrangement where applicable, and protective bonding
  • Verify status indicator, backup protection and connection integrity
  • Inspect for damaged SPD, fault indication, incorrect connection, loose terminal, missing earth connection and incorrect device rating/type
  • Verify: protected equipment → SPD → bonding/earth path
Hold Point

Hold Point: an SPD is only effective if its associated earthing/bonding path is correctly installed and low impedance for the intended surge current path.

F

Result Evaluation & Acceptance

2 topics
TC-EBT-016Earth-Test Result Evaluation
Verification Checks
  • Evaluate earth resistance, earth continuity, bonding continuity and lightning-protection continuity where tested
  • Evaluate equipment earth connections, SPD condition and physical installation
  • Compare against approved project requirements, earthing design, applicable standards/specifications, manufacturer requirements, approved testing procedure and previous test results where available
  • Identify excessive earth resistance, open earth path, high bonding resistance, missing bond, corroded connection, damaged conductor, SPD fault and lightning-protection defect
  • Classify each result: PASS, FAIL, RETEST REQUIRED or INVESTIGATION REQUIRED
Acceptance / Record

Acceptance Criteria: every earthing and bonding test result is technically reviewed against approved requirements before it is accepted.

TC-EBT-017Earthing Acceptance Test
Verification Checks
  • Confirm earth pits, electrodes, conductors and connections are verified, and earth resistance and continuity are tested
  • Confirm equipment, fixture, transformer, sign, CCR and panel bonding are verified, and bonding continuity is tested
  • Confirm lightning-protection arrangement, down conductors where applicable, earth connection and bonding are verified
  • Confirm SPD is installed, type/rating verified, connection verified, status verified and earth/bonding path verified
  • Confirm test results are evaluated, defects closed, retesting completed and test records completed
  • Record final status: earthing system accepted
Acceptance / Record

Acceptance Criteria: the complete earthing and bonding system — earth system, bonding, lightning protection and surge protection — is verified, tested, evaluated and formally accepted as ready for operation.

G

Documentation

1 topic
TC-EBT-018Earthing Test Report
Verification Checks
  • Record project information: airport, project, location, system/area, contractor, testing organization, test engineer, test date
  • Record earthing-system information: earth-pit ID, earth-electrode type, earth conductor, earth-pit location, associated equipment, earthing arrangement
  • Compile earth resistance results: earth pit ID, location, method, instrument, calibration due, resistance, criterion, result
  • Compile earth continuity results by equipment: CCR, panel, transformer, sign, fixture
  • Compile bonding results, lightning-protection results and surge-protection results
  • Log defects/corrective actions with observation, location, corrective action, retest and status
  • Record final acceptance status — PASS, FAIL, RETEST REQUIRED or CONDITIONAL — and obtain sign-off: tested by, checked by, electrical engineer, contractor representative, client/consultant, date
Acceptance / Record

Acceptance Criteria: a dedicated, traceable Earthing & Bonding Test Report is completed and signed off — this closes Division 13 and supports final system acceptance.

Earthing & Bonding Commissioning Flow

Division 13 runs as a single continuous sequence, from earth-pit verification through to the final Earthing & Bonding Test Report and system acceptance.

1
Pre-Commissioning Review

Confirm approved earthing layout, design and earth-pit schedule.

2
Earth-Pit & Electrode

Verify earth-pit construction and installed earth electrode.

3
Earth-Conductor Verification

Trace and verify the conductor from electrode to earth bar.

4
Equipment-Earth Verification

Verify each item of equipment is connected to the earth system.

5
Bonding Verification

Verify equipotential bonding between metallic components.

6
Fixture/Transformer/Sign/CCR/Panel

Verify bonding across each applicable equipment type.

7
Earth Continuity Test

Test the continuous earth path from equipment to earth bar.

8
Earth Resistance Test

Measure earth-pit/electrode/system resistance by the approved method.

9
Bonding Continuity Test

Test continuity across bonding connections.

10
Lightning & Surge Protection

Verify lightning-protection and surge-protection arrangements.

11
Result Evaluation & Acceptance

Evaluate results and complete the earthing acceptance test.

12
Test Report & Sign-Off

Complete the Earthing & Bonding Test Report and obtain sign-off.
