Product Overview

PAPI Mounting Stand & Frangible Coupling

What It Is

Precision AGL Solutions supplies PAPI mounting stands and associated hardware for new installations, replacement works and maintenance. A stand typically provides a rigid support that holds the light unit at its specified position while allowing the mechanical adjustments needed for commissioning and alignment.

Select the mounting system as part of the complete PAPI installation, considering height, longitudinal and lateral position, vertical adjustment, azimuth alignment, foundation interface, maintenance access and any frangibility requirements.

The Arrangement May Include

  • PAPI mounting stand or support frame
  • Equipment mounting brackets and adjustable interfaces
  • Base plates, anchor bolts and foundation hardware
  • Levelling and height adjustment
  • Horizontal alignment and vertical aiming interface
  • Mechanical locking hardware
  • Protective fasteners and fixings
  • Frangible coupling / breakaway interface where specified
  • Maintenance-access provisions

The exact configuration depends on the PAPI manufacturer, light-unit construction, installation geometry and project requirements.

Frangible Coupling

A Controlled, Predictable Breakaway Interface

What It Is

A frangible coupling is a mechanically controlled interface giving a predictable structural separation or breakaway characteristic as required by the applicable airfield installation design. Frangibility requirements concern reducing the hazard presented by certain structures or equipment supports in the event of an aircraft or vehicle impact.

PAPI light units stand in the runway strip and must be frangible under ICAO Annex 14 and FAA requirements, so the coupling is normally part of each mounting leg. Do not assume every PAPI uses an identical coupling; select and install the one approved for the equipment model as part of its structural configuration.

FAA AC 150/5345-28H Mounting Requirements

What the FAA Equipment Standard Requires of the Mounting

Mounting Legs

  • At least three adjustable legs for levelling, or two legs if equivalent rigidity and levelling capability are demonstrated
  • Levelling must cope with one side of the unit being up to 1 in (25 mm) higher or lower than the other
  • Each leg includes the housing mounting and level-adjusting hardware, a frangible coupling to FAA drawing C-6046 (or an equivalent part that passes the frangibility test) and a flange for a concrete pad
  • The installer supplies the 2-inch electrical metallic tubing

Limits and Qualification Tests

  • Light unit not higher than 40 in (1 m) at its minimum mounting height
  • Weight not more than 100 lb (45 kg) per light unit, or 150 lb (68 kg) with an integral power and control unit
  • Adjusting hardware vibration-resistant, so the optical system cannot move
  • Frangibility of the legs equal to the 2-inch coupling of drawing C-6046, per AC 150/5220-23A
  • Rigidity: after a 15 lb/ft² static load for 5 hours the beam pattern must stay within ¼ in (6 mm) at 20 ft (6 m)
  • Wind: 100 mph (161 km/h) without displacing the pattern beyond the rigidity limit

These are equipment-qualification requirements. Your PAPI manufacturer's drawings and the approved project design set the actual mounting dimensions, coupling and breakaway height.

Verification

Alignment Is Verified After Mounting, Not Assumed

After Installation or Re-aiming

The stand must hold the unit level and locked so the aiming angle does not drift. After installation, and after any impact, foundation work or re-aiming, verify the angular setting with approved instruments and record it. A new or relocated PAPI is also subject to flight inspection under the authority's requirements.

Frangibility

PAPI units must be frangible and mounted as low as their function allows (Annex 14, Vol. I, Chapters 5 and 9). FAA practice puts the breakaway point at or just above grade. The PAPI manufacturer's coupling drawing and the approved design set the exact point, so do not substitute a coupling.

FAQ

PAPI Mounting Stand & Frangible Coupling — Frequently Asked Questions

What is a PAPI mounting stand?

A PAPI mounting stand is a rigid support that holds the light unit at its specified position while allowing the mechanical adjustments needed for commissioning and alignment. The arrangement may include a support frame, equipment brackets, base plates and anchor bolts, levelling and height adjustment, horizontal alignment and vertical aiming interfaces, locking hardware and, where specified, a frangible coupling. The exact configuration depends on the PAPI manufacturer, light-unit construction and project requirements.

Why does the mounting arrangement matter for PAPI performance?

The light unit must remain in a precisely defined position and orientation to give the intended visual approach guidance. A mechanically unstable or poorly configured assembly can introduce movement, misalignment or difficulty during periodic inspection. A suitable arrangement gives stable support, controlled installation height, accurate positioning, adequate adjustment, secure locking, resistance to vibration, environmental durability and access for inspection and maintenance.

What is a frangible coupling and do PAPI units need one?

A frangible coupling is a mechanically controlled interface giving a predictable breakaway characteristic, reducing the hazard of an aircraft or vehicle impact. PAPI light units stand in the runway strip and must be frangible under ICAO Annex 14 and FAA requirements, so the coupling is normally part of each mounting leg. Do not assume every PAPI uses an identical coupling; select and install the one approved for the equipment model.

Where is the breakaway point of a PAPI mounting?

PAPI units must be frangible and mounted as low as their function allows (Annex 14, Vol. I, Chapters 5 and 9), and FAA practice puts the breakaway point at or just above grade. The PAPI manufacturer's coupling drawing and the approved design set the exact point, so do not substitute a coupling or replace it with an unrelated breakaway component without confirming compatibility with the complete installation design.

What does FAA AC 150/5345-28H require of PAPI mounting legs?

The standard calls for at least three adjustable legs for levelling, or two legs if equivalent rigidity and levelling capability are demonstrated, able to cope with one side of the unit being up to 1 in (25 mm) higher or lower than the other. Each leg includes the housing mounting and level-adjusting hardware, a frangible coupling to FAA drawing C-6046 (or an equivalent part that passes the frangibility test) and a flange for a concrete pad. The installer supplies the 2-inch electrical metallic tubing.

What height, weight and loading limits apply to FAA PAPI equipment?

The light unit must be no higher than 40 in (1 m) at its minimum mounting height and weigh no more than 100 lb (45 kg), or 150 lb (68 kg) with an integral power and control unit. Adjusting hardware must be vibration-resistant, and after a 15 lb/ft² static load for 5 hours the beam pattern must stay within ¼ in (6 mm) at 20 ft (6 m); the unit must also withstand 100 mph (161 km/h) wind. These are equipment-qualification requirements, and the manufacturer's drawings and approved project design set the actual dimensions.

What adjustments should a PAPI mounting stand provide?

Vertical adjustment sets the required equipment elevation and accommodates tolerances, horizontal or azimuth adjustment sets orientation relative to the runway centreline, levelling adjustment compensates for small foundation or installation tolerances, and mechanical locking secures the established position. The available adjustment range is manufacturer-specific, so confirm it from the applicable equipment drawing.

How is PAPI alignment verified after mounting?

The stand must hold the unit level and locked so the aiming angle does not drift. After installation, and after any impact, foundation work or re-aiming, verify the angular setting with approved instruments and record it. A new or relocated PAPI is also subject to flight inspection under the authority's requirements.

What should be checked before selecting a replacement PAPI stand?

Survey the existing foundation and mounting arrangement first, and do not select on external dimensions alone. Check the PAPI manufacturer and model, number of units, mounting-hole pattern and equipment interface, foundation dimensions, anchor-bolt arrangement and finished surface elevation, required equipment height, available adjustment range, and whether a frangible arrangement is required and which coupling type is approved.

How should the stand and frangible coupling be inspected?

Visually inspect for corrosion, cracked or damaged components, bent brackets, loose fasteners, coating deterioration, foundation damage, evidence of impact, coupling damage and missing hardware, and check fastener security, mounting stability, and the adjustment and locking mechanisms. For the coupling, look for deformation, cracks, corrosion, damaged threads, incorrect fasteners, unauthorised modifications and improper replacement components. A coupling that has experienced an impact or has visible structural damage should be evaluated under the manufacturer and project procedures before the PAPI is returned to service.

What information is needed to request PAPI mounting hardware?

Provide the PAPI manufacturer and model, number of light units, whether it is a new installation or replacement, existing mounting stand details, foundation dimensions, anchor-bolt arrangement, required mounting height, project location and environment, frangible coupling requirement and existing coupling details, required quantity, the applicable airport specification and civil or structural requirements. For replacement projects, photographs and measurements of the existing mounting arrangement help.
