Frequently Asked Questions

Quick answers about the AGL Engineering Forms & Templates library, its 25 divisions, and the 15-field standard template.

Q1What is the AGL Engineering Forms & Templates library?

It is the documentation layer of the AGL Knowledge Hub: every form, template, checklist, register and certificate an Airfield Ground Lighting (AGL) engineer needs to record project, design, installation, inspection, testing, commissioning, maintenance and asset-management work. It is organized across 25 divisions holding 625+ core forms, all built on one 15-field standard template.

Q2How is this library different from the SOP Library and the handbooks?

The SOP Library tells the engineer what to do, and the Inspection, Testing & Commissioning, and Maintenance handbooks tell them how to do it. The Forms & Templates library is what the engineer fills out while doing the work: the checklist, test report, certificate or register that turns a field activity into a permanent, auditable record. Those records then feed the AGL Failure Database, the AGL Asset Lifecycle Guide, case studies and the Topic Hub.

Q3How many divisions are there, and how are they grouped?

There are 25 divisions with 25 forms scoped in each, giving 625+ core forms. Divisions 01 to 03 cover the project front end (management, design and procurement). Divisions 04 to 10 cover the field lifecycle, from installation through quality control. Divisions 11 to 19 cover asset, spares, safety, calibration and equipment-specific records. Divisions 20 to 23 cover documentation control, compliance, audit and closeout, and Divisions 24 and 25 cover digital forms and the master library.

Q4What is the 15-field standard template?

Every form in the library, whether a checklist, report, register or certificate, is built on the same 15-field skeleton so any engineer can pick up any form and know where to find what they need. The fields are: Document Number, Document Title, Purpose, Applicable Standards, Project Information, Equipment Information, Data Entry Fields, Inspection/Test/Maintenance Section, Remarks, Engineer Signature, Reviewer Signature, Approval Section, Revision History, Related SOP and Related Topic Hub.

Q5How large is the full library planned to be?

The recommended master architecture scales from 25 divisions and 625+ core forms to 300+ additional forms, 200+ editable templates, 200+ checklists, 150+ reports, 100+ registers, 50+ certificates, 200+ digital forms and 100+ Excel tools.

Q6Are all the forms available now?

The master structure is complete across all 25 divisions (625+ core forms). Full division content, digital forms and Excel tools are being published progressively, so some division pages are still marked as in development.

Q7Which divisions cover installation, inspection, testing, commissioning and maintenance?

The field lifecycle is covered by Division 04 (Installation Forms), Division 05 (Inspection Forms), Division 06 (Testing Forms), Division 07 (Commissioning & Handover Forms), Division 08 (Maintenance Forms), Division 09 (Troubleshooting & Failure Investigation Forms) and Division 10 (Quality Assurance & Quality Control Forms).

Q8Where are the asset, spares, safety, calibration and equipment-specific forms?

Division 11 covers Asset Management, Division 12 Spare Parts & Inventory, Division 13 Safety & Permit, and Division 14 Calibration & Measurement. Equipment-specific records are in Division 15 (Photometry & Optical Testing), Division 16 (Cable, Connector & Electrical Circuit Records), Division 17 (PAPI, APAPI & Visual Guidance), Division 18 (Airfield Signs) and Division 19 (ALCMS & Control Systems).

Q9Where can I find the compliance, audit, handover, digital and master library forms?

Division 20 covers Documentation & Records Control, Division 21 Compliance & Standards (ICAO, FAA, IEC, BIS/IS and DGCA checklists, deviation requests and final compliance certificates), Division 22 Audit & Management Review, and Division 23 Project Handover & Closeout. Division 24 covers Digital Forms & Automation, targeting 200+ digital forms and 100+ Excel tools. Division 25 is the Master Forms Library & Document Packages, with master registers for every form type and flagship packages such as the Project, Installation, Testing & Commissioning, Maintenance and Engineering Office packs, plus a Complete AGL Project Documentation Pack.
