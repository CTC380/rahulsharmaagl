Frequently Asked Questions

Quick answers about the AGL Engineering Case Studies library.

Q1What are the AGL Engineering Case Studies?

They are a library of 100 Airfield Ground Lighting (AGL) engineering case studies from Precision AGL Academy. Each one walks through a fault investigation from symptoms and hypotheses to testing, root cause, corrective action and verification. They are engineering training simulations, not reports of actual airport incidents.

Q2Are these real airport incidents?

No. The cases are written as real-world-style engineering scenarios to teach fault-finding, and each case page states that it is a training simulation. They are designed so that readers can recognise similar failure patterns in their own systems.

Q3What topics do the case studies cover?

They are grouped into 10 categories: Series Circuit, Cable and Electrical Faults; AGL Isolating Transformers; Constant Current Regulators (CCR); Runway Lighting; Taxiway Lighting; AGL Photometry; PAPI and Approach Lighting; AGL Signs; ALCMS, SCADA and Control; and Advanced Maintenance and Engineering.

Q4How many case studies are in each category?

Series Circuit, Cable and Electrical Faults has 15 (AGL-CS-001 to 015), Isolating Transformers has 15 (016 to 030) and CCR has 10 (031 to 040). Runway Lighting has 10 (041 to 050), Taxiway Lighting has 10 (051 to 060) and AGL Photometry has 15 (061 to 075). PAPI and Approach Lighting has 10 (076 to 085), AGL Signs has 5 (086 to 090), ALCMS, SCADA and Control has 8 (091 to 098) and Advanced Maintenance and Engineering has 2 (099 to 100).

Q5How is each case study structured?

Each case typically includes an overview, learning objectives, system background, problem statement, initial observations, test equipment, a step-by-step investigation, root cause, corrective action and post-repair verification. Most also include engineering lessons, an engineer's decision point, a knowledge check, interview questions and an FAQ.

Q6How do I find a specific case study?

Use the search box to search by case ID (for example AGL-CS-061) or by title, or use the category filter to browse one topic at a time. Every case links to its own page.

Q7Who are the case studies for?

They suit AGL maintenance technicians, airfield electrical engineers, students and anyone preparing for AGL troubleshooting interviews or wanting a systematic diagnostic method.

Q8Do the case studies replace airport procedures or manufacturer documentation?

No. They are for learning and do not replace an airport's approved maintenance, isolation, switching and safety procedures, applicable standards or manufacturer documentation. Any real testing or repair must follow those requirements.

Q9Where can I learn more after reading the case studies?

Precision AGL Academy offers AGL training that builds the underlying knowledge, including fundamentals, electrical systems, fault finding, photometry, installation, testing, commissioning and maintenance.
