06 · Case Example

Delhi Airport Runway 11R/29L — 2026 Case Example

A current example of large-scale runway rehabilitation is Indira Gandhi International Airport, Delhi. This example demonstrates that runway rehabilitation can involve much more than resurfacing.

Delhi Airport's Runway 11R/29L underwent a comprehensive rehabilitation programme beginning in February 2026, including runway resurfacing, pavement strengthening, a new Rapid Exit Taxiway, ILS work, AGL upgrades and associated civil and electrical improvements.

The rehabilitation involved the removal and reinstallation of around 2,000 AGL fixtures. The upgraded runway returned to operational use on September 20, 2026, after inspections, validation and regulatory clearances.

Delhi Airport's current fact sheet identifies Runway 11R/29L as CAT III and lists it as a 4,430 m runway. AGL work needed to be coordinated across: Pavement → Lighting Fixtures → Electrical Circuits → ILS → Taxiways → Civil Works → Testing → Operational Approval.

09 · LED Modernisation

LED Retrofit Projects

LED retrofit is an important modernisation application involving replacement of conventional lighting equipment with LED-based fixtures — across runway edge, threshold, end, centreline, touchdown zone, taxiway and approach lighting, plus airfield signs.

LED retrofit is a system-compatibility project, not simply a fixture replacement project.

Key takeaway: each application has a different engineering starting point. For a new airport, the focus is integrated design. For expansion, it's integration with existing infrastructure. For rehabilitation, it's removal, reinstatement, replacement and testing. For LED retrofit, it's system compatibility and photometric performance. For emergency lighting, it's temporary operational requirements. For maintenance, it's serviceability, diagnosis and restoration.

AGL requirements are project-specific. An application or equipment arrangement used at one Indian airport should not automatically be assumed to apply to another — the exact configuration should always be established from the latest applicable airport documentation, approved drawings, technical specification, regulatory requirements and project procedures. The Delhi Runway 11R/29L rehabilitation is a current example, not a universal template for Indian runway projects.

FAQs

Frequently Asked Questions

What kinds of India AGL projects are typical?
New airport projects, airport expansion, runway rehabilitation, LED retrofit, emergency and temporary lighting, and maintenance and modernisation of existing infrastructure.
What does AGL support during runway rehabilitation involve?
Removal, replacement, reinstatement and testing of AGL during runway rehabilitation, as seen in Delhi Airport's 2026 Runway 11R/29L rehabilitation involving nearly 2,000 AGL fixtures.
When is temporary or emergency AGL lighting needed?
Temporary lighting arrangements support rehabilitation, construction, emergency restoration and other temporary operational requirements.
Is LED retrofit just a fixture swap?
No. LED retrofit is a system-compatibility project — the existing CCR, series circuit, transformers, connectors and control system should be assessed before selecting replacement LED equipment.
What's the difference between a new AGL project and a retrofit?
A new project can be designed as a complete architecture (requirement → design → equipment → installation → testing). A retrofit must first survey and assess compatibility with the existing system before new equipment is introduced.
What documentation is needed for an AGL project?
Depending on scope: BOQ, technical specification, approved drawings, equipment schedules, technical submittals, compliance matrices, test certificates, inspection reports, photometric and commissioning reports, and as-built/O&M documentation.
