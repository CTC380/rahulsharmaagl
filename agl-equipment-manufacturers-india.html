Isolating Transformers & Constant Current Regulators

AGL Isolating Transformers

An important component of airport series lighting circuits.

  • Rated power & primary current
  • Secondary characteristics
  • Electrical insulation
  • Environmental protection
  • Connector arrangement, testing & traceability

Constant Current Regulators

Supply controlled current to AGL series circuits.

  • Current rating & current steps
  • Regulation & protection
  • Monitoring, control & communication

Manufacturer vs Supplier vs System Integrator

These terms should not automatically be treated as identical — one organisation can perform more than one role.

Manufacturer

Product engineering and manufacturing.

  • Product design & manufacturing
  • Testing & quality control
  • Technical documentation

Supplier

Sources and supplies equipment from one or more manufacturers.

System Integrator

Combines multiple products and systems into a complete solution.

  • Integration, control & monitoring
  • Installation & testing
  • Commissioning

FAQs

Frequently Asked Questions

What does India's AGL manufacturing ecosystem cover?
India has an established ecosystem of electrical and lighting manufacturers supporting airport infrastructure, including component manufacturers for transformers, regulators, fixtures and connectors.
What should an AGL equipment manufacturer consider?
Relevant Indian Standards, applicable ICAO and FAA requirements, airport-specific technical requirements, type/routine/acceptance testing, traceability, quality documentation and product consistency.
Which standards remain part of the Indian aerodrome-lighting framework?
BIS records show standards for isolating transformers, constant-current regulators, inset fixtures and installation/maintenance remain part of the standards framework.
What's the difference between a manufacturer, a supplier and a system integrator?
A manufacturer is responsible for product engineering and manufacturing; a supplier sources and supplies equipment from one or more manufacturers; a system integrator combines multiple products into a complete, installed and commissioned solution. One organisation can perform more than one role.
What should a manufacturer's technical submittal include?
A complete submittal typically covers the product description, technical data sheet, general arrangement drawing, electrical/mechanical/optical characteristics, applicable standards, compliance statement, type and routine test documents, installation instructions, O&M documents and spare-parts information.
