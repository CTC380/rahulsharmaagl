Division 16 · PHOTO · Objective Optical-Performance Verification

AGL Photometry Testing

The division that answers whether the AGL system actually produces the optical performance it was designed for. Photometry is not a final walk-around to confirm the lights are on — it is a calibrated, methodology-driven measurement process that verifies intensity, beam alignment, colour, uniformity, distribution, visibility and system-specific performance for every runway, taxiway, approach, PAPI and sign system in scope.

Why this is a major division: a fixture can be visibly illuminated and still fail photometric acceptance because of incorrect aiming, inadequate intensity, optical contamination, damaged optics, colour deviation, poor distribution or non-uniform performance. Division 16 treats light intensity, beam alignment, colour, uniformity, photometric compliance and visibility assessment as the framework's core measured parameters — none of which can be confirmed by looking at a lit fixture from the runway.

Verification Sequence

Division 16 runs as a staged measurement programme — each stage below depends on the one before it, and the division closes only once every applicable system has a documented, classified result.

Before Measurement Begins

Two requirement groups should be closed out before the photometric test team starts working through the topic list.

Reference Package

  • Approved test drawings, test-point/grid layout and measurement methodology
  • Applicable specification, regulatory requirement or standard defining acceptance criteria
  • Fixture identification, circuit and CCR/intensity-step schedule
  • PAPI alignment and calibration verification records from Division 09
  • Prior electrical and functional test records confirming fixtures are operational

Equipment & Access

  • Confirm every photometric instrument to be used has a valid, traceable calibration certificate
  • Confirm the certificate serial number matches the physical instrument in use
  • Confirm the correct circuit and intensity step can be energized for the test
  • Confirm test-area control and required operational/ATC coordination are in place
  • Do not treat an illuminated fixture as evidence of acceptable performance — verify it

A

Preparation, Equipment & Calibration

3 topics
TC-PHOTO-001Photometric Test Preparation
Verification Checks
  • Confirm photometric test scope, systems and areas (runway/taxiway/approach/PAPI/signage) are defined
  • Confirm the test schedule, required intensity steps and test configuration are approved
  • Verify test drawings, fixture identification and the test point/grid layout are available
  • Confirm the measurement methodology and applicable acceptance criteria are defined before testing
  • Confirm electrical and functional testing are complete and fixtures are operational
  • Confirm required operational coordination (ATC/airside) and test-area control are in place
Acceptance / Record

Readiness Criteria: test scope, methodology, acceptance criteria and operational coordination are all confirmed before any photometric measurement begins.

TC-PHOTO-002Photometric Equipment Verification
Verification Checks
  • Verify instrument identification — manufacturer, model, serial number and measurement range
  • Confirm measurement accuracy is appropriate for the parameter being tested
  • Inspect detector and optical components for damage or contamination
  • Verify mounting arrangement, accessories and data-acquisition system/software configuration
  • Confirm battery/power condition is adequate for the planned test duration
  • Record instrument condition and status before use
Acceptance / Record

Readiness Criteria: every instrument to be used is identified, physically sound, and confirmed functioning correctly before measurement.

TC-PHOTO-003Calibration Verification
Verification Checks
  • Verify the calibration certificate for each instrument to be used
  • Confirm the certificate serial number corresponds to the actual instrument in use
  • Verify calibration date, validity period and traceability where required
  • Confirm the certificate covers the applicable measurement parameters
  • Check the physical/measurement condition of the instrument against its calibration status
  • Record calibration status for every instrument on the equipment register
Hold Point

Hold Point: formal acceptance measurements do not proceed on any instrument whose calibration status cannot be demonstrated, or whose certificate serial number does not match the instrument in use.

B

Measurement Setup

1 topic
TC-PHOTO-004Measurement Setup Verification
Verification Checks
  • Verify measurement location, geometry and detector position against the approved methodology
  • Confirm measurement height, reference direction and distance are correctly set
  • Verify the test grid and measurement plane match the approved test method
  • Confirm fixture orientation and equipment alignment at each measurement point
  • Verify data-recording configuration matches the planned test
  • Compare the approved test method, measurement setup and actual field setup for consistency
Acceptance / Record

Readiness Criteria: the field measurement setup matches the approved methodology at every measurement point before readings are recorded as valid.

C

Core Photometric Measurements

6 topics
TC-PHOTO-005Light-Intensity Measurement
Verification Checks
  • Measure fixture intensity at each applicable test position and intensity step
  • Confirm the correct intensity setting and circuit are energized for the test
  • Record measured intensity against the required value for each fixture
  • Evaluate individual fixture, circuit and system-level intensity performance
  • Assess intensity consistency across the tested population
  • Classify each measurement against the applicable acceptance criterion
Acceptance / Record

Readiness Criteria: measured intensity meets the required value at every tested intensity step, with deviations classified and logged.

TC-PHOTO-006Beam-Alignment Measurement
Verification Checks
  • Verify horizontal and vertical aiming against the designed beam direction
  • Identify beam centre and reference axis for each fixture tested
  • Compare designed beam direction against actual measured beam direction
  • Identify horizontal or vertical misalignment, incorrect orientation or mechanical movement
  • Distinguish alignment deviations caused by incorrect fixture aiming versus installation error
  • Record required alignment, measured alignment and deviation for each fixture
Acceptance / Record

Readiness Criteria: measured beam alignment matches the designed beam direction within the applicable deviation tolerance.

TC-PHOTO-007Colour Verification
Verification Checks
  • Verify fixture and light-source colour against the correct colour designation
  • Confirm applicable chromaticity/performance requirement for each lighting system
  • Apply the required measurement/assessment method rather than visual judgement alone
  • Check colour consistency across runway, threshold, approach, taxiway, PAPI and sign lighting
  • Compare measured/observed colour against the applicable requirement
  • Record and classify any colour deviation identified
Acceptance / Record

Readiness Criteria: measured colour meets the applicable chromaticity/performance requirement for each system, verified by objective measurement rather than visual judgement alone.

TC-PHOTO-008Uniformity Verification
Verification Checks
  • Evaluate fixture-to-fixture and spatial uniformity across the applicable measurement area
  • Identify dark areas, excessively bright areas or distribution inconsistency
  • Record maximum, minimum and uniformity ratio against the required parameter
  • Investigate failed fixtures, misaligned fixtures or incorrect intensity settings
  • Investigate optical contamination or incorrect fixture spacing as possible causes
  • Confirm uniformity results are classified against the applicable requirement
Acceptance / Record

Readiness Criteria: measured uniformity meets the applicable requirement across the test area, with any deviation traced to a specific cause.

TC-PHOTO-009Photometric Distribution Verification
Verification Checks
  • Verify horizontal and vertical distribution against the intended optical performance
  • Confirm beam spread, beam centre and cut-off characteristics where applicable
  • Compare measured distribution against the approved/required photometric distribution
  • Identify missing portions of beam or excessive deviation from the required distribution
  • Investigate incorrect aiming, optical damage or incorrect fixture type/orientation as causes
  • Record distribution consistency across the tested fixture population
Acceptance / Record

Readiness Criteria: measured photometric distribution corresponds to the approved distribution, with no unexplained missing beam segments or excessive deviation.

TC-PHOTO-010Fixture Aiming Verification
Verification Checks
  • Verify fixture orientation, horizontal and vertical alignment against the aiming reference
  • Confirm beam centre position matches the approved aiming requirement
  • Verify mechanical locking of the aiming mechanism after adjustment
  • Cross-check mechanical alignment verification against photometric aiming verification
  • Confirm that mechanical installation and resulting optical direction are both verified
  • Record final installed aiming status for each fixture
Acceptance / Record

Readiness Criteria: photometric aiming verification confirms the resulting optical direction matches the approved requirement, in addition to mechanical alignment.

D

System-Specific Photometry

5 topics
TC-PHOTO-011Runway Photometry
Verification Checks
  • Verify intensity, beam alignment, colour, uniformity and distribution for runway edge, threshold, runway end, centreline and touchdown-zone lights
  • Confirm each applicable runway lighting system is included within the approved test scope
  • Evaluate fixture-to-fixture performance and intensity-step performance across the runway system
  • Compare measured runway performance against the applicable requirement
  • Classify overall runway photometric compliance
  • Record results for every runway lighting system tested
Acceptance / Record

Readiness Criteria: every applicable runway lighting system meets the required intensity, alignment, colour, uniformity and distribution criteria.

TC-PHOTO-012Taxiway Photometry
Verification Checks
  • Verify intensity, alignment, colour, uniformity and distribution for taxiway edge, centreline, clearance-bar and rapid-exit lighting
  • Confirm fixture consistency across the complete taxiway system
  • Check the fixture-to-circuit-to-intensity-setting-to-optical-performance chain for each system
  • Compare measured taxiway performance against the applicable requirement
  • Investigate any fixture or circuit-level deviation identified
  • Record results for every applicable taxiway lighting system
Acceptance / Record

Readiness Criteria: every applicable taxiway lighting system meets the required photometric criteria across its full fixture-to-performance chain.

TC-PHOTO-013Approach-Light Photometry
Verification Checks
  • Verify intensity, beam alignment, colour, distribution and uniformity for the approach lighting system
  • Evaluate light-bar and crossbar performance individually and as a coordinated system
  • Verify fixture aiming across the complete approach-light array
  • Assess the approach system as a spatially coordinated optical system, not individual lamps
  • Compare measured approach-light performance against the applicable requirement
  • Record results for individual fixtures, light bars, crossbars and the complete system
Acceptance / Record

Readiness Criteria: the approach lighting system meets the applicable requirement as a coordinated optical system, not only at the individual-fixture level.

TC-PHOTO-014PAPI Photometry
Verification Checks
  • Verify intensity, beam distribution and beam transition characteristics for the PAPI/APAPI system
  • Verify optical alignment, colour and unit-to-unit consistency across the PAPI array
  • Coordinate photometric results with PAPI elevation setting, azimuth and installation geometry
  • Cross-reference photometric results with PAPI alignment and calibration verification performed under Division 09
  • Compare measured PAPI performance against the applicable requirement
  • Record photometric results for each PAPI unit
Acceptance / Record

Readiness Criteria: PAPI photometric performance meets the applicable requirement and is consistent with the alignment and calibration verification recorded under Division 09.

TC-PHOTO-015Sign Illumination Verification
Verification Checks
  • Verify sign illumination, brightness, uniformity, colour and visibility against the applicable requirement
  • Inspect for dark segments, uneven illumination or LED degradation
  • Verify LED and driver performance where applicable
  • Inspect for optical contamination or incorrect colour on illuminated signs
  • Compare measured sign illumination against the applicable requirement
  • Record results for every airfield sign within the test scope
Acceptance / Record

Readiness Criteria: every tested airfield sign meets the required illumination, brightness, uniformity, colour and visibility criteria.

E

LED Performance

1 topic
TC-PHOTO-016LED Performance Verification
Verification Checks
  • Verify light output, intensity, colour, uniformity and optical distribution of LED-based fixtures
  • Verify beam alignment and intensity-step response of LED fixtures
  • Check for LED degradation or individual LED/module failure
  • Check for colour variation, optical contamination or driver-related performance problems
  • Assess fixture-to-fixture consistency across the LED population tested
  • Confirm a fixture being visibly on is not treated as evidence of acceptable photometric performance
Hold Point

Hold Point: an LED fixture that is illuminated but has not been photometrically verified is not accepted as performing correctly — visible output alone is not proof of compliance.

F

Compliance & Analysis

3 topics
TC-PHOTO-017Photometric Compliance Assessment
Verification Checks
  • Confirm the applicable acceptance basis — specification, regulatory requirement, design or standard — for each measurement
  • Evaluate intensity, alignment, colour, uniformity, distribution, visibility and system-specific criteria against that basis
  • Classify each measurement as Pass, Fail, Retest Required or Investigation Required
  • Confirm acceptance criteria are explicitly identified in the test report, not left as an unstated generic threshold
  • Cross-check classifications against the raw measurement records
  • Escalate any Fail or Investigation Required result before proceeding to acceptance
Hold Point

Hold Point: acceptance criteria must be explicitly stated against every classified result — an unstated or generic threshold is not accepted as a valid compliance basis.

TC-PHOTO-018Visibility Assessment
Verification Checks
  • Assess whether installed lighting provides the required operational visibility
  • Consider intensity, spatial distribution, uniformity, beam alignment and colour together
  • Assess visibility consistency, dark zones, misleading bright areas and poor beam coverage
  • Evaluate colour differentiation and operational identification of the lighting pattern
  • Consider background/environmental conditions where relevant to visibility
  • Confirm visibility assessment complements, rather than replaces, objective photometric measurement
Acceptance / Record

Readiness Criteria: visibility assessment is documented alongside, and consistent with, the objective photometric measurements — not used as a substitute for them.

TC-PHOTO-019Photometric Test-Result Analysis
Verification Checks
  • Analyse individual fixture, circuit, intensity-step, alignment, colour, uniformity and distribution results
  • Review system averages/statistics, minimum/maximum results, outliers and failed points where applicable
  • Investigate abnormal results against potential causes — misalignment, contamination, damaged optics, LED degradation, incorrect setup
  • Apply the measurement–validation–comparison–deviation–cause investigation–corrective action–retest sequence to every abnormal result
  • Distinguish instrument/test-setup error from genuine fixture or system performance deviation
  • Confirm trends across circuits or systems are identified, not only individual point failures
Acceptance / Record

Readiness Criteria: every abnormal or failed result has a documented investigation, identified cause and corrective action before retest.

G

Acceptance

1 topic
TC-PHOTO-020Photometric Acceptance Report
Verification Checks
  • Compile project information, test equipment register and test configuration for the completed scope
  • Compile runway, taxiway, approach-light, PAPI and sign illumination results into the report
  • Compile the photometric defect/punch-list with corrective action and retest status
  • Confirm intensity, alignment, colour, uniformity, distribution and compliance are verified for every applicable system
  • Confirm all failed results have been investigated, corrected and retested
  • Obtain sign-off establishing photometric compliance and issue the acceptance report
Acceptance / Record

Readiness Criteria: the acceptance report provides complete, signed evidence that every applicable system has been measured, evaluated and found compliant — closing Division 16.

The Photometric Acceptance Chain

The framework's core principle: a fixture can be illuminated and still fail acceptance at any link in this chain. Photometric commissioning is never reduced to "the lights are on and look bright."

Correct Fixture
Correct Installation
Correct Aiming
Correct Intensity
Correct Beam Distribution
Correct Colour
Acceptable Uniformity
Required Visibility
Measured Compliance
Documented Acceptance
Photometric Acceptance = Measurement Quality + Calibration + Correct Setup + Intensity + Alignment + Colour + Uniformity + Distribution + System-Specific Photometry + Visibility + Compliance Analysis → Photometric System Accepted

Abnormal-Result Investigation Flow

When a photometric measurement deviates from the requirement, the same investigation sequence applies across every topic in this division.

1
Measurement

Record the raw measured value under the approved methodology.

2
Validation

Confirm the measurement itself, setup and instrument calibration were correct.

3
Comparison

Compare against the applicable requirement or approved design value.

4
Deviation

Quantify the deviation and classify Pass / Fail / Retest / Investigation Required.

5
Cause Investigation

Identify misalignment, contamination, damaged optics, LED degradation, or incorrect setup.

6
Corrective Action

Rectify the fixture, cabling, aiming or setup issue identified.

7
Retest

Re-measure under the same methodology and confirm closure.

8
Record

Update the defect/punch-list before the system is carried into the acceptance report.
