13 · Electrical Testing

Electrical Testing After Installation

The exact test method, test voltage and acceptance criteria should be established from the applicable project specification and test procedure.

Continuity Testing

Identifies open circuit, incorrect connection, connection failure or circuit discontinuity, following the applicable project requirements.

Insulation Resistance Testing

Assesses electrical insulation condition of primary circuit, cables, equipment and connections; instrument, voltage, duration and criteria come from the project procedure.

Functional Verification

Fixture, circuit, CCR, current-step, control, monitoring, fault indication, sign and stop bar operation, confirming intended operation.

Photometric Verification

Light intensity, beam direction/alignment, colour/chromaticity — helps identify orientation, alignment, optical damage or location issues.

FAQs

Frequently Asked Questions

What does AGL installation involve?
Coordination between lighting equipment, electrical infrastructure, civil works and airfield operational requirements, covering both primary and secondary circuits.
What are the key installation quality checks?
Correct positioning and orientation, cable and connector inspection, transformer installation, electrical testing, fixture alignment, functional verification and final commissioning.
Which Indian standard governs AGL fitting installation?
BIS lists IS 12309 Part 1 as the Indian code of practice for installation of aerodrome lighting fittings, with Part 2 covering maintenance.
Why does a compliant fixture sometimes still cause poor lighting performance?
Installation problems can occur even when the supplied equipment is technically compliant. A fixture installed at the wrong position, facing the wrong direction, with an incorrectly set beam or a poor electrical connection can affect lighting performance even though the product itself is compliant.
What is a typical AGL installation testing sequence?
Visual inspection, cable and connector inspection, continuity testing, insulation testing, transformer verification, CCR verification, fixture operation, control and monitoring checks, fixture alignment, photometry where required, final functional verification, then commissioning.
Does AGL installation need extra checks during an LED retrofit?
Yes. LED retrofit installation requires a compatibility review of the existing CCR, series circuit, transformer, cable, connector and control system before the physical work begins, and only after compatibility is established should the final installation arrangement be confirmed.
