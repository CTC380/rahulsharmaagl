Privacy Policy

How Precision AGL Academy collects, uses and protects your information.

Last updated: September 2026

This Privacy Policy applies to Precision AGL Academy's website and enrollment process, operated by Rahul Sharma. It describes the categories of information we collect and how we use them; it does not name specific third-party providers, since these may change from time to time as described in Section 4.

1. What We Collect

When you enroll in a program, contact us, or browse our website, we may collect: your name, email address, phone number, payment details (processed by our payment provider, not stored by us directly), and general usage data such as pages visited.

2. How We Use It

  • To process enrollment and deliver course access
  • To issue certificates and respond to certificate verification requests
  • To communicate about your program, including schedule and content updates
  • To respond to inquiries submitted via WhatsApp, email or the contact form
  • To improve our programs and website

3. What We Don't Do

We do not sell your personal data to third parties. We do not share your data with advertisers.

4. Third-Party Services & International Transfers

We use third-party service providers to help operate the Academy — for example, for payment processing, video/course hosting, email or WhatsApp communication, and website analytics. These providers process your data as our service providers, under their own privacy and security terms, only to the extent needed to deliver that service. This website also loads fonts from Google Fonts and icons from the Cloudflare cdnjs network, so those providers receive your IP address when pages load. Some of these providers may store or process data on servers located outside India; where this happens, we use providers that apply reasonable contractual and technical safeguards to protect your data in transit and at rest.

5. Data Security

We take reasonable technical and organizational measures — such as restricting access to enrollment and payment records to those who need it to operate the Academy, and relying on our providers' own security measures for payment and hosting — to protect your data against unauthorized access, loss or misuse. No method of transmission or storage is completely secure, and we cannot guarantee absolute security. If a personal data breach affecting you occurs, we will notify you and the authorities as required by law.

6. Data Retention

We retain enrollment and certificate records for as long as reasonably necessary to support certificate verification and program history, and to meet any applicable legal, tax or accounting requirements.

7. Children's Privacy

Our programs are intended for participants who meet the eligibility requirements in our Terms & Conditions (generally 18 years or older, or enrolling with a parent/guardian or sponsoring employer). We do not knowingly collect personal data directly from children without appropriate parental/guardian or institutional involvement.

8. Your Rights

You can request access to, correction of, or deletion of your personal data by contacting us. Where we rely on your consent, you may withdraw it at any time (this does not affect processing already carried out), and you may nominate another person to exercise these rights on your behalf if you die or become unable to do so. We will respond within a reasonable time, subject to any records we are required to keep for legal, tax or certification purposes. If you are not satisfied with our response, you may escalate to our Grievance Officer under Section 10.

9. Cookies

Our website may use basic cookies or similar technologies for essential site functionality and analytics. You can control cookies through your browser settings.
