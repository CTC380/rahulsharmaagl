Division 02 of 25 · SAL Series · Flagship Programme

Smart Airfield Lighting

One of the core flagship programmes of the AGL Innovation Hub. Division 02 sits immediately after Innovation Strategy & Roadmap and defines the technology domain of intelligent AGL — intelligent control, adaptive brightness, smart monitoring, self-diagnostics, energy optimization and remote monitoring. The objective is to transform conventional AGL from a primarily manually monitored lighting infrastructure into an intelligent, connected, condition-aware and energy-aware airfield lighting system.

SAL-03 Adaptive Lighting Control
Purpose

Develop lighting-control systems capable of adapting lighting operation according to defined operational conditions.

Concept

Instead of treating lighting intensity as a fixed command, an intelligent system evaluates available operational inputs and applies an approved lighting-control strategy.

Potential Inputs
  • Operational condition
  • Lighting system status
  • Selected intensity level
  • Equipment condition
  • Environmental information
  • Control-system status
  • Maintenance condition
Innovation Areas
  • Adaptive intensity control
  • Automated control logic
  • Intelligent control algorithms
  • Condition-based lighting operation
  • Centralized intelligent control
Important Principle

Adaptive control must remain subject to approved operational requirements, safety requirements and applicable regulatory/airport procedures. Innovation should not override the required operational control philosophy.

Most Important Design Principle

Division 02 should remain focused on intelligent AGL as a lighting discipline — intelligent control, adaptive brightness, smart monitoring, self-diagnostics, energy optimization and remote monitoring of the lighting system itself, not the broader mechanics of AI, IoT, robotics or digital-twin platforms.

The general-purpose technology domains move into Divisions 03–07 (AI, IoT, Digital Engineering, Robotics, Sustainability), engineering and product innovation move into Divisions 08–17, and the prototyping, PoC, pilot and validation mechanics move into Divisions 18–25. These functions are deliberately kept separate.

Its strongest connection is to Connected Airfield Infrastructure (SAL-17), which forms the foundation for later divisions such as AI for AGL, IoT & Connected AGL, Digital Engineering, AGL Automation, Digital AGL Control & Monitoring and Intelligent Maintenance & Predictive AGL, and to the AGL Failure Database, which supplies recurring fault patterns that Intelligent Fault Indication (SAL-12) is designed to catch earlier.

Division 02 converts conventional, manually monitored AGL into an intelligent, connected, condition-aware and energy-aware airfield lighting system — always within approved operational, safety and regulatory requirements — and hands that connected foundation forward to the technology, prototype, validation and knowledge-development engines of Divisions 03–25.
