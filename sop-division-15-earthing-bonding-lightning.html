Earthing, Bonding & Lightning Protection SOPs

Controlled procedures for AGL earthing, equipotential bonding, earth grid and electrode installation, earth resistance and continuity testing, lightning protection and surge protection device (SPD) inspection, lightning-damage assessment and periodic earthing verification. Applicable SOP range: SOP-301 to SOP-320.

Initial Issue

Golden Rule — Earthing is not simply a low-resistance measurement. A safe AGL earthing system requires the correct architecture, reliable protective conductors, sound equipotential bonding, verified earth connections, effective lightning/surge protection and traceable testing. Every measured value must be interpreted against the approved design, test method and applicable acceptance criteria.

SOP-301 AGL Equipment Earthing
Purpose

To ensure metallic AGL equipment is safely connected to the approved earthing system and that fault currents have an appropriate return path.

Equipment Covered (As Applicable)
  • CCRs.
  • Transformer enclosures.
  • Airfield signs.
  • Metal fixture assemblies.
  • Base cans.
  • Control panels.
  • Junction boxes.
  • Metallic support structures.
  • Other exposed conductive parts.
Procedure
  1. Review approved earthing drawing.
  2. Identify equipment earth point.
  3. Inspect earth conductor.
  4. Verify conductor size/type.
  5. Inspect termination.
  6. Clean contact surfaces where required.
  7. Connect earth conductor.
  8. Tighten connection to manufacturer-approved torque.
  9. Protect connection against corrosion.
  10. Perform continuity testing.
  11. Perform earth-system testing where applicable.
  12. Record results.
Acceptance

All required exposed conductive parts shall be connected to the approved protective/equipotential bonding system.

SOP-302 CCR Earthing
Purpose

To establish a controlled procedure for protective earthing of Constant Current Regulators.

Procedure
  1. Isolate CCR under LOTO (Division 02).
  2. Identify designated protective-earth terminal.
  3. Inspect earth conductor.
  4. Verify connection to approved earth system.
  5. Check terminal tightness.
  6. Inspect for corrosion.
  7. Verify cabinet bonding.
  8. Test continuity.
  9. Perform earth-system verification as required.
  10. Record results.

Critical Check — CCR protective earth shall not be treated as optional equipment grounding. The cabinet and applicable internal/external conductive parts shall follow the approved earthing design.

SOP-303 Transformer Earthing
Purpose

To ensure applicable metallic parts associated with AGL isolation transformers are correctly bonded/earthed according to the approved design.

Procedure
  1. Identify transformer arrangement.
  2. Review approved earthing philosophy.
  3. Inspect transformer enclosure/metallic components where applicable.
  4. Inspect earth/bonding conductor.
  5. Verify termination.
  6. Check corrosion.
  7. Verify continuity.
  8. Record result.

Important — Do not create an unintended connection between isolated AGL series-circuit conductors and earth. Isolation-transformer circuit architecture must be preserved.

SOP-307 Earth Grid Installation
Purpose

To establish a controlled procedure for installation of an approved earth grid/network.

Pre-Installation

Verify:

  • Approved earthing drawing.
  • Grid layout.
  • Conductor type/size.
  • Earth electrode locations.
  • Jointing method.
  • Inspection points.
  • Test points.
  • Separation from other utilities.
  • Counterpoise conductor route, jointing, bonding and electrode interval (where specified).
  • Protection requirements.
Procedure
  1. Establish grid route.
  2. Prepare excavation/installation area.
  3. Install approved earth conductors.
  4. Install electrodes where specified.
  5. Complete approved joints/connections.
  6. Provide test points.
  7. Protect against mechanical damage.
  8. Backfill after inspection approval.
  9. Record installation details.
  10. Perform applicable testing.

SOP-309 Earth Resistance Measurement
Purpose

To establish a standardized method for measuring earth resistance.

Test Preparation

Verify:

  • Approved test method.
  • Calibrated instrument.
  • Correct test leads.
  • Suitable test conditions.
  • Isolation from parallel paths where required.
  • Test-point identification.
Procedure
  1. Confirm no thunderstorm/lightning activity in the vicinity; suspend work on earthing and lightning-protection conductors when lightning is forecast or observed.
  2. Review approved test method.
  3. Identify earth system.
  4. Isolate equipment where required.
  5. Connect test instrument.
  6. Perform measurement using the approved method.
  7. Repeat measurement where required.
  8. Record values.
  9. Compare against applicable acceptance criteria.
  10. Investigate abnormal readings.
  11. Restore normal connections.

Important — Earth resistance results can be affected by soil conditions, electrode configuration and parallel earth paths. The test method and acceptance criteria must therefore be defined before testing.

SOP-314 SPD Replacement
Purpose

To establish a controlled procedure for replacement of defective or operated surge protection devices.

Procedure
  1. Identify SPD type/model.
  2. Verify replacement specification.
  3. Obtain work authorization.
  4. Isolate affected equipment/circuit.
  5. Apply LOTO where required.
  6. Verify safe condition.
  7. Remove defective SPD.
  8. Inspect mounting and wiring.
  9. Install approved replacement.
  10. Verify line/neutral/earth connections as applicable.
  11. Check associated protection.
  12. Restore power under controlled conditions.
  13. Verify SPD status.
  14. Check equipment operation.
  15. Record replacement.

Critical Rule — Never replace an SPD with a device having a different electrical specification merely because it physically fits.

  5. Inspect transformers.
  6. Inspect fixtures.

Earthing & Bonding Master Workflow

For AGL earthing, bonding and lightning-protection systems:

APPROVED EARTHING DESIGN EARTH GRID INSTALLATION EARTH ELECTRODE INSTALLATION EARTH CONNECTIONS EQUIPMENT BONDING CCR EARTHING TRANSFORMER EARTHING/BONDING SIGN EARTHING FIXTURE / BASE CAN BONDING CONTINUITY TESTING EARTH RESISTANCE TESTING LIGHTNING PROTECTION INSPECTION SPD INSPECTION SYSTEM ACCEPTANCE PERIODIC VERIFICATION

Lightning Event Response Workflow

Following a lightning event or suspected strike affecting AGL equipment:

LIGHTNING EVENT / SUSPECTED STRIKE ALCMS ALARM & EVENT REVIEW EQUIPMENT STATUS REVIEW CCR INSPECTION AGL CIRCUIT INSPECTION TRANSFORMER / FIXTURE / SIGN INSPECTION SPD STATUS CHECK EARTHING / BONDING INSPECTION ELECTRICAL TESTING FUNCTIONAL TESTING DAMAGED COMPONENT REPLACEMENT RETEST OPERATIONAL VERIFICATION RETURN TO SERVICE

Earthing & Lightning Protection Quality Hold Points

  • HP-01 — Earthing Design — Approved earthing and bonding drawings available.
  • HP-02 — Earth Grid — Grid installation inspected before concealment.
  • HP-03 — Earth Electrodes — Electrode installation and connection verified.
  • HP-04 — Equipment Bonding — Required conductive parts bonded according to design.
  • HP-05 — Continuity — Protective-earth/bonding continuity verified.
  • HP-06 — Earth Resistance — Measured using the approved method and compared with the applicable acceptance criterion.
  • HP-07 — Lightning Protection — Accessible components inspected.
  • HP-08 — SPD — SPD condition/status verified.
  • HP-09 — Lightning Event — Affected AGL equipment inspected before release.
  • HP-10 — Records — Test results traceable to equipment and test location.
  • HP-11 — Periodic Verification — Trend/deterioration reviewed against previous results.
  • HP-12 — Final Release — All critical defects closed and required tests passed.

Common Failure Modes

  • 1. Loose Earth Connection — Can increase impedance and compromise fault-current paths.
  • 2. Corroded Earth Connection — Can progressively increase connection resistance.
  • 3. Broken Bonding Strap — Can leave metallic components electrically isolated from the intended bonding network.
  • 4. Damaged Earth Conductor — Can result from excavation, civil work or mechanical damage.
  • 5. Flooded Earth Pit — Can make inspection difficult and accelerate deterioration of connections.
  • 6. Poor Test-Point Identification — Makes periodic testing and maintenance less reliable.
  • 7. SPD Operated After Surge — The equipment may appear normal while surge protection has already been consumed/damaged.
  • 8. Lightning Damage Hidden Inside Equipment — External visual inspection alone may not identify damage to electronics, drivers, communication interfaces, CCR control components or ALCMS components.
  • 9. Incorrect Earth Test Method — Parallel paths and incorrect test configuration can produce misleading measurements.
  • 10. Unauthorized Bonding — An unintended connection can alter the designed electrical architecture, particularly in systems using isolation transformers and series circuits.

Frequently Asked Questions

Quick answers about Earthing, Bonding & Lightning Protection SOPs in the AGL SOP Library.

Q1What is Division 15 — Earthing, Bonding & Lightning Protection SOPs?

Division 15 is a set of 20 procedures (SOP-301 to SOP-320, Rev 0) for AGL earthing, equipotential bonding, earth grid and electrode installation, earth resistance and continuity testing, lightning protection and surge protection device (SPD) inspection, lightning-damage assessment and periodic earthing verification.

Q2Is earthing just a low resistance reading?

No. The Golden Rule says a safe AGL earthing system needs the correct architecture, reliable protective conductors, sound equipotential bonding, verified connections, effective lightning and surge protection, and traceable testing. Every measured value must be interpreted against the approved design, test method and acceptance criteria.

Q3What is the earthing and bonding master workflow?

Approved earthing design, earth grid and electrode installation, earth connections, equipment bonding, then CCR, transformer, sign, fixture and base-can earthing or bonding. Then continuity testing, earth resistance testing, lightning protection and SPD inspection, system acceptance and periodic verification.

Q4How is earth resistance measured?

Verify the approved test method, calibrated instrument, correct leads, suitable conditions and isolation from parallel paths where required. Confirm no thunderstorm or lightning activity nearby, and suspend work on earthing and lightning-protection conductors when lightning is forecast or observed. Measure using the approved method, repeat where required, record and compare against criteria (SOP-309).

Q5What is checked when inspecting surge protection devices?

The SPD status indicator, physical damage, overheating, discoloration, loose terminals, wiring, the associated protective device, the earth connection and any replacement indicator. Defective SPDs are replaced using the approved procedure and the condition recorded (SOP-313).

Q6What should be done after a lightning event?

Follow SOP-316 before returning equipment to service. Review ALCMS alarms and events, then inspect CCRs, series circuits, transformers, fixtures, signs, connectors, SPDs and earthing or bonding. Isolate affected series circuits and perform insulation-resistance testing with the CCR disconnected before re-energization, then run electrical, functional and operational lighting tests, replace damaged components and release after acceptance.

Q7What does periodic earthing verification include?

SOP-320 reviews previous results and trends, inspects accessible earth connections, and checks CCR earthing, transformer, fixture, base-can and sign bonding, earth pits, electrodes, the earth grid, any counterpoise conductor, lightning protection and SPDs. It performs continuity and earth-resistance tests on the approved schedule and compares results with approved criteria.
