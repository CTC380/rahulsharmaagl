Division 10 · SIGN · Illuminated Airfield Signs

Airfield Sign Testing & Commissioning

Following Division 09 — PAPI/APAPI Testing & Commissioning, Division 10 establishes the complete commissioning process for illuminated airfield signs. It verifies that each sign is correctly installed, identified, positioned, oriented, mechanically secured, electrically connected, illuminated and visible, and that its message, colour, brightness and functional performance comply with the approved design and applicable requirements before final acceptance.

Philosophy: a sign is evaluated as a complete chain — correct sign → correct message → correct location → correct orientation → correct mounting → correct electrical connection → healthy cable → healthy connector → correct LED module → correct driver → correct voltage → correct illumination → correct brightness → correct colour → adequate visibility → correct functional response → accepted airfield sign. Message verification and visibility verification are separate acceptance criteria — a sign may be electrically perfect and fully illuminated but still unacceptable if the message is incorrect, the sign is incorrectly oriented, or the operational visibility is inadequate.

Before Testing Begins

Two conditions should be established before any airfield sign is worked through this division.

Numerical Limits Are Not Universal

  • Brightness acceptance values come from the applicable approved specification/standard, not assumed from this topic list
  • Input and internal voltage acceptance ranges are taken from manufacturer and project documentation
  • Internal measurements are only performed according to the manufacturer's approved test procedure — do not perform invasive measurements on sealed or sensitive electronics unless permitted
  • A correctly illuminated sign with an incorrect message is a failed sign — message accuracy is never inferred from illumination

Division Boundary

  • Division 10 follows Division 09 — PAPI/APAPI Testing & Commissioning in the overall sequence
  • Message verification and visibility verification are distinct acceptance criteria from electrical and illumination testing
  • Visibility testing is a system/operational verification, not simply a close-range visual inspection
  • No sign is released as commissioned until every applicable item in this division is closed and reported

A

Identification & Message

3 topics
TC-SIGN-001Sign Installation Verification
Verification Checks
  • Verify correct sign type, category, size, and location
  • Verify correct message, orientation, mounting arrangement, and support structure
  • Verify correct foundation/base, electrical connection, circuit, and identification
  • Cross-check: approved sign layout → sign schedule → approved drawings → physical installation
  • Inspect for missing sign, wrong sign, wrong location, incorrect mounting, damaged sign, incomplete installation, incorrect electrical connection, or damaged support structure
Hold Point

Hold Point: any deviation from the approved sign layout or schedule is resolved before proceeding.

TC-SIGN-002Sign Identification Verification
Verification Checks
  • Verify sign ID, sign number, location, and sign type
  • Verify manufacturer, model, and serial number where applicable
  • Verify circuit identification and associated transformer/power supply where applicable
  • Confirm traceability: physical sign → sign schedule → drawing → asset register → circuit
Acceptance / Record

Acceptance Criteria: every installed sign is uniquely and traceably identified across every reference document.

TC-SIGN-003Sign Message Verification
Verification Checks
  • Verify sign legend/message, characters, character sequence, numbers, and letters
  • Verify arrows/symbols where applicable, background, colour, configuration, and message orientation
  • Cross-check: approved sign schedule → drawing → installed sign
  • Check for wrong letter, wrong number, missing character, incorrect arrow, reversed message, incorrect sign type, or incorrect legend
Hold Point

Hold Point: a correctly illuminated sign with an incorrect message is a failed sign — this is one of the most important sign commissioning checks.

B

Positional Verification

2 topics
TC-SIGN-004Sign Location Verification
Verification Checks
  • Verify longitudinal location, transverse location, and distance from the relevant pavement/runway/taxiway reference
  • Verify offset, position relative to the associated taxiway/runway, and clearance from adjacent objects
  • Verify against approved drawings, sign location schedule, and survey information where applicable
  • Record sign ID, design location, actual location, deviation, acceptance basis, and result
Acceptance / Record

Acceptance Criteria: the sign's physical location matches the approved layout within the applicable deviation basis.

TC-SIGN-005Sign Orientation Verification
Verification Checks
  • Verify sign face orientation, horizontal orientation, and relationship to taxiway/runway
  • Verify viewing direction, sign alignment, and correct angle relative to intended traffic movement
  • Check for sign facing wrong direction, excessive rotation, incorrect orientation, misaligned sign face, or obstructed viewing direction
Hold Point

Hold Point: orientation is verified against the approved sign layout and applicable aerodrome criteria — never assumed from installation alone.

C

Mounting & Electrical Connection

3 topics
TC-SIGN-006Sign Mounting Verification
Verification Checks
  • Verify mounting structure, base/foundation, supports, bolts, nuts, washers, and brackets
  • Verify sign enclosure, mechanical stability, height where applicable, and frangible arrangement where applicable
  • Check for loose mounting, damaged supports, corrosion, structural deformation, incorrect height, incorrect hardware, or excessive movement
  • Verify and record applicable mounting torque where torque values are specified
Acceptance / Record

Acceptance Criteria: the sign structure is mechanically stable with no loose, damaged, or corroded hardware.

TC-SIGN-007Sign Electrical Connection Verification
Verification Checks
  • Verify correct circuit, supply connection, cable termination, and internal wiring
  • Verify protective devices, earthing/bonding where applicable, polarity where applicable, and terminal condition
  • Verify control connection where applicable
  • Trace: CCR/power source → primary circuit → transformer/power supply → sign → internal driver/LED system
  • Check for incorrect circuit, loose terminal, wrong polarity, poor termination, damaged cable, or incorrect power supply
Hold Point

Hold Point: incorrect polarity or a wrong circuit connection is resolved before proceeding to cable and connector testing.

TC-SIGN-008Sign Cable Testing
Verification Checks
  • Verify cable identification, cable route, and cable type
  • Verify cable continuity, insulation where applicable, termination, joints, cable entry, and mechanical protection
  • Tests may include continuity, insulation resistance where applicable, cable identification, and termination verification
  • Record sign ID, cable ID, test, result, acceptance criterion, and status
Acceptance / Record

Acceptance Criteria: cable continuity, insulation where applicable, and termination are all verified against the project specification.

D

Connector & Equipment

3 topics
TC-SIGN-009Sign Connector Testing
Verification Checks
  • Verify correct connector, connector identification, contact condition, and continuity
  • Verify mating, locking, sealing, cable termination, mechanical condition, and moisture condition
  • Inspect for corrosion, loose contact, poor mating, damaged seal, water ingress, or mechanical damage
  • Trace: cable → connector → sign power/input → driver → LED module
Acceptance / Record

Acceptance Criteria: every relevant connector is correctly mated, locked, sealed, and free of corrosion or moisture ingress.

TC-SIGN-010LED-Module Testing
Verification Checks
  • Verify correct LED module, module identification, manufacturer/model where applicable, and rating
  • Verify installation, physical condition, electrical connection, LED operation, and illumination uniformity
  • Inspect for dark LEDs, dark sections, flickering, uneven illumination, discoloration, damaged LED module, or loose connection
Hold Point

Hold Point: a sign is not accepted solely because some LEDs illuminate — verify the complete sign face.

TC-SIGN-011Driver Testing
Verification Checks
  • Verify driver identification, correct rating, input connection, and output connection
  • Verify output stability, physical condition, protection, and alarm/fault indication where provided
  • Check for overheating, loose terminals, damaged driver, abnormal noise, unstable output, or driver fault indication
  • Trace: input supply → driver → LED module → illuminated sign face
Acceptance / Record

Acceptance Criteria: the driver delivers a stable, correctly rated output with no fault indication or abnormal heating.

E

Voltage Verification

2 topics
TC-SIGN-012Input-Voltage Verification
Verification Checks
  • Verify input voltage, supply frequency where applicable, supply stability, correct supply source, and correct circuit
  • Record sign ID, required input, measured input, acceptance basis, and result
  • Evaluate that the input voltage remains within the applicable operating range of the sign equipment
Acceptance / Record

Acceptance Criteria: measured input voltage stays within the manufacturer/project-defined operating range.

TC-SIGN-013Internal-Voltage Verification
Verification Checks
  • Verify driver output, LED module supply, and internal power supply where accessible and appropriate
  • Verify control voltage and internal auxiliary supply where applicable
  • Record sign ID, test point, required voltage, measured voltage, and result
Hold Point

Hold Point: internal measurements are only performed according to the manufacturer's approved test procedure — do not perform invasive measurements on sealed or sensitive electronic equipment unless permitted.

F

Optical & Visibility

4 topics
TC-SIGN-014Illumination Testing
Verification Checks
  • Verify the entire sign is illuminated with no dark section, no dark character, and no intermittent operation
  • Confirm no abnormal flickering, uniform illumination, and correct response to switching
  • Test sequence: energize → stabilize → inspect complete face → switch OFF/ON → repeat → record
  • Inspect under appropriate viewing conditions — assess from the intended operational viewing direction as well as during close visual inspection
Acceptance / Record

Acceptance Criteria: the complete sign face illuminates uniformly with no dark sections and no abnormal or intermittent behaviour.

TC-SIGN-015Brightness Verification
Verification Checks
  • Verify brightness level, uniformity, intensity setting, and dimming/control response where applicable
  • Verify brightness consistency across the sign face
  • Where multiple brightness levels are provided, test minimum, intermediate, and maximum settings
  • Record sign ID, setting, measured/observed brightness, acceptance criterion, and result
Hold Point

Hold Point: brightness acceptance values come from the applicable approved specification/standard rather than being assumed.

TC-SIGN-016Colour Verification
Verification Checks
  • Verify correct background colour, correct legend colour, and correct illumination colour
  • Verify colour consistency with no abnormal colour shift and no discolouration
  • Check LED colour, optical material, sign face, legend, and internal optical components
  • Use calibrated colour/photometric equipment and the approved measurement procedure where measurement is required
Acceptance / Record

Acceptance Criteria: background, legend, and illumination colours are correct, consistent, and free of abnormal shift or discolouration.

TC-SIGN-017Visibility Verification
Verification Checks
  • Verify visibility, legibility, contrast, correct viewing direction, and no obstruction
  • Verify correct illumination, no excessive glare, and no abnormal dark areas
  • Inspect from the intended aircraft/vehicle movement direction, relevant taxiway approach, relevant runway interface, and other designated operational viewing positions
  • Check for obstructions: vegetation, equipment, vehicles, structures, temporary objects, and other airfield fixtures
Hold Point

Hold Point: visibility testing is a system/operational verification, not simply a close-range visual inspection.

G

Functional & Acceptance

3 topics
TC-SIGN-018Functional Testing
Verification Checks
  • Verify ON operation, OFF operation, switching, and brightness/dimming
  • Verify control response, remote operation where applicable, local operation where applicable
  • Verify status indication and fault indication where applicable
  • Integrated sequence: command → power/control system → sign → LED/driver → illumination → status
  • Test multiple operating conditions where applicable: normal operation, minimum brightness, maximum brightness, remote command, local command, recovery after switching
Acceptance / Record

Acceptance Criteria: the sign responds correctly across every applicable operating condition and control path.

TC-SIGN-019Sign Acceptance Testing
Verification Checks
  • Confirm installation, identification, message, location, orientation, and mounting verified
  • Confirm electrical connection, cable, connector, input voltage, internal voltage where applicable, and earthing/bonding verified where applicable
  • Confirm LED modules, driver, internal components, and physical condition acceptable
  • Confirm illumination, brightness, colour, and visibility verified
  • Confirm ON/OFF, switching, brightness control, remote/local control where applicable, and status/fault indication where applicable
  • Confirm punch-list reviewed, corrective actions completed, retesting completed, results accepted, and documentation completed
Acceptance / Record

Acceptance Criteria: every installation, electrical, equipment, optical, and functional item is verified, closed, and documented before the sign is accepted for service.

TC-SIGN-020Sign Commissioning Report
Verification Checks
  • Record project information: airport, project, runway/taxiway, sign location, sign ID, test date, contractor, testing engineer, client/consultant
  • Record sign identification: type, message, manufacturer, model, serial number, LED module, driver, circuit ID, power supply
  • Compile installation verification: location, orientation, mounting, height where applicable, support structure, foundation/base
  • Compile electrical results: input voltage, internal voltage where applicable, cable continuity, insulation where applicable, connector condition, earthing/bonding, driver output where applicable
  • Compile optical results: illumination, brightness, colour, visibility, uniformity, photometric results where applicable
  • Compile functional test: ON, OFF, switching, dimming/brightness, local control, remote control, status, alarm/fault
  • Log defects/corrective actions with sign ID, corrective action, retest, and status
  • Record final status (PASS, FAIL, RETEST REQUIRED, CONDITIONAL) and obtain sign-off: tested by, checked by, contractor, client/consultant, date
Acceptance / Record

Acceptance Criteria: each sign or defined sign group has an individual, traceable commissioning report — this closes Division 10 for that unit and releases the airfield sign as accepted.
