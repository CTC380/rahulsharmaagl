Division 07 · TWY · Taxiway Lighting System

Taxiway Lighting System Commissioning

Individually accepted taxiway fixtures are only the starting point. Division 07 commissions the complete taxiway lighting system — edge, centreline, clearance bars, exit lighting and rapid-exit lighting — as one integrated whole, verifying that circuits, cables, fixtures, connectors, current, intensity, switching, control, alarms and photometric performance all operate correctly together.

Philosophy: this division follows Division 06 — Runway Lighting System Commissioning — and applies the same overall approach: System Identification → Circuit Verification → Cable/Connector/Fixture Verification → Energization → Current → Intensity → Switching → Control → Alarm → Photometry → Functional Test → Acceptance. Division 05 answers whether an individual fixture is acceptable; Division 07 answers whether the complete taxiway lighting system operates correctly.

Chain of Acceptance

The taxiway lighting system is evaluated as a complete chain — every link has to hold before the system is accepted. Even if every individual fixture has passed Division 05, the system can still fail Division 07 on any one of these links.

Before Testing Begins

Two conditions should be established before Division 07 commissioning of any taxiway lighting subsystem begins.

Division Boundaries

  • Division 05 establishes the detailed individual-fixture testing methodology; Division 07 confirms that accepted fixtures function correctly within the complete taxiway lighting system
  • Connector identification, continuity, contact, mating, locking and sealing also sit within the broader Cable & Connector Testing division
  • The complete ALCMS/SCADA/PLC control-system integration remains within the applicable dedicated control-system commissioning scope; Division 07 verifies the taxiway lighting system's response to those control commands
  • The dedicated photometry division establishes the detailed measurement methodology; Division 07 performs the taxiway system-level photometric verification

System-Level Independence

  • The system-level acceptance gate must remain independent of individual fixture acceptance
  • Incorrect circuit loading, cable insulation failure, connector failure, incorrect current or intensity response, switching failure, control-system failure, alarm failure or photometric non-compliance can each fail the system even with fully accepted fixtures
  • Any abnormal current or intensity result should trigger investigation of fixture, transformer, cable, connector, circuit loading and CCR operation before proceeding
  • No taxiway lighting subsystem is released to the integrated functional test until every applicable item in this division is closed

A

Lighting Subsystem Commissioning

5 topics
TC-TWY-001Taxiway Edge-Light Commissioning
Verification Checks
  • Verify correct fixture type, location, spacing, and identification
  • Verify correct orientation, circuit, and colour/configuration as applicable
  • Verify fixture illumination, intensity operation, switching, and control response
  • Confirm system traceability: CCR → Primary Circuit → Isolation Transformers → Connectors → Taxiway Edge Fixtures
  • Check for missing, incorrect, or dark fixture
  • Check for incorrect orientation, damaged fixture, or abnormal illumination
  • Check for incorrect circuit connection
Acceptance / Record

Acceptance Criteria: the complete edge-light system is verified end-to-end from CCR to fixture with correct illumination, intensity operation, and control response.

TC-TWY-002Taxiway Centreline-Light Commissioning
Verification Checks
  • Verify fixture identification, location, and spacing/configuration
  • Verify orientation, circuit identity, cable continuity, and insulation
  • Verify connector condition, fixture operation, current, and intensity steps
  • Verify switching, control, and functional check
  • Confirm correct operation at each applicable intensity setting
  • Check for dark fixtures, incorrect sequence, or uneven illumination
  • Check for incorrect intensity, intermittent operation, or circuit interruption
Acceptance / Record

Acceptance Criteria: centreline lighting operates correctly at every applicable intensity setting with no circuit interruption.

TC-TWY-003Clearance-Bar Commissioning
Verification Checks
  • Verify correct location, fixture arrangement, and circuit
  • Verify correct orientation and illumination
  • Verify switching, intensity, and control response
  • Verify alarm/status where applicable
  • Perform functional test: Command → Circuit Response → Clearance-Bar Illumination → Control Indication
Acceptance / Record

Acceptance Criteria: the clearance bar operates as intended under all applicable operating conditions, where installed.

TC-TWY-004Taxiway Exit-Light Commissioning
Verification Checks
  • Verify correct location, fixture type, and orientation
  • Verify correct circuit, cable, and connector
  • Verify illumination, switching, and intensity
  • Verify control and acceptance
Acceptance / Record

Acceptance Criteria: all applicable exit-light fixtures operate correctly and respond to the designated control commands, where provided.

TC-TWY-005Rapid-Exit Taxiway-Light Commissioning
Verification Checks
  • Verify correct rapid-exit location and fixture configuration
  • Verify fixture spacing, circuit identity, cable condition, and connector condition
  • Verify fixture operation, current, intensity, switching, and control
  • Perform integrated verification with the associated taxiway lighting system
Acceptance / Record

Acceptance Criteria: the rapid-exit lighting system operates correctly in conjunction with the associated taxiway lighting system, where provided.

B

Circuit & Component Testing

4 topics
TC-TWY-006Taxiway Circuit Testing
Verification Checks
  • Verify circuit identification and circuit continuity
  • Verify cable continuity and isolation transformer continuity
  • Verify connector continuity and circuit insulation
  • Verify primary and secondary circuit condition
  • Confirm circuit configuration against approved test sequence
  • Test sequence: Identify Circuit → Isolate → Continuity Test → Insulation Test → Review Results → Restore Circuit
  • Record circuit ID, test point, test method, measured result, acceptance criterion, and result
Hold Point

Hold Point: circuit insulation acceptance values are taken from the applicable project/manufacturer procedure, never an arbitrary universal figure.

TC-TWY-007Cable Testing
Verification Checks
  • Verify cable identification, route, continuity, and insulation
  • Verify cable termination, joints, and condition
  • Verify cable connection to transformer and to fixture
  • Confirm traceability: CCR → Primary Cable → Joint → Transformer → Secondary Lead → Fixture
  • Check for open circuit and insulation deterioration
  • Check for damaged cable, incorrect termination, or incorrect circuit
  • Check for poor joint or moisture-related deterioration
Acceptance / Record

Acceptance Criteria: the cable network is verified continuous, correctly insulated, and correctly traceable from CCR through to fixture.

TC-TWY-008Fixture Testing
Verification Checks
  • Verify fixture identification, type, rating, and mounting
  • Verify height where applicable, alignment, and orientation
  • Verify lens, prism where applicable, and lamp/LED module
  • Verify secondary lead, connector, and insulation where applicable
  • Verify continuity, illumination, and operation
Note

Division boundary: Division 05 establishes the detailed individual-fixture testing methodology — Division 07 confirms that accepted fixtures function correctly within the complete taxiway lighting system.

Acceptance / Record

Acceptance Criteria: fixtures are confirmed to function correctly within the integrated taxiway lighting system.

TC-TWY-009Connector Testing
Verification Checks
  • Verify correct connector and connector identification
  • Verify contact condition, continuity, and mating
  • Verify locking, sealing, and cable termination
  • Verify moisture condition and mechanical integrity
  • Check: Transformer → Connector → Fixture
  • Check: Cable → Joint/Connector → Transformer, where applicable
Acceptance / Record

Acceptance Criteria: connector assemblies are verified for continuity, contact, mating, locking, sealing, and mechanical integrity.

C

Electrical Performance

2 topics
TC-TWY-010Current Verification
Verification Checks
  • Verify CCR output current and taxiway circuit current
  • Verify current at each applicable intensity step and current stability
  • Verify agreement between CCR indication and measured condition
  • Record commanded step, measured current, acceptance criterion (project/manufacturer), and result for each intensity step
  • On abnormal current, investigate fixture, transformer, cable, and connector condition
  • On abnormal current, investigate circuit loading and CCR operation
Hold Point

Hold Point: any abnormal current result at any intensity step is investigated and resolved before proceeding.

TC-TWY-011Intensity-Step Verification
Verification Checks
  • Verify minimum intensity and intermediate intensities
  • Verify maximum intensity and correct step transition
  • Verify correct circuit current at each step
  • Verify correct fixture response and control indication
  • Test sequence: Select Intensity → Verify CCR → Verify Current → Observe Taxiway Lights → Record
Acceptance / Record

Acceptance Criteria: the test demonstrates that the intensity command reaches the complete taxiway lighting system, not merely the CCR.

D

Control & Alarm Systems

3 topics
TC-TWY-012Switching Verification
Verification Checks
  • Verify ON and OFF operation
  • Verify intensity selection
  • Verify local switching and remote switching where applicable
  • Verify correct switching response and correct indication
  • Confirm no unintended operation
  • Sequence: Command → CCR → Circuit → Fixture → Status
Acceptance / Record

Acceptance Criteria: switching functions are confirmed correct at every applicable point with no unintended operation.

TC-TWY-013Control-System Verification
Verification Checks
  • Verify local control and remote control
  • Verify ON/OFF command and intensity command
  • Verify status feedback and command acknowledgement
  • Verify control mode and interlocks where applicable
  • Perform integrated control test: Control Command → CCR Response → Taxiway Circuit Response → Fixture Response → Control-System Feedback
Note

The complete ALCMS/SCADA/PLC integration remains within the applicable dedicated control-system commissioning scope; Division 07 verifies the taxiway lighting system's response to those control commands.

Acceptance / Record

Acceptance Criteria: the taxiway lighting system responds correctly to all applicable control commands.

TC-TWY-014Alarm Verification
Verification Checks (Where Provided)
  • Verify circuit failure alarm and CCR alarm
  • Verify current abnormality alarm and equipment fault
  • Verify control-system alarm and communication failure
  • Verify lighting-system fault indication
  • Test: Fault Condition → Detection → Alarm → Local Indication → Remote Indication → Reset/Recovery
  • Record alarm type, test condition, indication, remote status, recovery, and result
Acceptance / Record

Acceptance Criteria: applicable alarms and fault indications are confirmed to detect, indicate, and recover correctly.

E

Photometric & Functional Verification

2 topics
TC-TWY-015Taxiway Lighting Photometric Verification
Verification Checks
  • Verify light intensity, distribution, and beam direction as applicable
  • Verify uniformity and fixture alignment effect
  • Verify intensity performance and system-level photometric compliance
  • Before testing, confirm fixture installation and alignment are complete
  • Before testing, confirm correct circuit operating and correct intensity selected
  • Before testing, confirm CCR output verified, photometric equipment calibrated, and test configuration established
  • Record taxiway ID, lighting system, test date/condition, intensity step, photometric equipment, calibration status, measurement points, results, acceptance criterion, and pass/fail
Note

The dedicated photometry division treats photometry as a technical activity in its own right, covering equipment verification, calibration, and detailed measurement methodology. Division 07 = taxiway system-level photometric verification.

Hold Point

Hold Point: photometric equipment calibration status is verified valid before any measurement is recorded.

TC-TWY-016System Functional Test
Verification Checks
  • Verify edge, centreline, clearance-bar, exit, and rapid-exit lighting operational status
  • Verify circuit, current, intensity, and switching for each subsystem as applicable
  • Verify circuit continuity, cable testing, connector testing, and current confirmed complete
  • Verify local control, remote control where applicable, intensity control, and status feedback
  • Verify alarm functions, fault indication, and recovery
  • Verify photometric verification completed, calibration verified, and results evaluated
Acceptance / Record

Acceptance Criteria: the complete integrated taxiway lighting system passes the functional test across every subsystem, electrical, control, alarm, and photometric requirement.

F

Final Acceptance

1 topic
TC-TWY-017Taxiway Acceptance Test
Acceptance Requirements
  • Installation Compliance: correct lighting configuration, fixture locations, fixture types, and circuit configuration
  • Electrical Integrity: circuit continuity, cable integrity, connector integrity, insulation condition, and correct current
  • Functional Performance: fixture operation, switching, intensity steps, and correct system response
  • Control: local control, remote control where applicable, status feedback, and command response
  • Alarm: fault detection, alarm indication, and recovery
  • Photometric Performance: applicable measurements completed and acceptance criteria satisfied
  • Record final status: PASS, FAIL, RETEST, CONDITIONAL, or N/A
Acceptance / Record

Acceptance Criteria (final logic): Accepted Fixtures + Healthy Circuit + Healthy Cables + Healthy Connectors + Correct Current + Correct Intensity + Correct Switching + Correct Control + Correct Alarms + Photometric Compliance = ACCEPTED TAXIWAY LIGHTING SYSTEM. The system-level acceptance gate remains independent of individual fixture acceptance.

Quick Reference

Taxiway lighting commissioning checklist

A taxiway lighting commissioning checklist follows the division sequence. (1) Confirm fixtures have passed Division 05 and the system is identified against the drawing. (2) Verify taxiway edge, centreline, clearance-bar, exit and rapid-exit lighting for location, type and orientation. (3) Verify circuit identification, cables and connectors. (4) Verify CCR output and circuit current at each applicable intensity step. (5) Verify switching and control, local and remote. (6) Verify alarms where provided. (7) Verify photometric performance where required. (8) Run the system functional test and sign the taxiway acceptance test.

Taxiway lighting commissioning test

The taxiway lighting commissioning test proves the taxiway lighting works as one system. It checks that edge and centreline lighting give continuous guidance, that clearance bars and exit lighting operate on their own circuits, and that intensity steps and control commands give the expected response. Acceptance closes only when installation compliance, circuit and cable results, control and alarm behaviour and the system functional test are all documented.
