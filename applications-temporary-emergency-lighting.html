TEMPORARY & EMERGENCY AIRFIELD GROUND LIGHTING

Temporary and emergency Airfield Ground Lighting (AGL) can provide visual guidance where permanent lighting infrastructure is unavailable, damaged, under maintenance or temporarily out of service.

Temporary AGL applications may be used during runway rehabilitation, taxiway rehabilitation, construction activities, equipment replacement, emergency restoration and maintenance work. The temporary lighting arrangement should be planned around the affected airfield area, required coverage, installation conditions, power arrangement and operational requirements.

Temporary and emergency AGL applications can also cover replacement of damaged systems, maintenance work and other temporary operational requirements not addressed by permanent infrastructure.

03 · Planning

Planning Considerations

Temporary lighting applications should consider the following factors before deployment.

Required coverage — areas needing visual guidance and the extent of temporary lighting necessary
Lighting position — positioned per the approved temporary arrangement
Power arrangement — a suitable temporary power source and electrical arrangement
Deployment method — allowing installation, inspection, maintenance and removal
Electrical connections — compatible cables, connectors and transformers
Visibility — providing the required visual guidance for the application
Site conditions — ground conditions, construction activity, weather and access
Removal & maintenance — arrangements for inspection, relocation and removal

10 · FAQs

Frequently Asked Questions

Why is temporary AGL required?
Temporary AGL may be required when permanent lighting is unavailable, under construction, undergoing maintenance, damaged or temporarily out of service.
Can temporary lighting be used during runway rehabilitation?
Yes. Temporary lighting can form part of an appropriate rehabilitation strategy where required by the project.
Can temporary AGL be used during taxiway work?
Yes. Temporary lighting arrangements may be considered during taxiway construction, rehabilitation or maintenance activities.
Can temporary AGL connect to existing airport infrastructure?
Depending on the system, temporary equipment may need to interface with existing electrical infrastructure. Compatibility and the approved connection arrangement should be evaluated before implementation.
Is temporary AGL only used for emergencies?
No. Temporary AGL may also be used for planned rehabilitation, construction, maintenance, equipment replacement and other temporary operational requirements.
Does temporary AGL require testing?
Testing and verification may be required before the temporary installation is placed into operational use, depending on the project requirements.
