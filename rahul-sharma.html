Engineering With a Purpose

More Than Equipment. A Complete System.

Airfield Ground Lighting is not simply a collection of lamps, cables, transformers, and control equipment.

It is a connected engineering system.

  1. A lighting fitting is connected to a circuit.
  2. The circuit is connected to a CCR.
  3. The CCR is connected to control and monitoring.
  4. The lighting output is connected to photometry.
  5. Photometry is connected to technical requirements.
  6. Testing is connected to verification.
  7. Maintenance is connected to reliability.
  8. And all of these activities depend on people who understand the complete system.
This systems view shapes Rahul Sharma’s approach to AGL engineering.
