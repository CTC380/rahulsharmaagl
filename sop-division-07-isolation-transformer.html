SOP-141 Isolation Transformer Selection
Purpose

To ensure that the correct isolating transformer is selected for the intended AGL fixture and series-circuit application.

Selection Criteria

Verify:

  • Transformer type.
  • Primary current/rating.
  • Secondary voltage.
  • Rated VA/wattage.
  • Fixture compatibility.
  • Frequency.
  • Insulation system.
  • Environmental rating.
  • Connector compatibility.
  • Installation arrangement.
  • Applicable standards.
  • Manufacturer approval.
Procedure
  1. Identify the connected fixture/load.
  2. Determine required transformer rating.
  3. Verify primary circuit compatibility.
  4. Verify secondary output requirements.
  5. Check physical dimensions.
  6. Check connector requirements.
  7. Check environmental suitability.
  8. Verify technical compliance.
  9. Record selected model.
  10. Include transformer in the equipment schedule and BOQ.
SOP-142 Isolation Transformer Installation
Purpose

To ensure isolating transformers are installed correctly and protected against mechanical and environmental damage.

Pre-Installation Checks

Verify:

  • Correct transformer.
  • Correct rating.
  • Nameplate.
  • Physical condition.
  • Cable compatibility.
  • Connector condition.
  • Pit/base-can condition.
  • Drainage.
  • Installation orientation where specified.
Procedure
  1. Inspect transformer before installation.
  2. Confirm transformer identification.
  3. Verify installation location.
  4. Prepare the pit/base.
  5. Position the transformer as specified.
  6. Connect primary cable.
  7. Connect secondary cable.
  8. Protect cable connections.
  9. Verify drainage.
  10. Ensure the transformer is not subjected to mechanical stress.
  11. Complete inspection.
  12. Perform required tests.
SOP-143 Transformer Pit Installation
Purpose

To establish requirements for installing transformers within approved transformer pits.

Procedure
  1. Verify pit location.
  2. Inspect pit condition.
  3. Verify drainage.
  4. Remove water, dirt and debris.
  5. Inspect cable entries.
  6. Verify transformer support/position.
  7. Install transformer.
  8. Connect primary and secondary circuits.
  9. Protect connections.
  10. Verify cable routing.
  11. Confirm pit cover/interface.
  12. Perform final inspection.
Acceptance

  • Structurally sound.
  • Properly drained.

SOP-144 Transformer Positioning

Critical Rule — The transformer shall not be positioned in a manner that places continuous mechanical stress on the primary or secondary cables.

Purpose

To ensure that the transformer is correctly positioned within the approved installation location.

Procedure

  2. Confirm orientation where applicable.
  3. Position transformer without stressing cables.
  4. Maintain required clearance.
  5. Prevent movement.
  6. Ensure cable entries are not under tension.
  7. Verify accessibility for maintenance.
  8. Record final position.

SOP-151 Transformer Insulation Testing

Critical Rule — The test voltage and acceptance criteria shall not be invented or assumed; use the approved equipment/test specification.

Purpose

To assess insulation condition of the transformer and associated accessible connections where specified.

Preconditions

Before testing:

  • Obtain authorization.
  • Isolate the circuit.
  • Apply LOTO.
  • Verify absence of voltage.
  • Disconnect sensitive equipment where required.
  • Confirm test instrument suitability.
  • Establish a safe test area.
Procedure
  1. Identify transformer.
  2. Inspect transformer.
  3. Confirm test configuration.
  4. Select approved test voltage.
  5. Perform insulation test according to manufacturer/project requirements.
  6. Record test duration and result.
  7. Discharge the tested circuit after testing.
  8. Restore connections.
  9. Record the result in the transformer test report.

SOP-154 Transformer Output Verification

Procedure

  9. Record the result.

SOP-158 Transformer Water Protection
Purpose

To prevent water ingress and water-related deterioration of isolating transformers and associated connections.

Procedure

Inspect:

  • Transformer pit drainage.
  • Cable entry sealing.
  • Connector sealing.
  • Transformer housing/sealing.
  • Water accumulation.
  • Pit cover/interface.
  • Drainage outlet.
Corrective Action

If water is present:

  1. Do not automatically re-energize.
  2. Identify the source.
  3. Isolate the equipment as required.
  4. Remove standing water using an approved safe method.
  5. Inspect electrical components.
  6. Test insulation/electrical condition as required.
  7. Correct the drainage/sealing problem.
  8. Obtain technical clearance before return to service.

B. Quality Hold Points (HP-01 → HP-09)

Work shall not proceed past a hold point until the associated inspection or verification has been completed and released.

  • HP-01 — Incoming Inspection: Verify model, rating, serial number and physical condition.
  • HP-02 — Pit Release: Verify pit, drainage and cable-entry condition before installation.
  • HP-03 — Transformer Position: Verify location and mechanical condition before connection.
  • HP-04 — Primary Connection: Inspect primary connection before closure.
  • HP-05 — Secondary Connection: Inspect connector and fixture connection.
  • HP-06 — Electrical Testing: Complete applicable tests before energization.
  • HP-07 — Water Protection: Verify absence of unacceptable water ingress/accumulation.
  • HP-08 — Controlled Energization: Energize only after required checks are complete.
  • HP-09 — Final Acceptance: Complete documentation before equipment release.

D. Transformer Failure Modes to Monitor

The following conditions should be specifically monitored during AGL maintenance:

  1. Moisture Ingress — Persistent moisture can deteriorate insulation and connections.
  2. Connector Failure — Loose, corroded or poorly sealed connections can cause circuit interruption.
  3. Incorrect Transformer Rating — An incorrectly selected transformer can result in improper fixture operation or equipment stress.
  4. Cable Mechanical Stress — Excessive cable tension at the transformer connection can cause premature failure.
  5. Poor Drainage — Water accumulation around the transformer installation can accelerate deterioration.
  6. Physical Damage — Impact during installation or maintenance may compromise the transformer housing or connections.
  7. Incorrect Identification — Wrong transformer identification can lead to incorrect circuit maintenance.
  8. Improper Testing — Incorrect test configuration or unsuitable test voltage can produce misleading results or damage equipment.

Division 07 — AGL Isolation Transformer Golden Rule

"An isolating transformer is a critical interface between the AGL series circuit and the individual light. Correct selection, correct rating, correct connection, effective water protection, proper connector installation, controlled testing and complete documentation are essential for reliable AGL operation."

Frequently Asked Questions

Quick answers about Isolation Transformer SOPs in the AGL SOP Library.

Q1What is Division 07 — Isolation Transformer SOPs?

Division 07 is a set of 24 procedures (SOP-141 to SOP-164, Rev 0) for AGL series-circuit isolating transformers in transformer pits, base cans, ducts or other approved locations. It covers selection, positioning, primary and secondary connection, nameplate and polarity verification, insulation, continuity, ratio and output testing, water protection, handling, storage, replacement, removal and final acceptance.

Q2Where do transformer ratings and test limits come from?

From the approved transformer datasheet, manufacturer's instructions, project specification and applicable standards. This includes ratings, primary and secondary characteristics, insulation requirements, test voltages, acceptable limits and polarity or phase relationships. Generic test values must not be substituted.

Q3Can insulation test voltage be assumed?

No. The test voltage and acceptance criteria must not be invented or assumed; they come from the approved equipment or test specification (SOP-151). Testing requires authorization, isolation, LOTO, verification of absence of voltage and discharge of the tested circuit afterwards.

Q4How is transformer polarity verified?

AGL series circuits are not equivalent to conventional single-phase distribution, so polarity verification follows the actual transformer design and the manufacturer's approved test method (SOP-150). Identify the terminals, review the documentation, use an approved test method and record the result.

Q5What should happen if water is found around an isolating transformer?

Do not automatically re-energize (SOP-158). Identify the source, isolate the equipment as required, remove standing water by an approved safe method, inspect the components, test insulation and electrical condition as required, correct the drainage or sealing problem and obtain technical clearance before return to service.

Q6What does transformer acceptance inspection check?

SOP-163 checks manufacturer, model, rating, nameplate and serial number, physical condition, location, primary and secondary connections, connector condition, cable identification, pit condition, drainage and water protection. Insulation, continuity, ratio and output tests are confirmed where required, and documentation must be complete.

Q7What are the quality hold points for isolating transformers?

Incoming inspection, pit release, transformer position, primary connection, secondary connection, electrical testing, water protection, controlled energization and final acceptance.
