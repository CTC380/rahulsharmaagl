SOP-79 Base Can Installation
Purpose

To establish a controlled procedure for installing AGL base cans to the approved location, elevation, orientation and structural requirements.

Pre-Installation Checks

Verify:

  • Approved drawing is available.
  • Correct base-can type and size.
  • Correct location.
  • Foundation details.
  • Cable route.
  • Drainage arrangement.
  • Base-can elevation.
  • Required orientation.
  • Mounting hardware.
  • Manufacturer installation instructions.
Procedure
  1. Establish the approved installation coordinates.
  2. Prepare the excavation/foundation.
  3. Verify formation condition.
  4. Install required reinforcement/formwork where specified.
  5. Position the base can.
  6. Verify location and orientation.
  7. Connect cable entries.
  8. Provide the specified drainage arrangement.
  9. Secure the base can against movement.
  10. Place concrete/grout according to the approved design.
  11. Maintain the required elevation.
  12. Allow curing.
  13. Clean the base-can interior.
  14. Inspect cable entries and drainage.
  15. Record installation results.
Acceptance

The base can shall be:

  • Correctly positioned.
  • Correctly aligned.
  • Stable.
  • At the approved elevation.
  • Free from debris.
  • Properly drained.
  • Ready for fixture installation.
SOP-80 Base Can Positioning

Critical Requirement — Base-can position shall be established from approved survey/control references, not by visual estimation alone.

Purpose

To ensure that each base can is installed at the correct horizontal position.

Procedure
  1. Establish the survey reference.
  2. Verify the approved coordinates.
  3. Mark the installation point.
  4. Position the base can.
  5. Check longitudinal position.
  6. Check lateral position.
  7. Verify relationship with runway/taxiway centerline or edge.
  8. Confirm required clearance.
  9. Obtain survey verification before permanent fixing.
  10. Record the final position.
SOP-81 Base Can Level Verification
Purpose

To ensure that the base can is installed at the correct elevation relative to the finished pavement.

Procedure
  1. Establish the finished pavement reference.
  2. Measure base-can elevation.
  3. Check all relevant points.
  4. Compare against approved drawing tolerance.
  5. Correct the position before concrete/grout becomes permanent.
  6. Recheck after fixing.
  7. Record the result.
Acceptance

The installed base can shall meet the approved elevation and tolerance requirements.

SOP-82 Base Can Alignment

Note — Incorrect alignment can affect fixture orientation and subsequent photometric performance.

Purpose

To ensure correct angular and directional alignment of the base can.

Procedure
  1. Identify the required reference direction.
  2. Position the base can.
  3. Align mounting holes/slots.
  4. Verify orientation using approved survey equipment.
  5. Secure temporarily.
  6. Recheck alignment.
  7. Permanently fix the base can.
  8. Conduct final verification.
SOP-83 Base Can Grouting

Prohibited Condition — Do not compensate for an incorrectly positioned base can by using excessive or uncontrolled grout thickness.

Purpose

To establish the procedure for securing and stabilizing a base can using the specified grout or concrete system.

Pre-Grouting Checks

Verify:

  • Base can position.
  • Elevation.
  • Alignment.
  • Cable entry.
  • Drainage.
  • Formwork.
  • Cleanliness.
  • Grout specification.
  • Environmental conditions.
Procedure
  1. Prepare the grout according to the approved specification.
  2. Prevent movement of the base can.
  3. Place grout uniformly.
  4. Avoid trapping unwanted voids.
  5. Maintain required elevation.
  6. Finish the surface appropriately.
  7. Protect the installation during curing.
  8. Remove temporary supports only after adequate strength is achieved.
  9. Conduct final inspection.
SOP-84 Base Can Drainage

Important — The drainage arrangement shall not compromise pavement integrity or permit uncontrolled water migration into electrical infrastructure.

Purpose

To prevent water accumulation inside base cans and associated equipment cavities.

Procedure

Verify:

  • Drainage outlet.
  • Drainage path.
  • Connection to approved drainage system where applicable.
  • Absence of blockage.
  • Suitable surrounding formation.
  • No water accumulation.
Inspection

A drainage check shall be performed before fixture installation.

SOP-85 Base Can Earthing

Critical Rule — Do not rely on incidental contact between the base can and surrounding concrete or earth as a designed electrical connection.

Purpose

To provide the required bonding/earthing arrangement for metallic AGL base cans and associated equipment where specified.

Procedure
  1. Review approved earthing design.
  2. Identify the designated earth/bonding point.
  3. Inspect the conductor.
  4. Install the approved connection.
  5. Ensure the connection is mechanically secure.
  6. Protect the connection against damage/corrosion.
  7. Perform required continuity/resistance verification.
  8. Record the test result.
SOP-86 Base Can Cable Entry
Purpose

To ensure that cables enter the base can safely without damage or excessive mechanical stress.

Procedure
  1. Verify cable route.
  2. Identify entry point.
  3. Inspect cable condition.
  4. Provide the approved entry arrangement.
  5. Prevent sharp edges from contacting cable insulation.
  6. Maintain minimum bend radius.
  7. Prevent excessive pulling force.
  8. Secure the cable as specified.
  9. Maintain identification.
  10. Inspect before closing the installation.
SOP-87 Base Can Cable Sealing
Purpose

To prevent water, moisture, contaminants and unwanted material from entering the base can through cable entries.

Procedure
  1. Clean cable and entry area.
  2. Verify correct sealing components.
  3. Install approved glands/seals or sealing system.
  4. Ensure correct compression.
  5. Avoid damaging cable insulation.
  6. Verify sealing around all relevant entries.
  7. Inspect after installation.
  8. Record any defective or incomplete sealing.
Acceptance

Cable entries shall provide the protection specified by the approved design and equipment manufacturer.

  12. Conduct final inspection.
SOP-89 Deep Base Installation

  6. Verify vertical alignment.

SOP-90 Light Fixture Mounting
Purpose

To ensure AGL fixtures are correctly mounted and mechanically secured.

Procedure
  1. Verify correct fixture.
  2. Inspect fixture before installation.
  3. Verify mounting surface.
  4. Inspect gasket/sealing arrangement.
  5. Install approved hardware.
  6. Position fixture correctly.
  7. Tighten fasteners according to approved requirements.
  8. Verify orientation.
  9. Verify height.
  10. Verify alignment.

SOP-91 Fixture Mounting Torque

Critical Rule — Never substitute a generic torque value when the fixture manufacturer provides a specific approved value. Over-tightening may damage the fixture or mounting surface; under-tightening may permit movement or loosening.

Purpose

To ensure fixture fasteners are tightened according to approved manufacturer's requirements.

Requirements

Use:

  • Calibrated torque wrench.
  • Correct socket/tool.
  • Manufacturer/project-specified torque.
  • Correct fastener type.
Procedure
  1. Verify torque requirement.
  2. Check torque-wrench calibration status.
  3. Inspect threads and hardware.
  4. Tighten progressively where required.
  5. Apply final specified torque.
  6. Record torque verification where required.
SOP-92 Frangible Coupling Installation

Critical Requirement — The frangible element shall not be modified or substituted with an unapproved component.

Purpose

To ensure frangible couplings are installed correctly and maintain the intended frangibility characteristics.

Procedure
  1. Verify approved coupling type.
  2. Check identification.
  3. Inspect for damage.
  4. Verify mounting arrangement.
  5. Install according to manufacturer instructions.
  6. Do not modify, drill, weld or otherwise alter the coupling.
  7. Verify fixture height.
  8. Verify vertical alignment.
  9. Check fasteners.
  10. Record installation.

SOP-99 Pavement Interface Inspection

Critical Requirement — The final AGL installation shall provide a safe and controlled transition between the fixture/base-can assembly and the surrounding pavement, without creating an unacceptable aircraft, vehicle, drainage, structural or FOD hazard.

Purpose

To ensure that AGL equipment installed within or adjacent to pavement is correctly integrated with the pavement system.

Inspection Items

Geometry

  • Fixture position.
  • Fixture elevation.
  • Base-can elevation.
  • Pavement level.
  • Surface transition.
  • Clearance.

Mechanical

  • Fixture stability.
  • Mounting condition.
  • Fasteners.
  • Frangible coupling.
  • Base-can integrity.

Civil

  • Concrete/grout condition.
  • Cracks.
  • Voids.
  • Settlement.
  • Joint condition.
  • Drainage.

Electrical

  • Cable entry.
  • Cable sealing.
  • Transformer connection.
  • Connector condition.

Operational

Division-Wide Requirements

Common installation sequence, hold points, defect checklist and the golden rule that apply across all 21 Division 04 procedures.

A. Installation Quality Control Sequence

The recommended installation sequence is:

SURVEY EXCAVATE PREPARE FOUNDATION POSITION BASE CAN VERIFY LEVEL VERIFY ALIGNMENT CABLE ENTRY DRAINAGE EARTHING/BONDING FIX/CONCRETE/GROUT CURE INSPECT INSTALL FIXTURE TORQUE ALIGN HEIGHT CHECK PAVEMENT INTERFACE CHECK ELECTRICAL CONNECTION TEST FOD INSPECTION RELEASE

B. Critical Hold Points

The following activities should be treated as inspection hold points where required by the project ITP:

  • HP-01 Base Can Location — Do not permanently fix the base until location has been verified.
  • HP-02 Base Can Elevation — Verify elevation before concrete/grouting.
  • HP-03 Base Can Orientation — Verify orientation before permanent fixing.
  • HP-04 Cable Entry — Inspect cable entry and sealing before closure.
  • HP-05 Drainage — Verify drainage before final closure.
  • HP-06 Foundation — Inspect foundation condition before fixture installation.
  • HP-07 Fixture Mounting — Verify hardware and mounting arrangement.
  • HP-08 Fixture Height — Measure final fixture elevation.
  • HP-09 Fixture Orientation — Verify directional orientation before final acceptance.
  • HP-10 Pavement Interface — Inspect the final transition between AGL equipment and pavement.

C. Common Installation Defects to Check

During inspection, specifically look for:

  • Incorrect base-can location
  • Incorrect elevation
  • Incorrect orientation
  • Base-can movement
  • Poor grouting
  • Cracked concrete
  • Blocked drainage
  • Unsealed cable entries
  • Damaged cable insulation
  • Incorrect transformer connection
  • Loose fasteners
  • Incorrect torque
  • Damaged frangible coupling
  • Fixture misalignment
  • Incorrect fixture height
  • Incorrect fixture orientation
  • Pavement settlement
  • Pavement/interface gaps
  • Water accumulation
  • Loose material / FOD

D. Mandatory Safety Philosophy

Never

  • Fix a base can before location, elevation and orientation are verified.
  • Compensate for a misplaced base can with excessive grout thickness.
  • Use a generic torque value when a manufacturer-specific value exists.
  • Modify, drill or weld a frangible coupling.
  • Rely on incidental contact as a designed earth/bonding connection.

Always

  • Work from approved drawings and manufacturer instructions.
  • Verify hold points before proceeding to the next stage.
  • Confirm drainage, cable sealing and earthing before closure.
  • Recheck height, orientation and alignment after final tightening.
  • Conduct a joint civil/electrical pavement-interface inspection and FOD check before release.

Division 04 — Golden Rule

"Install the AGL foundation correctly before installing the light. Verify location, elevation, alignment, drainage, cable entry and pavement interface before final fixing. Then mount, torque, orient and align the fixture according to the approved design and manufacturer requirements."

Division 04 — SOP Approval Principle

Each airport should adapt these SOPs to its own approved project drawings, equipment manufacturer's installation instructions, civil works specifications, structural/geotechnical requirements, ITP/hold-point regime, and applicable national and international airport infrastructure requirements.

Approved project drawings and manufacturer-specific values shall take precedence wherever they impose more restrictive or more specific requirements than these SOPs.

Frequently Asked Questions

Quick answers about Civil Works, Base Cans & Mounting SOPs in the AGL SOP Library.

Q1What is Division 04 — Civil Works, Base Cans & Mounting SOPs?

Division 04 is a set of 21 procedures (SOP-79 to SOP-99, Rev 0) for the civil and mechanical installation of elevated and inset AGL fixtures. It covers base-can positioning, level and alignment, grouting, drainage, earthing, cable entry and sealing, shallow and deep bases, fixture mounting and torque, frangible couplings, and foundation, civil-interface and pavement-interface inspection.

Q2Can generic dimensions or torque values be used?

No. Final dimensions, tolerances, installation depths, torque values, drainage arrangements, grout specifications and foundation details must come from approved project drawings, the manufacturer's installation instructions and applicable airport or regulatory requirements. Generic values must not be substituted where manufacturer-specific values exist.

Q3What are the critical hold points for base cans and fixtures?

Where required by the project ITP, hold points HP-01 to HP-10 cover base-can location, elevation and orientation, cable entry, drainage, foundation, fixture mounting, fixture height, fixture orientation and the pavement interface. The base must not be permanently fixed until its location has been verified.

Q4What is the required installation sequence?

Survey, excavate, prepare foundation, position the base can, verify level and alignment, then cable entry, drainage, earthing and bonding, fixing or grouting, cure and inspect. Then install the fixture, torque, align, check height, check the pavement interface, make the electrical connection, test, do the FOD inspection and release.

Q5How should fixture mounting torque be set?

Never substitute a generic torque value when the manufacturer provides an approved one (SOP-91). Use a calibrated torque wrench, verify its calibration status, tighten progressively where required to the specified torque, and record the verification. Over-tightening can damage the fixture or surface; under-tightening can allow movement.

Q6What are the rules for frangible couplings?

The frangible element must not be modified or replaced with an unapproved component (SOP-92). Verify the approved type and identification, inspect for damage, install per manufacturer instructions, and do not drill, weld or otherwise alter the coupling. Then verify fixture height and vertical alignment and record the installation.

Q7How is base-can drainage and cable sealing checked?

Drainage (SOP-84) must not compromise pavement integrity or let water migrate into electrical infrastructure. Verify the outlet, path, absence of blockage and no water accumulation before fixture installation. Cable entries (SOP-87) must be cleaned, fitted with approved glands or seals at the correct compression, inspected and any defective sealing recorded.

Q8Which installation defects should be checked during inspection?

Incorrect base-can location, elevation or orientation, base-can movement, poor grouting, cracked concrete, blocked drainage, unsealed cable entries, damaged cable insulation, incorrect transformer connection, loose fasteners, incorrect torque, damaged frangible coupling, and incorrect fixture alignment, height or orientation.
