Photometry & Optical Testing Forms

The 25 forms that establish a controlled photometric record for AGL lighting systems — from photometry planning and equipment verification through field measurement, data validation, compliance assessment, defect identification, retest, acceptance and long-term trend analysis. Grouped into 11 categories spanning testing & inspection, system-specific photometry, intensity/colour/chromaticity, alignment & measurement grid, photometer control, field data management, non-conformance & retest, acceptance & reporting, trend & performance analysis, defect & equipment control, and project closeout, this division is the controlled optical-performance database for the AGL system — working closely with Division 06 (Testing), Division 14 (Calibration & Measurement), Division 05 (Inspection), Division 09 (Failure Investigation) and Division 11 (Asset Management).

A · Photometry Testing & Inspection

2 of 2 forms
PHO-01 Photometry Test Sheet Test Sheet
Purpose

The primary field/test form for recording photometric measurements of AGL lighting systems.

Key Fields
  • Project/Airport, location, runway/taxiway/approach/PAPI
  • Test date, test time, test reference, work order, test engineer
  • Weather/environmental conditions
  • Photometer ID, manufacturer, model, serial number, calibration status, calibration due date
  • Fixture type, fixture ID, circuit ID, CCR setting/intensity step
  • Test configuration, measurement position/grid
  • Measurement point, required value, measured value, unit, acceptance criterion, result, remarks
  • Final result: Pass / Fail / Conditional
PHO-02 Photometry Inspection Form Inspection
Purpose

To record visual and technical inspection before photometric measurement.

Key Fields
  • Fixture condition, lens/optical condition, housing condition
  • Mounting, alignment, elevation, orientation
  • Contamination, physical damage
  • Circuit identification, CCR condition, transformer condition
  • Connector condition, cable condition
  • Measurement area clear, equipment calibrated
  • Measurement grid established, environmental conditions suitable
  • Result: Ready for Photometric Test / Not Ready

B · System-Specific Photometry

5 of 5 forms
PHO-03 Runway Photometry Record Record
Purpose

To record photometric measurements associated with runway lighting.

Key Fields
  • Airport, runway, runway direction, fixture type, circuit, CCR, test date
  • Runway station, longitudinal position, transverse position
  • Fixture position, measurement point
  • Required intensity, measured intensity, unit, compliance status
  • Uniformity, deficient locations, failed fixtures
  • Alignment issues, intensity abnormalities
  • Summary: total points, compliant points, non-compliant points, overall result
PHO-04 Taxiway Photometry Record Record
Purpose

To record optical performance of taxiway lighting.

Key Fields
  • Taxiway, segment/location, fixture type, circuit, CCR/intensity setting, test date
  • Fixture/location, measurement point, required value, actual value, result
  • Defects: low intensity, non-uniform output
  • Defects: misalignment, optical contamination, damaged fixture
PHO-05 Approach Lighting Photometry Record
Purpose

To record photometric performance of approach lighting systems.

Key Fields
  • Approach system, runway, approach direction, fixture type, circuit, test configuration
  • Light station, fixture position, measurement point
  • Intensity, required criterion, result
  • Assessment: intensity, alignment, uniformity where applicable
  • Assessment: optical condition, system compliance
PHO-06 PAPI Photometry Record Record
Purpose

To document optical performance measurements of PAPI/APAPI systems.

Key Fields
  • Runway, PAPI side, unit/box identification, number of units, fixture type, location
  • Unit, beam information, intensity, alignment
  • Elevation/angle reference where applicable, required criterion, measured result
  • Assessment: optical performance, beam alignment
  • Assessment: colour performance, unit-to-unit consistency, overall compliance
PHO-07 Fixture Photometry Record Record
Purpose

To establish an individual fixture-level photometric record.

Key Fields
  • Fixture ID, manufacturer, model, serial number, fixture type
  • Location, circuit, transformer
  • Optical data: intensity, beam direction, alignment, colour, operating condition
  • Condition: lens, optical assembly, LED/light source
  • Condition: housing, contamination, physical damage
  • Result: Pass / Fail

PHO-13 Photometer Setup Checklist Checklist
Purpose

To verify correct setup of the photometric measurement system before testing.

Key Fields
  • Photometer ID, sensor/detector, manufacturer, model, serial number, calibration status
  • Equipment identification confirmed / calibration valid
  • Sensor installed correctly / instrument zero/reference checked where applicable
  • Measurement range selected / grid loaded/established
  • Measurement orientation verified / accessories installed
  • Environmental conditions recorded / data acquisition system checked
  • Final: Setup Accepted / Not Accepted
PHO-14 Photometer Calibration Verification Verification
Purpose

To verify the calibration status and suitability of the photometer immediately before photometric testing.

Key Fields
  • Photometer ID, manufacturer, model, serial number
  • Calibration certificate, calibration date, calibration due date
  • Certificate valid / instrument identity matches
  • Calibration range suitable / physical condition acceptable
  • Functional verification completed
  • Result: Valid / Invalid
This form should directly reference Division 14 — Calibration & Measurement.

PHO-15 Photometry Field Record Field Record
Purpose

To provide the complete field record generated during actual photometric measurements.

Key Fields
  • Date, time, location, weather, ambient conditions, operational status
  • Photometer, calibration status, accessories, software/data acquisition system where applicable
  • Measurement record: point, fixture, setting, required, measured, result, remarks
  • Field sign-off: test engineer, witness, client/consultant where applicable
PHO-16 Photometry Data Validation Validation
Purpose

To verify that recorded photometric data is complete, consistent and technically usable before issuing the final report.

Key Fields
  • Correct project/location / correct measurement grid
  • Correct fixture identification / correct test configuration
  • Photometer identification recorded / calibration valid
  • All required points measured / units consistent
  • No unexplained missing data / abnormal values investigated
  • Data transcription verified / calculations checked
  • Validation result: Valid / Invalid / Requires Review

G · Non-Conformance & Retest

2 of 2 forms
PHO-17 Photometry Non-Conformance NCR
Purpose

To record photometric results that fail the applicable acceptance requirement.

Key Fields
  • NCR/photometry NC no., project, location, fixture/system
  • Measurement point, required value, measured value, deviation
  • Classification: Minor / Major / Critical
  • Possible causes: fixture failure, misalignment, optical contamination, LED/light-source degradation
  • Possible causes: incorrect installation, circuit issue, CCR/intensity issue, measurement/setup issue
  • Action: adjustment, cleaning, fixture replacement, circuit investigation, recalibration/reverification, retest
PHO-18 Photometry Retest Record Record
Purpose

To document measurements repeated after correction of a photometric non-conformance.

Key Fields
  • References: original test, non-conformance, corrective action, work order, fixture/system
  • Original measurement, corrected measurement, required value, retest value, result
  • Closure: corrective action verified / retest passed / NC closed

Frequently Asked Questions

Quick answers about the 25 Division 15 Photometry & Optical Testing forms, how they are grouped, and how they connect to the rest of the library.

Q1What is Division 15 — Photometry & Optical Testing Forms in the AGL Engineering Forms & Templates library?

Division 15 is a set of 25 photometry & optical testing forms (PHO-01 to PHO-25) in the AGL Engineering Forms & Templates library. It establishes a controlled photometric record for AGL lighting systems, from photometry planning and equipment verification through field measurement, data validation, compliance assessment, retest, acceptance and long-term trend analysis. The forms are grouped into 11 categories: Photometry Testing & Inspection, System-Specific Photometry, Intensity, Colour & Chromaticity, Alignment & Measurement Grid, Photometer Control, Field Data Management, Non-Conformance & Retest, Acceptance & Reporting, Trend & Performance Analysis, Defect & Equipment Control and Project Closeout.

Q2Which forms are included in Photometry Testing & Inspection, System-Specific Photometry and Intensity, Colour & Chromaticity?

Photometry Testing & Inspection (2 forms): PHO-01 Photometry Test Sheet, PHO-02 Photometry Inspection Form; System-Specific Photometry (5 forms): PHO-03 Runway Photometry Record, PHO-04 Taxiway Photometry Record, PHO-05 Approach Lighting Photometry, PHO-06 PAPI Photometry Record, PHO-07 Fixture Photometry Record; Intensity, Colour & Chromaticity (3 forms): PHO-08 Light Intensity Record, PHO-09 Colour Measurement Record, PHO-10 Chromaticity Record.

Q3Which forms are included in Alignment & Measurement Grid, Photometer Control, Field Data Management and Non-Conformance & Retest?

Alignment & Measurement Grid (2 forms): PHO-11 Beam Alignment Record, PHO-12 Measurement Grid Sheet; Photometer Control (2 forms): PHO-13 Photometer Setup Checklist, PHO-14 Photometer Calibration Verification; Field Data Management (2 forms): PHO-15 Photometry Field Record, PHO-16 Photometry Data Validation; Non-Conformance & Retest (2 forms): PHO-17 Photometry Non-Conformance, PHO-18 Photometry Retest Record.

Q4Which forms are included in Acceptance & Reporting, Trend & Performance Analysis, Defect & Equipment Control and Project Closeout?

Acceptance & Reporting (2 forms): PHO-19 Photometry Acceptance Certificate, PHO-20 Photometry Summary Report; Trend & Performance Analysis (2 forms): PHO-21 Photometry Trend Record, PHO-22 Before/After Photometry Record; Defect & Equipment Control (2 forms): PHO-23 Photometric Defect Register, PHO-24 Photometry Equipment Log; Project Closeout (1 form): PHO-25 Photometry Project Closeout.

Q5What is the difference between the Light Intensity, Colour Measurement and Chromaticity records?

The Light Intensity Record (PHO-08) records measured luminous intensity at defined measurement positions. The Colour Measurement Record (PHO-09) records measured colour characteristics of AGL light sources, and the Chromaticity Record (PHO-10) maintains the detailed chromaticity measurement record. The Measurement Grid Sheet (PHO-12) establishes the standardized measurement points, and the Beam Alignment Record (PHO-11) documents optical beam alignment.

Q6What happens when photometric results fail acceptance?

The Photometry Non-Conformance (PHO-17) records results that fail the applicable acceptance requirement, and the Photometry Retest Record (PHO-18) documents measurements repeated after correction. Acceptance is formally confirmed on the Photometry Acceptance Certificate (PHO-19), summarized in the Photometry Summary Report (PHO-20), and defects are kept on the Photometric Defect Register (PHO-23).

Q7How is the photometer itself controlled?

The Photometer Setup Checklist (PHO-13) verifies correct setup of the measurement system before testing, and the Photometer Calibration Verification (PHO-14) verifies calibration status and suitability immediately before testing. The Photometry Equipment Log (PHO-24) maintains usage and traceability of the equipment.

Q8Can the forms in Division 15 be downloaded and filled in?

Yes. Each form has a Download as Word button that produces an uncontrolled sample Word template with letterhead, a document-control block, ruled entry fields and a three-tier approval table, ready to print or fill on-screen. Checklist-format forms such as Photometer Setup Checklist (PHO-13) download with a checkbox and a verified-by line for each item. The forms can also be searched by keyword or form ID.

Q9How does Division 15 connect to the rest of the photometry record?

Division 15 is the AGL photometric measurement, optical performance, compliance, validation and trend-analysis layer. Every measurement should trace back to an approved measurement grid and a calibrated photometer, and forward into the Photometry Acceptance Certificate, the Summary Report and long-term trend records.
