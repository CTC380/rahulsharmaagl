A · Project Completion & Certification

6 of 6 forms
HCO-01 Project Completion Checklist Checklist
Purpose

The master checklist establishing whether the project is ready for formal completion and handover, consolidating verification across every functional area of the AGL project.

Key Fields
  • Project management: scope completed, milestones completed, approved changes incorporated, outstanding works identified
  • Engineering: approved drawings completed, calculations finalized, design changes incorporated
  • Procurement: approved materials supplied, certificates received, warranty documents received, spare parts available
  • Installation: fixtures, CCRs, transformers, cables, connectors, earthing, PAPI, signs, ALCMS installed
  • Testing: electrical, equipment, and functional testing completed, photometry completed, FAT/SAT completed where applicable
  • Quality: inspections completed, NCRs closed, observations closed, punch list closed
  • Documentation: as-built drawings, O&M manuals, test reports, asset register, certificates
  • Final status: Complete / Incomplete / Complete with approved outstanding items
Feeds every completion certificate in Section A below.

Frequently Asked Questions

Quick answers about the 25 Division 23 handover and closeout forms, from completion certificates to the final project archive.

Q1What is Division 23 — Project Handover & Closeout Forms in the AGL Engineering Forms & Templates library?

Division 23 is a set of 25 forms (HCO-01 to HCO-25) that carry an AGL project from construction through completion, inspection, testing, commissioning, defect closure, acceptance, handover, operational readiness, final closeout and archive. The forms are grouped into six categories. It is the final consolidation layer of the library and the gateway from project execution into operational asset management.

Q2What must be checked before an AGL project is formally complete?

The Project Completion Checklist (HCO-01) is the master checklist that establishes whether the project is ready for formal completion and handover, consolidating verification across project management, engineering, procurement, installation and the other functional areas. It is supported by five completion certificates: Mechanical (HCO-02), Electrical (HCO-03), Installation (HCO-04), Testing (HCO-05) and Commissioning (HCO-06).

Q3What is the difference between Punch List Closure, Snag Closure and Defect Closure?

Punch List Closure (HCO-07) tracks each punch-list item from identification to verified closure, moving through Open, Assigned, Corrected, Verified and Closed. Snag Closure (HCO-08) verifies closure of installation and workmanship snags across asset categories, evidenced by before-and-after photographs. Defect Closure (HCO-09) closes technical defects found during execution; a defect that has been corrected is not automatically closed, because closure requires verification of the corrective action.

Q4What is the difference between Final Acceptance, the Taking-Over Certificate and the Handover Certificate?

Final Inspection (HCO-10) is the comprehensive inspection of the AGL, physical, electrical and documentation aspects before acceptance. Final Acceptance (HCO-11) records formal acceptance against defined conditions, with a result of Accepted, Accepted with Approved Outstanding Items, or Not Accepted. The Taking-Over Certificate (HCO-12) documents transfer of the completed works to the client or owner where the contract provides for it, and the Handover Certificate (HCO-13) confirms formal transfer of the AGL system, documentation and deliverables to the operating organization.

Q5Which handover documents are covered in Division 23?

The Handover Documentation category has six forms: As-Built Drawing Checklist (HCO-14), O&M Manual Checklist (HCO-15), Test Report Handover (HCO-16), Asset Register Handover (HCO-17), Spare Parts Handover (HCO-18) and Warranty Handover (HCO-19). The As-Built Drawing Checklist verifies the correct drawing number and revision against the actual installed condition, and the Warranty Handover links directly to the asset register handover so warranty documents stay traceable.

Q6How is operator training documented?

The Training Record (HCO-20) documents training delivered to airport or operator personnel, including the topic, trainer, participants, duration and attendance, across topics such as AGL system operation, CCR operation, ALCMS, PAPI, airfield signs, maintenance, troubleshooting, electrical safety, LOTO, photometry, testing and emergency response. The Training Completion Certificate (HCO-21) confirms completion of that training.

Q7What do the lessons learned, documentation closeout, final report and archive forms do?

Project Lessons Learned (HCO-22) captures knowledge that can improve future AGL projects and feeds the AGL Knowledge Base. Project Documentation Closeout (HCO-23) confirms all project documentation has been completed, approved and archived across every functional area. The Final Project Report (HCO-24) is the final consolidated technical and management report, and the Project Archive Record (HCO-25) establishes the final controlled archive, including digital and physical location, custodian, retention requirement and retrieval status.

Q8Can the Division 23 forms be downloaded and filled in?

Yes. Each entry has a Download as Word button that produces an uncontrolled sample Word template with letterhead, a document-control block, ruled entry fields and a three-tier approval table (Prepared By, Reviewed By, Approved By) as layout only, to be adapted under your own document-control system before use.

Q9How does Division 23 connect to the other divisions?

Division 23 reconciles the evidence generated by Divisions 01 to 22 and shows, through records and not just a signature, that the airport or operator can take over the AGL system with complete technical, operational, asset, safety, compliance and documentary confidence. It draws execution evidence from Division 01, installed-condition evidence from Division 04, test evidence from Division 06, the asset register from Division 11 and controlled records from Division 20.
