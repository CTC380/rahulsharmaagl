A · Calibration Control

5 of 5 forms
CAL-01 Calibration Register Register
Purpose

Maintains the master register of every test and measurement instrument in use across AGL testing, commissioning, and maintenance, tying each one to its current calibration status.

Key Fields
  • Instrument ID, type, manufacturer, model, serial number
  • Range and accuracy/class
  • Department, owner/custodian, storage location
  • Calibration certificate no., laboratory, date, due date, interval
  • Status: calibrated / due for calibration / overdue / out of calibration / under repair / retired
CAL-02 Calibration Certificate Record Record
Purpose

Maintains traceability of the calibration certificate issued for each instrument and confirms the certificate was reviewed and accepted before the instrument entered service.

Key Fields
  • Instrument ID, type, manufacturer, model, serial number
  • Certificate no., calibration laboratory
  • Calibration date, due date, calibration standard/reference, result, certificate location
  • Verification: certificate reviewed, identity matches, calibration period valid, required range covered, certificate accepted
  • Reviewed by, review date, remarks
CAL-03 Instrument Identification Sheet Reference Sheet
Purpose

Creates the technical identity record for each measurement instrument, so its range, accuracy, and ownership are documented once and referenced by every other form in the division.

Key Fields
  • Instrument ID, type, manufacturer, model, serial number, part number
  • Range, resolution, accuracy, measurement parameters
  • Department, custodian, location, purchase date, supplier
  • Status: active / in storage / under calibration / under repair / retired
CAL-04 Instrument Issue Record Record
Purpose

Records the issue of test equipment to authorized personnel, confirming the instrument was calibrated, functional, and complete before it left the store.

Key Fields
  • Issue no., instrument ID, description, serial number
  • Issued to, department/company, date/time issued
  • Intended work, project/location, work order, permit where applicable
  • Pre-issue verification: calibration valid, instrument functional, physical condition acceptable, accessories complete, battery/power condition, calibration label visible
  • Issued by, received by
CAL-05 Instrument Return Record Record
Purpose

Documents the return of test equipment after use and captures its condition on return, so any damage or calibration concern is flagged before the instrument is reissued.

Key Fields
  • Return no., instrument ID, returned by, date/time
  • Work/project, usage duration
  • Return inspection: physical condition, display/function, leads/probes, accessories, battery, damage, calibration label
  • Status: returned to service / cleaning required / verification required / repair required / calibration required / quarantine

B · Instrument Verification

2 of 2 forms
CAL-06 Instrument Verification Verification Record
Purpose

Verifies that an instrument is suitable and functioning correctly before it is used for a measurement, covering identity, calibration status, and a functional or known-value check.

Key Fields
  • Instrument identity, calibration status
  • Physical and functional condition: display, leads/probes, accessories, battery
  • Zero/reference check, known-value verification where applicable
  • Result: acceptable / not acceptable / further investigation required
  • Verified by, date, instrument status
CAL-07 Measurement Equipment Checklist Checklist
Purpose

General pre-use checklist confirming an instrument, its range, its calibration, and the measurement environment are all suitable before a measurement is taken.

Key Fields
  • Correct instrument and range selected
  • Calibration valid, calibration label available, instrument undamaged
  • Leads/probes suitable, battery adequate, accessories available
  • Functional check completed, measurement environment suitable, test procedure available
  • Final: equipment fit for measurement — yes / no

C · Equipment-Specific Verification

4 of 4 forms
CAL-08 Multimeter Verification Verification Record
Purpose

Verifies the operational suitability of a multimeter before it is used for electrical measurements, checking each measurement function against a known reference.

Key Fields
  • Instrument ID, calibration validity
  • Display, battery, test leads, probe condition
  • Continuity, voltage, resistance, and current (where applicable) functions, range selection
  • Known reference/check value, measured value, acceptance criterion, result
  • Status: fit / not fit for use
CAL-09 Clamp Meter Verification Verification Record
Purpose

Verifies a clamp meter before current measurement, confirming the clamp jaw, zero function, and current range are all functioning correctly.

Key Fields
  • Calibration status, clamp jaw condition
  • Display, battery, zero function
  • Current range, measurement function, leads/accessories where applicable
  • Reference/check condition, measured result, acceptance criterion, result
CAL-10 Insulation Tester Verification Verification Record
Purpose

Verifies the condition and suitability of an insulation-resistance tester before it is used on primary AGL cables, secondary circuits, or equipment wiring.

Key Fields
  • Calibration status, instrument condition, test leads
  • Test voltage selection, display, battery, functional operation, zero/lead check where applicable
  • Application: primary AGL cables, secondary circuits, equipment wiring, other applicable electrical equipment
  • Selected test voltage, reference/check, result, acceptance status
CAL-11 Earth Tester Verification Verification Record
Purpose

Verifies an earth-resistance testing instrument before earth measurements, confirming its leads, stakes, and measurement function are all in order.

Key Fields
  • Calibration, instrument condition, test leads, stakes/accessories, battery
  • Measurement function, zero/reference check where applicable
  • Verification method, reference/check value, instrument reading
  • Acceptance criterion, result

CAL-12 Photometer Calibration Record Record
Purpose

Maintains the calibration record for photometric measurement equipment, so photometry results can be traced back to a valid, in-date calibration.

Key Fields
  • Photometer ID, manufacturer, model, serial number, detector/sensor identification
  • Measurement range, resolution/accuracy, calibration certificate
  • Calibration date, laboratory, due date, reference, certificate number
  • Verification: calibration valid, sensor condition, accessories, measurement suitability
  • Calibration status, remarks, reviewer
CAL-13 Photometry Equipment Verification Checklist
Purpose

Verifies that a photometer, light meter, or photometric measurement system is ready before it is used for runway, taxiway, approach, or PAPI photometric measurements.

Key Fields
  • Equipment identification verified, calibration valid
  • Sensor condition acceptable, software/version verified where applicable
  • Measurement range suitable, required accessories available
  • Functional verification completed, environmental conditions suitable
  • Result: ready / not ready
CAL-14 CCR Test Equipment Record Record
Purpose

Controls the test equipment used for Constant Current Regulator testing, linking each measurement instrument to the CCR and test report it was used against.

Key Fields
  • Instrument ID, type, manufacturer, model, serial number
  • Measurement parameter: output current, voltage, load, frequency, power, alarm/control measurements where applicable
  • Calibration status, verification status
  • Test date, CCR ID, test report reference
CAL-15 Transformer Test Equipment Record Record
Purpose

Controls the instruments used for AGL isolation-transformer testing, so insulation-resistance, continuity, and voltage results can be traced to a verified instrument.

Key Fields
  • Transformer ID, instrument ID, instrument type
  • Applications: insulation resistance, continuity, voltage measurement, ratio-related verification where applicable, other approved transformer tests
  • Calibration status, verification status
  • Test date, test report
CAL-16 Cable Test Equipment Record Record
Purpose

Maintains traceability of the equipment used for AGL cable testing, from insulation-resistance and continuity checks through to fault diagnosis.

Key Fields
  • Cable/circuit ID, instrument ID, manufacturer, model, serial number
  • Applications: insulation resistance, continuity, cable identification, fault diagnosis, other approved cable tests
  • Calibration status
  • Test date, test report reference

E · Calibration Status Control

3 of 3 forms
CAL-17 Calibration Due-Date Tracker Tracker
Purpose

Gives advance visibility of upcoming calibration requirements across the whole equipment fleet, so instruments are scheduled or withdrawn before they run out of calibration.

Key Fields
  • Instrument ID, instrument, last calibration, due date, days remaining, status
  • Status categories: valid / due soon / due / overdue / under calibration
  • Management actions: calibration scheduled, instrument withdrawn, replacement instrument assigned, calibration completed
CAL-18 Calibration Status Label Label
Purpose

Provides immediate visual identification of an instrument's calibration status at the point of use, kept consistent with the master Calibration Register.

Key Fields
  • Instrument ID, calibration status, calibration date, due date
  • Certificate/reference, calibration provider where required
  • Status: calibrated / calibration due / out of calibration — do not use / under calibration
CAL-19 Out-of-Calibration Report Report
Purpose

Formally controls an instrument found to be outside its calibration validity or otherwise unsuitable for measurement, and assesses which prior measurements it may have affected.

Key Fields
  • Immediate actions: stop use, identify, mark/label, remove from service, quarantine where appropriate
  • Investigation: last valid calibration, date problem identified, work performed since last verification
  • Impact assessment: tests, reports, and equipment potentially affected, need for retesting
  • Disposition: recalibrate / repair and calibrate / replace / retire

Key Fields

CAL-23 Instrument Replacement Record Record
Purpose

Documents the replacement of a test or measurement instrument, from the reason for replacement through to registering and issuing the new instrument.

Key Fields
  • Existing instrument: ID, type, manufacturer, model, serial number, age, condition, failure/damage history
  • Replacement reason: end of life, failure, damage, obsolescence, calibration issue, insufficient accuracy, unsupported equipment, new measurement requirement
  • New instrument: ID, manufacturer, model, serial number, range, accuracy, calibration certificate, due date
  • Closure: old instrument removed, new instrument registered, calibration status recorded, users informed

Frequently Asked Questions

Quick answers about the 25 Division 14 Calibration & Measurement forms, how they are grouped, and how they connect to the rest of the library.

Q1What is Division 14 — Calibration & Measurement Forms in the AGL Engineering Forms & Templates library?

Division 14 is a set of 25 calibration & measurement forms (CAL-01 to CAL-25) in the AGL Engineering Forms & Templates library. It makes every AGL testing, commissioning, maintenance and photometry measurement traceable to an instrument that is correctly identified, calibration-valid and verified fit for use. The forms are grouped into 8 categories: Calibration Control, Instrument Verification, Equipment-Specific Verification, Photometry & AGL Test Equipment, Calibration Status Control, Measurement Quality, Equipment Maintenance & Damage and Audit & Lifecycle.

Q2Which forms are included in Calibration Control, Instrument Verification and Equipment-Specific Verification?

Calibration Control (5 forms): CAL-01 Calibration Register, CAL-02 Calibration Certificate Record, CAL-03 Instrument Identification Sheet, CAL-04 Instrument Issue Record, CAL-05 Instrument Return Record; Instrument Verification (2 forms): CAL-06 Instrument Verification, CAL-07 Measurement Equipment Checklist; Equipment-Specific Verification (4 forms): CAL-08 Multimeter Verification, CAL-09 Clamp Meter Verification, CAL-10 Insulation Tester Verification, CAL-11 Earth Tester Verification.

Q3Which forms are included in Photometry & AGL Test Equipment, Calibration Status Control and Measurement Quality?

Photometry & AGL Test Equipment (5 forms): CAL-12 Photometer Calibration Record, CAL-13 Photometry Equipment Verification, CAL-14 CCR Test Equipment Record, CAL-15 Transformer Test Equipment Record, CAL-16 Cable Test Equipment Record; Calibration Status Control (3 forms): CAL-17 Calibration Due-Date Tracker, CAL-18 Calibration Status Label, CAL-19 Out-of-Calibration Report; Measurement Quality (1 form): CAL-20 Measurement Uncertainty Record.

Q4Which forms are included in Equipment Maintenance & Damage and Audit & Lifecycle?

Equipment Maintenance & Damage (3 forms): CAL-21 Test Equipment Maintenance, CAL-22 Instrument Damage Report, CAL-23 Instrument Replacement Record; Audit & Lifecycle (2 forms): CAL-24 Calibration Audit, CAL-25 Measurement Equipment Lifecycle.

Q5What is the difference between calibration records and instrument verification forms?

Calibration records maintain the instrument's calibration history: the Calibration Register (CAL-01) is the master register of instruments, and the Calibration Certificate Record (CAL-02) maintains traceability of each certificate. Verification forms check that an instrument is suitable and working before use. The Instrument Verification form (CAL-06) does this generally, and CAL-08 to CAL-11 cover the multimeter, clamp meter, insulation tester and earth tester.

Q6How does Division 14 control calibration status and out-of-calibration instruments?

The Calibration Due-Date Tracker (CAL-17) gives advance visibility of upcoming calibrations across the fleet, and the Calibration Status Label (CAL-18) shows an instrument's status at the point of use. The Out-of-Calibration Report (CAL-19) formally controls an instrument found outside its calibration validity. The Instrument Damage Report (CAL-22) records damage and assesses its impact on measurement validity.

Q7Can the forms in Division 14 be downloaded and filled in?

Yes. Each form has a Download as Word button that produces an uncontrolled sample Word template with letterhead, a document-control block, ruled entry fields and a three-tier approval table, ready to print or fill on-screen. Checklist-format forms such as Measurement Equipment Checklist (CAL-07) download with a checkbox and a verified-by line for each item. The forms can also be searched by keyword or form ID.
