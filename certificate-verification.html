FAQ

Verification Questions

Why isn't verification instant?

We'd rather confirm every certificate against our actual records than show an automated "Valid" badge with nothing real behind it. A manual check takes a little longer but means the answer you get is accurate.

Can an employer or client verify a certificate directly?

Yes. Anyone holding a Certificate ID — the candidate, an employer, or a client — can use this page to request confirmation.

What if the Certificate ID doesn't match the AGL-YYYY-##### format?

Send it to us anyway along with the candidate name; older or corporate-batch certificates may use a different reference format, and we'll confirm it manually.

How long does confirmation take?

Typically 1–2 business days. WhatsApp requests are usually the fastest way to reach us.

Verification & Data Notice

Verification is currently manual. A confirmation only states whether a Certificate ID and name match Academy records. It is not a government licence check or an ICAO/FAA accreditation check.

Only request verification for a certificate you hold, or with the certificate holder’s permission. The Certificate ID and name you enter are sent by you through WhatsApp or email, which are third-party services, and are used only to answer your request. See the Privacy Policy.
