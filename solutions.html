About Us

Who We Are

Precision AGL Solutions is a specialized aviation company dedicated to Airfield Ground Lighting (AGL) systems, airport visual aids, and technical consultancy services. Our mission is to provide reliable, innovative solutions — aligned with applicable ICAO, FAA, DGCA and relevant technical requirements — that enhance runway safety, operational efficiency, and airport performance.

We support airports, consultants, contractors, and aviation organizations with end-to-end expertise in the design, testing, maintenance, inspection, and optimization of AGL systems in accordance with ICAO, FAA, DGCA, and relevant international standards.

FAQ

AGL Solutions & Services — Frequently Asked Questions

What services does Precision AGL Solutions provide?

Ten core services: AGL consultancy, photometric testing and verification, inspection and maintenance, airport systems integration, training and skill development, documentation and compliance, airfield lighting systems supply and installation, airport project management, AGL commissioning and acceptance testing, and AGL troubleshooting and fault diagnosis.

Which standards does the work refer to?

Technical work is undertaken with reference to ICAO Annex 14, the FAA AC 150/5340 and 150/5345 series, DGCA requirements, AAI specifications, applicable Indian Standards, IEC standards and airport-specific operator specifications. These are cited as applicable guidance; being listed is not itself a certification or compliance claim for any specific project.

What does photometric testing cover?

Runway and taxiway light testing, PAPI photometric verification and signage luminance measurement, followed by verification and performance analysis. Deliverables are a photometric test report, a test and verification report, a performance dataset and a non-conformance summary.

What is included in commissioning and acceptance testing?

Pre-commissioning inspection, cable continuity and insulation testing, series-circuit verification, CCR testing, transformer verification and fixture installation inspection, then PAPI alignment, photometric verification, sign testing, functional and control-system testing, final documentation and acceptance support.

What kinds of AGL faults can you diagnose?

Runway and taxiway faults such as lamp, transformer, connector, cable, earth and series-circuit faults; CCR current instability, step or brilliance problems, alarms and output problems; PAPI alignment, colour, intensity and angle problems; and sign LED, driver, luminance and circuit faults. The site also has a guided AGL Troubleshooting Engine.

Do you also supply products?

Yes. The product portfolio is organised by application and mapped to ICAO Annex 14 and FAA AC 150/5340 designations where a recognised equivalent exists. It covers runway, taxiway, approach, PAPI, signs, electrical infrastructure, meteorological aids, obstruction lighting, civil infrastructure and spares. The full catalogue is on the Products page.

Who do you work with?

International, domestic and greenfield airports, military airbases, heliports, airport consultants, EPC contractors and aviation training institutes.

What is your working process?

Five steps: understand the project requirements and site conditions, assess the existing AGL system or technical requirements, engineer the design, testing or technical solution, verify through inspection, testing and compliance review, and deliver reports, recommendations, training and support.

Can project names be disclosed?

Project names are disclosed only where contractually permitted. Undisclosed engagements are referenced by category and location.
