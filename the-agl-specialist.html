My Specialized Technical Focus

AGL Photometry

AGL photometry has become one of the central areas of my professional work. My methodology is built around three core pillars:

TEST

  • Intensity
  • Beam alignment
  • Chromaticity
  • Electrical performance

ANALYSE

  • Photometric data
  • Performance deviation
  • Failure patterns
  • Compliance status

REPORT

  • Test reports
  • Corrective actions
  • Compliance documentation
Electrical testing tells us whether the equipment is powered. Photometric testing tells us whether the visual guidance system is performing as required.
