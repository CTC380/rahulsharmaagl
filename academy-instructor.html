Professional Profile

Field Engineer, Then Instructor

Rahul Sharma is an Airfield Ground Lighting (AGL) Specialist and electrical engineer working in the airport lighting industry in India. He is affiliated with Creative Trading Co. (CTC), an AGL manufacturer based in Faridabad, Haryana, and also runs his own consultancy, Precision AGL Solutions, through which he works across multiple Indian airports on AGL engineering, testing and project execution.

Precision AGL Academy grew directly out of that field work. Rather than starting from a generic electrical or aviation syllabus, the curriculum is structured around the systems, standards, and problems that actually show up on an AGL project — series-circuit architecture, CCR operation, photometric acceptance testing, and the ICAO/FAA/Indian standards that govern them.

Teaching Approach

An Airfield Lighting Instructor From the Field

As an airfield lighting instructor, Rahul Sharma teaches from the same material he works with on airport projects: series circuits, CCRs, photometric testing and the standards behind them. Lessons follow the order work happens on site, with fundamentals first, then design, then troubleshooting, supported by case studies drawn from real airport work.

The programs are written for electrical engineers, fresh graduates and career-switchers who want airport-specific knowledge without needing prior airside experience.

Experience & Exposure

What the Curriculum Is Built On

AGL Electrical Systems

Series-circuit AGL architecture, Constant Current Regulator (CCR) systems, isolating transformers, primary/secondary cabling and L-823 connectors — the electrical backbone of every AGL installation.

Airport Project Exposure

Field experience spans runway, taxiway, approach lighting and PAPI systems across a portfolio of 39+ airports in India, with Cochin International Airport (CIAL) as a flagship project reference.

Photometric Testing

Hands-on photometric testing and acceptance evaluation of AGL fixtures — measurement, calibration considerations, and pass/fail assessment against published intensity and colour tolerances.

Standards & Compliance

Working knowledge of ICAO Annex 14, FAA Advisory Circulars (AC 150/5340 & 5345 series), and Indian standards including IS 12290 and IS 7785, applied to real compliance matrices and specification review.

ALCMS & Control Systems

Exposure to Airfield Lighting Control & Monitoring Systems (ALCMS), CCR control and monitoring, and the CAT I/II/III operational requirements that shape lighting and control design.

Airfield Civil & Site Works

Direct exposure to airfield civil works alongside AGL installation — the coordination between electrical and civil scope that field engineers have to manage on an active airport site.

Flagship Project Reference — Cochin International Airport (CIAL)

CIAL is referenced across the Academy's case studies and course material as a flagship project — used to ground design exercises, compliance walkthroughs and troubleshooting scenarios in a real, large-scale AGL installation rather than abstract examples.

Training Philosophy

Why the Academy Teaches This Way

"AGL is a small, specialized field — but it's a field with real consequences. The goal isn't to hand out a certificate; it's to get someone site-ready, able to read a circuit, interpret a standard, and actually troubleshoot a fault."

That's the thinking behind how Precision AGL Academy is structured: fundamentals before design, design before troubleshooting, and case studies drawn from real airport work rather than generic electrical theory. Programs are built so electrical engineers, fresh graduates and career-switchers can build airport-specific knowledge from the ground up, without assuming prior airside experience.

Quick answers about Rahul Sharma and the field background behind Precision AGL Academy.

Q1Who teaches Precision AGL Academy?

Q2How much field experience does the instructor have?
