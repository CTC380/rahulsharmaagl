Overview

AGL Technical Resources Library

Airfield Ground Lighting (AGL) technical datasheets and PDF guides help engineers, consultants, airport authorities, contractors and maintenance teams understand equipment specifications and verify product suitability for a project.

These documents provide essential information about electrical ratings, construction, dimensions, connections, environmental suitability, photometric performance, testing requirements and applicable standards.

The AGL Technical Resources Library is intended to organise product catalogues, manufacturer datasheets, engineering reference documents, inspection checklists and study guides in one place.

Use the information in each document to support technical evaluation and product selection. Before procurement, installation, testing or commissioning, confirm that the document applies to the exact product model and project configuration.

1 · Catalogues

Airfield Lighting Product Catalogues

An AGL product catalogue provides an overview of a manufacturer's product range, equipment families and available configurations.

Depending on the manufacturer, a catalogue may cover

  • Runway edge, threshold and runway-end lights.
  • Runway centreline and touchdown zone lighting.
  • Taxiway edge and centreline lights.
  • Approach lighting systems.
  • Precision Approach Path Indicator (PAPI) systems.
  • Constant Current Regulators (CCRs).
  • AGL isolating transformers.
  • Primary cables and connector systems.
  • Airfield signs.
  • Airfield Lighting Control and Monitoring Systems (ALCMS).
  • Associated accessories and installation components.

What to check: Before shortlisting a product, identify the exact model, intended application, available ratings, mounting arrangement and associated accessories. A catalogue provides an overview of the product range; detailed datasheets, technical drawings, test reports and compliance documents may be required for project evaluation and acceptance.

2 · CCR

Constant Current Regulator (CCR) Datasheets

A Constant Current Regulator supplies regulated current to an AGL series circuit within its specified operating limits. Its datasheet helps engineers verify compatibility with the proposed lighting circuit and associated control equipment.

A CCR datasheet should identify

  • Manufacturer, model and equipment identification.
  • Rated input voltage, frequency and supply requirements.
  • Rated output current.
  • Output capacity and applicable load limits.
  • Available intensity-control settings.
  • Regulation and performance characteristics.
  • Protection and alarm functions.
  • Local and remote control interfaces.
  • Monitoring and communication options.
  • Environmental and installation requirements.
  • Relevant test, qualification and compliance documentation.

Why CCR datasheets matter: CCR information supports equipment selection, electrical design, procurement, technical submittals and commissioning preparation. The selected CCR must be compatible with the intended series circuit, required load, control arrangement and project specification. Use the manufacturer’s approved installation and operating manual for wiring, configuration, commissioning and maintenance procedures.

3 · Transformers

AGL Isolating Transformer Datasheets

An AGL isolating transformer provides electrical separation between the primary series circuit and its associated secondary lighting load. Its datasheet should identify the transformer rating, electrical characteristics, construction and connection arrangements needed for technical evaluation.

Key information to verify

  • Manufacturer and model.
  • Rated power or wattage.
  • Primary current rating.
  • Secondary current rating.
  • Rated frequency.
  • Primary and secondary lead arrangements.
  • Connector type and compatibility.
  • Insulation and encapsulation details.
  • Environmental and installation suitability.
  • Applicable product specifications and test documentation.

Selection considerations: Confirm that the transformer is suitable for the intended light fitting and approved circuit design. Check the required wattage, current ratings, frequency, connector arrangement and applicable project requirements before selecting a replacement. Similar-looking connectors do not necessarily establish electrical or mechanical compatibility.

4 · Cables & Connectors

AGL Cable and Connector Datasheets

Primary cables, connectors, joints and secondary leads are essential parts of an AGL electrical installation. Their specifications must be suitable for the intended circuit and installation environment.

Cable datasheets

  • Conductor material and cross-sectional area.
  • Cable construction and insulation type.
  • Rated voltage and relevant electrical characteristics.
  • Temperature range.
  • Environmental and mechanical properties.
  • Applicable test requirements.
  • Intended application and installation restrictions.

Connector datasheets

  • Connector type and configuration.
  • Compatible cable sizes.
  • Electrical ratings.
  • Plug and receptacle arrangement.
  • Sealing and environmental protection.
  • Materials and construction.
  • Assembly and installation instructions.
  • Applicable test and compliance documents.

Why this documentation matters: Correct selection helps engineers verify compatibility between the primary cable, connector assembly, transformer and associated equipment. Always check the manufacturer’s product identification and technical documentation. Do not assume that connectors from different product families are interchangeable simply because their dimensions appear similar.

5 · Lighting Fixtures

Runway, Taxiway and Approach Lighting Datasheets

Lighting fixture datasheets provide the technical information required to assess whether a product is suitable for its intended airfield application. Relevant documents may cover:

Runway lighting

Runway edge lights, threshold lights, runway-end lights, centreline lights and touchdown zone lights.

Taxiway lighting

Taxiway edge lights, taxiway centreline lights and other applicable taxiway lighting equipment.

Approach lighting

Approach light fittings, light bars, associated accessories and system-specific components.

PAPI systems

Equipment configuration, optical characteristics, mounting requirements, electrical specifications and applicable performance or qualification information.

Information to verify in a fixture datasheet

  • Intended application and product type.
  • Manufacturer and model.
  • Light source and optical characteristics.
  • Applicable photometric requirements.
  • Electrical supply and compatibility.
  • Mounting and installation arrangements.
  • Environmental protection and operating conditions.
  • Maintenance and replacement requirements.
  • Applicable qualification, test and compliance documentation.

6 · ALCMS

ALCMS and Control System Documents

An Airfield Lighting Control and Monitoring System (ALCMS) coordinates specified lighting control functions and, where provided, monitoring and status indications.

The technical document set may include

  • System architecture diagrams.
  • Control and monitoring interface descriptions.
  • Approved wiring diagrams.
  • Equipment manuals.
  • Operating instructions.
  • Maintenance procedures.
  • Communication interface documentation.
  • Configuration records.
  • Alarm and status descriptions.
  • Approved test and commissioning procedures.

What to check: Confirm that the document applies to the installed system version, equipment configuration and project interfaces. For operating airports, maintenance personnel should use the current approved documentation associated with the installed system. Generic architecture diagrams are useful for learning but should not replace approved system-specific records.

9 · Selection Process

How to Select the Correct AGL Datasheet

Before using a datasheet for technical evaluation or procurement, follow this process:

01

Identify the equipment.

Record the equipment category, manufacturer and exact model.

02

Confirm the application.

Check whether the product is intended for the required runway, taxiway, approach or electrical-system application.

03

Verify the ratings.

Review the applicable electrical, optical, environmental and mechanical specifications.

04

Check compatibility.

Confirm compatibility with the circuit, connectors, control interfaces and associated equipment.

05

Review compliance evidence.

Identify the applicable standards and any required qualification reports, test certificates or declarations.

06

Check the document revision.

Confirm that the datasheet is current and corresponds to the offered product.

07

Compare with project requirements.

Evaluate the document against the approved specification and technical submittal requirements.

08

Retain the records.

Keep the accepted datasheet and supporting documents with the project and equipment records.

A catalogue or datasheet supports product evaluation but does not automatically demonstrate full project compliance or acceptance.

11 · Document Control

AGL Document Control and Technical Quality

Document control helps ensure that engineers use the correct information for the correct product and project. Before publishing a technical document, verify:

Accuracy

The document corresponds to the stated manufacturer, model and scope.

Revision

The displayed revision matches the actual file.

Traceability

The source or issuing authority is identified.

Readability

Tables, drawings, symbols and technical details remain legible in the published PDF.

Integrity

The file opens correctly and is complete.

Permissions

Publication and distribution are authorized.

Status

The page clearly distinguishes current, superseded, educational and project-specific material.

For operational airport maintenance, installation and acceptance, use the applicable approved documentation. An online resource library should not replace controlled project records.

FAQ

Frequently Asked Questions

Q1Where can I find an airfield lighting product catalogue?

Start with the relevant manufacturer's official website or authorized product resource page. Confirm that the catalogue covers the required model and configuration.

Q2Which details should I check in an AGL datasheet?

Check the exact model, intended application, electrical ratings, dimensions, connections, environmental suitability, photometric characteristics where relevant, and applicable standards or qualification documents.

Q3Can a product catalogue replace a project-specific technical submittal?

No. A catalogue supports initial product selection, but a project may also require detailed datasheets, drawings, test reports, certificates and compliance statements.

Q4Where can I find CCR datasheets?

Use the resource category for Constant Current Regulators and verify the manufacturer, model, output current, capacity and control configuration. Use the corresponding equipment manual for installation and maintenance.

Q5What should an AGL isolating transformer datasheet include?

It should identify the transformer rating, primary and secondary current, frequency, construction, connector arrangement and applicable technical requirements.

Q6Are AGL PDF guides suitable for installation work?

Educational PDFs can help explain equipment and system principles. Installation and maintenance must follow the applicable approved project drawings, manufacturer instructions and authorized procedures.

Q7Why is the document revision important?

The revision helps identify the version of the document being used. An outdated datasheet or manual may describe a previous product configuration or superseded requirements.

Q8Can I use a manufacturer's datasheet as proof of compliance?

Not necessarily. A datasheet provides technical information, but project acceptance may require additional evidence, such as qualification reports, test certificates, declarations or other specified documentation.

Q9What should I do if a datasheet is not available?

Request the document using the manufacturer's name, exact product model and intended application. Confirm that any supplied copy is authentic, current and authorized for use.

Q10Can I download AGL CAD drawings from this library?

Only if the relevant drawing files have been uploaded, reviewed and approved for distribution. Detailed project drawings may be subject to confidentiality, copyright or other restrictions.

Q11What is an airfield lighting product catalogue?

An airfield lighting product catalogue lists a manufacturer's range, such as lights, CCRs, transformers, cables, signs and control equipment, with model summaries. Use it to shortlist products, then check the datasheet before specifying.

Q12What is an airfield lighting technical datasheet?

An airfield lighting technical datasheet is the manufacturer's specification sheet for one product, listing ratings, dimensions, standards compliance, photometric data and installation details. Specify and approve equipment from the datasheet, not from the catalogue alone.

Q13What is an airfield ground lighting datasheet and an airfield lighting datasheet?

An airfield ground lighting datasheet, also called an airfield lighting datasheet, gives the technical data for an AGL product. Look for ratings, standards such as FAA or ICAO compliance, materials, and installation limits.

Q14What is an airfield ground lighting PDF and an airfield lighting PDF?

An airfield ground lighting PDF or airfield lighting PDF is a downloadable document such as a guide, checklist or manual. Confirm the issue date and revision before use, and compare it with the latest official source.

Q15What do an airport lighting PDF and an airport lighting datasheet include?

An airport lighting PDF is usually a guide or reference document. An airport lighting datasheet is a product's specification. Use the PDF for learning and the datasheet for equipment details.

Q16What do a runway lighting PDF and a runway lighting datasheet cover?

A runway lighting PDF covers concepts, layouts and maintenance of runway lights. A runway lighting datasheet covers a product such as an edge or centreline light: ratings, photometrics and mounting.

Q17What do a taxiway lighting PDF and a taxiway lighting datasheet cover?

A taxiway lighting PDF explains taxiway lighting systems. A taxiway lighting datasheet describes a specific product such as an edge or centreline fixture with its ratings and standards.

Q18What is an approach lighting PDF?

An approach lighting PDF is a guide or reference on approach lighting systems, layouts and maintenance. For equipment details, use the manufacturer's datasheet.

Q19What are a constant current regulator PDF and a constant current regulator datasheet?

A constant current regulator PDF is a guide or manual. A constant current regulator datasheet lists the CCR's ratings, output current steps, efficiency, protection and standards such as the relevant FAA or IEC requirements.

Q20What are an airfield lighting isolation transformer PDF and an airfield lighting isolation transformer datasheet?

An airfield lighting isolation transformer PDF explains the transformer's role and use. The datasheet gives ratings, primary and secondary current, insulation and dimensions.

Q21What are an airfield lighting cable PDF and an airfield lighting cable datasheet?

An airfield lighting cable PDF explains cable types and installation. An airfield lighting cable datasheet gives conductor size, insulation, voltage rating and standards.

Q22What are an airfield lighting control system PDF and an airfield lighting control system datasheet?

An airfield lighting control system PDF explains architecture and operation. An airfield lighting control system datasheet gives hardware ratings, communication protocols and interfaces.

Q23What are an ALCMS PDF and an ALCMS datasheet?

An ALCMS PDF is a guide or manual for the control and monitoring system. An ALCMS datasheet lists the specification of a product or module, such as supported protocols and interfaces.

Q24What are a runway edge lights PDF and a runway edge lights datasheet?

A runway edge lights PDF is a guide or maintenance document for runway edge lights. A runway edge lights datasheet lists a fixture's ratings, optical performance and mounting details.

Q25Is there an AGL inspection checklist PDF?

An AGL inspection checklist PDF is a printable checklist for recording inspection results. Use the version that matches your airport's procedures and keep records of completed checklists.

Engineering & Expert Notes

RS
By Rahul Sharma
Airfield Ground Lighting Specialist
A Datasheet Is a Starting Point, Not an Approval
Use

Datasheets describe a product under stated conditions. Confirm that the version, rating and approval apply to your installation before relying on a figure.

Check Revision and Date Before Using a Value
Document Control

Superseded sheets circulate for years. Note the revision on every document you rely on and replace old copies when a newer release is issued.

Compare Like With Like When Reading Ratings
Comparison

Different manufacturers may quote values at different conditions or test methods. Check the footnotes before treating two numbers as equivalent.

Keep Datasheets With the Project Record
Records

Store the sheets used for submittals with the as-built dossier so future maintenance knows exactly what was approved and installed.

Cross-Check Against the Standard, Not Only the Brochure
Verification

Marketing summaries can simplify or omit conditions. Where compliance matters, check the underlying standard and the manufacturer's test evidence.

Download From Controlled Sources
Sources

Prefer documents from the manufacturer or your document control system. Unknown copies may be altered, outdated or incomplete.
