One Connected Ecosystem for Airfield Ground Lighting

Precision AGL Ecosystem is a connected platform bringing together AGL engineering, technical knowledge, learning, standards, tools, testing, operations, troubleshooting and continuous improvement. Common Questions

Frequently Asked Questions

Q1What is the Precision AGL Ecosystem?

It is a connected platform bringing together AGL engineering, technical knowledge, learning, standards, tools, testing, operations, troubleshooting and continuous improvement, built on the principle that AGL knowledge should not exist as disconnected information.

Q2What does the ecosystem include?

Eight areas: AGL Solutions; AGL Knowledge Hub; AGL Academy; Engineering & Field Practice; Standards & Compliance; Testing, Inspection & Verification; Operations & Maintenance; and Engineering Tools & Documentation.

Q3What is in the AGL Knowledge Hub?

AGL Fundamentals, Visual Encyclopedia, Component Library, Standards Companion, Technical Articles, Topic Hub, Comparison Library, AGL Glossary, Quiz Center and AGL Toolkit.

Q4What does the AGL Academy cover?

Fundamentals, technical concepts, AGL systems, components, testing, standards, inspection, maintenance, troubleshooting and professional learning, connected to real engineering subjects rather than isolated theory.

Q5What is the AGL engineering lifecycle?

Learn, Understand, Design, Build, Test, Operate, Solve and Improve. Two functions work across every stage: Assess, and Connect Everything.

Q6Which standards does the ecosystem cover?

ICAO, FAA, DGCA and applicable Indian standards, together with technical requirements, inspection and testing criteria and compliance documentation.

Q7What testing, inspection and verification resources are included?

The Testing & Commissioning Manual, Inspection Handbook, Testing Guides, Photometry Resources, Failure Database and Troubleshooting Atlas.

Q8What engineering tools and documentation are provided?

Engineering Forms & Templates, calculators and tools, technical checklists, BOQ and specification resources, drawings and references, testing records, inspection records and technical documentation.
