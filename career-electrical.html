05 · FAQ

Frequently Asked Questions

Short answers about the electrical engineering question bank, how the drill and scoring work, and the topics it covers.

Q1What is the Electrical Engineering for AGL question bank?

It is a 200-question reference and drill on the electrical engineering an AGL engineer relies on: Ohm's law, AC fundamentals, transformers, earthing, insulation resistance, cable calculations, protection, relays, contactors and electrical safety. Every question has a model answer, an explanation, key terms and references.

Q2What topics does the question bank cover?

Ten topics: Ohm's Law (20), AC Fundamentals (25), Transformers (25), Earthing (20), Insulation Resistance (20), Cable Calculations (25), Protection (20), Relays (15), Contactors (15), Electrical Safety (15).

Q3What difficulty levels are included?

Four levels: Beginner (58), Intermediate (81), Advanced (54) and Expert (7). Beginner questions cover definitions and purpose, while Advanced and Expert questions add multi-step calculations and practical AGL scenarios.

Q4What question formats does the bank use?

Technical explanation (102), numerical (41), practical scenario (19), procedure (8), AGL-specific (8), troubleshooting (6) and safety (4), plus a handful of test, inspection, interpretation and standards-reference questions.

Q5How does Drill Mode work, and how is my answer scored?

Drill Mode gives a shuffled run through the bank, filtered by level and topic if you choose. Type your own answer and check it, or just reveal the model answer. Typed answers are scored locally in your browser by weighted key-term matching with stemming and typo tolerance, plus overall wording overlap. It is a close approximation, not true AI grading, and for numerical questions you should always compare your working with the model answer.

Q6Are there worked calculations in the bank?

Yes. About 41 questions are numerical, covering voltage drop, series and parallel resistance, power relationships (P = VI, I²R and V²/R), and cable resistance and voltage-drop problems set in simplified AGL circuits. Each shows the answer and the reasoning behind it.

Q7Why does an AGL engineer need Ohm's law?

Ohm's law links voltage, current and resistance, and the power relationships built on it let you check load, cable loss and the voltage reaching a device. The Ohm's Law topic (20 questions) builds from definitions up to integrated AGL circuit problems.

Q8What does the bank cover on earthing and insulation resistance?

Earthing (20 questions) covers why a controlled connection to earth keeps exposed conductive parts at a safer potential and gives fault current an intended path. Insulation Resistance (20 questions) covers what the measurement means, how it is tested and how readings are interpreted.

Q9Does it cover protection, relays and contactors?

Yes. Protection (20) covers how overloads, short circuits, open circuits and faults are detected and cleared, Relays (15) covers coil-and-contact switching devices, and Contactors (15) covers switching devices designed to make and break power circuits repeatedly.

Q10What does the electrical safety topic cover?

Fifteen questions on hazards AGL maintenance personnel must consider before starting work, including electric shock, arc flash, unexpected energisation, backfeed or induced voltage, stored energy, defective insulation and unsafe grounding. It supports, but does not replace, your site procedures and permit-to-work rules.

Q11Which standards does the question bank refer to?

Many answers are grounded in general electrical engineering principles (91 questions), alongside FAA AC 150/5340-26C (59), FAA AC 150/5370-10H (44), FAA AC 150/5340-30J (26) and FAA AC 150/5345-47C (19). Protection, contactor and cable questions also cite NFPA 70E (10) and NFPA 70 (9).

Q12Is this useful for AGL job interviews?

Yes. Interviewers often start with electrical basics before moving to AGL systems. The 41 numerical and 19 practical-scenario questions help you practise showing your working aloud, and the 7 expert questions mirror harder design and diagnosis discussions.
