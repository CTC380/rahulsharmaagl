Test Equipment, Calibration & Engineering Quality

Every division before this one produces a result. Division 25 asks whether that result can be trusted — was the instrument correctly identified, in calibration, and appropriate for the method used; was the acceptance limit traceable to an approved basis; was the record reviewed. It is deliberately positioned as the final technical-quality division of the framework, because the validity of every preceding test in Divisions 01–24 depends on the reliability of the test equipment, the suitability of the test method, and the quality of the recorded measurement behind it.

Governing Principle: a test result is only as reliable as the test method, test equipment, calibration status, measurement conditions, and technical review supporting that result. Division 25 does not repeat the performance tests of earlier divisions — it audits the instruments, calibration status, and review process that stand behind them.

Quality Gate Sequence

The result-quality gate runs as a fixed sequence — no critical measurement is treated as final acceptance evidence until every stage below has been satisfied.

StageWhat It Confirms
Raw Value & UnitThe raw measured value is recorded with correct units before any evaluation takes place
Equipment & CalibrationThe test equipment used is correctly identified and its calibration status is valid at the time of test
Acceptance-Limit CheckThe acceptance limit applied is traceable to an approved specification, drawing, standard, or procedure
Result EvaluationThe measured value is evaluated against the correct acceptance limit for a Pass/Fail outcome
Technical ReviewThe result and its supporting evidence are independently reviewed for completeness and consistency
Retest / Corrective ActionRetesting or corrective action is completed and documented wherever required
Final AcceptanceThe reviewed, traceable result is closed out and archived as part of the commissioning record

A

Test Equipment

15 topics
TC-TEQ-001Insulation-Resistance Tester
Verification Checks
  • Confirm equipment identification — make, model, and serial number
  • Verify measurement range and test-voltage capability against the circuit rating
  • Confirm calibration status is valid before use
  • Inspect test leads for damage or wear, and check battery/power condition
  • Verify display/function condition and correct test-range selection
  • Confirm applicable use — primary cable, secondary cable, equipment wiring, transformer circuits, control circuits, or other insulated circuits
Acceptance / Record

Readiness Criteria: every insulation-resistance tester used is correctly identified, in calibration, and set to the range appropriate for the circuit under test.

TC-TEQ-002Earth-Resistance Tester
Verification Checks
  • Confirm instrument identification and measurement range
  • Verify the test method capability matches the site condition
  • Inspect leads/probes for condition, and confirm calibration status
  • Verify test configuration and environmental/site conditions before measurement
  • Record instrument ID and test location against every measurement
  • Record instrument ID, test method, location, measured resistance, date, and calibration reference
Acceptance / Record

Readiness Criteria: instrument identity, method, and calibration reference are recorded against every earth-resistance result.

TC-TEQ-003Multimeter
Verification Checks
  • Confirm the CAT rating is appropriate to the intended measurement
  • Inspect leads and fuses for condition, and check the display
  • Verify calibration and functional condition
  • Confirm the correct measurement range for AC/DC voltage, resistance, continuity, current, or frequency
  • Confirm the meter is safe and suitable for the electrical environment being measured
Acceptance / Record

Readiness Criteria: the meter and leads are appropriate for the electrical environment and measurement being performed, and are within calibration.

TC-TEQ-004Clamp Meter
Verification Checks
  • Confirm current range and AC/DC capability match the expected load
  • Inspect jaw condition and lead condition where applicable
  • Verify calibration and functional operation, and check the display
  • Confirm applicable use — CCR current, supply current, panel current, or equipment current
Acceptance / Record

Readiness Criteria: clamp meter range and jaw condition are confirmed suitable for the specific current measurement being performed.

TC-TEQ-005Power Analyzer
Verification Checks
  • Confirm measurement capability against required parameters — voltage, current, power, power factor, frequency, energy, or harmonics
  • Verify voltage/current range, accuracy, and calibration status
  • Confirm connection configuration and measurement setup before test
  • Record actual measured values rather than only a Pass/Fail statement where quantitative performance is required
Acceptance / Record

Readiness Criteria: measurement setup and calibration are verified before test, and actual measured values are recorded wherever quantitative performance is required.

TC-TEQ-006High-Voltage Test Equipment
Verification Checks
  • Confirm test-voltage capability, equipment rating, and calibration
  • Verify leads, test accessories, and safety condition including interlocks/protection
  • Follow manufacturer instructions for setup and operation
  • Confirm an approved test procedure, isolation, work permit, and LOTO are in place before testing
  • Confirm test-area control, warning arrangements, and proving-dead requirements
  • Confirm discharge/earthing requirements are completed after test
Hold Point

Hold Point: high-voltage testing does not proceed without an approved test procedure and the full set of associated safety controls in place.

TC-TEQ-007Cable-Test Equipment
Verification Checks
  • Confirm test range, accuracy, and calibration of the continuity, insulation-resistance, resistance-measurement, or fault-location equipment used
  • Inspect test leads and accessories for condition
  • Confirm the applicable cable/test method for the circuit under test
  • Record equipment ID and test configuration in the cable test report
Acceptance / Record

Readiness Criteria: equipment ID and test configuration are recorded in every cable test report, with the correct method applied for the cable type.

TC-TEQ-008CCR Test Equipment
Verification Checks
  • Confirm capability for output current, input voltage, and output voltage measurement
  • Confirm capability for current regulation, step response, and load response measurement
  • Confirm capability for protection response and alarm response verification
  • Verify equipment suitability, calibration, and measurement range
  • Confirm connection arrangement and test configuration before commissioning tests
Acceptance / Record

Readiness Criteria: CCR test equipment capability, calibration, and connection arrangement are confirmed before performance testing begins.

TC-TEQ-009Photometric Equipment
Verification Checks
  • Confirm photometer identification, detector configuration, and measurement range
  • Verify measurement geometry and calibration status
  • Inspect optical condition and accessories, and confirm software/configuration where applicable
  • Verify alignment and environmental conditions before testing
  • Verify the applicable test procedure is being followed
  • Record equipment identification against every result
Acceptance / Record

Readiness Criteria: photometric equipment calibration, alignment, and environmental conditions are verified and recorded before any photometric result is accepted.

TC-TEQ-010Colour-Measurement Equipment
Verification Checks
  • Confirm measurement capability against the applicable colour parameters
  • Verify calibration and optical condition
  • Confirm measurement geometry and test setup
  • Record instrument, calibration reference, measurement condition, result, and acceptance criterion
Acceptance / Record

Readiness Criteria: colour-measurement results record instrument, calibration reference, and the applicable acceptance criterion alongside the measured value.

TC-TEQ-011Alignment Equipment
Verification Checks
  • Confirm accuracy and calibration of survey, optical, digital-level, or laser alignment equipment
  • Verify the reference system and setup used
  • Confirm alignment condition against the applicable use — runway/taxiway fixtures, approach lights, PAPI, signs, or light bars
  • Confirm the equipment is appropriate for the specific AGL fixture being aligned
Acceptance / Record

Readiness Criteria: alignment equipment accuracy, calibration, and reference system are confirmed appropriate to the specific fixture type being aligned.

TC-TEQ-012Optical Measurement Equipment
Verification Checks
  • Confirm optical measurement capability and detector condition
  • Verify measurement range and calibration
  • Confirm measurement geometry and software/data handling where applicable
  • Maintain traceability from instrument through measurement, fixture, and location to result
Acceptance / Record

Readiness Criteria: a clear traceability chain — instrument to measurement to fixture to location to result — is maintained for every optical measurement.

TC-TEQ-013Communication-Test Equipment
Verification Checks
  • Confirm interface compatibility with the system under test
  • Verify test capability and configuration
  • Confirm calibration where applicable, and check functional condition before use
  • Applicable to communication analyzers, interface testers, protocol diagnostic, or serial communication equipment
Acceptance / Record

Readiness Criteria: communication-test equipment configuration and interface compatibility are confirmed before use on any control or comms circuit.

TC-TEQ-014Network-Test Equipment
Verification Checks
  • Confirm equipment configuration for network connectivity and link status
  • Verify communication testing, network diagnostics, and device identification capability
  • Confirm correct test method and test environment for the networked system (ALCMS and related)
  • Confirm test records are retained
Acceptance / Record

Readiness Criteria: network-test equipment configuration and test method are confirmed appropriate to the networked system under test, with records retained.

TC-TEQ-015Calibration Equipment
Verification Checks
  • Confirm calibration status and reference standard of equipment used to verify or calibrate instruments
  • Verify traceability, accuracy, and measurement range
  • Confirm environmental conditions and the applicable procedure are followed
Acceptance / Record

Readiness Criteria: calibration equipment itself is appropriately controlled, traceable, and used under the correct environmental conditions and procedure.

B

Calibration

10 topics
TC-TEQ-016Calibration-Status Verification
Verification Checks
  • Check the calibration label and certificate before use
  • Confirm equipment ID and serial number match the certificate
  • Verify calibration date and validity period
  • Confirm current status is CALIBRATED/VALID rather than NOT VALID FOR USE
Hold Point

Hold Point: an instrument found NOT VALID FOR USE is not used for any test, regardless of urgency, until its calibration status is resolved.

TC-TEQ-017Calibration-Certificate Verification
Verification Checks
  • Match the certificate to the actual instrument — make, model, and serial number
  • Match the certificate number, calibration date, and validity to the instrument in hand
  • Do not accept a certificate merely because the manufacturer/model appears similar
  • Confirm the certificate corresponds to the specific serial number used for the test
Acceptance / Record

Readiness Criteria: every calibration certificate referenced in a test record is confirmed to belong to the exact instrument, by serial number, used for that test.

TC-TEQ-018Calibration Interval Control
Verification Checks
  • Maintain a calibration register recording equipment, ID, serial number, last calibration, and next due date
  • Track calibration due dates and upcoming calibration
  • Identify expired and overdue equipment
  • Record any temporary restrictions on use
Acceptance / Record

Readiness Criteria: the calibration register is current, and no overdue instrument remains available for use without a documented restriction.

TC-TEQ-019Instrument Identification
Verification Checks
  • Confirm every controlled measurement instrument carries a unique equipment ID
  • Verify manufacturer, model, and serial number are recorded against that ID
  • Verify calibration status, owner/department, and location are recorded
  • Confirm ID format is consistent — e.g. AGL-MET-001, AGL-PHOTO-001, AGL-EARTH-001
Acceptance / Record

Readiness Criteria: every controlled instrument in use has a unique, consistently formatted ID linked to its calibration and ownership record.

TC-TEQ-020Measurement Traceability
Verification Checks
  • Confirm the chain from test result to test record is complete
  • Confirm the chain from test-equipment ID to serial number to calibration certificate is complete
  • Verify the calibration reference/traceability is identifiable
  • Confirm this chain can be reconstructed for any result under audit or dispute
Acceptance / Record

Readiness Criteria: the full traceability chain from result back to calibration reference can be reconstructed for any test record, particularly for audits, disputes, and acceptance testing.

TC-TEQ-021Measurement Uncertainty Awareness
Verification Checks
  • Consider instrument accuracy, resolution, and environmental conditions
  • Consider measurement method, operator influence, and test setup
  • Consider repeatability and calibration condition
  • Where a result sits close to the acceptance limit, apply measurement uncertainty and the applicable project/test-method requirement rather than treating the displayed value as absolutely exact
Acceptance / Record

Readiness Criteria: measurement uncertainty is explicitly considered for any acceptance decision resting on a value close to its limit, per the applicable project/test-method requirement.

TC-TEQ-022Out-of-Calibration Equipment Control
Verification Checks
  • Identify the equipment and stop its use immediately
  • Mark/control the equipment, and determine affected tests and test dates
  • Review calibration history and assess impact on previous results
  • Retest where required and document the decision
  • Ask whether any commissioning results were generated using equipment not valid for use at the time of measurement
Hold Point

Hold Point: any result potentially generated with out-of-calibration equipment is flagged for technical review before it is relied on for acceptance.

TC-TEQ-023Instrument Maintenance
Verification Checks
  • Maintain instrument body, display, leads, probes, clamps, and connectors
  • Maintain batteries, chargers, optical components, accessories, and carrying cases
  • Record maintenance date, work performed, and fault/repair details
  • Record verification and return-to-service status
Acceptance / Record

Readiness Criteria: maintenance activity and return-to-service status are recorded for every instrument taken out of service for repair.

TC-TEQ-024Instrument Storage
Verification Checks
  • Protect equipment from moisture, dust, and excessive temperature
  • Protect equipment from physical damage, shock, and unauthorized use
  • Protect equipment from chemical contamination and improper battery storage
  • For photometric equipment, give special attention to optical surfaces, detector protection, calibration condition, storage environment, and mechanical alignment
Acceptance / Record

Readiness Criteria: storage conditions protect calibration condition and physical integrity, with photometric equipment given additional optical and alignment protection.

TC-TEQ-025Instrument Pre-Use Verification
Verification Checks
  • Confirm correct instrument and correct measurement range before each critical test
  • Verify calibration is valid and physical condition is satisfactory
  • Check leads/probes and battery/power satisfactory
  • Complete a functional check, and confirm correct accessories and test configuration
Acceptance / Record

Readiness Criteria: the full pre-use checklist is completed and satisfactory before any critical test begins.

C

Test Quality

10 topics
TC-TEQ-026Test-Method Validation
Verification Checks
  • Review the test objective, equipment, and test configuration against the intended purpose
  • Review the measurement method, acceptance criteria, and environmental conditions
  • Confirm safety requirements and recording requirements are addressed
  • Recognize that the correct instrument used with the wrong method can still produce an invalid result
Acceptance / Record

Readiness Criteria: the selected test method is confirmed technically suitable for the intended measurement before the result is relied on.

TC-TEQ-027Test-Result Review
Verification Checks
  • Review equipment identification, test date, test method, instrument, and calibration for every important result
  • Check measured values against acceptance criteria and the Pass/Fail outcome
  • Look for missing values, impossible values, and unit errors
  • Look for transcription errors, incorrect acceptance limits, and inconsistent results
Acceptance / Record

Readiness Criteria: every important test result passes a technical review that specifically screens for missing, impossible, or inconsistent values before acceptance.

TC-TEQ-028Acceptance-Limit Verification
Verification Checks
  • Confirm the acceptance criterion used comes from an approved specification, drawing, or standard
  • Confirm it may otherwise come from a manufacturer requirement, approved test procedure, or contract requirement
  • Do not create an acceptance limit merely because a value appears technically reasonable
  • Confirm the applicable acceptance basis is identified in the test documentation
Hold Point

Hold Point: a result is not accepted against a limit that cannot be traced to an approved technical basis.

TC-TEQ-029Test-Data Quality Review
Verification Checks
  • Check for complete data, correct units, and correct equipment ID
  • Check for correct circuit ID, correct location, and correct date
  • Confirm records are legible with no unexplained alterations
  • Verify corrections are traceable and supporting raw data is retained where applicable
Acceptance / Record

Readiness Criteria: recorded test data is complete, correctly identified, and free of unexplained alteration.

TC-TEQ-030Repeatability Verification
Verification Checks
  • Where appropriate, confirm repeated measurements use the same instrument, test configuration, and test point
  • Confirm the same environmental conditions and procedure where practical
  • Investigate large unexplained variation, unstable measurements, or operator-dependent results
  • Investigate equipment malfunction, poor test connection, or environmental influence
  • Remember repeatability is not the same as accuracy — a measurement can be repeatable but systematically incorrect
Acceptance / Record

Readiness Criteria: unexplained variation between repeated measurements is investigated and resolved before the result is accepted as reliable.

TC-TEQ-031Test-Record Review
Verification Checks
  • Verify test-procedure reference, equipment ID, and test-equipment ID
  • Verify calibration reference and test values are recorded
  • Confirm acceptance criteria and result are recorded
  • Verify engineer signature, witness, date, and revision are complete
Acceptance / Record

Readiness Criteria: every completed test record carries a full, signed set of identification, values, and revision information before it is filed.

TC-TEQ-032Technical Review
Verification Checks
  • Conduct an independent technical review of test methodology, results, and acceptance limits
  • Review abnormal values, failed tests, and retesting
  • Review photometry, CCR performance, and system integration where applicable
  • Ask whether the recorded evidence actually demonstrates compliance with the specified requirement
Acceptance / Record

Readiness Criteria: critical documentation passes a technical review that confirms the recorded evidence demonstrates compliance with the specified requirement — not merely a Pass label.

TC-TEQ-033Independent Verification
Verification Checks
  • Where required by the project quality plan, independently verify critical electrical and photometric measurements
  • Independently verify PAPI alignment, CCR performance, and system integration where required
  • Independently verify final acceptance testing where required
  • Record the original result, independent verification result, reviewer, date, method, and final disposition
Acceptance / Record

Readiness Criteria: independent verification, where required, is fully traceable — original result, verification result, reviewer, and disposition are all on record.

TC-TEQ-034Revision Control
Verification Checks
  • Control revisions of test procedures, test forms, checklists, and calibration procedures
  • Control revisions of equipment guides, acceptance criteria, commissioning reports, test records, and engineering procedures
  • Confirm the current revision is identified and previous revisions are controlled
  • Confirm changes are documented, approval is recorded, and obsolete documents are prevented from unintended use
Acceptance / Record

Readiness Criteria: only the current, approved revision of any controlled document is in active use, with obsolete versions withdrawn.

TC-TEQ-035Test-Procedure Audit
Verification Checks
  • Audit the procedure for correct test objective, scope, applicable equipment, and test method
  • Audit correct safety requirements, test configuration, measurement parameters, and acceptance criteria
  • Audit correct test sequence, recording format, calibration requirements, and responsibility
  • Audit correct witness requirements, revision, and approval
  • Ask whether the procedure is approved, current, technically appropriate, correctly referenced for acceptance limits, and consistent with the actual equipment used
Acceptance / Record

Readiness Criteria: the test procedure itself is confirmed approved, current, technically appropriate, and consistent with the equipment actually used — closing the Testing & Commissioning Manual on a procedure that has itself been verified.

Engineering Quality Principles

Eight principles run underneath every topic in this division — they are what the checklists above exist to enforce.

1

Measurement Traceability

Every critical result should be traceable to the instrument and calibration status.

2

Method Validity

A technically inappropriate test method cannot produce a reliable acceptance decision merely because the instrument is calibrated.

3

Acceptance-Basis Control

Acceptance limits must come from the applicable approved technical basis.

4

Raw Data Preservation

Where practical, retain the original measurement data supporting the final report.

5

Independent Review

Critical results should receive appropriate technical review.

6

Out-of-Calibration Control

Results potentially affected by invalid equipment must be identified and technically assessed.

7

Revision Control

Only the approved/current test procedure should be used.

8

Evidence-Based Acceptance

A Pass/Fail decision should be supported by identifiable technical evidence.

Q5What is the Quality Gate Sequence in Division 25?

The result-quality gate runs as a fixed sequence — no critical measurement is treated as final acceptance evidence until every stage below has been satisfied. Raw Value & Unit: The raw measured value is recorded with correct units before any evaluation takes place; Equipment & Calibration: The test equipment used is correctly identified and its calibration status is valid at the time of test; Acceptance-Limit Check: The acceptance limit applied is traceable to an approved specification, drawing, standard, or procedure; Result Evaluation: The measured value is evaluated against the correct acceptance limit for a Pass/Fail outcome; Technical Review: The result and its supporting evidence are independently reviewed for completeness and consistency; Retest / Corrective Action: Retesting or corrective action is completed and documented wherever required; Final Acceptance: The reviewed, traceable result is closed out and archived as part of the commissioning record.

Q6What engineering quality principles apply in Division 25?

Eight principles run underneath every topic in this division — they are what the checklists above exist to enforce. (1) Measurement Traceability — Every critical result should be traceable to the instrument and calibration status; (2) Method Validity — A technically inappropriate test method cannot produce a reliable acceptance decision merely because the instrument is calibrated; (3) Acceptance-Basis Control — Acceptance limits must come from the applicable approved technical basis; (4) Raw Data Preservation — Where practical, retain the original measurement data supporting the final report; (5) Independent Review — Critical results should receive appropriate technical review; (6) Out-of-Calibration Control — Results potentially affected by invalid equipment must be identified and technically assessed; (7) Revision Control — Only the approved/current test procedure should be used; (8) Evidence-Based Acceptance — A Pass/Fail decision should be supported by identifiable technical evidence.

Q7What happens when an instrument is found out of calibration in Division 25?

When an instrument is discovered out of calibration mid-project, the same nine-step sequence applies regardless of which division's test it affects. (1) Identify — Determine which instrument is out of calibration; (2) Remove From Service — Stop use of the instrument immediately; (3) Identify Affected Tests — Determine which tests and dates used the instrument; (4) Review History — Check the calibration history and prior due dates; (5) Assess Impact — Determine the potential impact on affected results; (6) Technical Assessment — Formally review the affected results; (7) Retest — Retest where required and update results; (8) Document — Record the corrective action taken; (9) Return to Service — Restore the equipment to controlled status once recalibrated.
