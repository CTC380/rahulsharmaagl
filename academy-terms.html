2. Our Programs

The Academy offers several categories of programs, each with its own format, duration and fee, as described on the relevant program page at the time of enrollment:

  • Micro Courses — short, focused online modules (typically 2–5 hours) on a single AGL topic.
  • Professional & Specialist Programs — online courses (typically 20–50 hours) covering a defined AGL subject area such as electrical systems, design, or automation.
  • Flagship Programs — including the Advanced AGL Design & Compliance Program, AGL Photometry Specialist Program, and Airport Lighting Maintenance Engineer Program.
  • Master Diploma in Airfield Ground Lighting Engineering — a comprehensive, multi-month online diploma-level program.
  • Hands-On & On-Site Workshops — in-person, multi-day programs involving live field work and real equipment exposure, described further in Section 5.
  • Corporate AGL Certification Program — a customized, organization-level on-site training and certification track, described further in Section 6.

Current program fees, durations and curricula are listed on the respective program pages and are subject to change for future enrollments as described in Section 11.
