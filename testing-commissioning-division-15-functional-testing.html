Division 15 · FUNC · Integrated Operational Testing

Functional Testing

This division verifies how the complete AGL system behaves during actual operational conditions — energization, de-energization, intensity control, switching, alarms, faults, control modes, interlocks, emergency operation, failure response, recovery, power restoration, and standby operation. Divisions 02–14 establish that individual equipment, circuits, and control interfaces are technically ready; Division 15 demonstrates that they work together as an operational system.

Distinction: component testing proves that equipment works. Functional testing proves that the equipment works correctly together as an operational AGL system. Every topic in this division follows the same underlying chain — Command → System Response → Field Operation → Monitoring/Feedback → Alarm/Indication → Recovery — before the system is carried forward into performance/photometric verification and final acceptance.

Before Functional Testing Begins

Two requirement groups should be closed out before the Division 15 team starts working through the topic list.

Reference Package

  • Approved control philosophy, functional requirement, and emergency philosophy documents
  • Circuit, CCR, and ALCMS/HMI schedules with correct circuit-to-CCR association
  • Approved alarm matrix and interlock logic schedule
  • Divisions 02–14 test records showing equipment-level PASS status
  • Standby/backup system design and approved transfer philosophy

Coordination & Access

  • Confirm Divisions 01–14 are closed or formally accepted with open items
  • Coordinate energization windows and emergency/failure simulations with ATC/airport operations
  • Confirm personnel are briefed on which circuits/modes will be commanded live
  • Do not assume automatic power-restoration behaviour is acceptable — verify it against the approved control philosophy

The 18 Topics

Grouped by functional-test focus, in the order the system is normally worked through — click any topic to expand its verification checks and pass criteria.

A

Circuit Functional Operation

3 topics
TC-FUNC-001Circuit Operation Testing
Verification Checks
  • Verify correct circuit identification
  • Verify correct CCR association
  • Verify correct circuit selection
  • Verify correct connected load
  • Verify correct fixture operation
  • Verify correct monitoring, control response, and status feedback
  • Select circuit, energize, observe the complete circuit, then de-energize
  • Check for dark fixtures, incorrect fixtures operating, or abnormal current
Note

Sequence: Select Circuit → Energize → Observe Complete Circuit → Verify Field Fixtures → Verify CCR → Verify ALCMS/HMI Status → De-energize.

Acceptance / Record

Pass Criteria: the circuit energizes and de-energizes correctly with the right fixtures, CCR response, and ALCMS/HMI status — no dark fixtures, incorrect operation, or unexpected alarms.

TC-FUNC-002Circuit Energization Test
Verification Checks
  • Verify the energization command, CCR response, and circuit current
  • Verify fixture illumination across the circuit
  • Verify control-system indication and alarm status
  • Verify equipment status following energization
  • Record command, CCR response, field response, and feedback per circuit
  • Check for delayed energization, unexpected trip, or abnormal current
Note

Sequence: Energization Command → CCR → Series Circuit → Fixtures → Feedback.

Acceptance / Record

Pass Criteria: the circuit energizes promptly on command with correct CCR response, normal current, correct fixture response, and correct status indication.

TC-FUNC-003Circuit De-Energization Test
Verification Checks
  • Verify the OFF command and CCR response
  • Verify circuit de-energization and complete fixture extinction
  • Verify status feedback and alarm behaviour on shutdown
  • Confirm the circuit reaches the intended OFF state
  • Confirm no unintended operation of another circuit occurs
  • Check for fixtures remaining energized, delayed shutdown, or wrong circuit switching OFF
Acceptance / Record

Pass Criteria: the commanded circuit reaches OFF completely and promptly, with correct status feedback and no unintended effect on any other circuit.

B

Intensity & Switching

2 topics
TC-FUNC-004Intensity-Control Test
Verification Checks
  • Test every applicable intensity step from the operating interface through to the field
  • Verify correct command and correct CCR response at each step
  • Verify correct field intensity response and correct HMI feedback
  • Confirm no unintended circuit change occurs during a step change
  • Record commanded step, CCR step, field response, and HMI feedback per circuit
Note

Sequence: Operator Command → HMI/ALCMS → PLC/Controller → CCR → Series Circuit → AGL Fixtures.

Acceptance / Record

Pass Criteria: every commanded intensity step is reproduced correctly at the CCR, in the field, and on the HMI feedback, with no unintended circuit change.

TC-FUNC-005Switching-Logic Test
Verification Checks
  • Verify individual, group, sequential, and intensity switching
  • Verify automatic switching where applicable, and manual switching
  • Verify interlock logic and status feedback during switching
  • Check for wrong circuit operation or wrong sequence
  • Check for unintended simultaneous switching or logic conflict
Note

Sequence: Command → Logic → Output → Equipment → Feedback.

Acceptance / Record

Pass Criteria: the programmed switching logic operates only the intended equipment, in the intended sequence, with correct feedback and no logic conflict.

C

Alarms & Faults

3 topics
TC-FUNC-006Alarm Verification
Verification Checks
  • Test applicable alarms — CCR alarm, circuit fault, equipment fault, communication fault
  • Test power failure, control-system fault, emergency condition, and project-defined alarms
  • Verify the alarm is generated, displayed, and recorded correctly
  • Record test condition, alarm generated, HMI, SCADA, and history per alarm
Note

Sequence: Fault/Condition → Detection → Alarm → HMI/SCADA → Operator Indication → Alarm History.

Acceptance / Record

Pass Criteria: every applicable alarm generates correctly, displays on HMI/SCADA, and is captured in the alarm history.

TC-FUNC-007Fault Indication Test
Verification Checks
  • Verify correct fault detection and correct equipment identification
  • Verify correct fault message, alarm indication, status, and event logging
  • Confirm the indication identifies the actual affected equipment/circuit, not a generic system fault
  • Example: a CCR-03 fault should not appear merely as "AGL System Fault" if the approved system is designed to identify the specific CCR
Acceptance / Record

Pass Criteria: the fault indication correctly names the specific affected equipment/circuit, per the approved system design, and logs the event.

TC-FUNC-008Control-Function Test
Verification Checks
  • Verify equipment selection, ON/OFF, and intensity control
  • Verify group control, manual control, and automatic control
  • Verify mode selection, emergency control, and status feedback
  • Test each control function individually, then as part of the complete operational sequence
Acceptance / Record

Pass Criteria: every applicable control function operates correctly both individually and as part of the full operational sequence.

D

Local, Remote & Emergency Operation

3 topics
TC-FUNC-009Local-Control Test
Verification Checks
  • Verify local ON and local OFF operation
  • Verify intensity control where applicable from the local interface
  • Verify local status indication and equipment response
  • Verify remote indication where applicable
  • Confirm the system follows the approved local/remote control priority hierarchy
Acceptance / Record

Pass Criteria: local control operates correctly and the approved local/remote control priority is respected.

TC-FUNC-010Remote-Control Test
Verification Checks
  • Verify remote ON, remote OFF, and intensity command from ALCMS/HMI/SCADA
  • Verify equipment selection, status feedback, and alarm feedback
  • Confirm only the intended equipment responds to the remote command
Note

Sequence: Remote Command → Control System → CCR → Circuit → Fixture → Feedback.

Acceptance / Record

Pass Criteria: remote commands reach only the intended equipment, with correct field response and correct status/alarm feedback.

TC-FUNC-011Emergency-Operation Test
Verification Checks
  • Verify emergency control, emergency switching, and emergency lighting configuration
  • Verify emergency mode indication and control priority
  • Verify alarm generation, communication, and recovery during emergency operation
Note

Sequence: Emergency Condition/Command → Emergency Logic → Required AGL Response → Field Verification → HMI/SCADA Verification.

Hold Point

Hold Point: emergency testing is coordinated with airport operations/ATC whenever the test affects operational lighting.

E

Interlock & Sequence Testing

2 topics
TC-FUNC-012Equipment Interlock Test
Verification Checks
  • Verify local/remote interlock and CCR interlock
  • Verify circuit interlock and control-source interlock
  • Verify emergency interlock and conflicting-command interlock
  • Verify maintenance interlock where applicable
  • Confirm the correct equipment remains protected and correct indication appears
Note

Sequence: Attempt Applicable Conflicting/Prohibited Operation → Interlock Operates → Unintended Operation Prevented → Correct Indication Generated.

Acceptance / Record

Pass Criteria: every applicable interlock prevents the prohibited operation, generates correct indication, and the event is recorded where applicable.

TC-FUNC-013System Sequence Test
Verification Checks
  • Verify the complete predefined operating sequence, step by step, in the correct order
  • Check sequence timing where specified, and equipment response at each step
  • Check interlocks, feedback, alarms, and the final system condition
  • Record each sequence step against expected and actual response
Note

Sequence: Operator Command → ALCMS → CCR Selection → Circuit Energization → Intensity Selection → Field Lighting Operation → Status Feedback → Alarm Monitoring.

Acceptance / Record

Pass Criteria: every step of the predefined sequence occurs in the correct order, within any specified timing, ending in the correct final system condition. This is one of the most important tests in Division 15.

F

Failure & Recovery

4 topics
TC-FUNC-014Failure-Response Test
Verification Checks
  • Test approved failure conditions — CCR failure, circuit failure, control communication failure
  • Test power interruption, controller failure, HMI failure, and network/communication failure
  • Verify correct failure detection, correct alarm, and correct affected-equipment identification
  • Confirm no unsafe or unintended switching occurs, and correct fallback behaviour applies
Note

Sequence: Failure → Detection → Alarm → Indication → Required System Response.

Acceptance / Record

Pass Criteria: the approved failure condition is detected, alarmed, and correctly identified, with the required fallback response and no unsafe switching.

TC-FUNC-015Recovery Test
Verification Checks
  • Verify the fault clears correctly and the alarm clears correctly
  • Verify equipment returns to the intended state and communication restores
  • Verify status feedback restores and the event is recorded, with no residual fault
  • Check for a manual-reset requirement versus automatic recovery, and any persistent alarm
Note

Sequence: Normal Operation → Controlled Fault → Fault Detection → Alarm → Fault Removal → System Recovery → Normal Operation.

Acceptance / Record

Pass Criteria: the system returns cleanly to normal operation once the fault is removed, with no residual fault, persistent alarm, or unexpected equipment operation.

TC-FUNC-016Power-Restoration Test
Verification Checks
  • Verify CCR, ALCMS, PLC restart, and HMI restart behaviour after power restoration
  • Verify communication restoration and field equipment status
  • Verify alarm generation, event logging, and the required operating state after restoration
  • Confirm automatic restoration is not assumed acceptable — check against the approved control philosophy
Note

Sequence: Normal Operation → Controlled Power Interruption → System Response → Power Restoration → System Initialization → Equipment Recovery → Status Verification.

Acceptance / Record

Pass Criteria: after power restoration, the system returns to the correct predefined state as required by the approved control philosophy — not merely whatever state results automatically.

TC-FUNC-017Standby-System Test
Verification Checks
  • Verify standby power supply, UPS, backup control system, and redundant communication where provided
  • Verify standby PLC, backup HMI, and emergency control system where provided
  • Verify standby availability, automatic/manual transfer, and transfer indication
  • Verify equipment, control, and monitoring continuity, plus return to normal supply/system
Note

Sequence: Normal System → Standby Condition → Transfer → Standby Operation → Monitoring → Return to Normal.

Acceptance / Record

Pass Criteria: the standby/backup system transfers correctly, maintains continuity of equipment, control, and monitoring, and returns cleanly to the normal system.

G

Final Functional Acceptance

1 topic
TC-FUNC-018Functional Acceptance Test
Verification Checks
  • Confirm normal operation — circuit, energization, de-energization, intensity, switching, and status all verified
  • Confirm control — control functions, local control, remote control, mode transfer, and equipment selection all verified
  • Confirm safety/interlock — interlocks, conflicting commands, emergency operation, and required safety responses all verified
  • Confirm alarm/fault — alarm generation, fault indication, correct equipment identification, and event/alarm history all verified
  • Confirm failure/recovery — failure response, recovery, power restoration, communication recovery, and standby system all verified
  • Confirm all functional tests are completed, results evaluated, punch-list reviewed, defects corrected, and retesting completed
Acceptance / Record

Readiness Criteria: every category (A–F above) is closed with a PASS, RETEST REQUIRED items are cleared or formally accepted, and Functional Acceptance is signed — closing Division 15 and handing the system into performance/photometric verification and final handover.

Failure & Recovery Handling

When a functional test uncovers a genuine deviation rather than an approved test condition, the same sequence applies across every topic in this division.

1
Detect

Confirm exactly which command, step, or condition produced the unexpected result.

2
Isolate

Identify the specific affected circuit, CCR, or control element — not a generic system fault.

3
Classify

Safety-critical / test-blocking / minor, and whether it affects operational lighting.

4
Log

Add to the punch-list with owner and target date.

5
Correct

Rectify the equipment, logic, or configuration causing the deviation.

6
Retest

Re-run the specific functional test against the same pass criteria.

7
Record

Update the FUNC matrix and punch-list before sign-off.

8
Escalate

Unresolved test-blocking items withhold Functional Acceptance sign-off.
