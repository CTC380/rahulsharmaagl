Most Important Design Principle

Division 08 should remain the product technology layer. It converts requirements defined elsewhere in the Innovation Hub into next-generation products — it does not itself define strategy, AI/IoT capability, or digital-twin infrastructure.

Division 02 — Smart Airfield Lighting defines requirements for intelligent fixtures and lighting systems; Division 03 — AI for AGL supplies AI capability for self-diagnosis and product analytics; Division 04 — IoT & Connected AGL supplies connectivity for smart products; Division 05 — Digital Engineering supplies digital product models and digital twins; Division 06 — Robotics & Autonomous Inspection supplies automated inspection and field-performance data; Division 07 — Sustainable AGL supplies energy, carbon, material and circular-economy requirements. Division 08 converts these requirements into next-generation products, Division 15 — Intelligent Maintenance feeds back product-condition and lifecycle-performance data, and Division 25 — Innovation Database & Project Intelligence stores the resulting product-innovation knowledge and lessons learned.

Its strongest connection is to the AGL Product Encyclopedia, which becomes the structured knowledge base for every product this division evaluates or develops — each record carrying specifications, reliability data, field performance, failure history, maturity level and evaluation status through to Preferred / Reference Product status.

Division 08 turns engineering problems and technology gaps into standardized, qualified, field-proven AGL products, with the Product Encyclopedia as the permanent record of that journey.
