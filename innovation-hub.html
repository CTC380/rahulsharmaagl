AGL Innovation Hub

The future-technology layer of the AGL Knowledge Hub — smart lighting, artificial intelligence, IoT, digital engineering, robotics, sustainability, new products, emerging technologies, automation, prototyping, proof-of-concept and pilot projects, technology benchmarking, IP and commercialization, research collaboration, technology watch, and the master innovation database. Organized across 25 divisions.

All 25 divisions (441 topics & fields) are structured and published.

What makes this different from the other AGL references: the Failure Database, Inspection Handbook, and Maintenance Handbook exist to store and apply known engineering knowledge. The Innovation Hub exists to create it. Its core function isn't to document what's already known — it's to turn AGL problems and ideas into validated future engineering solutions, through a structured cycle of concept, prototype, proof-of-concept, pilot, and technical review, before that solution ever reaches the other references.

Every innovation moves through twelve stages: 1. Problem; 2. Idea; 3. Technology Review; 4. Concept; 5. Feasibility; 6. Prototype; 7. Proof of Concept; 8. Pilot; 9. Validation; 10. Technical Review; 11. Product / Engineering Implementation; 12. Knowledge Publication.
