Factory Acceptance Testing (FAT)

Before any CCR, isolation transformer, AGL fixture, or associated system leaves the manufacturer's facility, it passes through a formal Factory Acceptance Test. Division 17 verifies — at the point of manufacture — that the supplied equipment conforms to the approved design, technical specification, drawings, ratings, and functional requirements agreed for the AGL project, with objective documented evidence before dispatch to site.

Philosophy: FAT proves the manufactured equipment/system is acceptable before shipment. Site testing proves the delivered and installed equipment performs correctly in its actual airport installation after shipment. Division 17 is therefore a distinct pre-dispatch gate — it should not duplicate, and should not be confused with, the site-based scopes covered in Divisions 02–16.

Four Levels of Confidence

FAT is not simply "the manufacturer demonstrates that the equipment turns ON." A proper FAT establishes four escalating levels of confidence before the equipment is accepted for dispatch.

LEVEL 1

Product Conformity

"Is the correct equipment being supplied?"
  • Model
  • Rating
  • Components
  • Materials
  • Configuration
LEVEL 2

Electrical & Technical Performance

"Does the equipment meet its specified technical parameters?"
  • Electrical
  • Performance
  • Protection
  • Photometry where applicable
LEVEL 3

Functional Performance

"Does the equipment perform its intended functions?"
  • Control
  • Alarms
  • Communication
  • Functional operation
LEVEL 4

Documented Acceptance

"Is there objective evidence that the equipment has passed?"
  • Test records & calibration certificates
  • Witness records
  • Punch list & NCR closure
  • FAT report & certificate

Before Testing Begins

Two conditions should be established before any equipment is progressed through electrical, functional, or performance FAT.

Test the Actual Supply Configuration

  • The FAT should test the actual equipment configuration intended for supply, not merely a generic manufacturer's model
  • Correct document revision, approved status, model, rating, configuration, applicable options, and component list are verified before testing
  • No unapproved substitution should be accepted merely because the replacement appears technically similar
  • Acceptance criteria and numerical limits come from the approved specification and FAT procedure, never assumed

Division Boundary

  • FAT = equipment/system accepted before shipment
  • SAT/Site Testing = equipment accepted after installation (Divisions 01–16)
  • Functional testing at FAT verifies the equipment's own functions; complete installed-system behaviour is verified on site
  • Factory photometric testing does not automatically replace site photometric testing

A

Planning & Documentation

3 topics
TC-FAT-001FAT Planning
Verification Checks
  • Confirm equipment/system covered by FAT, FAT location, and FAT date
  • Confirm manufacturer/supplier, client/consultant participation, and test personnel
  • Confirm witness requirements, test equipment, and test procedures
  • Confirm applicable drawings, technical specifications, and acceptance criteria
  • Confirm test duration and required records
  • Identify what will be tested, how, who will witness, PASS/FAIL basis, failure process, and documentation to be issued
Acceptance / Record

Acceptance Criteria: FAT procedure and test schedule are approved, test equipment and drawings are available, client witness is confirmed, and acceptance criteria are defined before testing begins.

TC-FAT-002FAT Procedure Preparation
Verification Checks
  • Confirm the procedure defines scope, equipment identification, and applicable documents
  • Confirm test sequence, methodology, and test equipment are documented
  • Confirm safety requirements and acceptance criteria are stated
  • Confirm recording format and witness points are identified
  • Confirm NCR process, retesting procedure, and final acceptance procedure are included
  • Verify alignment: approved specification → approved design → manufacturer documentation → FAT requirements
Acceptance / Record

Acceptance Criteria: the formal FAT procedure is prepared and approved before execution, fully traceable to the approved specification and design.

TC-FAT-003FAT Documentation Review
Verification Checks
  • Review approved drawings, datasheets, technical specifications, and equipment schedules
  • Review nameplate information, wiring/schematic/GA/control drawings, and communication architecture
  • Review protection settings, test procedures, calibration certificates, and prior test records where applicable
  • Verify correct document revision, approved status, equipment model, rating, configuration, applicable options, and component list
Hold Point

Hold Point: the FAT should test the actual equipment configuration intended for supply, not merely a generic manufacturer's model — resolve any documentation discrepancy before physical testing.

B

Physical Equipment Verification

4 topics
TC-FAT-004Equipment Visual Inspection
Verification Checks
  • Inspect general construction, enclosure, paint/coating, and mechanical condition
  • Inspect components, wiring, terminals, cable entries, connectors, and cooling/ventilation
  • Inspect labels, warning signs, internal arrangement, and accessibility
  • Check for physical damage, loose components, poor workmanship, and incorrect assembly
  • Check for missing components, unidentified wiring, damaged terminals, and incorrect cable routing
Hold Point

Hold Point: a detailed physical inspection is completed before energizing or electrically testing the equipment.

TC-FAT-005Nameplate Verification
Verification Checks
  • Record manufacturer, model, serial number, and equipment type from the nameplate
  • Record voltage, current, frequency, power rating, and protection rating
  • Record other project-required nameplate information
  • Cross-check: approved datasheet ↔ nameplate ↔ actual equipment
Acceptance / Record

Acceptance Criteria: nameplate data is fully legible and consistent across the datasheet, nameplate, and actual equipment.

TC-FAT-006Rating Verification
Verification Checks
  • Verify input/output voltage, current, power, and frequency as applicable
  • Verify insulation level, environmental rating, protection rating, and temperature rating
  • Verify load capacity and communication specification where applicable
  • Compare: approved specification → datasheet → actual equipment
  • Record result as PASS, FAIL, or CLARIFICATION REQUIRED
Acceptance / Record

Acceptance Criteria: all applicable ratings on the actual equipment match the approved specification and datasheet.

TC-FAT-007Material/Component Verification
Verification Checks
  • Verify component manufacturer, model, rating, and quantity
  • Verify approved-alternative status for any substituted component
  • Verify major electrical, control, protection, communication, and mechanical components
  • Confirm no unapproved substitution is accepted merely because the replacement appears technically similar
Acceptance / Record

Acceptance Criteria: the major components and materials correspond to the approved equipment configuration and BOM.

C

Electrical & Performance Testing

4 topics
TC-FAT-008Electrical Testing
Verification Checks
  • Perform insulation resistance, continuity, and earth continuity testing as applicable
  • Verify voltage, current, and circuit resistance
  • Verify wiring, polarity, control-circuit, and auxiliary supply
  • Record: electrical test → recorded result → acceptance criterion → PASS/FAIL
Acceptance / Record

Acceptance Criteria: every applicable electrical test result meets the acceptance criterion defined in the approved FAT procedure.

TC-FAT-009Functional Testing
Verification Checks
  • Verify ON/OFF operation, mode selection, local control, and remote control as applicable
  • Verify automatic/manual operation, intensity control, display, and status indication
  • Verify fault indication and reset functions
  • Test principle: command → equipment function → output → feedback
  • Verify actual operation against the approved functional requirement
Acceptance / Record

Acceptance Criteria: functional testing answers "does it operate?" — every applicable function operates as intended before performance testing proceeds.

TC-FAT-010Performance Testing
Verification Checks
  • Verify output current, output voltage, regulation, and stability as applicable
  • Verify load response, efficiency where specified, and response time
  • Verify operating range, thermal performance, intensity performance, and communication performance
Acceptance / Record

Acceptance Criteria: performance testing answers "does it operate within the required performance parameters?" — results fall within the specified operating range.

TC-FAT-011Protection Testing
Verification Checks
  • Verify overcurrent, overvoltage, and undervoltage protection as applicable
  • Verify overtemperature, short-circuit, and earth-fault protection
  • Verify surge protection, circuit protection, and internal equipment protection
  • Test: fault/simulated test condition → protection operates → correct indication → required equipment response
Hold Point

Hold Point: protection testing follows the manufacturer's approved procedure and safe test limits at all times.

D

Control, Alarm & Communication

3 topics
TC-FAT-012Alarm Testing
Verification Checks
  • Test equipment fault, protection trip, and overtemperature alarms
  • Test communication failure, power failure, internal fault, and other project-defined alarms
  • Verify alarm generated, correct alarm displayed, and correct equipment identified
  • Verify alarm status recorded and reset/clear operation
Acceptance / Record

Acceptance Criteria: every applicable alarm generates correctly, displays the correct indication, and resets/clears as designed.

TC-FAT-013Control Testing
Verification Checks
  • Verify local, remote, manual, and automatic control
  • Verify intensity control, mode selection, start/stop, and reset
  • Verify emergency/control inputs where applicable
  • Test: control command → internal logic → equipment response → status feedback
Acceptance / Record

Acceptance Criteria: all control functions provided by the equipment respond correctly and produce accurate status feedback.

TC-FAT-014Communication Testing
Verification Checks
  • Verify Ethernet, serial communication, PLC interface, SCADA interface, and ALCMS interface as applicable
  • Verify remote monitoring, remote control, and communication status
  • Test control system → equipment, and equipment → control system
  • Verify correct equipment address, data, command, status, and alarm
  • Verify communication recovery after interruption where applicable
Acceptance / Record

Acceptance Criteria: all applicable communication interfaces exchange correct data, commands, status, and alarms in both directions.

E

Photometric FAT

1 topic
TC-FAT-015Photometric Testing Where Applicable
Verification Checks
  • Applicable to AGL fixtures, approach-light fixtures, PAPI units, airfield signs, LED modules, and other optical equipment
  • Verify light intensity, beam distribution, and beam alignment
  • Verify colour and other optical characteristics
  • Verify uniformity and intensity-step performance where applicable
  • Compare: factory measurement → approved photometric requirement
Hold Point

Hold Point: factory photometric testing establishes the equipment's manufactured performance only — it does not automatically replace site photometric testing under actual installation geometry and conditions.

F

Client Witness Testing

1 topic
TC-FAT-016Client Witness Testing
Verification Checks
  • Before witness testing, confirm the FAT procedure is approved and equipment is ready
  • Confirm test equipment is calibrated, documentation is available, and test personnel are available
  • Confirm test resultsheets are prepared and witness points are identified
  • During testing, record test performed, condition, measured result, and acceptance criterion
  • Record witness comments, PASS/FAIL, and any deviations
Acceptance / Record

Acceptance Criteria: designated FAT witness tests are conducted with the client/consultant or other nominated representatives and fully recorded.

G

Non-Conformance & Corrective Action

4 topics
TC-FAT-017FAT Punch-List
Verification Checks
  • Document missing labels, documentation corrections, and minor finishing issues
  • Document missing certificates, minor configuration issues, and pending accessories
  • Document drawing corrections and other incomplete or outstanding minor items
  • Record observation, classification, responsible party, target date, and status
Acceptance / Record

Acceptance Criteria: all incomplete, outstanding, or minor issues identified during FAT are formally logged with a responsible party and target date.

TC-FAT-018FAT Non-Conformance
Verification Checks
  • Confirm the NCR identifies equipment, test, requirement, actual result, and deviation
  • Confirm cause is established where possible, with corrective action and responsible party assigned
  • Confirm retest requirement and final disposition are recorded
  • Disposition options: correct and retest, accept with approved deviation, replace component, reconfigure, reject equipment, or further investigation
Hold Point

Hold Point: a formal NCR is raised wherever the equipment or test result does not meet the approved requirement.

TC-FAT-019FAT Corrective Action
Verification Checks
  • Follow the process: NCR → root/technical cause → corrective action → inspection → retest → verification → NCR closure
  • Verify corrective action addresses the root/technical cause, not only the symptom
  • Retain objective evidence of successful retesting wherever testing is required
Hold Point

Hold Point: do not close an NCR merely because a corrective-action statement has been issued.

TC-FAT-020FAT Retesting
Verification Checks
  • Confirm the original failed condition and completed corrective action
  • Repeat the affected test and confirm the result meets the requirement
  • Confirm associated functions remain unaffected by the corrective action
  • Record original test, NCR, corrective action, retest result, and status
Hold Point

Hold Point: where a modification affects other functions, additional related testing is performed rather than retesting only the originally failed parameter.

H

Final Acceptance & Certification

3 topics
TC-FAT-021FAT Acceptance
Verification Checks
  • Confirm documentation: FAT procedure, drawings, datasheets, test certificates, and calibration certificates
  • Confirm physical: visual inspection, nameplate, ratings, and components verified
  • Confirm electrical: testing completed, results acceptable, and protection tested
  • Confirm functional: control, alarms, communication, and performance tested
  • Confirm photometric results acceptable where applicable, and client witness comments addressed
  • Confirm punch-list reviewed, NCRs closed or formally dispositioned, and retesting completed
  • Record final status: ACCEPTED, ACCEPTED WITH PUNCH LIST, CONDITIONAL ACCEPTANCE, RETEST REQUIRED, or NOT ACCEPTED
Acceptance / Record

Acceptance Criteria: FAT acceptance is based on the complete technical evidence across documentation, physical, electrical, functional, photometric, witness, and quality checklists.

TC-FAT-022FAT Report
Verification Checks
  • Compile cover page: project, airport, equipment, manufacturer, model, serial number, FAT location, FAT date
  • Compile FAT scope, reference documents, and equipment identification table
  • Compile test-equipment/calibration table and complete test results across all applicable categories
  • Compile client witness record, punch list, and NCR register
  • State the final FAT conclusion clearly: has the equipment met the agreed FAT requirements?
Acceptance / Record

Acceptance Criteria: the final FAT report provides complete, structured, objective evidence of testing for the equipment/system covered.

TC-FAT-023FAT Certificate
Verification Checks
  • Confirm certificate identifies project, airport, equipment, manufacturer, model, and serial number
  • Confirm FAT date, location, procedure reference, and report reference are stated
  • Confirm final acceptance status and punch-list/NCR status are stated
  • Confirm sign-off by client/consultant, manufacturer, and testing engineer
  • Confirm the declaration records that recorded results have been reviewed and the equipment is accepted/rejected subject to stated conditions
Acceptance / Record

Acceptance Criteria: the FAT Certificate is issued only after completion of the agreed FAT process, and forms the formal pre-dispatch release record for the equipment.

Division 17 runs as a single continuous sequence per equipment/system, from planning through to the pre-dispatch FAT certificate.
