Division 24 · Digital Transformation Layer

Digital Forms & Automation

Division 24 turns the paper and manual forms built across Divisions 01-23 into a controlled digital ecosystem: Digital Forms → Excel Engineering Tools → Automated Records → Dashboards → Mobile Field Data → Central AGL Database → Engineering Intelligence. This division matters because the overall framework targets 200+ digital forms and 100+ Excel engineering tools — Division 24 is the layer that makes them work as one system instead of scattered spreadsheets.

Frequently Asked Questions

Quick answers about the 25 Division 24 digital forms, Excel engineering tools and automation mechanisms.

Q1What is Division 24 — Digital Forms & Automation in the AGL Engineering Forms & Templates library?

Division 24 is the digital transformation layer that turns the paper and manual forms of Divisions 01 to 23 into a controlled digital ecosystem: Digital Forms, Excel Engineering Tools, Automated Records, Dashboards, Mobile Field Data, a Central AGL Database and Engineering Intelligence. It contains 25 items (DIG-01 to DIG-25) in three categories: Digital Forms (10), Excel Engineering Tools (10) and the Automation Engine (5).

Q2What is the difference between the Digital Forms, the Excel Engineering Tools and the Automation Engine?

The Digital Forms (DIG-01 to DIG-10) are structured digital records for inspection, maintenance, testing, commissioning, NCR, work order, fault report, asset, site report and checklist. The Excel Engineering Tools (DIG-11 to DIG-20) are automated tools such as the inspection checklist, test report, cable and equipment schedules, BOQ, asset register and the maintenance, spare-parts, calibration and warranty trackers. The Automation Engine (DIG-21 to DIG-25) covers auto-generated reports, auto-numbering, automatic revision control, dashboard-based forms and mobile field forms.

Q3What do the digital forms capture and automate?

The Digital Inspection Form (DIG-01) converts paper inspection checklists into one structured digital record with automatic inspection numbering, date and time stamps, mandatory-field validation and automatic defect generation. The Digital NCR (DIG-05) carries a non-conformance through review, root cause, corrective action, verification, effectiveness check and closure, with automatic NCR numbering, due-date and escalation tracking, responsible-person assignment and aging calculation.

Q4How do the Excel engineering tools automate checklists and quantities?

The Automated Inspection Checklist (DIG-11) provides dropdown selections, automatic numbering, pass or fail logic and a live completion dashboard, and it can be printed as a controlled report. The Automated BOQ (DIG-15) tracks each item from planned quantity through order, delivery, installation and testing, calculating balance as BOQ quantity minus installed quantity and delivery percentage as delivered quantity divided by BOQ quantity times 100.

Q5How does auto-numbering prevent duplicate record IDs?

Auto-Numbering (DIG-22) assigns every digital record a unique controlled number the moment it is created, for example AGL-INS-2026-0001 for an inspection, AGL-MNT-2026-0001 for maintenance, AGL-TST-2026-0001 for testing, AGL-NCR-2026-0001 for an NCR, AGL-FAL-2026-0001 for a failure and AGL-AST-2026-0001 for an asset. The principle is that no two records ever share the same ID.

Q6How does automatic revision control work?

Automatic Revision Control (DIG-23) manages changes to digital forms and engineering documents through a formal revision chain such as Rev 00, Rev 01, Rev 02 and Rev 03, recording the date, description of change, and who prepared, checked and approved it. Previously approved revisions remain permanently retrievable as controlled historical records.

Q7What do dashboards and auto-generated reports provide?

Dashboard-Based Forms (DIG-24) turn controlled form records into live management information across project, maintenance, asset, reliability, quality, safety and photometry dashboards. Auto-Generated Reports (DIG-21) produce daily, weekly, monthly and annual reports directly from controlled digital data, so nobody has to compile a spreadsheet by hand.

Q8Can field data be captured on a smartphone or tablet?

Yes. Mobile Field Forms (DIG-25) let engineers and technicians record inspection, maintenance and defect data directly from the airfield on a smartphone or tablet. The form supports offline data capture where required, automatic date and time, user identification, asset selection, photographs, GPS location where appropriate, a digital signature, and automatic sync once connectivity is restored. QR or barcode scanning is included where implemented.

Q9How does Division 24 connect to Divisions 01–23, and can its forms be downloaded?

Divisions 01 to 23 define what gets recorded, and Division 24 defines how it is recorded, automated and connected, so one asset record such as AGL-AST-000125 can link forward to its installation, inspection, test, maintenance and failure history, spares, warranty and photometry. The overall framework targets 200+ digital forms and 100+ Excel engineering tools. Yes. Each entry has a Download as Word button that produces an uncontrolled sample Word template with letterhead, a document-control block, ruled entry fields and a three-tier approval table (Prepared By, Reviewed By, Approved By) as layout only, to be adapted under your own document-control system before use.
